## Southwest Baggage Policies

Southwest Airlines has clear rules about baggage size and weight. Knowing these rules helps avoid fees and travel stress. The airline offers a generous baggage policy compared to many other carriers. It allows passengers to bring carry-on bags and checked luggage with specific limits. Understanding these limits is important before packing your suitcase.

### Carry-on Allowance

Southwest lets each passenger bring one carry-on bag and one personal item. The carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. Size limits are 10 x 16 x 24 inches. The personal item should fit under the seat. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. There is no weight limit for carry-ons, but you must be able to lift it into the bin.

### Checked Bag Limits

Passengers can check up to two bags for free on Southwest flights. Each checked bag must weigh 50 pounds or less. The maximum size allowed is 62 inches in total. This means adding length, width, and height. Bags exceeding these limits may have extra fees. Checking bags beyond two will also cost extra. Always weigh your suitcase before heading to the airport.

### Oversized And Overweight Fees

Bags over 50 pounds but under 100 pounds face overweight fees. The fee usually costs around $75 per bag. Bags over 100 pounds are not accepted. Oversized bags larger than 62 inches incur additional fees. These fees can be higher than overweight charges. Southwest recommends keeping bags within size and weight limits to avoid fees and delays.
