## Material Care Tips

Proper care of your Brahmin bag’s material ensures it stays beautiful and durable for years. The unique leather and finishes require gentle handling and specific care routines. Paying attention to these details helps maintain the bag’s luxury feel and look.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe off dust regularly. Avoid harsh chemicals or household cleaners as they can damage the leather’s finish.

For stains, dab gently with a damp cloth and mild soap—never rub aggressively. Test any cleaner on a hidden spot first to see how the material reacts.

If your bag gets wet, blot excess moisture immediately and let it air dry naturally. Avoid heat sources like hairdryers, which can crack the leather.

### Storage Recommendations

Store your Brahmin bag in a dust bag or pillowcase to protect it from dust and scratches. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent color fading.

Maintain the bag’s shape by stuffing it with tissue paper or bubble wrap when not in use. Hanging bags can cause straps to stretch, so lay them flat or upright on a shelf.

### Repair And Maintenance

Small scuffs can often be treated with a leather conditioner designed for Brahmin’s specific leather type. Regular conditioning keeps the leather supple and prevents cracking.

If you notice any loose stitches or hardware issues, don’t delay repairs. Visiting a professional leather repair shop can save you from more expensive damage later.

Have you ever ignored a small scratch only to find it worsened over time? Timely attention can extend the life of your bag significantly.
