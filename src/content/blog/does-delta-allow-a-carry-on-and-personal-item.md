## Delta Carry-on Policy

Understanding Delta's carry-on policy is key to a smooth travel experience. Knowing what you can bring onboard without hassle saves you time and stress at the airport. Let's break down the essential details about Delta’s carry-on rules so you can pack smart and travel light.

### Carry-on Size Limits

Delta allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. Your carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin and cannot exceed **22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm)**, including handles and wheels.

Your personal item should fit under the seat in front of you. Typical examples include a backpack, purse, or laptop bag. The size limit for personal items is generally smaller but not strictly defined, so choose a compact bag that easily slides under the seat.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike some airlines, Delta does not impose a specific weight limit on carry-on bags. However, your bag must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead compartment without assistance.

Keep in mind that heavier bags can slow you down during boarding and make it harder to retrieve items mid-flight. Have you ever struggled to lift a bulky bag? Choosing a lighter carry-on can improve your travel comfort significantly.

### Allowed Items

Delta permits standard carry-on items including clothes, electronics, and essential travel gear. Liquids, gels, and aerosols are allowed but must follow TSA’s 3-1-1 rule: containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, all fitting in a single quart-sized bag.

Sharp objects, firearms, and hazardous materials are strictly prohibited in carry-on luggage. Medications and baby supplies are exceptions but should be declared at security checkpoints.

Thinking about packing your favorite travel gadgets or souvenirs? Double-check Delta’s guidelines to avoid surprises at the gate.
