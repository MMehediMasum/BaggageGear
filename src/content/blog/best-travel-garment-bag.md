## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Garment Bag Fits Most Business Trips?



A 40-43 inch garment bag fits most suits and dresses. It offers enough space for multiple outfits and accessories. Bags like Lazebox 43" and BALEINE 42" are ideal for business travel.



### Are Waterproof Garment Bags Better For Travel?



Yes, waterproof garment bags protect clothes from rain and spills. Options like MATEIN and Moulyan offer waterproof materials, ensuring wrinkle-free, clean clothes during trips.



### Can Garment Bags Hold Shoes And Accessories?



Many garment bags include extra compartments for shoes and accessories. Moulyan’s 3-in-1 bag and BALEINE’s heavy-duty bag offer dedicated shoe storage pockets.



### Do Rolling Garment Bags Improve Travel Convenience?



Rolling garment bags like DELSEY PARIS Helium DLX ease transport through airports. They reduce carrying strain and allow smooth mobility with spinner wheels.



### How To Choose A Garment Bag For Long Dresses?



Choose garment bags around 60-65 inches long for long dresses and gowns. MISSLO 65" bags offer ideal length and waterproof protection for wedding dresses and coats.



### Are Convertible Garment Bags Versatile For Different Trips?



Convertible bags like Modoker 2-in-1 and Moulyan 3-in-1 adapt for carry-on or duffel use. They offer flexible carrying styles for various travel needs.
