A Type A plug adapter is ideal for Japan. It supports 2-prong flat pins used in Japan’s electrical outlets. Choose adapters with multiple USB ports for charging convenience.

No, US devices use 3-prong plugs. Japan uses 2-prong Type A plugs. A travel adapter converts plug shape but not voltage. Check device voltage compatibility before use.



### Do These Adapters Support Usb-c Charging In Japan?



Yes, many travel adapters include USB-C ports. They allow fast charging of compatible devices like smartphones and tablets while in Japan.



### Are These Travel Adapters Compatible With Other Countries?
