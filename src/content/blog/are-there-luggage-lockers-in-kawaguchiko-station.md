## Luggage Storage Options

Traveling through Kawaguchiko Station can be much more comfortable if you know where to safely store your luggage. Whether you want to explore the nearby sights hands-free or simply avoid carrying heavy bags, the station offers several luggage storage options. Understanding these choices can save you time and stress, especially during busy travel days.

### Station Lockers Availability

Kawaguchiko Station provides several coin-operated lockers right inside the station building. These lockers are located near the ticket gates, making it easy to drop off your bags before heading out. However, during peak travel seasons, such as cherry blossom or autumn foliage periods, these lockers can fill up quickly.

It’s a good idea to arrive early or have a backup plan if you can’t find an available locker. Some nearby shops and hotels also offer luggage storage services, which can be a convenient alternative.

### Sizes And Prices

The lockers come in various sizes to fit different luggage types. Typically, you’ll find small, medium, and large lockers. Small lockers usually hold backpacks or handbags, medium lockers fit carry-on suitcases, and large lockers accommodate bigger travel bags.

### How To Use

Find the coin lockers near the station’s main entrance or close to the ticket gates. Each locker has a door with a key slot or a digital panel. Open an empty locker, place your luggage inside, and then close the door securely.

You will need to either insert coins or follow instructions on the screen to lock it. Remember to keep the key or the receipt with the locker number safe, as you’ll need it to retrieve your items. Some lockers have size labels, so choose one that fits your bags comfortably.

### Payment Methods

Most coin lockers at Kawaguchiko Station accept 100-yen coins, which are returned when you unlock the locker. However, some newer lockers might accept electronic payments or IC cards like Suica or Pasmo. It’s wise to carry enough change just in case the electronic option is unavailable or out of order.

If you find a locker that requires a key deposit, keep the key until you return to unlock it. You can usually use the locker for a day or longer, but check the specific time limits posted on the lockers.

### Tips For Security

Always double-check that the locker is firmly locked before walking away. Avoid using lockers that appear damaged or have unclear instructions. If you have valuable items, consider using station lockers that are monitored or ask staff for advice on secure options.

Think about how accessible your locker is while exploring. Choose a locker location that’s easy to find and not too isolated. Have you ever lost a locker key or forgotten the number? Write down your locker number and keep the key in your wallet or phone case to avoid any hassle.
