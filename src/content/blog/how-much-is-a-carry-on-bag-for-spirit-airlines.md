## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Spirit Airlines has a unique carry-on policy that can catch many travelers off guard. Understanding their rules can save you from unexpected fees and stress at the gate. Knowing exactly what counts as a carry-on and what size limits apply lets you pack smart and avoid last-minute hassles.

### Size And Weight Limits

Spirit Airlines allows one free personal item per passenger, but their carry-on bags come with strict size limits. Your carry-on bag must not exceed **22 x 18 x 10 inches**, including handles and wheels. This size is smaller than many other airlines, so measure your bag carefully before you fly.

There is no official weight limit for carry-ons, but you must be able to lift your bag into the overhead bin without help. If your bag is too big or heavy, Spirit will charge you a fee at the gate. Have you ever faced surprise fees because your bag was slightly larger than allowed? It’s a common experience that you can avoid with a little preparation.

### Personal Item Vs Carry-on Bag

Spirit distinguishes clearly between a personal item and a carry-on bag. Your free personal item must fit under the seat in front of you and cannot exceed **18 x 14 x 8 inches**. This includes items like backpacks, purses, or laptop bags.

If you want to bring a larger carry-on bag, you’ll need to pay an additional fee. Many travelers don’t realize that their "carry-on" bag is considered a second item and will be charged accordingly. Have you checked how your bag is categorized before? It can save you money and frustration.

Here’s a quick comparison:

| Item | Size Limit | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal Item | 18 x 14 x 8 inches | Free |
| Carry-On Bag | 22 x 18 x 10 inches | Fee applies |

Planning your packing around these rules can make your trip smoother. What will you pack in your personal item to avoid paying extra for a carry-on?

### Standard Fee Rates

Spirit Airlines charges for carry-on bags based on size and weight limits. A standard carry-on bag usually costs between **$35 and $65**if you pay during booking. If you wait until airport check-in, fees can jump to **$65 to $75**, which is a big difference.

Keep in mind, Spirit Airlines allows a personal item for free that fits under the seat. If your bag is larger and doesn’t fit this category, you’ll likely pay the carry-on fee. Does your bag meet these size requirements? Checking before you pack can save money.

### Fees By Booking Method

How you book your ticket impacts the carry-on fee you’ll pay. Booking directly on Spirit’s website often offers the lowest fees for carry-ons. Third-party sites might not show these fees upfront, leading to surprises later on.

* **Online booking:**Cheapest carry-on fees, paid upfront.
* **Mobile app:**Similar to online booking, but sometimes with exclusive deals.
* **Airport counter:**Highest fees, usually avoidable by paying earlier.

From my experience, paying for carry-ons during online booking can save at least $20 compared to airport prices. Have you checked the fee differences before booking your last flight?

### Fees By Travel Stage

When you pay for your carry-on can drastically affect the cost. Spirit’s fee structure changes depending on whether you pay at booking, during online check-in, or at the airport.

## Additional Baggage Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=spirit-airlines-charges-extra-for-bags-beyond-the-free-carry-on-allowance.-understanding-these-additional-fees-helps-travelers-avoid-surprises-at-the-airport.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Spirit Airlines charges extra for bags beyond the free carry-on allowance. Understanding these additional fees helps travelers avoid surprises at the airport. Extra costs apply to checked bags, overweight items, and oversized luggage.

These fees vary by route, time of booking, and baggage size. Booking bags online usually costs less than paying at the airport. Knowing the rules saves money and stress.

### Checked Bags Costs

Checked bag fees depend on when and where you pay. Booking online before your flight offers the lowest rates. Paying at the airport usually costs more.

* First checked bag fee: around $30 to $45
* Second checked bag fee: around $40 to $55
* Third bag and beyond: higher fees, often over $100

Prices vary by flight route and season. Check Spirit’s website for exact fees before traveling.

### Overweight And Oversized Charges

Bags that weigh over 40 pounds or measure more than 62 inches face extra fees. These charges can be costly and add up quickly.

* Overweight bags (41-50 lbs): additional $30 to $50
* Overweight bags (51-70 lbs): fees rise to $100 or more
* Oversized bags (over 62 inches): extra $50 to $100

Spirit Airlines does not accept bags over 70 pounds. Prepare your luggage carefully to avoid these fees.
