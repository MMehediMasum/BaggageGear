## Delta's Baggage Policy

Delta Airlines has clear rules about baggage. Knowing these helps avoid surprises at the airport. Their baggage policy covers both checked and carry-on bags. Fees depend on the ticket type and flight route. Below is a breakdown of what you need to know.

### Standard Checked Bag Allowance

Most Delta flights allow one free checked bag for certain ticket classes. Basic Economy tickets usually do not include a free checked bag. For other classes, the first checked bag weighs up to 50 pounds (23 kg). Its size must not exceed 62 inches (158 cm) in total dimensions. The second checked bag may have extra fees. Oversized or overweight bags will also cost more. Fees vary by route and ticket type.

### Carry-on Bag Rules

Delta lets passengers bring one carry-on bag and one personal item. The carry-on bag size limit is 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm). The personal item can be a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. Both items must fit under the seat or in the overhead bin. Carry-on bags do not have a weight limit. Some items like strollers or medical equipment have special rules.




## Fees For Checked Bags

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=delta-airlines-charges-fees-for-checked-bags-on-most-flights.-these-fees-vary-based-on-the-number-of-bags-and-your-ticket-type.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Delta Airlines charges fees for checked bags on most flights. These fees vary based on the number of bags and your ticket type. Understanding these fees helps plan your travel budget and avoid surprises at the airport.

### First Bag Fee

The first checked bag usually costs $30 on domestic flights. This fee applies to most economy class tickets. Some Delta SkyMiles members and premium passengers may get this fee waived. Carrying a bag under the weight limit also helps avoid extra charges.

### Second Bag Fee

Checking a second bag typically costs $40 on U.S. domestic routes. This charge applies after the first bag fee is paid. The second bag fee can add up quickly for families or long trips. Some credit cards may cover these fees as a benefit.

### Additional Bag Charges

Bags beyond the second one have higher fees. The price can range from $100 to $200 per bag. Overweight or oversized bags cost even more. International flights have different rules and fees for extra bags. Always check Delta's website for current rates before traveling.

## Exemptions And Waivers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=delta-airlines-offers-certain-exemptions-and-waivers-on-baggage-fees.-these-help-passengers-avoid-paying-to-check-bags-in-specific-cases.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Delta Airlines offers certain exemptions and waivers on baggage fees. These help passengers avoid paying to check bags in specific cases. Understanding these exceptions can save money and stress during travel. Some passengers qualify automatically, while others need to meet specific criteria.

### Elite Status Benefits

Delta rewards its frequent flyers with elite status benefits. Members of Delta SkyMiles Medallion Program get free checked bags. The number of free bags depends on the status level.

* Silver Medallion: 1 free checked bag
* Gold Medallion: 2 free checked bags
* Platinum and Diamond Medallion: 3 free checked bags

These benefits apply to the member and up to eight travel companions on the same reservation. Elite status travelers avoid baggage fees as part of their loyalty perks.

### Credit Card Perks

Delta’s branded credit cards offer free checked bags as a key benefit. Cardholders usually get 1 or 2 free bags per flight on Delta-operated flights. This perk applies to the cardholder and up to eight people on the same reservation.

* Delta SkyMiles® Gold Card: 1 free checked bag
* Delta SkyMiles® Platinum and Reserve Cards: 1 or 2 free checked bags

Using these cards can reduce travel costs by waiving baggage fees automatically.

### Military And Special Cases

Delta supports active-duty military members with baggage fee waivers. Eligible personnel and their dependents often check bags for free. This applies on both domestic and international flights.

Other special cases may include passengers with disabilities or certain medical needs. Delta may allow extra or free checked bags for essential equipment or supplies.

Let’s break down the key details so you can pack smartly and avoid surprises.

### Weight Limits

Delta sets a clear weight limit for checked bags, usually 50 pounds (23 kilograms) for standard economy tickets. Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds (24 to 32 kilograms) incur overweight fees. If your bag tips the scale over 70 pounds, expect significantly higher charges or even rejection of the bag.

Have you ever tried lifting a heavy suitcase only to realize it’s just too much? That’s a good sign you might face extra fees. Using a luggage scale at home can help you keep track before you head to the airport.

### Size Restrictions

Size matters as much as weight. Delta considers a bag oversize if its total dimensions (length + width + height) exceed 62 inches (158 cm). Oversized bags don’t just take up more space; they also come with additional fees that can add up quickly.

Think about that big suitcase you love to pack—it could cost you more if it’s too large. Measuring your luggage before traveling can prevent last-minute fees and hassle.

Paying for your checked bag online before you arrive at the airport often costs less. Delta usually charges a lower fee if you add your bag during the booking process or through the app. This approach also speeds up your check-in since your bag fee is already settled.

At the airport, fees can be higher and payment may take longer, especially during busy times. You might face longer lines and added stress when paying at the counter or kiosk. Have you noticed how much smoother your travel feels when you handle baggage fees ahead of time?
