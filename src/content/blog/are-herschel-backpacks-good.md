## Brand History

Herschel Supply Co. has a rich brand history that shapes its backpacks today. The company blends heritage with modern style. This balance appeals to many users worldwide. Understanding the brand’s story helps explain why their backpacks stand out.

### Origins And Growth

Herschel Supply Co. started in 2009 in Canada. Two brothers, Jamie and Lyndon Cormack, founded it. They named the brand after their hometown, Herschel, Saskatchewan.

Their goal was to create simple, functional bags with a timeless look. Growth came quickly as demand rose globally. Today, Herschel backpacks are sold in over 70 countries. The company keeps expanding its product range and customer base.

### Design Philosophy

Herschel backpacks focus on clean, classic designs. They combine utility with style for everyday use. Each bag includes practical features like padded straps and laptop sleeves.

The brand uses durable materials to ensure long-lasting quality. Patterns and colors remain subtle but trendy. This approach suits many lifestyles and ages.
