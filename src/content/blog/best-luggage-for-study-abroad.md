## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Luggage Size For Study Abroad Trips?



For study abroad, a 20 to 29-inch suitcase works best. It fits essentials and meets airline carry-on or checked baggage rules. Expandable models add packing flexibility, ideal for longer stays or extra souvenirs.



### Which Luggage Features Are Important For Study Abroad Students?



Lightweight, durable hard shell or expandable softside suitcases with TSA locks and spinner wheels are ideal. These features protect belongings, ease travel, and comply with airport security for smooth trips.



### Are Luggage Sets Better For Long Study Abroad Trips?



Yes, luggage sets offer multiple sizes for different trip lengths. They provide organized packing options and matching styles, perfect for varied travel needs during a study abroad program.



### How Do Tsa Locks Benefit Study Abroad Travelers?



TSA locks allow security to inspect luggage without damage. This ensures your belongings stay secure while meeting airport safety requirements, giving peace of mind during international travel.



### Can I Use A Backpack As Carry-on Luggage For Study Abroad?



Yes, a travel backpack with laptop compartments fits airline carry-on rules. It’s practical for daily use, offers easy access to essentials, and complements checked luggage for study abroad students.



### Why Choose A Digital Luggage Scale For Study Abroad?



A digital luggage scale prevents overweight baggage fees by accurately measuring weight. It helps students pack efficiently and avoid extra charges at the airport.
