Away ships worldwide, but international delivery times vary widely. Typically, international orders take 7 to 14 business days, influenced by customs and local postal services. Shipping fees and duties may also apply depending on your country.

Let’s break down the key factors that play a role in the shipping timeline.

### Order Processing Time

Order processing is the first step after you place your Away luggage order. This phase includes verifying payment, packaging, and preparing the item for shipment. Some orders may take longer if there’s high demand or if customization is involved.

Think about a time when you ordered something online and received a shipping confirmation days later. That wait was likely the order processing period. If you want your luggage sooner, check if Away offers expedited processing or priority handling options.

### Shipping Distance

The distance between the warehouse and your delivery address is a major factor. Shipping within the same country usually takes less time compared to international deliveries. For example, if you’re in the US and Away ships from a domestic warehouse, your luggage may arrive in a few days.

But if you’re ordering from overseas, expect longer transit times. This is because your package might travel through multiple hubs before reaching you. Have you ever tracked a package and noticed it bouncing between cities? That’s the shipping network at work, affecting how fast your luggage arrives.

### Customs And Import Regulations

Customs clearance can add unpredictability to delivery times, especially for international orders. Your luggage must pass through customs inspections, which sometimes involve extra paperwork or fees. Delays can occur if customs officials need more information or if there are restrictions on certain materials.

For instance, some countries have strict rules about what types of luggage materials are allowed or require special duties. If you’re ordering Away luggage internationally, it’s wise to check your country’s import regulations beforehand. Have you encountered customs delays before? Planning ahead can save you from unexpected waits.

Shipping delays can be frustrating, especially when you’re eagerly waiting for your Away luggage to arrive. Understanding the common reasons behind these delays helps you manage expectations and plan better. Let’s look at some key factors that often slow down the shipping process.

### Weather Conditions

Severe weather can disrupt shipping routes and delay deliveries. Snowstorms, hurricanes, or heavy rain may force carriers to pause operations for safety reasons. Have you ever experienced a package stuck because of a sudden storm? It’s a reminder to check weather forecasts when tracking your shipment.

### High Demand Periods

During holidays or sales events, shipping volumes spike dramatically. This overload can stretch carrier resources thin, causing delays. If you order your Away luggage during Black Friday or the holiday season, expect longer wait times. Planning ahead could help you avoid the rush and get your luggage sooner.

### Logistics And Carrier Issues

Sometimes, delays happen behind the scenes due to logistical challenges. This includes sorting errors, misrouted packages, or labor shortages at shipping facilities. If your tracking shows no updates for a while, it might be stuck in processing. Staying in touch with customer service can give you more clarity on your shipment’s status.
