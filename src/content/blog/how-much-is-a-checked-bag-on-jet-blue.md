## Jetblue Baggage Policy 2026

JetBlue's baggage policy in 2026 offers clear guidelines for travelers. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees and travel stress. This section covers the standard allowance, carry-on rules, and checked bag limits.

### Standard Allowance

JetBlue allows one personal item for free. This item must fit under the seat in front of you. It includes small backpacks, purses, or laptop bags. Most tickets do not include a free checked bag. Some fare types may have different rules.

### Carry-on Rules

Passengers can bring one carry-on bag on board. The bag size must not exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches. It should fit in the overhead bin. Carry-on bags include suitcases, duffel bags, and larger backpacks. No weight limit applies, but the bag must be manageable.

### Checked Bag Limits

Checked bags are allowed but usually require a fee. Each bag must weigh under 50 pounds and measure less than 62 inches total (length + width + height). The first checked bag fee depends on the route and fare type. JetBlue offers discounts for Blue, Blue Plus, and Mint members.

Understanding JetBlue's checked bag fees can save you money and avoid surprises at the airport. Each bag you check has a specific charge based on its order and your ticket type. Here’s a clear breakdown of what you’ll pay for your checked bags.

### First Bag Charges

Your first checked bag on JetBlue typically costs $35 for most standard fares. However, if you have a Blue Basic ticket, checked bags are not included, so you’ll pay this fee separately. JetBlue’s TrueBlue Mosaic members and those flying Mint class enjoy waived first bag fees, which can be a real money saver if you fly often.

### Second Bag Charges

The second checked bag costs $45, which is slightly higher than the first. This fee applies regardless of your fare type unless you’re a Mosaic member or flying Mint. If you’re packing for a longer trip, think about whether you can redistribute items to avoid this extra cost or if paying $45 is worth the convenience.

### Additional Bag Fees

Any bags beyond the second come with a steep $150 fee per bag. This high charge encourages travelers to pack efficiently or consider shipping extra items separately. If you find yourself regularly needing more than two checked bags, it might be smarter to upgrade your ticket or look into JetBlue’s baggage bundles.

Blue Basic is JetBlue's lowest fare. It does not include a free checked bag. The first checked bag costs $35 on domestic flights. Fees apply per bag and per segment. This fare suits travelers who pack light or carry only a personal item.

### Blue

Blue fare includes one free checked bag on most flights. The second bag costs $35. This fare is good for travelers needing one checked bag. It balances cost and convenience well.

### Blue Plus

Blue Plus offers two free checked bags. It also includes more legroom and early boarding. This fare is ideal for families or longer trips. It reduces the overall cost of checked bags.

### Mint

Mint is JetBlue's premium cabin. It includes two free checked bags with higher weight limits. Mint passengers enjoy luxury seats and extra perks. This fare targets business travelers and those seeking comfort.

Jet Blue charges extra fees for bags that are too heavy or too large. These fees apply when your checked baggage exceeds the airline's standard size and weight limits. Knowing these charges helps avoid surprises at the airport.

Excess and oversize baggage fees vary depending on the bag's weight and dimensions. Special items also have different fees. Understanding these rules can save time and money during travel.

### Overweight Charges

Bags weighing more than 50 pounds but less than 100 pounds face overweight fees. The cost usually increases with the bag's weight. Bags over 100 pounds are often not accepted. Travelers should weigh bags before arriving at the airport to avoid these charges.

### Oversize Charges

Standard checked bags must stay within size limits. The maximum combined length, width, and height is usually 62 inches. Bags exceeding this size will incur oversize fees. These fees can be higher than overweight charges. Oversize bags may also require special handling.

### Special Items

Items like sports equipment, musical instruments, and strollers have unique fees. These items might be too large or fragile for regular baggage. Jet Blue sets specific rules and charges for such items. It is best to check these fees before packing special items.
