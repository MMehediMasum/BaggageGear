## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of Hard Shell Checked Luggage?



Hard shell luggage offers strong impact resistance and protects belongings from damage. It is often lightweight and scratch-resistant. Many models feature expandable compartments for extra packing space. Multi-directional spinner wheels provide easy mobility. TSA-approved locks enhance security during travel. These features make hard shell luggage ideal for frequent flyers.



### Which Luggage Brand Offers The Best Durability?



Samsonite is renowned for durable hard shell luggage. Their polycarbonate and polypropylene shells resist cracking and scratching. Models like Samsonite Omni 2 and Winfield 2 combine lightweight design with tough construction. Travelpro and Amazon Basics also offer reliable, durable options at competitive prices.



Brand reputation and materials ensure long-lasting use.



### How Much Extra Space Does Expandable Luggage Provide?



Expandable hard shell luggage typically offers 10% to 15% more packing space. For example, Amazon Basics models expand up to 15%. This feature is useful for souvenirs or additional items on return trips. Expansion is easy with zippers that increase the suitcase depth.



It adds convenience without compromising structure.



### Are Tsa Locks Standard On These Hard Shell Suitcases?



Yes, most hard shell checked luggage models include TSA-approved locks. Brands like Samsonite, Travelpro, and OLIXIS provide built-in TSA locks. These locks allow TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. TSA locks enhance security and comply with airline regulations. They are essential for hassle-free airport screening.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Improve Luggage Mobility?



Spinner wheels rotate 360 degrees, allowing smooth movement in any direction. This design reduces strain on arms and shoulders during travel. Eight-wheel designs, like on Travelpro Maxlite Air, offer extra stability. Multi-directional wheels enhance maneuverability through crowded airports. Spinner wheels are a key feature of modern hard shell luggage.



### What Colors And Styles Are Available For Hard Shell Luggage?



Hard shell luggage comes in various colors like black, orange, silver, blue, pink, and ivory. Styles range from sleek and minimalist to multifunctional travel trunks. Brands offer both vibrant and neutral shades to suit personal preferences. Unique colors help identify luggage quickly on baggage claim carousels.



There’s a style for every traveler.
