### Are These Backpacks Durable And Water-resistant?



Yes, many of these backpacks are made from durable, water-resistant fabric. For instance, the Bentgo Kids Backpack features a water-resistant material. Yes, most backpacks for kindergarteners come with adjustable, padded straps. This feature ensures a comfortable fit for growing children.
