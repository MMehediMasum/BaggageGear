## Spirit Airlines Personal Item Policy

Understanding Spirit Airlines' personal item policy is key to avoiding unexpected fees and hassle at the airport. Spirit is known for its budget-friendly fares, but they also have strict rules about what counts as a personal item. Knowing these details helps you pack smart and save money.

### What Qualifies As A Personal Item

A personal item on Spirit Airlines is any small bag you can place under the seat in front of you. This includes backpacks, purses, laptop bags, or small duffel bags. If your backpack fits comfortably under the seat, it usually qualifies as your personal item.

Think about the last time you traveled with a backpack—did it fit under the seat? If yes, it’s likely acceptable. However, bulky or oversized backpacks might not qualify, leading to additional charges.

### Size And Weight Restrictions

Spirit strictly enforces size limits for personal items: the maximum allowed dimensions are 18 x 14 x 8 inches (45 x 35 x 20 cm). Weight is generally not specified, but the bag must be manageable enough to fit under the seat.

One traveler shared how a slightly larger backpack was flagged at the gate, and they had to pay extra to check it. This shows how crucial it is to measure your bag beforehand. Carrying a tape measure while packing can save you from last-minute surprises.

### Comparison With Carry-on Bags

Unlike personal items, carry-on bags on Spirit Airlines must fit in the overhead bin and adhere to larger size limits (22 x 18 x 10 inches). Carry-ons usually come with an additional fee unless you purchase a specific fare bundle.

Here’s a quick comparison:

| Feature | Personal Item | Carry-On Bag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Size Limit | 18 x 14 x 8 inches | 22 x 18 x 10 inches |
| Placement | Under seat | Overhead bin |
| Cost | Free with ticket | Usually requires fee |

Choosing between a personal item and a carry-on depends on your packing style and budget. Would you rather pay extra for more space or travel light with just a backpack? Your answer can shape your Spirit Airlines experience.

### Costs For Additional Carry-ons

Spirit charges for carry-on bags that go in the overhead bin. The price depends on when you pay—online or at the airport. Booking a carry-on during online check-in costs less.
