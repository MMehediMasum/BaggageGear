## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hard Side Luggage Better Than Soft Side?



Hard side luggage offers superior protection against impacts and weather. It is durable, scratch-resistant, and often lighter. Plus, it provides a sleek, modern look, ideal for rough travel conditions. Many models also come with expandable features and TSA locks for added security and convenience.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Hard Side Luggage?



Yes, spinner wheels improve maneuverability by rotating 360 degrees. They allow easy movement in tight spaces like airports. Most top models, including Samsonite and Amazon Basics, feature multi-directional spinner wheels for smooth, effortless travel. Spinner wheels reduce strain, making luggage easier to handle.



### How Important Is Luggage Expandability For Travelers?



Expandability is crucial for flexibility, allowing up to 15% more packing space. It helps accommodate souvenirs or extra items on return trips. Many hard side suitcases, like Amazon Basics and Samsonite Freeform, include expandable zippers to maximize storage without compromising durability.



### Do All Hard Side Luggage Come With Tsa Locks?



Most quality hard side luggage, including Samsonite and KROSER, feature built-in TSA-approved locks. These locks enhance security while allowing TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. TSA locks are essential for safe, hassle-free travel, especially on international flights.



### What Size Hard Side Luggage Is Best For Checked Baggage?



Large suitcases between 26 to 30 inches are ideal for checked baggage. Models like Samsonite Winfield 2 and U. S. Traveler Boren offer ample space and durability. They comply with airline size limits and provide enough room for longer trips.



### Can Hard Side Luggage Withstand Rough Handling?



Yes, hard side luggage is designed to resist rough handling. Materials like polycarbonate and ABS provide impact resistance and scratch protection. Brands such as Traveler's Choice and Wrangler ensure durability, making hard side luggage reliable for frequent travelers.
