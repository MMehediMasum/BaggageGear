## Ipsy Glam Bag Pricing Tiers

Ipsy offers different pricing tiers for its Glam Bag subscription. Each tier provides various products and perks. This way, you can pick the option that fits your beauty needs and budget. Understanding these tiers helps you decide which plan works best for you.

### Standard Glam Bag Cost

The Standard Glam Bag costs $15 per month. It includes five sample-sized beauty products. These samples are a mix of makeup, skincare, and haircare items. Subscribers receive personalized picks based on their beauty profile. The Standard Glam Bag is a good choice for those who want to try new products without a big commitment.

### Plus And Ultimate Options

The Plus Glam Bag costs $28 per month. It contains five full-size beauty products. Plus offers more value for those who want more of their favorite brands. The Ultimate Glam Bag is $55 per month. It includes 12 full-size products. This tier is designed for beauty lovers who want a wide variety of items. Both Plus and Ultimate come with extra perks like free shipping and early access to sales.
