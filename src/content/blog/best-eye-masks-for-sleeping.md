## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes 3d Contoured Eye Masks Better For Side Sleepers?



3D contoured masks provide zero eye pressure and a comfortable fit. They block light without touching your eyes. This design suits side sleepers by avoiding discomfort and ensuring restful sleep.



### How Do Silk Eye Masks Improve Sleep Quality?



Silk masks are soft, breathable, and gentle on skin. They reduce irritation and prevent moisture loss around eyes. This promotes better relaxation and deeper sleep, especially for sensitive skin.



### Are Adjustable Straps Important For Sleep Masks?



Yes, adjustable straps ensure a snug, personalized fit. They prevent the mask from slipping during sleep. This feature enhances comfort and effective light blocking for all head sizes.



### Can Eye Masks Reduce Puffiness And Dark Circles?



Certain masks, like silk or cooling ones, help reduce puffiness. They improve blood circulation and soothe tired eyes. Regular use can minimize dark circles and refresh your eye area.



### Which Eye Masks Are Best For Travel And Flights?



Masks with breathable fabric, 3D design, and light-blocking features are ideal. They offer comfort during long flights and block cabin light. Compact masks with travel pouches are convenient for trips.



### How Does Light Blocking Improve Sleep With An Eye Mask?



Complete light blocking signals your brain to produce melatonin. It helps you fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer. Effective light blocking improves sleep quality and overall restfulness.
