## United Airlines Carry-on Policies

United Airlines has clear rules for carry-on bags. These rules help travelers pack right and avoid delays at the gate. Knowing these rules saves time and stress during your trip.

United allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. Both must fit specific size and weight limits. The airline checks these rules strictly to keep cabins safe and comfortable.

### Carry-on Size Restrictions

Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin. The maximum size allowed is:

* 9 inches high
* 14 inches wide
* 22 inches long

Bags larger than this may need to be checked at the gate. This can cause extra fees and waiting time.

### Weight Limits For Carry-on Bags

United Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. However, passengers must be able to lift their bags into the overhead bins without help.

Heavy bags that cannot be lifted easily may be asked to be checked.

### Personal Item Allowances

Besides the carry-on bag, passengers can bring one personal item. This includes:

* Backpack
* Purse
* Laptop bag
* Small briefcase

The personal item must fit under the seat in front of you. Its maximum size is 9 inches high, 10 inches wide, and 17 inches long.

## Measuring Your Carry-on Bag

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-your-carry-on-bag-correctly-is-essential-to-avoid-surprises-at-the-airport.-airlines,-including-united,-have-strict-size-limits-that-your-bag-must-meet-to-qualify-as-carry-on.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring your carry-on bag correctly is essential to avoid surprises at the airport. Airlines, including United, have strict size limits that your bag must meet to qualify as carry-on. Taking a few moments to measure your bag ensures it fits the allowed dimensions and saves you the hassle of checking it unexpectedly.

### How To Measure Dimensions Correctly

Start by laying your bag on a flat surface. Use a measuring tape to check the length, width, and height of the bag, including handles and wheels.

Remember, some airlines count every protruding part, so don't forget to include side pockets or any external attachments. Write down each measurement to compare with United's carry-on size limits.

Have you ever been surprised by a bag that seemed small but didn’t fit the rules? Measuring every part carefully helps avoid that issue.

### Tips For Weighing Your Bag At Home

Weighing your bag before heading to the airport can save you from extra fees. Use a reliable luggage scale or a simple bathroom scale to check the weight.

If using a bathroom scale, first weigh yourself without the bag, then weigh yourself holding the bag, and subtract the two numbers. This gives you the bag's weight accurately.

Try packing your bag and weighing it multiple times if you’re unsure. Adjust the contents to keep the weight under United’s limits, so you don’t have to redistribute items at the gate.
