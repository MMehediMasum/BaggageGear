### Trusted Platforms

You want to use platforms known for authentic, quality secondhand goods. Websites like **Poshmark**, **eBay**, and **Depop**often have Beis luggage listings from verified sellers. These sites offer buyer protection policies, which add a layer of security when you’re shopping.

Facebook Marketplace and local resale apps like **OfferUp**can also be good options if you prefer to see the luggage in person before buying. However, these require more caution since there may be less oversight.

### What To Look For

Look closely at the photos and descriptions sellers provide. Check for any signs of damage like scratches, broken zippers, or stains. Even small defects can affect your luggage’s durability during travel.

Ask the seller about the luggage’s travel history—how often it was used and if it was stored carefully. A bag used twice but well-maintained can be better than one used frequently but neglected.

Verify the model name and color with Beis’s official website to avoid counterfeit products. Pay attention to the price too—if it’s too good to be true, it probably is.
