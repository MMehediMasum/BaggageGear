## Southwest Carry-on Rules

Knowing Southwest’s carry-on rules can save you time and stress at the airport. They have clear guidelines on what you can bring onboard, how big your bags can be, and what counts as a personal item. Understanding these details helps you pack smarter and avoid unexpected fees or delays.

### Size And Weight Limits

Southwest allows one carry-on bag per passenger with no specific weight limit, which is great if you tend to pack heavy. However, your bag must fit in the overhead bin and not exceed the size of 10 x 16 x 24 inches.

If your bag is too large or heavy, Southwest may require you to check it instead. I once packed a bulky suitcase that barely fit the size limit, and gate agents were quite strict about enforcing this rule.

### Personal Item Allowance

In addition to your carry-on bag, you can bring one personal item on board. This item should fit under the seat in front of you, such as a backpack, purse, or laptop bag.

Many travelers overlook this allowance and try to cram everything into one bag. Have you ever struggled to find space for your personal item? Keeping it separate can make your travel experience much smoother.

### Allowed Carry-on Items

Southwest permits typical carry-on items like clothing, electronics, and small souvenirs. Liquids must follow TSA’s 3-1-1 rule: containers 3.4 ounces or less, all fitting in one quart-sized bag.

* Sharp objects like knives or scissors longer than 4 inches are not allowed.
* Sports equipment and musical instruments have special policies, so check ahead.
* Food items are generally allowed but must be properly packed.

Have you ever been surprised by what you can or cannot bring on board? Checking Southwest’s rules before packing can help you avoid last-minute repacking or confiscations.

## Carry-on Restrictions To Avoid Fees

Understanding Southwest’s carry-on restrictions helps avoid extra fees and delays. Carry-on bags must meet size and weight rules. Certain items are banned for safety. Following guidelines keeps your travel smooth and stress-free.

### Prohibited Items

Southwest does not allow dangerous items in carry-ons. These include:

* Flammable liquids and gases
* Explosives and fireworks
* Sharp objects like knives and scissors
* Weapons or firearm parts
* Compressed gases such as propane
* Toxic substances and chemicals

Check the full list on Southwest’s website before packing. These rules protect everyone on the flight.

### Overweight And Oversized Bags

Carry-on size limit is 10 x 16 x 24 inches including handles and wheels. Bags larger than this must be checked. Southwest does not charge fees for standard carry-ons within size limits.

Weight is not strictly limited but very heavy bags can be hard to store. Oversized or overweight carry-ons may be gate-checked. This can cause delays and inconvenience.

### Tips To Stay Within Limits

* Use a soft-sided bag to fit in overhead bins better
* Measure bag dimensions before travel
* Pack only essentials to keep weight down
* Use packing cubes to organize and compress items
* Wear bulky items like jackets to save space
* Weigh your bag at home to avoid surprises

Following these tips helps keep carry-ons within Southwest’s rules. Travel lighter and avoid extra charges.

## Southwest’s Checked Bag Policy

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=southwest-airlines-offers-a-clear-and-customer-friendly-checked-bag-policy.-it-helps-travelers-understand-what-to-expect-when-bringing-luggage-on-their-trip.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Southwest Airlines offers a clear and customer-friendly checked bag policy. It helps travelers understand what to expect when bringing luggage on their trip. Knowing the rules can save time and money at the airport.

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. This policy makes it a popular choice for budget-conscious flyers. The size and weight limits are set to keep luggage safe and manageable.

### Fees And Allowances

Each passenger can check two bags at no extra cost. Bags must not exceed 50 pounds in weight or 62 inches in size. Overweight or oversized bags will incur fees. The first overweight bag costs $75, and the second is $100. Bags heavier than 100 pounds are not accepted.

### When To Check Your Bag

Checking bags early helps avoid long lines at the airport. Arrive at least 45 minutes before your flight if you have checked luggage. Southwest recommends checking bags at the airport counter or self-service kiosks. Online check-in does not include bag check.

### Tips For Checking Bags

* Label your bags inside and outside with your contact details.
* Use sturdy luggage to prevent damage.
* Keep valuables and important documents in your carry-on.
* Pack smartly to avoid overweight fees.
* Check the latest Southwest baggage rules before your trip.
