## Breeze Airlines Baggage Policy

Breeze Airlines keeps its baggage policy straightforward, but understanding the details can save you both time and money. Knowing exactly what counts as a carry-on versus a checked bag, along with the weight and size limits, helps avoid unexpected fees at the airport. Let’s break down the essentials so you can pack smart and travel stress-free.

### Carry-on Vs Checked Bags

You’re allowed one free personal item on Breeze Airlines, like a small backpack or purse. However, the larger carry-on bag that fits in the overhead bin usually requires a fee unless you have a specific fare type that includes it.

Checked bags are those you hand over at the counter to be stored in the plane’s cargo hold. Breeze charges for checked bags, so it’s important to know the exact cost and rules before you pack. Have you ever been surprised by a baggage fee at the gate? Planning ahead can prevent that hassle.

### Weight And Size Limits

Breeze Airlines sets specific weight and size limits to keep baggage handling safe and efficient. Typically, checked bags must not exceed 50 pounds and should fit within 62 linear inches (length + width + height).

| Baggage Type | Weight Limit | Size Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checked Bag | Up to 50 lbs (23 kg) | Up to 62 linear inches (157 cm) |
| Carry-On Bag | No specific weight limit, but must be manageable | Must fit in overhead bin (approx. 22 x 14 x 9 inches) |
| Personal Item | Not applicable | Must fit under seat (approx. 18 x 14 x 8 inches) |

Exceeding these limits can lead to hefty extra fees, sometimes more than the original checked bag charge. Have you ever packed that one extra sweater only to find out your bag is now overweight? Being precise with your packing can help you avoid that surprise.

## Checked Bag Fees Breakdown

Understanding the checked bag fees on Breeze Airlines can help you plan your travel budget better. ### First Checked Bag Cost

The first checked bag on Breeze typically costs $30 if you pay during booking or online before your flight. Paying at the airport usually increases the fee, so it’s cheaper to add your bag early. This fee covers bags up to 50 pounds, so be mindful of your bag’s weight to avoid extra charges.

### Second Checked Bag Charges

If you need to check a second bag, expect to pay around $40. This fee is slightly higher than the first, reflecting the extra handling and space required. Planning your packing to fit everything into one bag can help you skip this cost altogether.

### Additional Bags Pricing

For third and any additional checked bags, Breeze charges about $150 per bag. This steep increase encourages travelers to limit their luggage or consider shipping items separately. If you often travel with many bags, think about whether buying priority boarding or upgraded fare options could offer better value.

Consider your destination carefully. A flight from a major hub to a nearby city might charge less for checked bags than an international or longer domestic flight. Always check the baggage policy specific to your route before you finalize your booking.
