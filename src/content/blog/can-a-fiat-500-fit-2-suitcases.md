| Dimension | Measurement |

## Suitcase Size Comparison

### Common Suitcase Dimensions

Suitcases generally come in three sizes: carry-on, medium checked, and large checked. Carry-on suitcases usually measure around 22 x 14 x 9 inches, designed to fit in overhead bins. Medium checked suitcases are closer to 25-27 inches in height, and large ones can reach up to 30 inches or more.

Here’s a quick comparison:

| Suitcase Type | Typical Dimensions (inches) | Suitcase Volume (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carry-On | 22 x 14 x 9 | 35-40 liters |
| Medium Checked | 25-27 x 18 x 10 | 60-70 liters |
| Large Checked | 29-31 x 20 x 12 | 90+ liters |

Considering the Fiat 500’s trunk space is just about 9.5 cubic feet, carrying two large suitcases is unlikely. However, two carry-ons or a carry-on plus a medium suitcase might fit if packed carefully.

### Soft Vs Hard Shell Cases

Soft shell suitcases offer flexibility. Their fabric sides can compress slightly, allowing you to squeeze them into tighter spaces. This makes them a good choice if you’re trying to fit two bags into a small car trunk.

Hard shell cases, on the other hand, keep their rigid shape. They provide better protection for fragile items but are less forgiving when it comes to fitting into limited spaces. You might find yourself struggling to adjust or stack hard cases in a Fiat 500.

Think about what you typically pack. If you often carry delicate electronics or souvenirs, a hard shell might be worth the trade-off. But if maximizing space is your priority, soft shell bags could help you get both suitcases in without hassle.

## Fitting Two Suitcases

Wondering if you can fit two suitcases in a Fiat 500? It’s a common concern for travelers who love this compact car but worry about its limited space. Let’s break down how two suitcases can fit and what you can do to make the most of the space available.

### Standard Suitcase Fit

The Fiat 500 has a surprisingly practical trunk for its size, but it’s not huge. You can fit two small to medium-sized suitcases comfortably if you pack smartly. Larger suitcases might fit, but you’ll need to be strategic about positioning them.

Typically, the trunk space measures around 185 liters with the rear seats up. This means two carry-on sized suitcases (about 55 cm x 40 cm x 20 cm each) should fit side by side or slightly stacked, depending on their shape. Hard-shell suitcases with a slimmer profile work best here.

If you’re traveling with larger bags, folding down one rear seat can create extra space. This way, you can slide one suitcase back into the cabin area while still keeping one passenger seat available. Have you ever tried fitting bulky bags in a small car? It’s all about angle and placement.

### Tips For Efficient Packing

* **Choose soft-sided luggage:**These can squeeze into tighter spaces compared to rigid suitcases.
* **Use packing cubes:**They help compress clothes and organize your items, maximizing suitcase capacity.
* **Fill every gap:**Shoes, chargers, and smaller items can fit into nooks inside or around your suitcases.
* **Stack vertically:**Place one suitcase flat, then balance the second on top if the shape allows.
* **Fold rear seats:**Don’t hesitate to fold one or both rear seats for extra trunk space when needed.

Think about your last trip—did you struggle fitting everything in your car? Small changes in packing style can make a big difference. With the Fiat 500, it’s less about the size and more about how you use the space you have.
