## Liquid Limits For Carry-on Bags

Understanding the liquid limits for carry-on bags helps avoid delays at airport security. Airports enforce strict rules to keep travelers safe. Knowing these limits ensures a smooth screening process. Packing liquids correctly saves time and hassle.

### Standard Oz Restrictions

Most airports follow a common rule for liquids in carry-on bags. Each liquid container must hold 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less. All containers must fit inside one clear, quart-sized plastic bag. The bag must be sealed and easy to remove at security checkpoints. Only one bag per passenger is allowed.

This rule applies to liquids, gels, creams, and aerosols. Examples include shampoo, toothpaste, and perfume. Carrying larger containers, even if partially full, can cause them to be confiscated. Packing within these limits helps speed up security checks.

### Exceptions To The Rule

Some liquids are allowed beyond the standard limits for safety or medical reasons. Baby formula, breast milk, and medications are permitted in larger amounts. These items must be declared at the security checkpoint. Additional screening may be required for these exceptions.

Duty-free liquids purchased after security checkpoints can also exceed the 3.4-ounce limit. Keep receipts and packaging intact to show at boarding gates. Knowing these exceptions prevents unnecessary stress during travel.
