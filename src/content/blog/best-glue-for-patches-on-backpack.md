## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Glue For Backpack Patches?



FITTDYHE Fabric Glue is highly recommended for backpack patches. It's permanent and washable, making it ideal for fabrics like denim and polyester. Its strong adhesive ensures patches stay in place, even with frequent use.



### How Long Does Fabric Glue Take To Dry?



Most fabric glues, like Onarieer, dry within 24 hours. For optimal results, let the adhesive cure fully before washing or wearing. This ensures a strong and lasting bond.



### Is Fabric Glue Waterproof?



Yes, many fabric glues like Gorilla Waterproof Fabric Glue are designed to be waterproof. They withstand washing and outdoor conditions, ensuring your patches remain secure. Always check the product label for specific waterproofing capabilities.



### Can Fabric Glue Be Used On Leather?



Absolutely, products like Yeardera Fabric Glue are suitable for leather. They provide a strong, flexible bond, perfect for repairing leather backpacks and accessories. Ensure the glue is leather-compatible for best results.



### How Do I Apply Fabric Glue Correctly?



To apply, clean the surface and apply a thin layer of glue. Press the patch firmly onto the fabric. Let it dry completely before use. This ensures a strong, durable bond.



### Is Fabric Glue Safe For Skin Contact?



Yes, many brands like PHIXBEAR Fabric Glue are skin-friendly. They are designed to be non-irritating and safe for use. Always handle with care and avoid prolonged skin contact.
