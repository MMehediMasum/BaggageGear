## Battery Restrictions On Flights

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=battery-restrictions-on-flights-are-important-for-safety.-airlines-and-airports-follow-strict-rules-for-devices-with-lithium-ion-batteries.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Battery restrictions on flights are important for safety. Airlines and airports follow strict rules for devices with lithium-ion batteries. The Dyson Airwrap contains such a battery, so knowing these rules helps avoid delays or confiscation. Understanding what is allowed in carry-on luggage makes travel smoother and stress-free.

### Lithium-ion Battery Rules

Lithium-ion batteries power many electronics, including the Dyson Airwrap. These batteries can be dangerous if damaged or overheated. Airlines require passengers to carry devices with lithium-ion batteries only in carry-on bags. Checking them in is usually not allowed. This rule helps flight crews quickly handle any battery issues during the flight.

### Limits On Battery Capacity

Batteries have limits on their watt-hour (Wh) rating. Most airlines allow batteries up to 100 Wh without approval. The Dyson Airwrap’s battery typically falls within this limit. Batteries between 100 Wh and 160 Wh may need airline approval. Batteries over 160 Wh are generally not allowed on planes. Always check your device’s battery capacity before packing.

### Handling Spare Batteries

Spare lithium-ion batteries have special rules. They must be carried in carry-on luggage only. Spare batteries should be protected from short circuits. Use original packaging or cover terminals with tape. Airlines often limit the number of spare batteries per passenger. Avoid packing spare batteries in checked bags to prevent fire risks.
