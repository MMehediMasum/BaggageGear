## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Luggage Size Is Best For Overseas Travel?



For overseas travel, a 20-29 inch suitcase is ideal. It fits airline requirements and offers enough space for essentials. Choose expandable luggage to add extra capacity when needed. This size balances convenience and packing capacity for checked or carry-on luggage.



### Are Hardshell Or Softside Suitcases Better For Travel?



Hardshell suitcases offer better protection and durability. Softside luggage is lighter and more flexible for tight spaces. Choose based on your travel style: hardshell for fragile items, softside for easier packing and expandability. Both types come with spinner wheels for smooth mobility.



### How Important Is Luggage Weight For Long Trips?



Lightweight luggage reduces strain and allows more packing space within airline weight limits. Many models weigh under 10 pounds. Lightweight suitcases like Travelpro Maxlite or Coolife are ideal for long trips. This helps avoid extra fees and improves travel comfort.



### Do Expandable Suitcases Provide Enough Extra Space?



Yes, expandable suitcases increase packing space by 10-15%. They are perfect for souvenirs or extra clothing. Models like SwissGear Sion and Amazon Basics offer reliable expandability. This feature adds flexibility without sacrificing suitcase durability or mobility.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better Than Two-wheel Suitcases?



Spinner wheels provide 360-degree rotation for easier maneuvering in crowded airports. Two-wheel suitcases are more stable on rough terrain but less versatile indoors. For overseas travel, spinner wheels like those on Samsonite Freeform improve convenience and reduce fatigue.



### Is Tsa Lock Essential For International Luggage?



Yes, TSA locks allow security to inspect luggage without damage. They are required for most US and international airports. Luggage sets like Coolife and Vipbox include TSA-approved locks, enhancing security and peace of mind during overseas travel.



## Conclusion



Choosing the right luggage makes travel easier and more enjoyable. The SwissGear Sion and Travelpro Maxlite are great for those who like softside bags with extra space. For hard shell lovers, Coolife, Samsonite Freeform, and Vipbox offer strong protection and smooth wheels.



If you want lightweight and versatile options, Hanke’s foldable duffel and Amazon Basics sets are smart picks. Don’t forget to consider size and features like TSA locks or expandable compartments. Good luggage helps protect your belongings and moves easily through airports.



Pick what fits your travel style and needs best. With the right suitcase, your overseas trips will feel less stressful and more fun. Safe travels!
