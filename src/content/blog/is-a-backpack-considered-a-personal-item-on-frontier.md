## Frontier's Personal Item Policy

Understanding Frontier's personal item policy is essential before you pack for your trip. It helps avoid surprises at the gate and ensures your backpack or bag qualifies without extra fees. Knowing what counts as a personal item and the size limits can save you time and money.

### Size And Weight Limits

Frontier allows one personal item per passenger, but it must fit under the seat in front of you. The maximum dimensions are typically 18 x 14 x 8 inches. If your backpack exceeds these limits, it might not qualify as a personal item, and you may face additional charges.

Weight is less emphasized compared to size, but a heavy bag can be cumbersome to stow under the seat. Think about how easy it will be to lift and fit your backpack without struggling. Have you measured your backpack recently? It’s a good idea to check before heading to the airport.

### Allowed Item Types

Frontier accepts a variety of personal items including backpacks, purses, briefcases, laptop bags, and small duffel bags. Your backpack is considered a personal item as long as it meets size requirements. This means you can bring your everyday backpack without switching to a smaller bag.

Keep in mind, items like suitcases or larger carry-on bags are not considered personal items. If you carry a backpack along with a larger bag, expect to pay for the larger carry-on. A backpack is often mistaken for a carry-on bag. Carry-ons are larger and fit in the overhead bin. Backpacks can be either a personal item or a carry-on, depending on size. Frontier allows one personal item and one carry-on per passenger. If your backpack is small, it may count as a personal item. Large backpacks usually count as a carry-on and must fit size limits.

* Carry-ons: Larger, stored overhead, must meet size rules.
* Backpacks: Size varies, can be personal item or carry-on.

### What Counts As A Personal Item

A personal item is smaller than a carry-on. It fits under the seat in front of you. Common personal items include purses, small backpacks, and laptop bags. Frontier sets limits on size to qualify as a personal item. Overpacking can turn a personal item into a carry-on. This may cause extra charges at the gate.
