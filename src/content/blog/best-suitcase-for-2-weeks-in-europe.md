## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Suitcase Is Best For A 2-week Europe Trip?



A 24 to 29-inch suitcase is ideal for 2 weeks in Europe. It offers enough space without being too bulky. Models like Samsonite Winfield 2 and Travel Select Amsterdam fit well. Expandable designs add flexibility for souvenirs and extra items.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Suitcases Better For Europe Travel?



Hardside suitcases provide better protection and durability, ideal for frequent flights. Softside luggage offers flexibility and external pockets for easy access. For 2 weeks in Europe, hardside like Samsonite Omni 2 is great for security and weather resistance.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On A Suitcase?



Spinner wheels improve maneuverability, making it easy to navigate airports and streets. Four multi-directional wheels, like on Amazon Basics and Samsonite models, reduce strain. They provide smooth rolling on various surfaces, perfect for European travel.



### Is Expandable Luggage Useful For Two-week Trips?



Yes, expandable luggage offers extra packing space when needed. It’s handy for souvenirs or last-minute additions. Many models, like Samsonite Winfield 2 and Amazon Basics, expand up to 15%, balancing capacity and portability.



### Which Suitcase Features Are Crucial For European Flights?



TSA-approved locks, lightweight materials, and durable wheels are essential. Airline-approved sizes prevent extra fees and hassle. Samsonite Freeform and Away Large Checked Luggage include these features, ensuring smooth security checks and easy transport.



### Can A Two-piece Luggage Set Be Better Than One Large Suitcase?



Two-piece sets offer packing flexibility and easier handling. You can separate clothes and essentials or share luggage with a travel partner. Sets like Amazon Basics 4-Piece and Wrangler Quest cover carry-on and checked needs efficiently.
