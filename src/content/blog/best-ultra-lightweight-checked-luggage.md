## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Ultra Lightweight Checked Luggage Ideal For Travel?



Ultra lightweight checked luggage reduces overall travel weight, allowing more packing space. It’s easier to handle and complies with airline weight limits, minimizing extra fees and improving travel comfort.



### How Durable Are Samsonite Freeform And Uplift Luggage Models?



Samsonite Freeform and UpLIFT use durable polycarbonate and polypropylene shells. These materials resist impact and scratches, ensuring long-lasting protection for your belongings during travel.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Checked Luggage?



Yes, spinner wheels offer 360-degree maneuverability. They make handling large suitcases easier in airports, reducing strain and improving mobility on various surfaces.



### What Benefits Do Expandable Suitcases Provide?



Expandable suitcases increase packing capacity by up to 15%. They offer flexibility for souvenirs or extra items without needing a second bag.



### How Does Softside Luggage Compare To Hardshell Options?



Softside luggage is lightweight and flexible, fitting in tight spaces. Hardshell suitcases provide superior protection but may weigh more. Choice depends on travel needs and preferences.



### Which Luggage Brands Offer Tsa-approved Locks For Security?



Samsonite, Travelpro, and Amazon Basics luggage include TSA-approved locks. These locks allow airport security to inspect bags without damage, ensuring safe, hassle-free travel.
