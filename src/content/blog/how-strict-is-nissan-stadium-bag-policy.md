## Nissan Stadium Bag Policy Basics

Nissan Stadium enforces a clear bag policy to ensure safety and smooth entry for all visitors. Understanding the basics helps fans prepare before arrival. The policy details what types of bags are allowed, size limits, and banned items. This knowledge saves time and avoids surprises at security checkpoints.

### Allowed Bag Types

Only clear bags or small clutch bags are permitted inside Nissan Stadium. Clear bags must be made of plastic, vinyl, or PVC. They should be fully transparent, allowing staff to see contents easily. Small clutch bags without handles or straps are allowed. These must not exceed a specific size limit.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must not be larger than 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. This size fits most clear bags and small clutches. Backpacks, large purses, or coolers exceeding this size are not allowed. Carrying smaller bags helps speed up entry lines and keeps the venue safe.

### Prohibited Items

* Weapons of any kind, including knives and firearms
* Alcohol or outside food and drinks
* Illegal substances
* Large cameras, tripods, or recording devices
* Signs, banners, or flags that block views

Security checks all bags thoroughly. Items that violate the policy will not be allowed inside. Following these rules ensures a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

Guests with medical needs can carry necessary supplies in non-clear bags. Items like medications, insulin, or medical devices are permitted. Staff may inspect these bags carefully for safety.

Carrying proof of medical or infant care needs helps at entry points. It speeds up the process and avoids misunderstandings.

### Accessible Seating Considerations

Guests with accessible seating tickets receive additional accommodations. They may bring larger bags or assistive devices that do not fit the standard rules. Nissan Stadium staff provide support for these guests.
