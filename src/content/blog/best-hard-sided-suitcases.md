## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hard Sided Suitcases Better Than Soft Ones?



Hard sided suitcases offer superior protection for fragile items. They resist water and impacts better. Their sturdy shell prevents damage during travel. Many models include scratch-resistant surfaces and TSA locks for security. This makes them ideal for frequent travelers and rough handling.



### How Does Expandable Luggage Increase Packing Capacity?



Expandable luggage features zippers that open extra compartments. This allows up to 15% more packing space. It’s perfect for souvenirs or extra clothing. Expandable options like Amazon Basics and Samsonite models add flexibility without bulk. This feature enhances convenience for longer trips.



### Are Spinner Wheels More Durable Than Double Wheels?



Spinner wheels rotate 360 degrees, providing smooth maneuverability. Double wheels offer stability and can handle rough surfaces well. Both types are durable but suit different travel needs. Spinner wheels excel in airports, while double wheels suit uneven terrain. Choose based on your travel style.



### What Benefits Do Tsa Locks Provide On Hard Suitcases?



TSA locks allow airport security to inspect luggage without damage. They enhance suitcase security and ease travel through checkpoints. Many hard sided suitcases, like Amazon Basics and KROSER, include built-in TSA locks. This feature offers peace of mind and complies with airline regulations.



### How Important Is Lightweight Design In Hard Shell Luggage?



Lightweight hard shell luggage reduces overall travel weight. It helps avoid airline overweight fees and eases handling. Models like Kenneth Cole REACTION and Sweetcrispy balance durability with lightness. Lightweight design improves portability without sacrificing protection. It’s crucial for frequent flyers and long trips.
