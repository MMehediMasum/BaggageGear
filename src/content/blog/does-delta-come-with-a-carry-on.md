## Delta's Carry-on Policy

Understanding Delta's carry-on policy can save you time and avoid last-minute hassles at the airport. Knowing exactly what you can bring onboard helps you pack smarter and travel lighter. Let’s break down the key details of what Delta allows as carry-on luggage.

### Standard Allowance

Delta allows each passenger to bring one carry-on bag and one personal item onboard for free. The carry-on bag could be a suitcase, backpack, or duffel, while the personal item is usually a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.

These two items must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat in front of you. If your carry-on is too large or you already checked a bag, you may need to check the carry-on at the gate.

### Size And Weight Limits

Delta’s carry-on size limit is 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), including handles and wheels. This ensures your bag fits comfortably in the overhead bin without causing issues for other passengers.

Unlike some airlines, Delta does not have a specific weight limit for carry-ons, but your bag must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead compartment by yourself.

Have you ever struggled to stuff an oversized bag into the bin? Keeping your bag within these dimensions keeps boarding smooth and stress-free.

### Carry-on Vs Personal Item

Many travelers confuse the carry-on with the personal item. Your carry-on is your main bag, while the personal item is smaller and must fit under the seat in front of you.

Typical personal items include a handbag, briefcase, or small backpack. You can bring both onboard, but only one of each.

Think about what you need quick access to during the flight. Your personal item is perfect for essentials like your wallet, phone, or a book.

Delta Airlines offers different carry-on allowances based on the fare type you choose. Understanding these rules helps you pack smarter and avoid extra fees. Each fare class has specific guidelines for carry-on bags and personal items.

Basic Economy is the most affordable option but has strict carry-on rules. Passengers can bring one personal item like a backpack or purse. A full-sized carry-on bag is not included for free. You may have to pay to check a carry-on at the gate. This fare is ideal for light packers who travel with only essentials.

### Main Cabin

Main Cabin passengers get to bring one carry-on bag and one personal item at no extra cost. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin. Personal items should fit under the seat in front of you. This fare suits travelers who need more space for belongings without extra fees.

### Delta One And First Class

Delta One and First Class offer the most generous carry-on allowances. Passengers can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item free of charge. These bags must meet size limits for overhead bins and under-seat storage. This fare provides maximum comfort and convenience for travelers with more luggage.

## Tips For Packing Carry-on Bags

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-a-carry-on-bag-efficiently-can-save-you-time,-stress,-and-even-money-during-your-trip.-the-key-is-to-be-strategic-about-what-you-bring-and-how-you-organize-your-belongings.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing a carry-on bag efficiently can save you time, stress, and even money during your trip. The key is to be strategic about what you bring and how you organize your belongings. Let’s look at some practical tips that will help you pack smarter and travel lighter.

### Maximizing Space

Roll your clothes instead of folding them. This technique reduces wrinkles and creates more room in your bag.

Use packing cubes or compression bags. They keep your items organized and compress clothes to save space.

Place heavier items at the bottom near the wheels of your carry-on to balance the weight and make it easier to carry.

### Avoiding Common Mistakes

Don’t overpack—stick to essentials only. Overloading your bag can lead to stress at the gate and extra fees.

Avoid packing prohibited items like large liquids or sharp objects to prevent delays at security checks.

Don’t forget to check your airline’s carry-on size and weight limits before you pack. Delta, for example, allows one carry-on bag plus a personal item, but sizes matter.

### Essential Items To Pack

* **Important documents:**Passport, ID, boarding pass, and any necessary visas.
* **Electronics and chargers:**Phone, laptop, headphones, and power bank.
* **Comfort items:**A neck pillow, eye mask, and a light jacket or scarf.
* **Snacks and water bottle:**Fill your bottle after security to stay hydrated during the flight.

Think about what you’ll need immediately after landing. Do you have your keys or hotel information handy? Packing these essentials in your carry-on makes your journey smoother.

  


## Delta's Checked Baggage Options

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=delta-offers-a-variety-of-checked-baggage-options-to-suit-different-travel-needs.-understanding-these-options-helps-avoid-extra-costs-and-travel-stress.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Delta offers a variety of checked baggage options to suit different travel needs. Understanding these options helps avoid extra costs and travel stress. Travelers can check bags with specific size and weight limits, but fees may apply depending on the ticket type and destination.

Knowing the rules for overweight and oversized bags is important. Delta provides clear guidelines and fees for bags that exceed standard limits. Upgrading baggage allowance is possible, providing more flexibility for travelers with extra luggage.

### Fees And Allowances

Delta allows one free checked bag for many domestic flights. The weight limit per bag is usually 50 pounds (23 kg). The size limit is 62 inches (158 cm) total, including length, width, and height.

Additional bags or heavier items may cost extra. Fees vary by route and ticket type. Basic Economy tickets often have higher fees or no free checked bags.

### Overweight And Oversized Bags

Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds incur an overweight fee. Bags between 71 and 100 pounds have a higher charge. Items over 100 pounds are not accepted as checked baggage.

Oversized bags over 62 inches but less than 115 inches also face extra fees. Delta does not accept bags larger than 115 inches as checked luggage.

### How To Upgrade

Delta offers baggage upgrades for frequent flyers and premium ticket holders. Buying extra baggage allowance online is cheaper than at the airport. Travelers can add more bags or increase weight limits before their flight.

Upgrades can be done through the Delta website or mobile app. This ensures smoother check-in and less waiting time at the airport.

### What Are Delta's Carry-on Size Restrictions?

Delta's carry-on size limit is 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including wheels and handles. Bags exceeding this size may need to be checked or gate-checked, depending on space and flight rules.

### Can I Bring A Carry-on And A Personal Item On Delta?

Yes, Delta permits one carry-on bag plus one personal item like a purse or laptop bag. Both items must fit under the seat or in overhead bins.

Carry-ons are free on most Delta flights, including domestic and many international routes. Some basic economy fares may have restrictions, so always check your ticket details before traveling.
