## Choosing The Right Lock

Choosing the right lock for your Away luggage is essential for security and convenience. A good lock protects your belongings and meets travel regulations. Picking the right type depends on your travel needs and preferences.

### Types Of Luggage Locks

* **Combination Locks:**Use numbers to set a code. No keys needed.
* **Key Locks:**Simple and easy but keys can be lost.
* **TSA-Approved Locks:**Can be opened by airport security without damage.
* **Cable Locks:**Flexible cables that secure multiple zippers or bags.

### Benefits Of Tsa-approved Locks

* Airport security can open and relock your bag without breaking it.
* Meets Transportation Security Administration (TSA) regulations.
* Prevents damage to your luggage during inspections.
* Allows smoother and faster airport screening.

### Factors To Consider

* Choose a lock that fits your luggage size and style.
* Pick a lock type that you find easy to use and remember.
* Check if the lock is durable and weather-resistant.
* Consider how often you travel and the level of security needed.
* Ensure the lock complies with airline and TSA rules.

## Setting A Combination Lock

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=setting-a-combination-lock-on-your-away-luggage-is-a-straightforward-process-that-adds-a-layer-of-security-to-your-travels.-knowing-how-to-properly-set-and-customize-your-lock-ensures-that-only-you-can-access-your-belongings.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Setting a combination lock on your Away luggage is a straightforward process that adds a layer of security to your travels. Knowing how to properly set and customize your lock ensures that only you can access your belongings. Let's break down the steps to reset the factory code, create a strong combination, and test the lock effectively.

### Resetting Factory Codes

Your Away luggage comes with a default factory code, usually set to **000**. Before setting your own combination, you need to reset this code.

Start by locating the small reset button inside the lock, typically near the dials. Use a pen or a similar tool to press and hold this button while setting your new code.

Remember to keep the reset button pressed until you finish entering your desired combination. Letting go too early may cause the lock to remain on the factory code.

### Creating A Strong Combination

Choosing a strong combination is crucial for your luggage’s security. Avoid simple sequences like **123**or repetitive numbers such as **111**.

Think about using numbers that are meaningful but not obvious, like a memorable date mixed with random digits. For example, combining your favorite number with a non-related year can work well.

Write down your new combination somewhere safe, but not inside the luggage. Losing your code can cause unnecessary travel stress and delays.

### Testing The Lock

After setting your combination, test the lock several times before you travel. Spin the dials away from your new code and then enter it again to make sure it opens smoothly.

If the lock sticks or doesn’t open, repeat the reset process carefully. It’s better to discover any issues at home than at the airport.

Have you ever been locked out of your luggage? Taking a few extra minutes to test your lock can save you from that frustrating experience.

## Using Key Locks

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-key-locks-on-your-away-luggage-offers-a-straightforward-and-reliable-way-to-secure-your-belongings.-unlike-combination-locks,-key-locks-rely-on-a-physical-key,-making-them-less-prone-to-accidental-resets-or-forgotten-codes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using key locks on your Away luggage offers a straightforward and reliable way to secure your belongings. Unlike combination locks, key locks rely on a physical key, making them less prone to accidental resets or forgotten codes. However, managing your keys properly is crucial to avoid travel hassles.

### Key Management Tips

Keep your luggage key in a safe but easily accessible place. Many travelers attach a spare key to their wallet or phone case to avoid getting locked out. You can also take a photo of the key and save it on your phone as a backup.

Consider labeling your keys if you have multiple locks to prevent confusion. Using a small key organizer can help you keep track of all your keys without losing them. Have you ever spent minutes digging through your bag only to realize the key was in your jacket pocket?

### Replacing Lost Keys

If you lose your key, don’t panic. Away offers replacement keys, but you’ll need to provide your lock’s serial number. This number is usually found on the lock itself or in the luggage documentation.

Contact Away’s customer service to request a replacement key. They may ask for proof of purchase to verify ownership. Meanwhile, avoid forcing the lock open as this can cause damage, leading to costly repairs.

  


## Securing Zippers And Compartments

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=securing-your-luggage-goes-beyond-just-locking-a-single-zipper.-many-bags-have-multiple-compartments-and-zippers-that-need-attention-to-keep-your-belongings-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Securing your luggage goes beyond just locking a single zipper. Many bags have multiple compartments and zippers that need attention to keep your belongings safe. Failing to secure all zippers can leave gaps that invite theft or accidental openings.

### Locking Multiple Zippers

Many suitcases feature two or more zippers on the same compartment. To lock these, you need a lock designed to secure multiple zipper pulls together. Look for locks with flexible cables or TSA-approved combination locks with a loop that can thread through each zipper pull.

Try threading the lock through all zipper pulls to keep them tightly closed. If your bag has separate compartments, consider locking each zipper individually or using a lock that can secure several compartments at once. Have you ever experienced a zipper burst open during travel? Securing multiple zippers can prevent that embarrassing and risky moment.

### Using Additional Security Accessories

Beyond the lock itself, add extra layers of security with accessories like zipper pulls, luggage straps, and cable ties. Zipper pulls with built-in locks or tamper-evident seals can alert you if someone tries to open your bag.

Luggage straps can wrap around your suitcase, making it harder for zippers to be pried open unnoticed. Cable ties offer a low-cost solution for temporary security, especially when you need quick and disposable protection. Have you considered how these small additions can deter opportunistic theft or accidental openings during busy travel days?




## Travel Tips For Lock Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-away-luggage-lock-in-good-shape-helps-protect-your-belongings.-proper-lock-maintenance-avoids-delays-and-travel-stress.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your Away luggage lock in good shape helps protect your belongings. Proper lock maintenance avoids delays and travel stress. Simple care routines keep your lock working smoothly for many trips.

Regular attention prevents common problems and extends lock life. Small habits make a big difference in lock performance. Here are key travel tips for maintaining your Away luggage lock.

### Avoiding Common Lock Issues

* Keep the lock clean and free of dust or dirt.
* Use a soft cloth to wipe the lock after each trip.
* Do not force the dials or keys if they feel stuck.
* Store luggage in a dry place to prevent rust or corrosion.
* Use a small amount of lubricant on the lock mechanism once a year.
* Avoid exposing the lock to harsh chemicals or extreme temperatures.

### When To Replace Your Lock

* Replace the lock if the combination no longer works properly.
* Change the lock if the mechanism feels loose or jammed.
* Consider a new lock if the outer casing is cracked or damaged.
* Replace the lock after a security breach or lost key incident.
* Choose a durable replacement lock designed for travel use.
