Layering isn’t just for clothes—it’s a packing technique too. Start by laying flat, heavy items like shoes or toiletry bags at the bottom of the suitcase. Then add rolled or folded clothes on top in layers, packing lighter items last.

## Maintaining The Carry On Closet

Maintaining the Solgaard Carry On Closet keeps it fresh and ready for every trip. Regular care extends its life and keeps it looking new. Small efforts can protect your bag from damage and dirt.

### Cleaning Tips

Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth after each trip. Use mild soap for stubborn stains. Avoid harsh chemicals that can harm the material. For the interior, vacuum small debris gently. Let the bag air dry completely before storing.

### Preventing Wear And Tear

Handle zippers with care to avoid damage. Do not overload the bag beyond its weight limit. Use packing cubes to organize and reduce stress on fabric. Avoid dragging the bag on rough surfaces. Inspect straps and handles regularly for any signs of wear.

### Storage Between Trips

Store the Carry On Closet in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent color fading. Keep it slightly open to allow air circulation. Use a dust cover or breathable bag to protect from dust. Do not store heavy items on top of it to keep its shape.
