## Backpack Policy At Elitch Gardens

Bringing a backpack to Elitch Gardens can make your day more convenient, but it’s important to understand their specific backpack policy before you pack your bag. Knowing the rules helps you avoid surprises at the entrance and ensures a smooth entry process. Let’s break down what you need to know about size limits, prohibited items, and security checks.

### Size And Type Restrictions

Elitch Gardens allows backpacks, but they must fit within certain size limits to avoid delays at security. Typically, backpacks larger than 12 x 12 x 6 inches may be subject to additional inspection or denied entry. Soft-sided backpacks are preferred over hard cases, as they are easier to check and carry around the park.

Think about your backpack’s dimensions before you arrive. Can your bag hold all essentials without being bulky? Smaller, lighter backpacks make it easier to enjoy rides without feeling weighed down.

### Prohibited Items Inside Backpacks

Elitch Gardens has a strict list of items you cannot bring inside, even if they fit in your backpack. This includes weapons, alcohol, drugs, glass containers, and outside food or beverages. They also prohibit drones, hoverboards, and large tripods.

Carefully check your bag before heading to the park. Have you ever forgotten something in your backpack only to be turned away at the gate? Avoid this by packing smart and leaving restricted items at home or in your car.

### Security Screening Process

At the park entrance, security staff will screen your backpack thoroughly. This may involve opening your bag and inspecting contents by hand or with a handheld scanner. Be prepared to remove items like water bottles or sunscreen for inspection.

Arriving early can help you get through security faster, especially during busy times. How do you usually prepare for security checks at crowded places? Staying calm and cooperative speeds up the process and gets you into the fun faster.




## Packing Smart For Your Visit

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-smart-for-your-visit-to-elitch-gardens-can-make-all-the-difference-between-a-stressful-day-and-a-fun-filled-adventure.-you-want-to-carry-what-you-need-without-being-weighed-down.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing smart for your visit to Elitch Gardens can make all the difference between a stressful day and a fun-filled adventure. You want to carry what you need without being weighed down. The right backpack setup helps you stay comfortable, enjoy the rides, and be prepared for anything that comes your way.

### Essentials To Carry

Start with water to stay hydrated in the summer heat. A refillable water bottle is ideal since you can find water stations throughout the park.

Pack sunscreen and sunglasses to protect your skin and eyes during long hours outdoors. A small first aid kit with band-aids and antiseptic wipes can come in handy after a minor scrape.

Don’t forget your phone, wallet, and park tickets. A portable phone charger can be a lifesaver if you plan to capture lots of photos or use the park app for ride wait times.

### Items To Leave Behind

Leave bulky or expensive items at home or in your car. Large cameras or extra gadgets can slow you down and risk damage on rides.

Food and drinks are generally not allowed inside, so avoid bringing snacks that may be confiscated at the entrance. Also, avoid glass containers and weapons of any kind; they are strictly prohibited for safety reasons.

Heavy books or unnecessary electronics can add weight without much benefit. Ask yourself if each item truly enhances your day before packing it.

### Weather-appropriate Gear

Check the weather forecast before you pack. If rain is expected, bring a compact poncho or waterproof jacket—umbrellas can be impractical in crowded areas.

On hot days, wear a hat and light, breathable clothing to stay cool. Consider a small towel or cooling cloth if you plan to spend time in water rides or splash zones.

For cooler evenings, a light sweater or hoodie can keep you comfortable without taking up much space in your backpack. Being ready for weather changes means you won’t have to cut your visit short.

Elitch Gardens offers several storage options to keep your belongings safe. Carrying a backpack inside the park is allowed, but storing it securely is important. You can enjoy rides without worrying about your stuff.

### Locker Rentals

Lockers are available for rent near major rides and entrances. Sizes vary to fit small bags or large backpacks. Renting a locker costs a small fee, and you can use it all day. Lockers provide a secure place to store valuables and extra items.

### Designated Bag Areas

Some rides have special bag areas where you can leave backpacks temporarily. These areas are supervised by staff and close to the ride entrance. Bags stay safe while you enjoy the attraction. Check ride rules for bag policies before entering.

### Tips For Managing Your Belongings

* Carry only essentials to avoid heavy backpacks.
* Use locker rentals for bigger items or valuables.
* Keep important items like phones and wallets in a small bag.
* Label your bags to spot them easily.
* Stay aware of your belongings while moving around.
