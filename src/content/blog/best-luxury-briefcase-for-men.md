### Are Leather Briefcases Durable For Everyday Use?



Yes, leather briefcases are highly durable for everyday use. Quality leather, like full-grain or genuine leather, ensures longevity. Brands like Time Resistance and BOSTANTEN offer robust leather briefcases. They maintain their appearance and structure over time, making them a reliable choice for daily commutes and business meetings.



### Do Luxury Briefcases Fit 15.6-inch Laptops?



Most luxury briefcases are designed to fit 15. 6-inch laptops. Models like BOSTANTEN and Luxorro specifically accommodate these sizes. They feature padded compartments to protect devices. This makes them perfect for professionals who need to carry their laptops securely and stylishly.



### What Features Should I Look For In A Briefcase?



When choosing a briefcase, look for quality leather, spacious compartments, and secure closures. Features like padded laptop sections and adjustable straps add convenience. Brands like Luxorro and Time Resistance offer these features, ensuring your briefcase is both functional and stylish for business use.



### How Do I Maintain A Leather Briefcase?



Maintaining a leather briefcase involves regular cleaning and conditioning. Use a soft cloth to wipe away dirt and apply leather conditioner. Store it in a cool, dry place. This preserves its quality and appearance over time, ensuring your briefcase remains a stylish and functional accessory.
