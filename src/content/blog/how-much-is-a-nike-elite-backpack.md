The type of materials affects the backpack's durability and weight. High-quality fabrics like ballistic nylon or polyester add strength. Reinforced stitching and padded straps improve comfort and longevity. Better materials usually raise the price but also increase value.

### Size And Capacity

Backpacks come in different sizes to fit various needs. Larger backpacks hold more items but cost more due to extra material. Smaller backpacks are cheaper but may not fit all essentials. Choosing the right size depends on how much you carry daily.

### Special Features

Extra features add convenience but can increase the cost. These include:

* Multiple compartments for organization
* Water-resistant coatings for protection
* Built-in laptop sleeves or chargers
* Adjustable straps for better fit

Backpacks with more features offer better usability but come at a higher price.
