## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Suitcase Is Best For International Travel?



A large suitcase between 28 and 31 inches is ideal for international travel. It offers ample packing space and meets most airline checked baggage requirements.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Suitcases Better For Travel?



Hardside suitcases offer better protection and durability. Softside suitcases are lighter and flexible for tight spaces. Choose based on your travel needs.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Large Suitcases?



Spinner wheels provide 360-degree movement, making it easier to maneuver heavy luggage through airports. They reduce strain and improve travel convenience.



### Do Expandable Suitcases Add Significant Packing Space?



Yes, expandable suitcases increase packing capacity by up to 15%. This feature is useful for souvenirs or extra items during international trips.



### Why Choose Tsa-approved Locks On Travel Luggage?



TSA-approved locks allow airport security to open and inspect luggage without damage. They provide security and comply with travel regulations.



### Which Materials Ensure Suitcase Durability For Long Trips?



Polycarbonate and ABS materials offer lightweight durability. They resist scratches and impact, ensuring your luggage withstands rough handling during international travel.
