## Stadium Bag Policies

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=stadiums-have-clear-rules-about-bags-to-keep-everyone-safe.-these-policies-tell-you-what-bags-are-allowed-and-what-items-you-can-bring.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Stadiums have clear rules about bags to keep everyone safe. These policies tell you what bags are allowed and what items you can bring. Knowing these rules helps you avoid problems at the entrance.

### Common Rules Across Venues

Most stadiums only allow small bags or clear bags. Backpacks and large purses are often not permitted. Bags must be easy to check quickly by security staff. Some venues require bags to be see-through for faster entry.

### Size Restrictions

Size limits are strict. Many stadiums allow bags no larger than 12 x 6 x 12 inches. This size fits most belt bags and small purses. Larger bags will usually be denied entry or must be checked in.

### Prohibited Items

Stadiums ban sharp objects, weapons, and illegal substances. Outside food and drinks are often not allowed. Some stadiums restrict cans, glass bottles, and coolers. Always check the venue’s specific list before you go.
