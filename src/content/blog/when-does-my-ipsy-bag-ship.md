## Ipsy Shipping Schedule

Understanding the Ipsy shipping schedule can save you from the frustration of waiting endlessly for your glam goodies. Knowing exactly when your bag ships helps you plan your excitement and manage expectations. Let’s break down how the shipping works so you’re never left wondering, “Where’s my Ipsy bag?”

### Monthly Shipping Dates

Your Ipsy bag typically ships within the first two weeks of every month. Most members receive tracking information between the 5th and 10th, but this can vary slightly based on your subscription type and location.

For example, if you subscribe on the 15th, your first bag will ship the following month during this window. Keeping an eye on your email for those tracking updates is key—you’ll know exactly when to expect your glam haul.

### Cutoff Times For Orders

There’s a specific cutoff date each month to ensure your order makes it into that month's shipment. Usually, you need to finalize your glam bag picks and any add-ons by the last day of the month prior to shipping.

Miss this cutoff, and your choices roll over to the next month’s shipment. This means if you wait too long, your bag could arrive weeks later than expected. Have you ever missed a cutoff? It’s frustrating, but now you can avoid it by marking your calendar!

Understanding the factors that affect the delivery of your Ipsy bag can help you manage expectations and plan better. Several elements come into play, from how long your order takes to process to the shipping method used. Even the time of year can shift delivery times unexpectedly.

### Processing Time

Your Ipsy bag doesn’t ship immediately after you subscribe or place an order. There is a processing period during which your bag is packed and prepared for shipment. This usually takes a few days but can extend if there’s a high volume of orders.

If you’ve noticed delays in receiving your bag, think about whether your order was placed near the beginning or end of the month. Early orders often ship faster because they enter the queue sooner.

### Shipping Method Variations

Ipsy uses different shipping methods depending on your location and the subscription plan. Some bags are sent via standard mail, while others might use expedited services. This affects how quickly your package arrives at your doorstep.

Have you ever received your Ipsy bag faster than a friend who ordered the same day? Shipping routes and carriers play a big role. Tracking your package can reveal which method is used and give you a clearer idea of the expected delivery date.

### Holiday Delays

During busy seasons like holidays, expect longer wait times. Postal services handle a surge in packages, which can slow down deliveries. Your Ipsy bag might get caught in this rush, delaying its arrival.

Planning ahead can save you frustration. If you want your bag as a gift or to try new products before an event, order early. Have you experienced holiday shipping delays that made you rethink your subscription timing?

## Tracking Your Ipsy Bag

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=tracking-your-ipsy-bag-is-essential-to-know-exactly-when-your-beauty-goodies-will-arrive.-it-gives-you-peace-of-mind-and-helps-you-plan-your-excitement.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Tracking your Ipsy bag is essential to know exactly when your beauty goodies will arrive. It gives you peace of mind and helps you plan your excitement. But sometimes, figuring out where your bag is can be tricky if you don’t know where to look or what to expect.

### How To Find Tracking Info

Once your Ipsy bag ships, you’ll get an email with tracking details. This email usually arrives within 24 to 48 hours after your bag is sent out.

You can also check the tracking number by logging into your Ipsy account:

* Go to the “My Account” section.
* Click on “Orders” or “Shipments.”
* Select the current month’s bag to find the tracking link.

Tracking links take you to the carrier’s website, where you can see real-time updates. Have you ever missed a package because you didn’t check the tracking? Staying proactive can save you that hassle.

### Common Tracking Issues

Sometimes, tracking info might not update immediately. This happens because shipping carriers take a few hours to scan the package.

If your tracking link shows “Label Created” but no movement, don’t panic. It often means the bag is still with the shipper or waiting for the first scan.

Another issue is receiving a tracking number that doesn’t work. This can happen if the email goes out before the package is handed over to the carrier. If this happens to you, try checking the status again after a day or contact Ipsy support.

Have you ever had tracking that stopped updating? It’s frustrating but usually temporary. Keeping an eye on your Ipsy bag’s journey can help you spot delays early and take action if needed.




## Shipping Regions And Times

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-where-your-ipsy-bag-ships-and-how-long-it-takes-helps-set-clear-expectations.-shipping-times-vary-by-region-and-shipping-method.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing where your Ipsy bag ships and how long it takes helps set clear expectations. Shipping times vary by region and shipping method. This guide explains the typical timelines for both domestic and international deliveries.

### Domestic Shipping Timeline

Ipsy ships most bags within the United States. Once your bag is ready, it usually ships between the 15th and 20th of each month. Delivery times depend on your location and carrier but generally take 3 to 7 business days.

Here is a quick overview:

* Order processing: 1-2 days
* Shipping window: 15th to 20th monthly
* Delivery time: 3-7 business days

Keep in mind weekends and holidays might delay delivery. Tracking information is sent via email once your bag ships.

### International Shipping Details

Ipsy offers international shipping to select countries. Shipping times vary more due to customs and local postal services. Expect delivery between 2 to 6 weeks depending on your location.

Important points include:

* Customs processing can add extra days
* Shipping usually starts after the 20th of each month
* Tracking may not be available in all countries

International orders may also have additional fees or taxes. Check local regulations to avoid surprises.

## Tips To Receive Your Bag Faster

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=waiting-for-your-ipsy-bag-can-feel-long.-a-few-simple-steps-can-help-speed-up-its-arrival.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Waiting for your Ipsy bag can feel long. A few simple steps can help speed up its arrival. These tips focus on making sure your bag ships quickly and reaches you without delay. Small actions on your part improve delivery time significantly.

### Address Accuracy

Check your shipping address carefully. A wrong or incomplete address causes delays. Make sure to include:

* Street number and name
* Apartment or unit number, if any
* City, state, and zip code
* Correct country, especially for international orders

Update your address in your Ipsy profile before the billing cycle ends. This helps avoid shipping errors and reroutes.

### Subscription Management

Keep your subscription details current. Review your payment method and billing info regularly. Late or failed payments delay your bag’s shipment. You can:

* Set reminders for payment dates
* Update card details promptly
* Pause your subscription if needed, but before billing closes

Managing your subscription well ensures Ipsy processes your order on time. Quick payments mean faster bag shipments.
