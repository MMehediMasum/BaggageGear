## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Carry-on Luggage Is Best For International Travel?



A 19 to 23-inch carry-on fits most international airline requirements. It offers enough space without checked baggage fees. Models like Samsonite Freeform 23-inch and Travelpro Maxlite 5 20-inch are ideal for lightweight, hassle-free travel.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Carry-ons Better For Flying?



Hardside luggage offers better protection and durability. Softside bags provide flexibility and more external pockets. Choose based on your travel needs; Samsonite Omni PC is hardside, while Travelpro Maxlite 5 is softside and expandable.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Carry-on Suitcases?



Spinner wheels enhance maneuverability and ease of movement in airports. Models like Samsonite Omni 2 with 360° spinner wheels reduce strain and improve travel comfort. They allow smooth navigation through crowded spaces.



### Do Expandable Carry-ons Provide Enough Extra Space?



Expandable carry-ons add up to 25% more packing space. This feature is useful for souvenirs or extra items. Amazon Basics 21" Hardside and Samsonite Omni PC offer reliable expandable compartments for flexible packing.



### Are Tsa-approved Locks Necessary For International Carry-ons?



Yes, TSA-approved locks allow security to inspect luggage without damage. Most models like Travelpro Maxlite Air V2 and Samsonite Freeform include TSA locks for safer, hassle-free travel during security checks.



### Can Backpacks Replace Carry-on Suitcases For International Trips?



Large capacity backpacks like MATEIN Weekender offer flexibility and comfort. They are ideal for business and casual travel with ample packing space. However, suitcases provide better organization and protection for fragile items.
