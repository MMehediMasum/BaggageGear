## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Noise Reduction Level Is Best For Sleeping Ear Plugs?



For sleeping, ear plugs with 30dB to 45dB noise reduction are ideal. They effectively block snoring and ambient sounds without discomfort. Higher levels like 50dB suit louder environments but may feel too isolating for sleep.



### Are Reusable Silicone Ear Plugs Safe For Nightly Use?



Yes, reusable silicone ear plugs are safe when cleaned regularly. They provide a soft, comfortable fit and can be used nightly without irritation. Proper hygiene extends their lifespan and prevents ear infections.



### How Do Noise Cancelling Ear Plugs Improve Sleep Quality?



Noise cancelling ear plugs block disruptive sounds, promoting deeper, uninterrupted sleep. They reduce stress from noise, improve focus, and help those sensitive to snoring or environmental sounds sleep better.



### Can Ear Plugs Protect Hearing At Concerts And During Sleep?



Yes, many ear plugs offer high-fidelity hearing protection suitable for concerts and sleep. They reduce harmful noise levels while preserving sound clarity, making them versatile for various noisy environments.



### What Is The Difference Between Foam And Silicone Ear Plugs?



Foam ear plugs expand to fit the ear canal and offer high noise reduction. Silicone plugs are reusable, softer, and provide a customizable fit with multiple eartip sizes for comfort and durability.
