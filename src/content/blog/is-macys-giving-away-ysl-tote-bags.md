## How To Spot Fake Giveaways

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=fake-giveaways-have-become-common-online.-scammers-use-popular-brands-like-macy's-and-luxury-items-such-as-ysl-tote-bags-to-trick-shoppers.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Fake giveaways have become common online. Scammers use popular brands like Macy's and luxury items such as YSL tote bags to trick shoppers. Spotting fake giveaways is key to staying safe and avoiding scams. Knowing what to watch for helps protect your personal data and money.

Below are signs that reveal fake giveaways and tips to keep your shopping experience secure.

### Red Flags In Online Ads

* Ads promise high-value items for free with minimal effort.
* Official Macy's logos look blurry or distorted.
* Links lead to unfamiliar or suspicious websites.
* Requests for personal information like credit card details.
* Offers that require payment for shipping or handling fees upfront.
* Urgent language pressuring you to act quickly.
* Spelling and grammar mistakes in ad text.

### Safety Tips For Shoppers

* Verify giveaways on Macy's official website or social media pages.
* Never share sensitive data through unknown links or forms.
* Check website URLs carefully for authenticity.
* Use antivirus software to block harmful sites.
* Ask questions in trusted online forums or communities.
* Ignore offers that seem too good to be true.
* Report suspicious ads to the platform or authorities.
