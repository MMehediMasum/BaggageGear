## Tsa Rules For Liquids

Traveling with liquids in your carry-on requires following specific TSA rules. The Transportation Security Administration limits the amount and size of liquids you can bring. This helps keep air travel safe for everyone. Knowing these rules avoids delays at the security checkpoint. It also ensures you can carry essential items like contact solution without trouble.

### Liquid Limits In Carry Ons

TSA allows each passenger to bring liquids in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less. All containers must fit in one clear, quart-sized bag. This bag must be removed from your carry-on during screening. Liquids include gels, creams, and aerosols, such as shampoo or lotion. Contact solution counts as a liquid and follows this rule.

* Each container ≤ 3.4 ounces (100 ml)
* All containers fit in one quart-sized bag
* Bag is separate during security screening

### Exceptions For Medications And Eye Care

TSA makes exceptions for essential liquids like medications and eye care products. Contact lens solution is allowed in larger amounts if medically necessary. Declare these items to the TSA officer during screening. You may need to show proof or explain the need. These items do not have to fit in the quart-sized bag. Carry them separately for easier inspection.
