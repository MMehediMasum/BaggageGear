## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Dimensions

Spirit Airlines has specific rules for carry-on bags. Knowing the right size helps avoid extra fees and hassle at the airport.

Carry-on bags must fit Spirit’s size limits to be allowed in the cabin. These rules keep the boarding process smooth and safe.

### Standard Size Limits

Spirit Airlines allows carry-on bags up to 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This size includes handles and wheels.

Bags larger than this size may need to be checked in. Measure your bag carefully to meet these rules.

### Weight Restrictions

Spirit Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. But keep your bag light for easy handling.

Heavy bags can be difficult to lift into overhead bins. Also, very heavy bags may cause delays at the gate.

### Personal Item Dimensions

In addition to a carry-on bag, Spirit allows one personal item.

The personal item must fit under the seat. Its size limit is 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

Common personal items include backpacks, purses, or small laptop bags.

### Tools To Measure Bag Size

Using the right tools makes measuring your carry-on quick and accurate. A simple tape measure works best because it bends around corners and edges easily.

* **Soft Tape Measure:**Flexible and ideal for checking length, width, and height.
* **Rigid Ruler:**Useful for measuring flat surfaces but less effective on soft bags.
* **Luggage Sizers:**Some travel stores sell plastic or metal frames designed to check if your bag fits airline size rules.

Try to measure the bag fully packed since Spirit Airlines includes wheels and handles in the size limits.

### Tips For In-home Testing

Test your bag’s size using everyday items before you leave home. This helps avoid surprises at the airport.

* Use a corner of your wall or door frame to mark the maximum allowed dimensions.
* Place your packed bag in this marked space to see if it fits comfortably.
* Check if the bag is easy to lift and carry, since Spirit’s overhead bins are often tight.

I once measured my bag only when I got to the airport and had to repack last minute. Avoid that hassle by doing this simple test early.

### Airport Measurement Checks

Airports often have sizing devices near check-in counters or boarding gates for a final size check. These are usually metal frames or boxes that your carry-on must fit inside.

* Slide your bag into the frame before heading to security or the gate.
* If it doesn’t fit, you might face extra charges or be forced to check the bag.
