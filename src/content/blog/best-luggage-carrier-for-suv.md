## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Luggage Carrier Size For An Suv Rooftop?



A 20 to 21 cubic feet rooftop carrier fits most SUVs well. It offers ample storage for road trips and camping gear. Choose carriers with reinforced straps and waterproof material for durability and security.



### Are Rooftop Cargo Carriers Waterproof And Weather-resistant?



Yes, many rooftop carriers like FIVKLEMNZ and MIDABAO use waterproof 840D fabric and coated zippers. This protects your luggage from rain and dust during travel. Look for 100% waterproof ratings for best protection.



### Can Rooftop Carriers Fit Suvs Without A Roof Rack?



Most carriers include anti-slip mats and reinforced straps to fit vehicles without racks. Models like MeeFar and Sailnovo are designed for all vehicles, ensuring secure attachment without additional hardware.



### How Secure Are Rooftop Carriers With Luggage Locks?



Carriers such as FIVKLEMNZ and MeeFar come with built-in luggage locks. These locks add an extra layer of security against theft. Combined with durable straps and door hooks, they keep your belongings safe on the road.



### What Weight Capacity Should I Consider For Suv Cargo Carriers?



Heavy-duty models like KAIRAY support up to 500 lbs, ideal for heavy loads. Most soft rooftop bags handle average luggage weight safely. Always check the vehicle’s roof weight limit to avoid damage.



### How Easy Is It To Install A Rooftop Luggage Carrier?



Installation is simple with included straps, hooks, and anti-slip mats. Most carriers take under 10 minutes to secure. No special tools are needed, making them convenient for quick trips or long vacations.
