## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Leather Weekender Bags Durable?



Leather weekender bags are durable due to their high-quality material. Full-grain leather is naturally strong and ages well. It resists wear and tear, making it ideal for frequent travel. These bags often feature reinforced stitching and sturdy hardware, enhancing their longevity.



Choose full-grain leather for the best durability.



### How To Care For Leather Travel Bags?



To care for leather travel bags, clean them with a damp cloth regularly. Use leather conditioner every few months to maintain flexibility. Store the bag in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Avoid overstuffing to prevent stretching. Proper care ensures your bag remains in great condition.



### Are Leather Duffel Bags Water-resistant?



Most leather duffel bags are not fully water-resistant. However, many are treated with water-repellent finishes. This helps protect against light rain and splashes. For full waterproof protection, consider using a rain cover. Always check product specifications for water resistance details.



### Can Leather Bags Fit A Laptop?



Many leather bags, like the Double Bus weekender, can fit a laptop. They often have dedicated compartments for electronics. Check the bag's dimensions to ensure your laptop fits. Look for padded sections for extra protection. It's important to choose a bag that secures your device properly.



### How To Choose The Right Size Weekender Bag?



To choose the right size, consider your travel needs. For short trips, a 21-inch bag may suffice. Longer trips might require a 24-inch bag. Consider what you typically pack. Ensure the bag fits in overhead compartments if flying. Check airline size restrictions to avoid issues.



### What Are The Benefits Of Full-grain Leather?



Full-grain leather is the highest quality leather available. It retains the natural texture and grain, offering unmatched durability. This type ages beautifully, developing a unique patina over time. It's more resistant to scratches and wear, making it perfect for bags.



Full-grain leather provides both style and longevity.
