## Jetblue Baggage Fees Overview

JetBlue baggage fees vary depending on the type of ticket and bag. Knowing these fees helps plan your travel budget better. This overview covers standard charges for checked bags on JetBlue flights.

JetBlue often includes one free personal item per passenger. Checked bags usually require a fee unless your ticket includes free checked bags.

### Standard Checked Bag Fees

For most JetBlue flights, the first checked bag costs $35. The second checked bag costs $45. Fees apply per bag each way. Prices can change based on your route.

### Mint Class And Mosaic Member Benefits

Mint Class passengers get up to two free checked bags. Mosaic members enjoy free first and second checked bags. These perks add value to premium tickets.

### Oversized And Overweight Bag Charges

Bags over 50 pounds face extra fees. Oversized bags also carry additional charges. Fees vary, so check JetBlue’s policies before packing.

### How To Pay For Checked Bags

* Pay online during booking for lower fees.
* Pay at check-in counters or kiosks.
* Airport fees may be higher than online rates.

## Excess And Oversize Baggage Fees

Understanding JetBlue's policies on excess and oversize baggage fees can save you from unexpected expenses at the airport. These fees apply when your bags exceed standard weight or size limits, so it's crucial to know the details before you pack. Let’s break down how these charges work and what you can expect.

### What Qualifies As Excess Baggage On Jetblue?

Excess baggage refers to bags that weigh more than the allowed 50 pounds (23 kg) for checked luggage. If your bag tips the scales beyond this limit, JetBlue charges an extra fee. This applies to each checked bag that exceeds the weight limit, so weighing your luggage at home can help you avoid this cost.

### Fees For Oversize Baggage

Oversize baggage means your bag is larger than the standard dimensions of 62 inches (length + width + height). JetBlue charges a fee for bags that measure between 63 and 80 inches. If your bag is over 80 inches, it might not be accepted, or you’ll face significantly higher fees.

### How Much Does Jetblue Charge For Excess And Oversize Bags?

| Bag Type | Fee | Details |

| Excess Weight (51-70 lbs) | $100 | Per bag over 50 lbs, up to 70 lbs |
| Excess Weight (71-100 lbs) | $200 | Per bag over 70 lbs, up to 100 lbs |
| Oversize (63-80 inches) | $150 | Per bag exceeding 62 inches |

### Tips To Avoid Excess And Oversize Fees

* Use a luggage scale at home to check your bag’s weight before heading to the airport.
* Measure your bag’s dimensions to ensure it fits within JetBlue’s limits.
* Consider redistributing items between bags to balance weight.
* Check if your fare includes free checked bags to plan accordingly.

## Comparing Jetblue Fees With Other Airlines

### Jetblue's Baggage Fees At A Glance

JetBlue charges $35 for the first checked bag and $45 for the second on most domestic flights. If you’re a Mosaic member or flying on certain fare types, you might get free checked bags. Keep in mind, fees can differ for international routes.

### How Jetblue Stacks Up Against Delta

Delta also charges $30 for the first checked bag and $40 for the second on domestic flights. Their loyalty program can waive these fees similar to JetBlue’s Mosaic. However, Delta’s bag fees tend to be slightly lower for the first bag compared to JetBlue.

### Comparing With American Airlines

American Airlines charges $30 for the first checked bag and $40 for the second on most flights. This matches Delta's fees and is a bit cheaper than JetBlue's first bag fee. American’s loyalty perks also offer free checked bags to elite members and some credit card holders.

### United Airlines’ Fees In Perspective

United charges $35 for the first checked bag and $45 for the second, identical to JetBlue. Their MileagePlus program offers free checked bags to frequent flyers. So, if you fly often with United or JetBlue, these fees might not apply to you.

Spirit charges $35 to $65 for the first checked bag depending on when you pay, and Frontier fees range from $30 to $60.
