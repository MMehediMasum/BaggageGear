## Sun Country Baggage Policy

Sun Country Airlines has clear rules for baggage to keep travel smooth. Knowing their baggage policy helps avoid extra fees and delays. Every passenger should understand what items they can bring and what costs to expect.

### Standard Baggage Allowance

Each passenger can check one bag for a fee. The price usually starts around $30 but can change based on the route or season. The checked bag must not weigh more than 50 pounds (23 kg). The size limit is 62 inches (158 cm) when adding length, width, and height.

Additional bags or overweight items cost extra. It is best to check the exact fees on the Sun Country website before your trip. Paying for bags online is often cheaper than at the airport.

### Carry-on Rules

Passengers may bring one carry-on bag and one personal item for free. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin. Maximum size is 24 x 16 x 10 inches (61 x 41 x 25 cm). Personal items include purses, backpacks, or laptop bags.

Carry-on bags must fit under the seat or in the overhead space. Liquids must follow TSA rules, with containers under 3.4 ounces (100 ml). Sun Country does not allow large sports equipment as carry-ons.

### First Bag Charges

The first checked bag on Sun Country usually costs around $30. This fee applies to most domestic flights. Some routes or fare types may have different prices or include the first bag for free.

### Second Bag Charges

The second checked bag fee is higher than the first. It typically costs about $40. This fee also depends on the flight route and ticket class. Planning for two bags means adding these fees to your travel budget.

### Additional Bags Costs

For the third bag and beyond, fees increase further. Each extra bag can cost $150 or more. Oversized or overweight bags may have extra charges too. Travelers should check Sun Country’s specific rules to avoid extra costs.
