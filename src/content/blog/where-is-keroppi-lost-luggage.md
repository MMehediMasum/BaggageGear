Filing a lost luggage claim is your next critical step. Do it as soon as you realize your bag is missing, ideally at the airport’s baggage service counter.

Buy only the essentials you need immediately. Focus on items like clothes, toiletries, and medications. Keep receipts for all purchases. These receipts support your compensation claim later. Check if your travel insurance covers lost luggage. Insurance may cover more than the airline’s compensation. Contact your insurance provider quickly.
