## Tips For Authentic Purchases

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=buying-briggs-&-riley-luggage-is-an-investment-in-quality-and-durability.-to-protect-that-investment,-you-need-to-be-sure-you’re-getting-an-authentic-product.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Buying Briggs & Riley luggage is an investment in quality and durability. To protect that investment, you need to be sure you’re getting an authentic product. Counterfeit or unauthorized sales can lead to disappointment and lost money. Here are some practical tips to help you confidently find genuine Briggs & Riley luggage.

### Checking Seller Credentials

Start by verifying the seller’s reputation. Look for authorized retailers listed on the official Briggs & Riley website. If you’re buying online, check reviews on trusted platforms like Trustpilot or the Better Business Bureau.

Ask the seller directly about their authorization to sell Briggs & Riley products. Genuine sellers usually provide proof of authenticity or offer warranties. If a deal seems too good to be true, pause and consider why the price is so low.

### Avoiding Counterfeits

Counterfeit luggage often mimics the look but fails on details. Pay close attention to the quality of materials, stitching, and hardware. Genuine Briggs & Riley pieces have smooth zippers, firm handles, and consistent branding.

Compare photos from the official site with the product you want to buy. Watch for misspelled logos or off-center tags. If possible, inspect the luggage in person before purchasing to feel the weight and craftsmanship.

Have you ever bought something online only to realize it wasn’t real? Taking a few extra steps can save you from that frustration and ensure you enjoy your luggage for years.
