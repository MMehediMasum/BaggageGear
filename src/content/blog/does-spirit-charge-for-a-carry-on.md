## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Spirit Airlines has a strict carry-on policy. Understanding it helps avoid extra fees at the airport. Knowing what bags are allowed and their size limits saves time and money. Here is a clear guide on Spirit Airlines carry-on rules.

### Types Of Carry-on Bags Allowed

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item for free. This item must fit under the seat in front. Common personal items include:

* Small backpacks
* Briefcases
* Purses
* Laptop bags

Any larger carry-on bag must be purchased separately. This includes standard carry-on suitcases or larger backpacks. Without payment, these bags are not allowed in the cabin.

### Size And Weight Limits

The personal item must not exceed 18 x 14 x 8 inches. This size ensures it fits under the seat. Spirit Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for personal items. Larger carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin. Their maximum size is 22 x 18 x 10 inches. Overweight or oversized bags may incur additional charges.

Spirit charges for all checked bags, and the price depends on when and how you pay. Booking your checked bag during your initial ticket purchase is cheaper than paying at the airport. Fees can range from $30 to $65 per bag each way, depending on your route and timing.

### Overweight And Oversized Charges

Spirit also enforces strict weight and size limits for both carry-on and checked bags. Bags over 40 pounds (18 kg) or larger than 62 linear inches (length + width + height) will incur extra fees. These overweight and oversized charges can be as high as $150 per bag.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Spirit Airlines Charge For A Carry-on Bag?

Yes, Spirit Airlines charges fees for carry-on bags. Fees vary based on when you pay—online, at the airport, or at the gate. It’s cheaper to pay for your carry-on during online booking to save money.

### What Is Spirit Airlines’ Carry-on Size Limit?

Spirit Airlines allows carry-on bags up to 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This size includes handles and wheels. Carry-ons exceeding these dimensions require checked baggage fees.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item For Free On Spirit?

Yes, Spirit allows one free personal item per passenger. The item must fit under the seat, measuring 18 x 14 x 8 inches or smaller.

### How Much Does Spirit Charge For Carry-on Bags?

Carry-on bag fees on Spirit vary between $35 and $65 or more. Prices depend on when and where you pay for the bag.
