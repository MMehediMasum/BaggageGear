## United Checked Bag Fees

United Airlines charges fees for checked bags on most flights. Knowing these fees helps plan your travel budget. Fees vary by destination, ticket type, and frequent flyer status. Understanding the costs for each bag can save money and avoid surprises at the airport.

### First Checked Bag Cost

The first checked bag usually costs $35 on domestic flights within the U.S. This fee applies to most economy tickets. Some premium tickets or elite members may get this bag free. The bag must weigh less than 50 pounds and meet size limits.

### Second Checked Bag Charges

The second checked bag fee is higher, typically $45 on domestic routes. This charge also depends on your ticket class and loyalty status. Both bags must meet the airline’s weight and size rules. Paying for the second bag in advance online may be cheaper.

### Additional Bag Fees

Extra bags beyond the second cost more, usually $150 each on domestic flights. Overweight or oversized bags incur extra charges starting at $100. International flights have different fees depending on the route. Always check United’s website for current rates before packing.

MileagePlus Elite Members enjoy special benefits, including free checked bags. Depending on the elite status, the number of free bags varies.

* Premier Silver members get 1 free checked bag.

### Credit Card Benefits

United credit card holders receive checked bag fee waivers. The exact benefit depends on the card type.

* United Explorer Card offers 1 free checked bag for the primary cardholder and one companion.

These waivers apply only when purchasing tickets directly with United Airlines.

### Military And Special Cases

Active duty military members traveling on orders get free checked bags. This benefit extends to their dependents.

Passengers with disabilities may qualify for special waivers. United Airlines provides assistance and fee exceptions for medical equipment.

## Bag Size And Weight Limits

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-the-bag-size-and-weight-limits-for-united-airlines-is-key-to-avoiding-unexpected-fees-at-the-airport.-united-sets-clear-rules-on-how-big-and-heavy-your-checked-bag-can-be,-so-you-can-pack-smart-and-save-money.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding the bag size and weight limits for United Airlines is key to avoiding unexpected fees at the airport. United sets clear rules on how big and heavy your checked bag can be, so you can pack smart and save money. Knowing these limits upfront helps you plan better and keeps your travel stress-free.

### Standard Weight Allowance

Your checked bag with United Airlines can weigh up to **50 pounds (23 kilograms)**without extra charges. The maximum size allowed is a total of **62 linear inches**— that’s the sum of length, width, and height. If your bag fits within these limits, you’re good to go without additional fees.

Think about that one trip where you packed everything but the kitchen sink and ended up paying a hefty fee. Sticking to the 50-pound limit helps avoid that surprise. Use a luggage scale at home to be sure before you leave.

### Oversized Bag Fees

If your bag is larger than 62 linear inches, United will charge an oversized bag fee. This applies to bags up to **115 linear inches**. Beyond that size, bags usually aren’t accepted as checked luggage and may need special handling.

Oversized bags can cost you an extra **$200**or more. It’s worth measuring your suitcase carefully to avoid this. Have you ever grabbed a last-minute souvenir that made your bag suddenly too big? It happens to the best of us.

### Overweight Charges

Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds incur an overweight fee, typically around **$100**. If your bag is between 71 and 100 pounds, the fee jumps to about **$200**. Bags over 100 pounds are usually not accepted as checked luggage.

This means packing heavier items in your carry-on or shipping them separately can save you money. Have you tried redistributing weight between bags to dodge these fees? It’s a small step with a big payoff.

## Tips To Save On Checked Bag Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=checked-bag-fees-can-add-up-quickly-during-travel.-saving-on-these-costs-is-easy-with-some-smart-choices.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Checked bag fees can add up quickly during travel. Saving on these costs is easy with some smart choices. Small changes in packing and payment habits help reduce fees. Follow these tips to keep your travel budget in check and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Pack Smart And Light

Travel light to avoid extra bag fees. Use smaller suitcases or backpacks. Choose versatile clothing that fits many situations. Roll clothes to save space and reduce wrinkles. Avoid packing heavy or bulky items unless necessary. Carry only essentials to fit within airline limits.

### Prepaying For Bags

Pay for checked bags before arriving at the airport. Online payment usually costs less than at check-in counters. Booking bags early helps you manage your budget better. Keep your confirmation handy to avoid payment errors. Prepaid bags also speed up the check-in process.

### Combine Bags When Possible

Share luggage space with travel companions. Combine items to reduce the number of bags checked. Distribute weight evenly to avoid overweight fees. Use packing cubes or bags to organize belongings. Combining bags saves money and makes travel easier.

  


## Alternative Options To Checked Bags

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=choosing-not-to-check-a-bag-can-save-money-and-speed-up-travel.-united-airlines-offers-several-alternatives-to-checked-luggage.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Choosing not to check a bag can save money and speed up travel. United Airlines offers several alternatives to checked luggage. These options help travelers carry their essentials without extra fees. Understanding these choices can make your journey easier and cheaper.

### Carry-on Allowances

United allows one free carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. Carry-ons must fit in the overhead bin. Personal items should fit under the seat. Size limits keep bags manageable and safe during the flight. These rules help avoid checked bag fees.

* Carry-on size: 9 x 14 x 22 inches (22 x 35 x 56 cm)
* Personal item size: 9 x 10 x 17 inches (22 x 25 x 43 cm)
* Examples of personal items: purse, laptop bag, small backpack

### Shipping Luggage

Shipping luggage is an option to avoid airport baggage fees. Courier services can deliver bags to your destination. This method saves time and reduces stress at the airport. Plan ahead to allow shipping time. Compare costs to ensure savings over checked bag fees.

* Pack bags according to courier rules
* Label luggage clearly with your contact details
* Track shipments online for safety

### Using United's Special Services

United offers special services for certain travelers. Military personnel, active-duty members, and those with disabilities may get baggage fee waivers. United MileagePlus Premier members also enjoy free checked bags. Check eligibility before booking. These services can reduce or remove baggage costs.

* Military members: up to 5 free checked bags
* MileagePlus Premier members: 1-3 free bags depending on status
* Passengers with disabilities: assistance and fee waivers available
