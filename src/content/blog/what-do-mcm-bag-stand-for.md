## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=proper-care-and-maintenance-keep-your-mcm-bag-looking-fresh-and-lasting-longer.-regular-cleaning-and-correct-storage-protect-the-material-and-shape.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Proper care and maintenance keep your MCM bag looking fresh and lasting longer. Regular cleaning and correct storage protect the material and shape. Simple steps help maintain its luxury and value. Follow easy tips to care for your bag daily.

### Cleaning Techniques

Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe off dust and dirt. For tougher spots, use a damp cloth with mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage leather or coated canvas. Pat dry with a clean towel after cleaning. Test any cleaner on a hidden area first. Let the bag air dry away from direct sunlight or heat.

### Storage Recommendations

* Store your bag in a dust bag or pillowcase to avoid scratches.
* Keep it in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
* Fill the bag with tissue paper or bubble wrap to keep its shape.
* Avoid hanging the bag by its straps to prevent stretching.
* Check the bag regularly for any signs of wear or damage.
