## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Carry-on Size For Airline Travel?



The ideal carry-on size is usually 20 to 22 inches. Most airlines accept this for overhead bins. Models like Amazon Basics 21" and Samsonite Freeform 23" fit these dimensions well. Always check your airline’s specific size rules before traveling.



### Which Carry-on Suitcases Offer The Most Durability?



Hardside suitcases like Samsonite Omni 2 and Travelers Club Chicago are highly durable. They feature scratch-resistant polycarbonate shells. These cases resist impacts and protect belongings. Softside options like SwissGear Sion offer flexibility but less protection.



### Are Expandable Carry-ons Worth Buying?



Expandable carry-ons like Amazon Basics 21" and Rockland Melbourne add up to 25% more space. They are great for extra packing needs. However, they may be heavier and bulkier. Choose based on your typical travel and packing style.



### Do Carry-ons With Spinner Wheels Improve Mobility?



Yes, spinner wheels offer 360-degree rotation. Models like Samsonite Freeform and KROSER ensure smooth, easy movement. They reduce strain and help navigate crowded airports. Spinner wheels are highly recommended for frequent travelers.



### What Are The Benefits Of Tsa-approved Locks On Carry-ons?



TSA-approved locks, found on Samsonite and BAGSMART luggage, allow TSA agents to open bags without damage. This enhances security and prevents forced entry. It’s essential for hassle-free security checks during air travel.
