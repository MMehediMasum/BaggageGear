## Step-by-step Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-your-calpak-luggage-lock-can-feel-tricky-at-first,-but-breaking-it-down-into-simple-steps-makes-it-manageable.-this-process-lets-you-create-a-new-combination-that-only-you-know,-improving-your-luggage’s-security.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting your Calpak luggage lock can feel tricky at first, but breaking it down into simple steps makes it manageable. This process lets you create a new combination that only you know, improving your luggage’s security. The key is to move carefully through each stage to avoid mistakes.

### Locating The Reset Button

Start by finding the reset button on your lock. It’s usually a small, recessed button near the dials or on the side of the lock body. You might need a pen or a small tool to press it, so have one handy.

Not spotting it right away? Don’t worry—check the edges and corners carefully. The button’s design is meant to prevent accidental resets, so it’s often hidden just enough to require some focus.

### Setting A New Combination

Once you press and hold the reset button, your lock’s dials will be ready for a new code. Choose a combination that’s easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess. Avoid obvious sequences like “000” or “123.”

Turn the dials slowly and deliberately to set your new numbers. If you rush, you might miss the precise alignment needed for the lock to register your chosen combination.

### Confirming The Reset

After setting your new code, release the reset button carefully. Try spinning the dials away from your new combination and then back to it to check if the lock opens smoothly. This test confirms that your reset was successful.

If the lock doesn’t open, don’t panic. Repeat the process, ensuring you hold the reset button firmly and set the numbers accurately. Taking your time here saves frustration later.

## Troubleshooting Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-a-calpak-luggage-lock-can-save-time-and-stress.-small-issues-often-cause-the-lock-to-not-work-correctly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting a Calpak luggage lock can save time and stress. Small issues often cause the lock to not work correctly. Knowing simple fixes helps avoid damage and keeps your luggage secure.

### Handling Stuck Locks

A stuck lock is common and usually easy to fix. Try these steps to free the lock:

* Gently jiggle the dials while pulling the lock shank.
* Use a small amount of lubricant like graphite powder on the mechanism.
* Do not force the lock open; it can break internal parts.
* Check the lock for dirt or debris blocking the movement.

Patience is key. Small movements often release the lock safely.

### When Reset Button Isn’t Visible

Some Calpak locks hide the reset button to prevent accidental changes. Here’s what to do:

* Look closely inside the shank hole or near the dials for a small button or pinhole.
* Use a pointed object like a pen tip to press the hidden reset button.
* Hold the button down while setting your new code.
* Release the button only after finishing the reset process.

Read the lock’s manual if available. Different models may have unique reset methods.

### Can I Change The Calpak Lock Code Anytime?

### Is It Safe To Reset Calpak Locks By Myself?
