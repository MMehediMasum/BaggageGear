Frontier offers a basic free carry-on option, but it’s not as generous as some other airlines. You can bring one personal item on board without extra charge. This personal item must fit under the seat in front of you.

Anything larger than that—like a traditional carry-on suitcase—usually requires a fee. This means you need to be strategic about what you pack if you want to avoid paying more. Have you ever been surprised by a carry-on fee at the gate? Knowing this policy upfront prevents that.

Your free personal item must not exceed these dimensions:

This size is roughly the equivalent of a standard backpack or a large purse. If your bag fits within these measurements, you’re good to go without paying extra. Oversized bags will be checked at the gate for a fee, so measure your bag before you travel.
