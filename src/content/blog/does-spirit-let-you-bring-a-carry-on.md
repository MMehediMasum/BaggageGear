## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Understanding Spirit Airlines' carry-on policy is essential before you pack for your trip. Spirit is known for its budget-friendly fares, but that comes with strict rules about what you can bring onboard. Knowing these rules can save you from unexpected fees and hassles at the airport.

### Carry-on Size Limits

Spirit allows one carry-on bag per passenger, but size matters a lot. Your carry-on must not exceed 22 x 18 x 10 inches, including handles and wheels. If your bag is bigger, expect extra charges or the need to check it in.

I once tried to sneak in a slightly larger bag, thinking it wouldn’t be noticed. Spoiler: it was noticed, and I ended up paying a hefty fee. Is it worth the risk to save a few minutes at the gate?

### Weight Restrictions

Surprisingly, Spirit Airlines does not impose a specific weight limit on carry-on bags. However, you must be able to lift your bag into the overhead bin without assistance. If your bag is too heavy for you to handle alone, gate agents may ask you to check it in.

This policy puts the responsibility on you. Think about the practical weight you can manage during boarding and deplaning. Could carrying a lighter bag make your airport experience smoother?

### Personal Item Rules

Along with a carry-on, you can bring one personal item like a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. This item must fit under the seat in front of you and cannot exceed 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

* Personal items are free of charge and don’t count as your carry-on.
* Items like a camera bag or a small diaper bag count as personal items.
* If you bring more than one personal item, expect to pay extra.

Have you ever tried to bring both a backpack and a purse without paying? Spirit’s strict enforcement makes it clear: only one personal item, period.

Adding a carry-on bag during booking is cheaper than adding it later. Spirit lets you pay for carry-ons while selecting your seat. This saves money and secures your space for the bag. Forgetting to add a carry-on during booking means paying more at the airport. Always add your carry-on bag early to avoid surprise costs.

### Online Vs Airport Fees

Spirit charges less for carry-ons booked online than at the airport. Online carry-on fees are up to twice cheaper than airport prices. Paying for your carry-on at the airport can be costly and cause delays. It is smart to pay for carry-ons online during or right after booking. Avoid the higher airport fees by planning ahead.

### Carry-on Bag Screening

Spirit Airlines strictly enforces carry-on bag size and weight limits. Your bag must fit under the seat in front of you or in the overhead bin if you’ve purchased a larger carry-on allowance. If your bag doesn’t meet their requirements, expect it to be checked at the gate, sometimes with a fee.

### Gate Checking Bags

If your carry-on is too large or you didn’t pay for a larger carry-on option, Spirit will gate check your bag. This means your bag is taken at the gate and placed in the cargo hold for free. You’ll pick it up at baggage claim after your flight.

This process can add extra wait time when landing, so pack essentials in a personal item. Think about what you’ll need immediately after landing and keep it with you. Have you packed your carry-on to avoid gate checking before?

### Priority Boarding Options

Spirit offers priority boarding for an additional fee or to passengers with specific fare types. Priority boarding lets you board earlier, helping you secure overhead bin space for your carry-on. This can be a big advantage if you want to keep your bag with you on the plane.

Consider paying for priority boarding if you value convenience and time. Have you noticed how much smoother boarding feels when you get on early? It’s a small upgrade that can make a big difference.

## Packing Strategies For Spirit Flights

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-for-spirit-airlines-can-feel-like-a-puzzle,-especially-with-their-strict-rules-about-carry-ons-and-personal-items.-you-want-to-bring-everything-you-need-without-paying-extra-fees-or-getting-stuck-at-the-gate.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing for Spirit Airlines can feel like a puzzle, especially with their strict rules about carry-ons and personal items. You want to bring everything you need without paying extra fees or getting stuck at the gate. Smart packing strategies make a big difference, helping you fit more into your allowed space while avoiding surprises at check-in.

### Maximizing Personal Item Space

Your personal item is the key to saving money on Spirit flights. This item must fit under the seat in front of you, so size matters a lot. Choose a backpack or tote that’s compact yet expandable.

Roll your clothes tightly to squeeze more into your personal item. Use packing cubes or compression bags to organize and compress your belongings further. Keep essentials like your phone charger, snacks, and a book in easy-to-reach pockets.

Have you tested your bag’s fit under a chair at home? It’s a simple step that saves stress at the airport.

### Smart Packing For Carry-ons

If you decide to bring a carry-on, make it count. Pick lightweight luggage with durable wheels to avoid extra strain. Prioritize items you’ll need during the flight or immediately after landing.

Limit shoes to one or two pairs, and wear your bulkiest items on the plane to save space. Layer your clothing to reduce the number of items packed. Don’t forget to leave room for souvenirs or essentials you might pick up during your trip.

### What To Leave Behind

Every inch of your bag counts, so be ruthless about what you pack. Skip heavy books and instead download e-books or podcasts. Avoid packing full-size toiletries; travel-sized bottles work just as well.

Ask yourself: Will I really use this during my trip? If the answer is no or maybe, leave it behind. Streamlining your items not only saves space but also lightens your load, making travel easier and more enjoyable.
