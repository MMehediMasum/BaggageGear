## Virgin Atlantic Carry-on Policy

Virgin Atlantic has clear rules about carry-on bags. Knowing these rules helps avoid problems at the airport. The airline sets limits on size and weight for carry-on luggage. This keeps the cabin safe and comfortable for all passengers.

Passengers should check their carry-on bags before boarding. This ensures the bags fit in the overhead bins or under the seat. Virgin Atlantic also lists some exceptions for special items.

### Allowed Dimensions And Weight

Virgin Atlantic allows one main carry-on bag per passenger. The size must not exceed 23 x 36 x 56 cm (9 x 14 x 22 inches). The weight limit is 10 kg (22 lbs). This weight includes handles and wheels.

In addition to the main bag, passengers can bring one personal item. This could be a handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack. The personal item must fit under the seat in front.

### Exceptions And Special Items

Some items do not count as carry-on luggage. These include:

* Coats and umbrellas
* Duty-free shopping bags
* Baby items like a stroller or diaper bag
* Medical devices needed during the flight

Musical instruments and sports equipment may need special handling. Passengers should check with Virgin Atlantic before travel. This avoids surprises and extra fees at the airport.

Yes, Virgin Atlantic may weigh carry-on bags at boarding to ensure they meet size and weight limits. This helps avoid extra fees and delays.

### What Is The Carry-on Weight Limit For Virgin Atlantic?

Virgin Atlantic allows carry-on bags up to 23kg (51lbs) for Upper Class and Premium passengers. Economy class has a 10kg (22lbs) limit.

### Are There Size Restrictions For Virgin Atlantic Carry-ons?

Yes, carry-on bags must not exceed 56 x 36 x 23 cm. Oversized bags may be checked in or incur fees.

### What Happens If My Carry-on Is Overweight On Virgin Atlantic?

If your carry-on exceeds weight or size limits, you may be asked to check it in. Additional fees could apply.
