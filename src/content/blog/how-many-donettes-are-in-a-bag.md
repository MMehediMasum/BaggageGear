## Tips For Storing Donettes

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=storing-donettes-properly-keeps-them-tasty-and-fresh-longer.-small-changes-in-storage-can-protect-their-flavor-and-texture.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Storing Donettes properly keeps them tasty and fresh longer. Small changes in storage can protect their flavor and texture. Use simple tips to enjoy every bite.

### Maintaining Freshness

Keep Donettes away from heat and sunlight. Store them in a cool, dry place. Avoid opening the bag too often to stop air from making them stale. Seal the bag tightly after each use.

### Proper Containers

Use airtight containers to keep Donettes fresh. Plastic containers with tight lids work well. Glass jars with seals also protect against moisture. Avoid containers that let air or humidity inside.

### Shelf Life

Donettes usually stay fresh for about one week after opening. Check the expiration date on the package before buying. Store them at room temperature for best taste. Refrigeration can dry them out faster.
