## Choosing The Right Combination

Choosing the right combination for your luggage lock is important for security and convenience. A good combination keeps your belongings safe and lets you open the lock easily. It should be easy to remember but hard for others to guess.

### Default Codes To Start With

Most combination locks come preset with a default code. Common defaults include:

* 000
* 123
* 999

Start by trying these codes to open your lock. After opening, change the code to a unique one for better security.

### Tips For Creating A Strong Combination

* Avoid simple sequences like 111 or 123.
* Use numbers that are easy for you to remember.
* Do not use your birth year or important dates.
* Mix numbers that are not related to your personal info.
* Pick a combination with at least three digits.
* Change your code if you think someone else knows it.

## Step-by-step Reset Process

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=resetting-the-combination-lock-on-your-away-luggage-is-simpler-than-you-might-think.-knowing-the-exact-steps-can-save-you-from-frustration-and-ensure-your-belongings-stay-secure.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Resetting the combination lock on your Away luggage is simpler than you might think. Knowing the exact steps can save you from frustration and ensure your belongings stay secure. Let’s walk through the process carefully so you can set a code that’s easy for you to remember but tough for others to guess.

### Setting The Lock To Factory Code

Start by aligning the dials to the factory default code, which is usually **0-0-0**. This is the only way to access the reset function. If your lock doesn’t open with this code, double-check your luggage manual or the label inside your suitcase for the original combination.

Have you ever struggled to remember if you changed the code or not? Sticking to the factory setting initially helps avoid that confusion.

### Activating The Reset Mode

Once the dials are set to the factory code, locate the small reset button or lever near the lock. Depending on the model, you might need a pen or a similar object to press it. Hold or slide this button as instructed—usually for about 5 seconds—to enter reset mode.

This step is crucial; if you miss it, your new code won’t be saved. Take your time and ensure the lock signals that it’s ready for a new combination.

### Entering Your New Combination

With the reset mode activated, carefully turn the dials to your preferred three-digit combination. Choose numbers that mean something to you but are not easily guessed, like your birthday or simple sequences. Avoid obvious choices such as 1-2-3 or 9-9-9.

Think about a number sequence you can recall without writing it down. How often do you struggle to remember pins or codes? This moment is your chance to create a memorable but secure combination.

### Finalizing The Reset

After setting your new combination, release the reset button or return the lever to its original position. Test the lock by scrambling the dials and then entering your new code to make sure it unlocks smoothly. If it doesn’t open, repeat the process carefully.

Double-checking now prevents the hassle of being locked out later. Have you ever experienced the panic of forgetting your luggage code at the airport? Avoid that by confirming your reset works immediately.




## Testing Your New Combination

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=testing-your-new-combination-is-crucial-after-setting-your-away-luggage-lock.-it-confirms-the-lock-works-perfectly-and-keeps-your-belongings-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Testing your new combination is crucial after setting your Away luggage lock. It confirms the lock works perfectly and keeps your belongings safe. This process helps avoid surprises at the airport or hotel.

Take your time to check the lock carefully. Make sure it opens smoothly without force. Testing also shows if the combination is set correctly or needs adjustment.

### Verifying Lock Opens Smoothly

Turn the dials to your new combination slowly and carefully. Pull the lock latch or button gently to open it.

* The lock should open without sticking or resistance.
* If it feels tight, turn the dials back and try again.
* Practice opening and closing the lock several times.

This helps the mechanism settle into the new code. It also builds confidence that your combination is working right.

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

Sometimes the lock won’t open as expected. This may be due to:

* Incorrect combination entry
* Dials not aligned properly
* Lock mechanism stuck or jammed

Reset the dials to your combination carefully. Make sure each number lines up perfectly with the indicator.

If the lock still won’t open, try these tips:

* Move the dials back and forth gently to loosen the mechanism.
* Press or slide the latch firmly but without force.
* Double-check the combination you set.

Patience helps. Avoid forcing the lock to prevent damage. If problems persist, consult Away’s customer support for help.

## Tips To Remember Your Combination

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=remembering-your-luggage-lock-combination-can-be-tricky,-especially-when-you-have-multiple-codes-to-keep-track-of.-yet,-forgetting-it-can-cause-unnecessary-stress-during-your-trip.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Remembering your luggage lock combination can be tricky, especially when you have multiple codes to keep track of. Yet, forgetting it can cause unnecessary stress during your trip. These tips will help you keep your combination secure and easily accessible when you need it.

### Safe Storage Ideas

Write your combination down, but don’t just leave it lying around. Store it in a discreet place, like inside your phone’s note app under a vague title or in a small piece of paper tucked into your wallet.

Some travelers prefer saving combinations in a digital password manager. This way, your code is protected by another layer of security and always with you on your phone.

Think about hiding the code in plain sight. For example, jot it down on a calendar on the day you set or last changed the combination. This subtle reminder might help you recall it without drawing attention.

### Using Mnemonics Or Patterns

Create a mnemonic that relates to your combination. If your code is 2739, you might think: “2 Tigers, 7 Eagles, 3 Bears, 9 Snakes.” This makes the numbers easier to remember by linking them to vivid images.

Patterns can also help. Look at the lock dial and see if your combination forms a shape or sequence, such as a zigzag or a straight line. You’ll find it easier to recall a visual pattern than random digits.

Ask yourself: does the combination relate to a meaningful date, like a birthday or anniversary? Adding a small twist—like adding 1 to each number—can keep it memorable and more secure.
