## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Best For Extra Large Suitcases?



Extra large suitcases typically range from 28 to 30 inches. They offer ample space for extended trips and bulky items. Models like the Amazon Basics 30" and Samsonite Freeform 28" fit this category well.



### Are Hardshell Or Softside Suitcases Better For Durability?



Hardshell suitcases, such as Samsonite Omni 2 and American Tourister Stratum 2. 0, provide better scratch resistance. Softside bags like SwissGear Sion offer flexibility but may be less impact-resistant.



### How Important Is Expandability In Large Suitcases?



Expandability adds up to 15% more packing space. Many models, including Amazon Basics and Travelpro Maxlite 5, feature expandable compartments for extra convenience.



### Do All Extra Large Suitcases Have Tsa Locks?



Most premium models, like Samsonite Freeform and Away Large Checked Luggage, include TSA-approved locks for security. It’s essential for hassle-free airport inspections.



### What Wheel Types Are Best For Large Suitcases?



Four multi-directional spinner wheels, as seen in SwissGear Sion and Travelpro Platinum Elite, provide smooth maneuverability and reduce strain during travel.



### Are Lightweight Materials Common In Extra Large Suitcases?



Yes, brands like Coolife and Samsonite use lightweight polycarbonate or aluminum frames. This reduces overall weight, making large suitcases easier to handle.
