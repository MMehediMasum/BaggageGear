## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Material For Carry-on Hard Shell Luggage?



Hard shell carry-on luggage is often made from ABS or polycarbonate. ABS is lightweight and affordable. Polycarbonate is more durable and flexible, offering better impact resistance. Both materials provide excellent protection, but polycarbonate usually lasts longer and withstands rough handling better.



### How Important Is Tsa Lock On Carry-on Luggage?



A TSA lock ensures airport security can open your luggage without damage. It keeps your belongings secure during travel. Most hard shell carry-ons, like Sweetcrispy and Samsonite, come with TSA-approved locks, adding convenience and peace of mind for frequent travelers.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Hard Shell Carry-ons?



Spinner wheels offer 360-degree rotation for smooth maneuverability. They reduce strain on your arms and make navigation through airports easier. Most carry-ons listed, such as Amazon Basics and LEVEL8, feature double or four spinner wheels for enhanced mobility and stability.



### Can I Expand Hard Shell Carry-on Luggage For Extra Space?



Yes, several models like Amazon Basics and Travelers Club Chicago offer expandable compartments. This feature increases packing capacity by up to 25%. It’s ideal for travelers needing extra room without checking in luggage, providing flexibility for varied trip lengths.



### Which Carry-on Size Is Best For Airline Approval?



Most airlines accept carry-ons sized around 20 to 22 inches. Luggage like BAGSMART 20 Inch and Samsonite Omni 2 20 Inch fits these requirements. Always check specific airline size rules to avoid gate check fees or delays.



### How Lightweight Are These Hard Shell Carry-ons?



Models like Sweetcrispy and LEVEL8 are designed to be lightweight for easy handling. They typically weigh between 5 to 7 pounds. Lightweight luggage helps meet airline weight limits and reduces travel fatigue during long trips.
