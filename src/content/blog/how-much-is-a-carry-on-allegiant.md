## Allegiant Carry-on Basics

Understanding the basics of Allegiant's carry-on policies helps avoid surprises at the airport. Knowing size and weight rules can save time and money. Allegiant Airlines has clear guidelines for carry-on luggage. These rules protect passengers and keep flights smooth. Let's explore the key points about Allegiant carry-ons.

### Carry-on Size Limits

Allegiant allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size is 22 x 14 x 9 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Bags larger than these dimensions may not fit in overhead bins. It’s best to measure your bag before heading to the airport. Small personal items like purses or laptop bags are allowed too. These must fit under the seat in front.

### Weight Restrictions

Allegiant does not have a strict weight limit for carry-ons. But heavy bags may be hard to lift into overhead compartments. Passengers should pack light to avoid difficulties. Checking weight helps prevent delays during boarding. Overweight bags might need to be checked, which can cost extra. Keep carry-ons manageable to ensure a smooth travel experience.

Allegiant allows carry-ons up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. The maximum weight is usually 40 pounds. Bags exceeding these limits may require checked baggage fees. It’s best to measure and weigh your bag before traveling to avoid extra charges.
