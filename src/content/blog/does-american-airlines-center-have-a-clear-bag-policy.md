The American Airlines Center has clear bag rules to keep guests safe and speed up entry. These rules help security check bags quickly. They also make the event experience smooth for everyone. Knowing these rules before arriving can save time and avoid hassle.

### Types Of Bags Allowed

The American Airlines Center allows only clear bags or small clutch bags. Clear bags must be made of see-through plastic, vinyl, or PVC. The maximum size allowed for clear bags is 12" x 6" x 12". * Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags

### Screening Process

First, you'll be asked to present your clear bag. American Airlines Center enforces a strict clear bag policy, so only transparent bags that meet their size requirements are allowed. This speeds up the screening because security can easily see what's inside without digging through your belongings.

Next, you'll place your bag and personal items in bins for X-ray scanning. You’ll walk through a metal detector or body scanner afterward. If the scanner detects anything unusual, a security officer may perform a quick pat-down or manual bag check.

* **Electronic devices:**Laptops and tablets usually need to be removed from bags for separate screening. Be prepared to take them out quickly.
* **Clothing with metal:**Heavy jewelry, belts, or clothing with metal can trigger alarms. Try to minimize these to speed up your passage.
