Taking good care of the backpack Luigi Mangione used helps keep it strong and lasting longer. Proper care prevents damage and keeps the material looking new. Small efforts make a big difference in the backpack's life.

### Cleaning Instructions

Use a soft brush to remove dirt and dust from the surface. Spot clean stains with mild soap and warm water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the fabric. Let the backpack air dry naturally, away from direct sunlight. Do not use a washing machine or dryer, as they may harm the backpack's shape and material.

### Storage Advice

Store the backpack in a cool, dry place to avoid mold and mildew. Keep it away from sharp objects that can cause tears. Fill the backpack with soft items like clothes to keep its shape. Avoid folding or crushing the backpack during storage. Use a dust cover or cloth bag for extra protection.

### Repair And Maintenance

Check zippers and straps regularly for signs of wear. Fix small tears or loose threads quickly to prevent bigger damage. Use fabric glue or patches for minor repairs. Replace broken zippers or buckles to keep the backpack functional. Regularly tighten screws or fasteners to maintain durability.
