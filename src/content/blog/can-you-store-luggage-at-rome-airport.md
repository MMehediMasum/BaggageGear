## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Store Luggage At Rome Airport Overnight?

Yes, Rome Airport offers luggage storage services. You can leave your bags safely overnight for a small fee. This service is available in Terminals 1 and 3, providing convenience for travelers with long layovers or early flights.

### How Much Does Luggage Storage Cost At Rome Airport?

Luggage storage prices at Rome Airport start around €6 per item for the first 5 hours. Longer storage periods increase the cost slightly. Prices vary by size and duration, making it affordable for short or extended storage needs.

### Where Is The Luggage Storage Located At Rome Airport?

Luggage storage is located near the arrivals area in Terminals 1 and 3. Clear signs guide you to the lockers and staffed counters. This makes it easy to find and use the service upon arrival or before departure.

### Are There Size Limits For Luggage Storage At Rome Airport?

Yes, Rome Airport’s storage facilities accept most standard luggage sizes. Oversized items may require special arrangements or incur extra charges. It’s best to check with the storage provider in advance for large or unusual bags.
