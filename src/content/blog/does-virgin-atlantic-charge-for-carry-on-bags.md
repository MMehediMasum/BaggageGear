## Virgin Atlantic Carry-on Policy

Virgin Atlantic has clear rules about carry-on bags. Knowing these rules helps avoid problems at the airport. You can bring your bag on board without paying extra if it meets the airline’s standards.

Check the size, weight, and type of bags allowed. Stick to these rules for a smooth travel experience.

### Allowed Carry-on Dimensions

Carry-on bags must fit within specific size limits. The maximum dimensions are:

* 23 x 36 x 56 cm (9 x 14 x 22 inches)

Bags larger than this may not be accepted as carry-on. Make sure your bag fits these measurements.

### Weight Restrictions

Virgin Atlantic limits the weight of carry-on bags. Each passenger can carry:

* Up to 10 kg (22 lbs) in total

Keep your bag under this limit to avoid extra fees or checking it in.

### Types Of Carry-on Bags Permitted

Virgin Atlantic allows different types of carry-on bags. Passengers can bring:

* One main cabin bag
* One personal item like a handbag or laptop bag

Both items must fit under the seat or in the overhead bin. Follow these rules to keep your carry-ons hassle-free.
