## Backpack Policy At Msg

The backpack policy at Madison Square Garden (MSG) is designed to ensure safety while keeping your experience hassle-free. Knowing the rules before you head to an event can save you time and frustration at the entrance. Let’s break down what you need to know about bringing a backpack into MSG.

### Size Restrictions

MSG strictly enforces size limits on backpacks and bags. Your backpack must be no larger than 14" x 14" x 6". This size ensures your bag fits through security checks quickly and doesn’t block aisles or seats.

If your backpack exceeds these dimensions, you might be asked to leave it outside or use a clear bag that meets the guidelines. Have you ever been caught off guard by unexpected size rules at a venue? Planning ahead helps avoid that hassle.

### Allowed Materials

Clear backpacks or bags made of transparent plastic are encouraged because they speed up entry. You can bring essentials like a wallet, phone, keys, and small personal items comfortably within the size limits.

Soft-sided bags are preferred. MSG allows backpacks with minimal metal parts to ease the security screening. Think about what you really need to carry—extra bulk can slow you down.

### Prohibited Items

Certain items are banned inside MSG, regardless of your backpack size. No weapons, illegal substances, outside food and beverages, or large professional cameras are allowed.

Even seemingly harmless items like selfie sticks or drones are prohibited. These rules keep everyone safe and ensure that your event runs smoothly.

Always check the official MSG website before your visit to confirm the latest list of prohibited items. Have you ever had to discard something important at security? It’s frustrating but better than missing the event.
