## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policies

Understanding Spirit Airlines’ carry-on policies can save you time and unexpected fees at the airport. The airline has specific rules about the size, weight, and types of carry-on items you can bring. Knowing these details helps you pack smarter and avoid last-minute hassles.

### Carry-on Size Limits

Spirit Airlines allows one carry-on bag per passenger, but it must fit within strict size limits. Your carry-on should not exceed **22 x 18 x 10 inches**, including handles and wheels. If your bag is larger, you’ll likely need to check it or pay additional fees.

Think about your last trip: Did your bag fit easily in the overhead bin, or did you struggle to shove it in? Spirit’s size rules mean you need a compact bag that slides in without trouble. It’s worth measuring your luggage before you pack.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike many airlines, Spirit Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. However, you must be able to lift your bag into the overhead compartment without help. This means your carry-on should be manageable for you to handle alone.

While you won’t see a strict weight cap, packing heavy can slow you down and cause stress. Have you ever tried lifting a heavy bag overhead? It’s not fun, and it can hold up the boarding process for everyone.

### Personal Item Rules

In addition to your carry-on, Spirit Airlines allows one personal item per passenger. This item must fit under the seat in front of you and should not exceed **18 x 14 x 8 inches**. Typical personal items include backpacks, purses, or laptop bags.

Don’t confuse this with the carry-on bag; personal items are smaller and meant to hold essentials like your wallet, phone, or a book. Have you ever tried fitting a large backpack under the seat? Spirit is strict about these dimensions, so choose wisely.

Spirit Airlines charges for standard carry-on bags based on when you pay.

* **During booking:**The lowest fee applies.
* **Before check-in:**Fees rise slightly.
* **At the airport:**Highest fees apply.

### Personal Item Fees

Spirit allows one free personal item per passenger. This must fit under the seat.

Items like small backpacks, purses, or laptop bags qualify. Larger items count as carry-ons and cost extra.

Extra fees may apply beyond the carry-on cost.

* Overweight or oversized bags incur extra charges.
