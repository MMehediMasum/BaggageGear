## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Used In The Best 3 Piece Luggage Sets?



Top 3 piece luggage sets use durable materials like polycarbonate, ABS, and PC-ABS blend. These materials offer strong impact resistance and lightweight features, ensuring long-lasting travel protection. Brands like Coolife, Samsonite, and SHOWKOO use these for sturdy yet lightweight suitcases.



### How Does The Tsa Lock Improve Luggage Security?



TSA locks allow secure locking while enabling TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. This feature is standard in many 3 piece sets like Coolife and TILYMOL. It ensures your belongings stay safe and compliant with airport security regulations, making travel hassle-free.



### Are Expandable Luggage Sets Worth Buying?



Yes, expandable luggage sets provide up to 25% more packing space. This feature is ideal for travelers who pack extra items or souvenirs. Sets like Amazon Basics and Westridge include expandable compartments, enhancing flexibility without compromising luggage durability.



### What Wheel Types Are Best For Smooth Travel?



Four multi-directional spinner wheels offer the best maneuverability and stability. Most top sets, including Coolife and Samsonite, feature these wheels for effortless 360-degree movement. Spinner wheels reduce strain and improve mobility on various surfaces, perfect for busy airports.



### How Do I Choose The Right 3 Piece Luggage Size?



Choose luggage sizes based on trip duration and airline carry-on rules. Common sets include 20-21” carry-ons and 24-30” checked bags. For example, Samsonite and Amazon Basics offer these size ranges, covering short trips to extended vacations efficiently.



### Can Hardshell Luggage Resist Scratches And Impacts?



Yes, hardshell luggage with scratch-resistant surfaces, like those from Amazon Basics and SHOWKOO, protect against daily wear. Materials like PC-ABS blends enhance durability and maintain a polished look. These cases safeguard contents while minimizing cosmetic damage during travel.
