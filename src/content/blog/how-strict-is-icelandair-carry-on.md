## Icelandair Carry-on Allowance

Understanding Icelandair’s carry-on allowance can save you from last-minute hassles at the airport. Knowing exactly what you can bring onboard helps you pack smart and avoid unexpected fees or repacking. Let’s look closely at the specific rules for size, weight, and the number of carry-on items allowed.

### Size And Weight Limits

Icelandair sets clear limits on the size and weight of your carry-on luggage. Your bag should not exceed **55 cm x 40 cm x 20 cm**(about 21.6 x 15.7 x 7.8 inches). This ensures it fits comfortably in the overhead compartments.

Weight matters too. The maximum weight allowed for your carry-on is **10 kilograms (22 pounds)**. Exceeding this can mean you'll need to check your bag or pay extra fees. Have you ever struggled with a bag that’s just a bit too heavy? This limit helps you avoid that stress.

### Number Of Carry-on Items Allowed

You can bring **one main carry-on bag**plus **one personal item**like a handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack. The personal item must fit under the seat in front of you.

This rule is practical. It lets you keep your essentials close without overcrowding the cabin. Think about your last flight—did you have enough space for your laptop and book? Icelandair’s policy ensures you won’t have to choose between them.

## Carry-on Restrictions

### Prohibited Items In Carry-on

Icelandair has a clear list of items that you cannot bring in your carry-on bag. Sharp objects like knives and scissors over 6 cm are not allowed. Firearms, explosives, and flammable materials are strictly forbidden.

Even some everyday items can be restricted. Sporting goods like baseball bats or golf clubs are not permitted as carry-on. If you're carrying tools, make sure they are small and non-threatening.

Have you ever been surprised by an item you thought was harmless getting confiscated? Double-checking the prohibited list can prevent that frustration.

### Liquids And Gels Regulations

Liquids and gels must follow the 100 ml rule on Icelandair flights. Each container should be 100 ml or less, and all containers must fit into a single, clear, resealable plastic bag no larger than 1 liter.

This bag must be taken out separately during security checks. Remember, this includes lotions, perfumes, toothpaste, and even drinks you might want to bring on board.

Think about your last trip—did you pack liquids without this rule in mind? It’s a quick fix that can save you time and avoid throwing away your favorite products at security.

Consider wearing your heaviest items, like boots and jackets, during the flight to free up room inside your bag. Have you tried vacuum-seal bags? Another mistake is packing liquids without proper containers or not following the 100ml rule for carry-ons. Avoid last-minute repacking by preparing your liquids in clear, resealable bags. Also, don’t forget to check Icelandair’s latest carry-on policies as they can change.
