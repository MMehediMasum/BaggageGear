## Allegiant Air Carry-on Policy

Understanding Allegiant Air's carry-on policy is crucial before packing your bags. It can save you from unexpected fees and hassle at the airport. Knowing exactly what you can bring onboard without extra charges helps you travel smarter and lighter.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Allegiant Air allows one standard carry-on bag per passenger, but here's the catch: this carry-on must fit under the seat in front of you. If your bag is too large for the under-seat space, it will need to be checked, which usually comes with an additional fee.

The maximum dimensions for a standard carry-on are typically around 24 x 16 x 10 inches. Keep this in mind when choosing your bag. Many travelers mistakenly assume they can bring a larger overhead bin bag for free, only to face charges at the gate.

Think about the last time you traveled—did your bag slide neatly under the seat? If not, consider downsizing to avoid fees and save time during boarding.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Besides the carry-on, you can bring one personal item onboard for free. This item must fit under the seat and be smaller than the carry-on allowance.

* Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.
* Dimensions generally should not exceed 18 x 14 x 8 inches.
* This is perfect for keeping essentials like your phone, wallet, and travel documents within easy reach.

Have you ever struggled to find space for your personal items during boarding? Allegiant's clear size limits help avoid that problem, but only if you pack smartly.
