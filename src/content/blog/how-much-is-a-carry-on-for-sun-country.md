## Sun Country Carry-on Allowance

Understanding Sun Country’s carry-on allowance helps avoid surprises at the airport. Knowing the rules lets you pack smart and travel stress-free. Sun Country Airlines has specific limits on carry-on size and weight. They also have clear rules for personal items.

### Size And Weight Limits

Sun Country allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size is 9 x 14 x 22 inches. This includes handles and wheels. The carry-on should fit in the overhead bin. The weight limit is 25 pounds. Bags over these limits may need checked baggage fees.

### Personal Item Policy

Passengers can bring one personal item in addition to the carry-on. Personal items include purses, laptop bags, or small backpacks. The size must not exceed 9 x 10 x 17 inches. The personal item should fit under the seat in front. No weight limit is stated, but it must be manageable by the passenger.
