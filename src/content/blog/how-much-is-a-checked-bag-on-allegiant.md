## Allegiant Baggage Policies

Allegiant Airlines has specific rules for baggage to keep travel simple and affordable. Knowing these rules helps avoid surprises at the airport. The airline charges extra for checked bags, but the fees depend on several factors.

### Standard Allowances

Passengers can bring one free personal item on board. This item must fit under the seat. Examples include a purse, small backpack, or laptop bag.

Checked bags are not included in the ticket price. Fees vary based on route and timing of payment. Generally, paying online before the flight is cheaper than at the airport.

Here is a quick summary of typical fees:

| Bag Type | Fee Range |
| --- | --- |
| 1st Checked Bag | $20 - $40 |
| 2nd Checked Bag | $30 - $50 |
| Oversized Bag | $50 - $100 |

Weight and size limits apply. Bags over 50 pounds or larger than 62 inches may cost more.

### Carry-on Vs Checked Bags

Carry-on bags let you keep belongings with you. They must fit in the overhead bin.

* Maximum size: 10 x 16 x 24 inches
* Weight limit varies by flight

Checked bags go in the airplane’s cargo hold. They allow more space but cost extra.

Choosing between carry-on and checked bags depends on your needs. Carry-ons save money and time. Checked bags are better for longer trips or more items.
