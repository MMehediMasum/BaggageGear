## Carry-on Policy For Basic Fare

### What’s Allowed Onboard

With JetBlue Basic Fare, you are allowed to bring one personal item free of charge. This item must fit under the seat in front of you, such as a backpack, purse, or laptop bag.

Unlike other fare types, the Basic Fare does **not**include a free carry-on bag that goes into the overhead bin. If you want to bring a standard carry-on suitcase, you will need to pay an additional fee.

Have you ever reached the gate only to find out your carry-on isn’t included? It’s a common frustration. Being aware of this policy upfront can save you from last-minute charges.

### Size And Weight Limits

Your personal item must meet specific size restrictions to qualify as carry-on for the Basic Fare. JetBlue requires this item to be no larger than **17 x 13 x 8 inches**. This ensures it fits easily under the seat.

Weight limits are generally flexible for personal items, but oversized or heavy bags might be questioned at the gate. It’s best to keep your bag light and compact to avoid any issues.

Remember, your carry-on bag, if purchased separately, can be up to **22 x 14 x 9 inches**. That’s larger and fits in the overhead bin, but you’ll pay extra for that privilege with a Basic Fare.
