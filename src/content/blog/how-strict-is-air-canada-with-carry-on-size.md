## Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding Air Canada's carry-on size limits can save you from last-minute gate hassles. Knowing the exact dimensions and weight allowed helps you pack smartly and avoid unexpected fees. Let’s break down what Air Canada expects from your carry-on luggage.

### Dimensions Allowed

Air Canada allows carry-on bags with maximum dimensions of **23 cm x 40 cm x 55 cm**(9 in x 15.5 in x 21.5 in), including handles and wheels.

This size fits comfortably in the overhead bins, which means your bag won’t be gate-checked due to size issues. If your bag is even a little bigger, you might face delays or extra charges, so measuring your luggage is a smart move.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike some airlines, Air Canada does not have a strict weight limit for carry-on bags on most flights.

However, your bag must be light enough for you to lift it into the overhead bin without assistance. If your carry-on is too heavy or bulky, gate agents may ask you to check it.

Keep in mind that your personal item, such as a backpack or purse, should fit under the seat in front of you and be easy to handle.
