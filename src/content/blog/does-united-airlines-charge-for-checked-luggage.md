## United Airlines Baggage Policy

Understanding United Airlines' baggage policy is key to avoiding unexpected fees and travel hassles. Whether you're packing for a quick weekend getaway or a longer trip, knowing what you can check in and what costs extra can save you time and money. Let’s break down the essentials so you can travel smarter.

### Standard Checked Baggage Allowance

United Airlines typically allows one checked bag for free on many domestic flights if you have certain fare types or elite status. Otherwise, most travelers pay a fee for their first checked bag. The fees can vary depending on your route and ticket class, so it’s smart to check your specific flight details ahead of time.

For international flights, the allowance often increases, with some destinations letting you check two bags without extra charge. Are you traveling with kids or carrying sports gear? Sometimes special items have different rules, so it’s worth investigating before you pack.

### Weight And Size Limits

Your checked bag must meet United's weight and size limits to avoid additional fees. Typically, the weight limit is 50 pounds (23 kg) per bag, and the maximum size is 62 linear inches (length + width + height). Going over these limits means you’ll pay overweight or oversized baggage fees, which can add up quickly.

You can avoid this by weighing your bags at home and measuring their dimensions before you leave. Have you ever been surprised by baggage fees at the airport? Planning ahead can keep your wallet happy.




## Checked Baggage Fees

Understanding United Airlines' checked baggage fees can save you from unexpected costs at the airport. These fees vary depending on the number of bags, their size, and your ticket type. Knowing exactly what to expect helps you pack smarter and budget better for your trip.

### First Bag Fee

United Airlines charges a fee for the first checked bag on most standard economy tickets. This fee typically ranges from $30 to $35 within the U.S., but it can vary depending on your route. If you have a MileagePlus Premier status or a premium cabin ticket, your first bag might be free.

Have you ever been surprised by a sudden baggage fee at check-in? Paying attention to this first bag fee can prevent that hassle and let you focus on your travel plans instead.

### Second Bag Fee

The second checked bag usually costs more than the first, often around $40 to $45. This fee applies even if your first bag fee was waived. It’s important to consider this if you tend to travel with multiple pieces of luggage.

Think about whether you really need that second bag or if you can combine items to avoid this extra charge. Small adjustments can make a big difference in your travel budget.

### Additional And Oversized Bags

Additional bags beyond the second often come with higher fees, sometimes $150 or more. Oversized or overweight bags also incur extra charges, depending on their dimensions and weight. United’s limits are usually 50 pounds per bag and 62 linear inches in size.

If you frequently travel with sports equipment or large items, checking these fees ahead of time is crucial. Planning how to pack or ship bulky items can save you both money and stress at the airport.

## Exceptions And Fee Waivers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=united-airlines-usually-charges-fees-for-checked-luggage.-some-travelers-may-not-have-to-pay-these-fees.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

United Airlines usually charges fees for checked luggage. Some travelers may not have to pay these fees. Certain exceptions and fee waivers apply. These depend on your status, payment method, or special circumstances. Understanding these exceptions can save money.

### Elite Status Benefits

United Airlines rewards frequent flyers with elite status. Members of MileagePlus Premier status get free checked bags. The number of free bags depends on the elite level.

* Premier Silver members get 1 free checked bag.
* Premier Gold members get 2 free checked bags.
* Premier Platinum and 1K members get 3 free checked bags.

This benefit applies to flights operated by United Airlines.

### Credit Card Perks

Certain United credit cards offer free checked bags. Cardholders and one companion flying on the same reservation can check bags for free.

* United Explorer Card
* United Club Card
* Other select United credit cards

This perk helps frequent travelers save on baggage fees.

### Military And Special Cases

Active-duty military members enjoy free checked bags on United flights. This applies to the member and their dependents. Proof of military ID is required at check-in.

Certain other groups may also qualify for fee waivers. Examples include passengers with disabilities and infants traveling with adults. Always check United’s policies before travel.

## How To Pay Checked Baggage Fees

### Online Payment Options

United Airlines allows passengers to pay baggage fees online. This option is available during booking or check-in on the airline’s website or mobile app.

* Secure payment through credit or debit cards
* Option to add bags to your reservation quickly
* Receive immediate confirmation of payment

Paying online helps avoid lines at the airport. It also locks in the fee price before traveling.

### Airport Payment Process

Passengers who skip online payment can pay at the airport. Payment is accepted at ticket counters and self-service kiosks.

* Cash or card accepted at counters
* Credit/debit cards accepted at kiosks
* Possible longer wait times during busy hours

Airport payment is convenient for last-minute decisions. Be prepared for potential delays, especially during peak travel times.

### Prepaid Baggage Advantages

Paying for checked bags before your trip offers clear benefits. Prepaid fees are often lower than airport charges.

* Guaranteed lower rates

* Less stress on travel day

Prepayment helps travelers plan their budget better. It also reduces hassle during airport check-in and boarding.

## Tips To Avoid Checked Baggage Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=avoiding-checked-baggage-fees-on-united-airlines-can-save-money-and-time-at-the-airport.-smart-packing-helps-travelers-carry-what-they-need-without-extra-costs.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Avoiding checked baggage fees on United Airlines can save money and time at the airport. Smart packing helps travelers carry what they need without extra costs. These tips guide you on how to travel light and use airline rules to your advantage.

### Packing Light And Carry-on Limits

Travel with only a carry-on to skip checked baggage fees. United Airlines allows one carry-on bag and one personal item for free. Choose a bag that fits the size limits easily. Pack essentials and wear bulky clothes to save space.

Use packing cubes to organize and compress clothes. Roll clothes instead of folding to reduce wrinkles and save space. Keep liquids in small containers under 3.4 ounces to follow TSA rules.

### Maximizing Allowances

Check your ticket type for baggage allowances. Some United tickets include one free checked bag. United MileagePlus members and certain credit card holders get extra perks.

Traveling with a companion? Combine your allowances for more bags. Pack heavier items in checked bags if you have free checked luggage. Weigh your bags at home to avoid overweight fees.

### Alternative Luggage Strategies

Use a backpack or duffel bag as a personal item to carry more. Ship bulky or heavy items to your destination by mail or courier. Buy United’s Premier Access or baggage bundles for savings on multiple bags.

Consider wearing layers of clothes to reduce packed items. Share luggage with family or friends traveling on the same flight. Choose lightweight luggage to avoid extra weight charges.






## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does United Airlines Charge For The First Checked Bag?

Yes, United Airlines usually charges for the first checked bag on most domestic flights. Fees vary by route and fare type. Some tickets, like basic economy, always have a fee, while premium fares may include free checked bags.

### How Much Is The Checked Bag Fee On United Airlines?

The checked bag fee on United Airlines typically starts at $35 for the first bag. Fees can increase for additional bags or overweight luggage. Always check the specific flight details, as fees may vary by destination and ticket class.

### Are Any United Airlines Passengers Exempt From Baggage Fees?

Yes, certain passengers are exempt from baggage fees. United MileagePlus Premier members, travelers with United credit cards, and international premium cabin flyers often get free checked bags. Always verify your eligibility before booking.

### Can I Prepay For Checked Baggage On United Airlines?

Yes, United Airlines allows prepayment for checked baggage during booking or online check-in. Prepaying usually costs less than paying at the airport. This option helps avoid long lines and unexpected fees.
