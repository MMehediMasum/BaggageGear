## Allegiant Carry-on Bag Policy

Understanding the Allegiant carry-on bag policy is essential before packing for your trip. Allegiant Air has clear rules about what you can bring onboard. These rules help keep flights safe and comfortable for everyone.

Knowing the size and weight limits can save you from extra fees. Also, it’s important to be aware of how many carry-on bags you can bring without paying more.

### Size And Weight Limits

Allegiant allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. The maximum size allowed is 22 x 14 x 9 inches.

The weight limit is 40 pounds. Bags over this weight may not be accepted as carry-on. Make sure your bag meets these rules to avoid extra charges at the airport.

### Included Carry-on Allowance

One carry-on bag is included with your ticket. Small personal items, like a purse or laptop bag, are also allowed. These items must fit under the seat in front of you.

Allegiant does not include a free checked bag. Carry-on is your main free luggage option. Extra bags or larger items will cost extra fees.

Allegiant typically charges for carry-on bags that exceed their size limits or for passengers who haven't paid for carry-on privileges upfront.

### At Booking Vs At Airport

Paying for your carry on bag at booking is usually cheaper. Allegiant offers lower fees when you add bags during online booking. Waiting to pay at the airport means higher fees. Airport fees can be double or more than online prices.

The airline charges extra for last-minute bag payments. This fee covers the cost of handling bags at the airport. It also helps speed up the check-in process for passengers who prepay.

### Impact Of Payment Timing

Early payment locks in the lowest fee for carry on bags. The closer to your flight, the higher the fee becomes. Paying online days before your flight saves money and time. Delaying payment may cause long lines and stress at the airport.

Some travelers may skip paying for carry on bags at booking. This choice often leads to expensive airport fees. Planning ahead ensures you avoid these extra costs. Always check Allegiant’s fee schedule before your trip.

## Tips To Avoid Extra Fees
