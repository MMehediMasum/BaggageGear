## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Backpacks Fit Under Airplane Seats?



Backpacks like the Taygeer, Kono, and ECOHUB are designed to fit under airplane seats. They usually measure around 17 inches or less. This size ensures compliance with most airline personal item policies, making travel easier and more convenient.

Yes, most backpacks listed, such as the Taygeer and HOTOR, are TSA flight approved. This means they meet airline carry-on size requirements. They allow quick security screening without removing the bag, saving time during airport checks.



### Do These Backpacks Include Usb Charging Ports?



Several backpacks, like the Taygeer and MATEIN models, feature built-in USB charging ports. This allows travelers to charge devices on the go. It’s a practical feature for business trips and long flights.

Backpacks like the Kono, MATEIN, and LIGHT FLIGHT are water-resistant. This protects belongings from rain or spills. Water resistance is essential for outdoor activities and unpredictable weather during travel.

### Are These Backpacks Suitable For Business And Casual Use?



Absolutely. Backpacks like LOVEVOOK and HOTOR are versatile for business trips and casual outings. They combine style and functionality, offering compartments for work essentials and personal items.
