### Fabric Selection

Monos uses high-grade fabrics for their luggage. They prefer polycarbonate shells for hard cases. This material is lightweight but tough against scratches and impacts. For soft cases, they select durable ballistic nylon. This fabric resists tears and water, protecting your belongings.

The fabrics also have a smooth finish. This adds a sleek look and easy cleaning. The brand values materials that keep the luggage looking new for years.

### Hardware Quality

Hardware is key in luggage performance. Monos uses strong zippers that glide smoothly. These zippers are designed to avoid snags and breakage. The handles are made of aluminum for strength and light weight.

Wheels are another focus. Monos fits their bags with silent, 360-degree spinner wheels. These wheels roll easily on different surfaces. The locking mechanisms are secure and simple to use. Each hardware component adds to the bag’s reliability and ease of handling.
