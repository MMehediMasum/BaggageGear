## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Backpack Airline Approved?



An airline-approved backpack typically fits under the seat or in overhead compartments. It should have dimensions within airline restrictions and be lightweight. Features like a USB charging port and multiple compartments enhance convenience. Look for backpacks like SHRRADOO or MATEIN, which offer these features and are designed for easy travel.



### How Does A Usb Charging Port Benefit Travelers?



A USB charging port allows travelers to charge devices on the go. It provides convenience without needing to find an outlet. Simply connect your power bank inside the backpack to the port. This feature is great for keeping your phone or tablet charged during long travels.



### Why Choose A Water-resistant Backpack For Travel?



A water-resistant backpack protects your belongings from rain and spills. It ensures that electronics and clothes stay dry. This feature is especially useful in unpredictable weather conditions. Backpacks like the TANGCORLE and LOVEVOOK models offer water resistance, making them reliable for travelers.



### Can These Backpacks Fit A 17-inch Laptop?



Yes, many of these backpacks are designed to fit 17-inch laptops. They offer padded compartments for safety and security. This feature is essential for travelers who need to carry their laptops. Models like the SHRRADOO and Travel Backpack 50L are perfect for tech-savvy travelers.



### What Makes A Backpack Expandable?



An expandable backpack offers additional storage space when needed. It allows you to carry more items without needing another bag. This is ideal for travelers who might pick up souvenirs or need extra space. The MATEIN and TANGCORLE models provide this flexibility for travel convenience.
