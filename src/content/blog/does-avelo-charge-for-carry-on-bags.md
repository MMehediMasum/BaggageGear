### Size And Weight Limits

Avelo sets clear limits on the size and weight of carry-on bags to keep boarding smooth. Your carry-on bag must fit within the dimensions of 22 x 14 x 9 inches (including handles and wheels). This size fits in the overhead bin, which is crucial for avoiding extra charges.

Weight-wise, Avelo doesn’t specify a strict limit, but your bag should be light enough for you to lift into the overhead compartment without help. If your carry-on is too large or heavy, you might be asked to check it, which can result in additional fees.

### Free Vs Paid Carry-on Bags

Here’s the part many travelers want to know: Does Avelo charge for carry-on bags? The answer depends on the type of carry-on you bring.

* **Personal Item:**Avelo allows one free personal item per passenger. This can be a backpack, purse, or laptop bag that fits under the seat in front of you.
* **Standard Carry-On:**Unlike some airlines, Avelo charges a fee if you want to bring a standard carry-on bag that goes in the overhead bin.

Many travelers overlook this and end up paying at the gate. Paying for your carry-on in advance online is usually cheaper and guarantees your bag travels with you in the cabin.
