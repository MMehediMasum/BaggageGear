## Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding carry-on size limits is key to a smooth travel experience with Frontier Airlines. Let's break down what you need to know about carry-on baggage on Frontier and how it stacks up against other airlines.

### Frontier Airlines Dimensions

Frontier Airlines allows a carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of **24 inches x 16 inches x 10 inches**(including handles and wheels). This size is designed to fit in the overhead bin comfortably. If your bag exceeds these dimensions, you’ll likely have to check it in and pay extra fees.

One tip from my own travel experience: measure your bag before heading to the airport. A quick check with a tape measure saved me from paying an unexpected $60 fee on a recent Frontier flight.

## Measuring Your Carry-on

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-your-carry-on-bag-correctly-is-vital-for-hassle-free-travel-with-frontier-airlines.-each-airline-has-specific-size-limits-for-carry-on-luggage.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring your carry-on bag correctly is vital for hassle-free travel with Frontier Airlines. Each airline has specific size limits for carry-on luggage. Knowing how to measure your bag ensures it meets these rules. This helps you avoid extra fees or forced check-ins at the airport.

Measure your bag carefully in three key dimensions. These include length, width, and height. You must include any parts that stick out, like handles and wheels. Accurate measurement tools make this task easier and more precise.

### Length, Width, And Height Explained

Length is the longest side of your bag, usually the front to back. Width is the side-to-side measurement across the bag’s widest point. Height is the distance from the bottom to the top, including the main body of the bag.

Carry-on size limits often combine these three dimensions. Frontier Airlines typically allows carry-ons around 24 inches total, but always check the current rules before packing.

### Including Handles And Wheels

Handles and wheels add to your bag's total size. Measure them as part of the length or height, depending on their position. Telescoping handles should be fully extended to get the correct measurement.

Ignoring these parts can lead to your bag being too large for the overhead bin or under the seat.

### Using Measuring Tools

Use a tape measure for the most accurate results. A soft tape is best for curved bags. Lay the bag flat on the floor for easier measuring.

If you don’t have a tape measure, a ruler or yardstick can work. Mark each dimension carefully and double-check your numbers. This simple step saves time and stress at the airport.

## Frontier's Carry-on Policies

Frontier Airlines has specific rules for carry-on bags. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees and delays. The policies focus on bag size, fees, and boarding order. Understanding the details makes your travel smoother.

### Fees And Charges

Frontier charges for most carry-on bags. Only a small personal item is free. Fees depend on when you pay:

* Booking time: Lowest fees
* Before check-in: Higher fees
* At gate: Highest fees

Pay early to save money on carry-on bags.

### Personal Item Rules

Every passenger can bring one personal item for free. The item must fit under the seat. Typical personal items include:

* Small backpack
* Purse
* Briefcase
* Laptop bag

The maximum size for a personal item is 14 x 18 x 8 inches. Larger bags count as carry-on and need a fee.

### Boarding Priorities

Frontier boards passengers by groups. Group number depends on bag type and purchase:

* Priority boarding: Paid carry-on bags
* Standard boarding: Only personal items
* Basic boarding: Last group, no bags paid

Paying for carry-on bags gives earlier boarding. Early boarding secures overhead bin space.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Are Frontier Airlines Carry-on Bag Size Limits?

Frontier Airlines allows carry-on bags up to 24 x 16 x 10 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Bags exceeding this size must be checked or paid for as checked luggage.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item On Frontier Flights?

Yes, Frontier permits one personal item per passenger. The item must fit under the seat and measure no more than 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

### Are Carry-on Bags Free On Frontier Airlines?

Carry-on bags are free only if you purchase the WORKS or PERKS bundle. Otherwise, a fee applies for carry-ons, depending on your fare and route.

Frontier strictly enforces carry-on size limits at gate check. Oversized bags may be gate-checked and incur fees, so measure your bag before travel.
