## Fenway Park Bag Policy Basics

Understanding Fenway Park's bag policy can save you time and hassle on game day. The rules are clear but strict, designed to speed up entry and keep everyone safe. Knowing what you can bring and what’s off-limits helps you enjoy the experience without surprises.

### Allowed Bag Types And Sizes

You can bring small bags that meet Fenway’s size limits. The park allows clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" and small clutch bags no larger than 4.5" x 6.5".

Backpacks and large purses are not allowed unless they fit inside a permitted clear bag. If you carry a bag that’s too big, expect to be turned away or asked to check it outside the stadium.

### Prohibited Items

Fenway Park bans items that could disrupt the game or pose safety risks. This includes weapons, alcohol from outside, coolers, and large umbrellas.

Food and drinks brought from outside are generally prohibited, but exceptions exist for medical needs. If you plan to bring something special, check the official list beforehand to avoid disappointment.

### Clear Bag Policy Details

The clear bag policy is the most important rule to remember. It means any bag you bring must be transparent and small enough to pass quickly through security checks.

This policy speeds up entry and reduces wait times, so you get to your seat faster. Have you ever stood in a long security line wishing you’d packed lighter? Fenway’s clear bag rule helps prevent that.
