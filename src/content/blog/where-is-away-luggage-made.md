## Origins Of Away Luggage

Away Luggage began with a simple idea: create suitcases that travelers truly need. The brand started as a response to common travel problems. It aimed to offer durable, stylish, and smart luggage at a fair price.

The story of Away is rooted in innovation and customer focus. Every step of the brand’s origin reflects a clear vision for better travel gear.

### Founders And Brand Vision

Steph Korey and Jen Rubio founded Away in 2015. They noticed many travelers struggled with heavy, ugly suitcases. Their goal was to design luggage that is lightweight and elegant.

They wanted to improve travel with simple, smart solutions. Their vision focused on combining function with style. Away luggage includes features like built-in chargers and durable materials.

### Initial Manufacturing Choices

Away chose to manufacture in factories in China. This decision helped keep costs low without losing quality. The brand worked closely with factories to ensure strong craftsmanship.

Materials like polycarbonate shells were selected for toughness and lightness. Away tested samples repeatedly to meet their high standards. This careful approach helped establish their reputation for reliable luggage.

Away luggage primarily uses polycarbonate shells for many of its hard-sided suitcases. This material is known for being lightweight yet highly resistant to impacts and scratches. You might find this surprising, but polycarbonate is the same material used in bulletproof glass, highlighting its toughness.

For soft-sided luggage, Away opts for high-quality nylon or polyester fabrics. These materials offer flexibility and water resistance, protecting your belongings from unexpected weather. Plus, the zippers and handles are made from sturdy metals and reinforced plastics designed to endure rough handling.

### Quality Control Processes

Every Away suitcase undergoes multiple quality checks before it reaches you. The company tests for durability by simulating real travel conditions like drops, tumbles, and rough handling. This hands-on approach ensures that your luggage can handle the stresses of airports and car trunks alike.

In addition to physical tests, Away also inspects the finer details such as smooth zipper operation and secure stitching. These might seem small, but they directly affect your travel experience. Have you ever struggled with a zipper that jams mid-trip? Away aims to eliminate that frustration.
