### Can I Use A Backpack As A Carry-on In Europe?



Yes, backpacks can be used as carry-ons in Europe. The ECOHUB and LOVEVOOK travel backpacks are airline-approved and spacious. They offer multiple compartments for organization and are water-resistant. These backpacks are perfect for travelers seeking a versatile and lightweight option.



### What Features Should I Look For In A Carry-on?
