## Spirit Airlines Personal Item Rules

Spirit Airlines has specific rules about what counts as a personal item. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees at the airport. A personal item is something small enough to fit under the seat in front of you. Spirit Airlines allows one personal item per passenger on board.

Understanding these rules is important for packing smart. It lets you bring essentials without paying for larger bags. Spirit Airlines is strict about size and weight limits for personal items.

### Size Limits For Personal Items

Spirit Airlines requires personal items to fit within 18 x 14 x 8 inches. This size includes handles and wheels. The item must fit completely under the seat in front of you. Bags larger than this may not be accepted as a personal item.

### Weight Restrictions

Spirit Airlines does not set a specific weight limit for personal items. Still, your item must be light enough to stow under the seat safely. Overweight items may be subject to extra fees or must be checked in. Carrying a lightweight bag helps avoid problems at the gate.

### Examples Of Allowed Personal Items

* Small backpacks
* Briefcases
* Purses
* Laptop bags
* Compact duffle bags that meet size limits

Not all duffle bags qualify as personal items. Only small duffle bags fitting size rules are allowed. Larger duffle bags must be checked or purchased as carry-on luggage.

### When A Duffle Bag Qualifies As A Personal Item

### Situations Requiring Carry-on Classification

If your duffle bag exceeds the personal item size limit, Spirit Airlines will classify it as a carry-on. Carry-on bags must not exceed 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This can happen if your duffle is large, rigid, or packed full.

Also, if you’ve already brought a personal item, your duffle bag will need to be your carry-on or checked bag. Spirit's strict baggage policy means you can’t bring both a large duffle and a personal item without paying extra.

Consider how you plan to use your duffle during the flight. If you want quick access to items, it’s better to keep the duffle within personal item size. Otherwise, be prepared to pay for a carry-on or check it in.
