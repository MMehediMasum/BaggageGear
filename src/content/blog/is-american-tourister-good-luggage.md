### History And Background

American Tourister has been around since 1933, building a legacy of affordable and durable luggage. It started as a family business and grew steadily before becoming part of Samsonite in 1993. This connection with a well-known global brand has helped American Tourister maintain strong standards and innovate continuously.

The brand’s long history shows its ability to adapt to changing travel needs and trends. Have you ever noticed how some brands just seem to stick around because they actually deliver? American Tourister is one of those brands.
