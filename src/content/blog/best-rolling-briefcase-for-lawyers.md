### How Do I Choose The Right Rolling Briefcase?



Choose a rolling briefcase based on your needs. Consider factors like laptop size compatibility, organization features, and material quality. Durability in rolling briefcases is enhanced by quality materials like ballistic nylon and reinforced wheels. Telescopic handles should be sturdy and easy to operate. Locks provide security, while well-padded compartments protect electronics. Opt for models with replaceable wheels and robust construction to ensure long-lasting use.



### Can Rolling Briefcases Fit Large Laptops?



Yes, many rolling briefcases can fit large laptops up to 17. 3 inches. Models like the Alpine Swiss and Solo New York are designed with spacious compartments. Ensure you verify the specific laptop compatibility before purchasing. Adequate padding in these compartments also ensures laptops are protected during travel.
