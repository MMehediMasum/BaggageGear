## Southwest Baggage Basics

Understanding Southwest baggage basics can save you from unexpected fees and stress at the airport. Knowing what qualifies as a standard bag helps you pack smarter and travel lighter. Let’s break down the essentials so you can breeze through your next Southwest flight.

### Types Of Baggage Allowed

Southwest allows two main types of baggage: carry-on bags and checked bags. Your carry-on must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat in front of you. Checked bags go in the cargo hold and require a fee unless you meet certain fare or loyalty criteria.

It’s easy to confuse personal items with carry-ons. A personal item can be a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack that fits under the seat. Many travelers don’t realize they can bring both a personal item and a carry-on without extra cost.

### Standard Bag Dimensions

A standard checked bag on Southwest can be up to 62 linear inches. This means adding the length, width, and height of your bag together. If your bag measures 27 inches long, 20 inches wide, and 15 inches high, you’re right on the limit.

For carry-ons, the maximum size is 10 x 16 x 24 inches. This ensures it fits comfortably in the overhead bin. I once tried bringing a bag slightly larger than this, and gate agents made me check it last minute, which cost me extra time and money.

### Weight Limits

Southwest sets a weight limit of 50 pounds for each checked bag. If your bag weighs more than that, you’ll face additional fees. Weigh your suitcase at home to avoid surprises at the airport.

Carry-on bags usually don’t have a strict weight limit, but you’ll need to lift it into the overhead bin yourself. If your bag is too heavy to manage, it might slow down boarding and draw unwanted attention.

## Carry-on Bag Rules

Southwest Airlines allows travelers to bring a carry-on bag and a personal item on board. Following the carry-on bag rules helps avoid extra charges and delays at the gate. Knowing these rules ensures your bags meet Southwest’s standards and fit in overhead bins or under the seat.

### Size Restrictions

Southwest sets clear size limits for carry-on bags. Your bag must not exceed 10 x 16 x 24 inches. This includes wheels and handles. Bags larger than these dimensions may need to be checked.

It’s best to measure your bag before packing. This prevents surprises at the airport. The size limit helps keep aisles and seats clear for all passengers.

### Personal Item Guidelines

A personal item can be a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. It must fit under the seat in front of you. The size limit is 18.5 x 8.5 x 13.5 inches. This rule helps ensure everyone has space for their items.

Personal items do not count as carry-on bags. You can bring both without extra cost. Keep your personal item small and easy to store.

### Carry-on Fees

Southwest does not charge fees for carry-on bags. You can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item free of charge. This policy saves money compared to many other airlines.

Be aware that oversized or overweight bags may still incur fees. Always check bag size before arriving at the airport to avoid surprises.

## Checked Baggage Policies

### Number Of Bags Allowed

Southwest Airlines stands out by allowing you to check in **two bags for free**on every flight. This is a rare benefit compared to many other airlines that charge for checked luggage.

However, if you need to bring more than two bags, additional fees will apply. Are you packing for a long trip or just a weekend getaway? This affects how many bags you might need and what fees could be waiting for you.

### Checked Bag Size And Weight

Each checked bag must not exceed **62 inches in total dimensions**(length + width + height). If your suitcase is larger, expect extra charges or the need to repack.

The weight limit per bag is **50 pounds**. Bags over this limit can lead to overweight fees, so weigh your luggage before heading to the airport.

Imagine rushing to the gate only to be told your bag is overweight. A simple scale at home or the airport kiosk can prevent this hassle.

### Fees And Charges

Southwest’s free two-checked-bags policy keeps costs down for many travelers. But if you have extra or overweight bags, fees apply:

* **Third bag fee:**$75 per bag
* **Overweight fee:**$75 for bags 51-100 pounds
* **Oversize fee:**$75 for bags over 62 inches but less than 80 inches

Knowing these fees upfront helps you decide if you should ship items or rearrange your packing. Have you ever been caught off guard by unexpected baggage fees? Planning ahead can save you both money and stress.

### Oversized And Overweight Bags

Southwest allows a specific size and weight limit for standard bags, but what happens if your bag is too big or heavy? Bags over 50 pounds or larger than 62 inches (length + width + height) are considered overweight or oversized. You will have to pay additional fees, which can add up quickly.

Think about your last trip: did you pack a heavy winter coat or souvenirs that pushed your bag’s weight? Checking your bag’s dimensions and weight before heading to the airport can avoid last-minute charges.

### Sports Equipment

Do you travel with sports gear like golf clubs or skis? Southwest treats these as special baggage with specific rules and fees. You must notify the airline and pack the equipment properly, often in a hard case or bag designed for the gear.

For example, golf bags should not exceed 50 pounds and must be packed with all clubs inside. Understanding these rules upfront lets you plan your trip better and prevents your sports gear from becoming a hassle at check-in.

### Musical Instruments

Musicians often worry about flying with their instruments. Southwest allows small instruments as carry-ons if they fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. Larger instruments usually require checked baggage or a special seat purchase.

Have you ever experienced a cracked guitar or a dented cello case? Protecting your instrument means checking with Southwest about their policies and investing in a sturdy case. This preparation can make your journey smoother and keep your instrument safe.

Label your bag with your name and contact info. Keep important items and documents in your carry-on bag. Pack a change of clothes and basic toiletries in your carry-on. Take a photo of your packed bag for reference. Report lost bags immediately at the Southwest desk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

A Standard Bag on Southwest is the regular checked luggage allowed per passenger. It typically weighs up to 50 pounds and measures 62 inches in combined dimensions. Southwest Airlines allows two free Standard Bags per ticket, making it a traveler-friendly baggage policy.

### How Many Standard Bags Can I Check For Free?

Southwest Airlines permits two free Standard Bags per passenger. Each bag must meet size and weight limits. Additional bags or overweight items incur extra fees. This generous policy is a key feature that attracts many travelers to Southwest.

### What Are The Size Limits For A Standard Bag?

A Standard Bag on Southwest must not exceed 62 linear inches. This means the total of length, width, and height should be 62 inches or less. Staying within these limits avoids extra charges and ensures smooth check-in.
