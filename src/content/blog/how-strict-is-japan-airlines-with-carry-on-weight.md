## Japan Airlines Carry-on Allowance

Japan Airlines has clear rules for carry-on baggage. Knowing these rules helps avoid surprises at the airport. The airline focuses on safety and comfort. Passengers must follow weight, size, and quantity limits. This ensures smooth boarding and a pleasant flight for everyone.

### Weight Limits For Carry-on Bags

Japan Airlines allows a specific weight for carry-on bags. The limit is usually 10 kilograms (22 pounds). This weight includes all items inside the bag. If the bag is heavier, it may need to be checked in. Staff at the gate or check-in counter will check the weight.

### Size Restrictions And Dimensions

The size of carry-on bags is also controlled. Japan Airlines permits bags up to 55 cm x 40 cm x 25 cm. These dimensions include handles and wheels. Bags larger than this size must go to the checked luggage. Proper bag size helps fit bags in overhead compartments.

### Number Of Allowed Carry-on Items

* One standard carry-on bag within the size and weight limit
* One personal item, such as a laptop bag or purse

Each passenger can bring two items. The personal item must fit under the seat. This rule allows passengers to carry essentials easily. Staff may ask to check the number of items during boarding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Japan Airlines allows carry-on bags up to 55 x 40 x 25 cm. This size includes handles and wheels. Bags exceeding these dimensions may not be accepted as carry-on and could require check-in. Always measure your bag to avoid last-minute issues.
