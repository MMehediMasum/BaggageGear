Struggling to pick the perfect bag for your next trip? Knowing how to choose the right carry on luggage can be overwhelming. With so many sizes, features, and materials available, it's hard to know where to start.

For travelers in the USA, carry-on luggage is essential for saving time and money, but navigating airline restrictions and choosing the right bag can be tricky. A well-chosen carry-on can make travel smoother, while the wrong one leads to headaches at the gate.

This guide simplifies the process, covering everything from understanding size limits to selecting the best features for your needs. We'll explore hard-shell vs. soft-sided options, essential features, material choices, and packing tips, ensuring you select a carry-on that’s perfect for your travel style and budget.

## What Size Carry-On Do I Need?

Choosing the right size carry-on starts with understanding airline restrictions. Most major US airlines, including United, American, Delta, and Southwest, have similar size limits, but it's always best to check directly with your airline before traveling. These limits typically include both the dimensions of the bag and a weight restriction.

A common maximum size for carry-on luggage is 22 inches x 14 inches x 9 inches (including handles and wheels). However, always confirm the specific requirements of the airline you are flying with, as international airlines may have different, sometimes stricter, rules.

To measure your luggage accurately:

* **Use a tape measure:** Measure the height, width, and depth of the bag, including any protruding parts like wheels and handles.
* **Check the manufacturer's specifications:** Look for the listed dimensions on the luggage tag or product description, but double-check them yourself.
* **Consider soft-sided bags:** These can sometimes be squeezed into overhead bins that are slightly smaller.

## Hard-Shell vs. Soft-Sided Carry-On: Which Is Best?

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-choice-between-hard-shell-and-soft-sided-carry-on-luggage-depends-on-your-travel-style-and-priorities.-each-type-offers-distinct-advantages-and-disadvantages.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The choice between hard-shell and soft-sided carry-on luggage depends on your travel style and priorities. Each type offers distinct advantages and disadvantages.

### Hard-Shell Pros and Cons

Hard-shell luggage is typically made from materials like polycarbonate or ABS plastic.

* **Pros:**
  + **Durability:** Excellent protection for fragile items.
  + **Water Resistance:** Generally more water-resistant than soft-sided bags.
  + **Scratch Resistance:** Newer models often feature textured surfaces to hide scratches, but smooth surfaces can show wear and tear.
* **Cons:**
  + **Weight:** Can be heavier than soft-sided options.
  + **Flexibility:** Less forgiving if you overpack, as there's little to no give.
  + **Organization:** Exterior pockets are rare, limiting quick-access storage.

### Soft-Sided Pros and Cons

Soft-sided luggage is usually made from nylon or polyester fabrics.

* **Pros:**
  + **Weight:** Typically lighter than hard-shell luggage.
  + **Flexibility:** Can be easier to squeeze into tight spaces.
  + **External Pockets:** Often includes exterior pockets for easy access to travel documents or other items.
  + **Expandability:** Many models offer expandable compartments for extra packing space.
* **Cons:**
  + **Durability:** Less protective of fragile items.
  + **Water Resistance:** Not as water-resistant as hard-shell luggage, though some have water-resistant coatings.
  + **Wear and Tear:** Can show wear and tear more easily.

## What Features Should I Look for in Carry-On Luggage?

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=beyond-size-and-construction,-certain-features-can-significantly-enhance-your-travel-experience.-here-are-some-essential-features-to-consider-when-choosing-carry-on-luggage:&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Beyond size and construction, certain features can significantly enhance your travel experience. Here are some essential features to consider when choosing carry-on luggage:

### Spinner vs. Two-Wheel Luggage

Spinner wheels (four wheels that rotate 360 degrees) offer effortless maneuverability, allowing you to push or pull your luggage upright. Two-wheel luggage (with inline skate wheels) is typically more durable on uneven surfaces and can be a better choice if you anticipate encountering rough terrain.

### Telescoping Handle Quality

A sturdy, adjustable telescoping handle is crucial for comfortable handling. Look for handles made from high-quality aluminum with minimal wobbling. Test the handle at different heights to ensure it locks securely and feels comfortable in your hand.

### Interior Compartments and Pockets

Good interior organization can make packing and unpacking much easier. Look for features like:

* **Dividers:** To separate clean and dirty clothes.
* **Compression straps:** To secure your belongings and maximize space.
* **Pockets:** Mesh pockets for small items, and water-resistant pockets for toiletries.

Other desirable features include:

* **TSA-approved locks:** For added security when checking your bag (if needed).
* **Integrated USB charging ports:** For charging devices on the go (requires a power bank).
* **Comfortable carry handles:** For easy lifting into overhead bins.

## What Materials Make the Best Carry-On Bags?

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-material-of-your-carry-on-luggage-affects-its-durability,-weight,-and-water-resistance.-here's-a-breakdown-of-common-luggage-materials:&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The material of your carry-on luggage affects its durability, weight, and water resistance. Here's a breakdown of common luggage materials:

* **Polycarbonate:** A lightweight and durable plastic that offers good impact resistance. It's a popular choice for hard-shell luggage.
* **ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene):** A less expensive plastic than polycarbonate. It's more rigid but also more prone to cracking under impact.
* **Nylon:** A strong and abrasion-resistant fabric commonly used in soft-sided luggage. Ballistic nylon is particularly durable.
* **Polyester:** A more affordable alternative to nylon. It's lighter but less durable and water-resistant.

Consider these factors when choosing a material:

* **Weight:** Lighter materials like polycarbonate and nylon can help you stay within airline weight restrictions.
* **Durability:** If you travel frequently or carry fragile items, invest in a more durable material like polycarbonate or ballistic nylon.
* **Water Resistance:** If you anticipate traveling in wet conditions, choose a water-resistant material or look for luggage with a water-resistant coating.

## How Much Should I Spend on a Carry-On Suitcase?

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-price-of-carry-on-luggage-can-vary-widely,-depending-on-the-brand,-materials,-features,-and-construction-quality.-setting-a-budget-beforehand-will-help-narrow-your-options.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The price of carry-on luggage can vary widely, depending on the brand, materials, features, and construction quality. Setting a budget beforehand will help narrow your options.

Here's a general price range:

* **Budget-Friendly (Under $100):** You can find basic carry-on suitcases at this price point, but expect lower-quality materials and fewer features. Durability may be a concern with frequent use.
* **Mid-Range ($100 - $250):** This range offers a good balance of quality, features, and durability. You'll find bags made from more robust materials with better construction and useful features like spinner wheels and TSA-approved locks.
* **High-End (Over $250):** Premium carry-on luggage boasts top-of-the-line materials, superior construction, and advanced features like integrated technology and enhanced organization. These bags are designed for frequent travelers who demand the best.

When determining your budget, consider how often you travel and the types of trips you take. If you only travel occasionally, a mid-range bag may suffice. However, if you're a frequent flyer, investing in a higher-quality, more durable carry-on will likely save you money in the long run.

## Carry-On Luggage: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the standard size for carry-on luggage on most US airlines?

Most major US airlines allow carry-on bags with maximum dimensions of 22 inches x 14 inches x 9 inches, including handles and wheels. However, it's crucial to check with your specific airline before your trip, as sizes and weight restrictions can vary, especially for international flights.

### What are the advantages of hard-shell carry-on luggage compared to soft-sided bags?

Hard-shell luggage offers superior protection for fragile items and is generally more water-resistant than soft-sided bags. While they can be heavier and offer less flexibility in packing, hard-shell suitcases are a solid choice for those prioritizing durability and protection.

### How can I maximize packing space in my carry-on suitcase?

To maximize space, roll your clothes instead of folding them, and use packing cubes to compress and organize your belongings. Wear your bulkiest items on the plane to save room in your bag, and be mindful of airline restrictions on liquids and toiletries.

### Are TSA-approved locks necessary for carry-on luggage?

TSA-approved locks aren't strictly necessary for carry-on luggage since you'll have the bag with you. However, they can provide an extra layer of security, especially if you anticipate gate-checking your bag at any point during your journey. These locks allow TSA agents to open and inspect your bag without damaging the lock.

### What is the best material for a durable carry-on bag?

For a durable carry-on bag, consider materials like polycarbonate for hard-shell luggage or ballistic nylon for soft-sided options. Polycarbonate is lightweight and impact-resistant, while ballistic nylon is strong and abrasion-resistant. Both materials can withstand the rigors of travel, ensuring your belongings stay protected.
