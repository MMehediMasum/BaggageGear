## How A Water Bag With Pennies Works

A bag of water filled with pennies is a simple tool used to deter flies and other small insects. The idea behind it seems unusual, but many people believe it helps keep pests away from certain areas. Understanding how this method works can clarify why some use it in homes and outdoor spaces.

### The Science Behind The Method

The water and pennies create a reflective surface that confuses insects. Light hits the water and reflects in many directions. This reflection makes it hard for flies to navigate or land nearby. The movement of water inside the bag adds to the effect, making the reflections change constantly.

Insects rely on clear signals to find food and places to rest. The changing light patterns disrupt these signals. Flies may see the reflections as predators or obstacles. This causes them to avoid the area around the bag.

### Role Of Pennies In Water Bags

Pennies inside the water add more reflections due to their shiny, copper surface. The metal increases the number of light points inside the bag. This creates a complex pattern that is harder for flies to ignore.

* The copper color of pennies enhances light scattering.
* Multiple pennies create many small reflections.
* They also weigh down the bag, keeping it stable.

The pennies make the water bag more effective than water alone. They help maintain the visual confusion needed to repel flies.

## How To Make And Use A Water Bag With Pennies

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=making-a-bag-of-water-with-pennies-is-a-simple,-hands-on-way-to-try-an-old-trick-believed-to-deter-flies-and-other-insects.-this-diy-project-requires-minimal-materials-and-can-be-completed-in-just-a-few-minutes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Making a bag of water with pennies is a simple, hands-on way to try an old trick believed to deter flies and other insects. This DIY project requires minimal materials and can be completed in just a few minutes. Once ready, you’ll be surprised how easy it is to use and place for the best results.

### Materials Needed

* Clear plastic bag (small to medium size works well)
* Clean water (tap water is fine)
* Several shiny pennies (the more, the better, usually 5-10)
* Rubber band or string to seal the bag
* Optional: tape to secure the bag in place

### Step-by-step Instructions

* Fill the plastic bag about three-quarters full with water.
* Add the pennies into the water, making sure they are clean and shiny for best effect.
* Squeeze out excess air and tightly seal the bag using a rubber band or string.
* Gently shake the bag to mix the pennies inside and ensure they are spread out.
* Your water bag with pennies is now ready to use.

### Placement Tips For Best Results

Hang or place the bag in areas where flies and insects are most active, such as near doorways, windows, or outdoor eating spots.

Make sure the bag is at eye level or slightly above; this positioning helps reflect light and create a visual deterrent.

If you are using multiple bags, space them out evenly to cover a wider area without cluttering the space.

Have you noticed specific spots where flies gather more often? Try placing the bag there first and observe if the number of insects decreases.

## Environmental Impact And Safety

Using a bag of water with pennies is a popular hack believed to deter flies and other insects. However, it's important to consider how this simple trick affects the environment and your safety. Understanding these factors can help you use this method responsibly and avoid unintended harm.

### Eco-friendly Considerations

The idea of hanging a bag filled with water and pennies might seem harmless, but it can have subtle environmental effects. The plastic bag, if not reused or recycled properly, contributes to plastic waste which harms wildlife and pollutes ecosystems.

Also, pennies are made mostly of zinc and copper. If the bag breaks or leaks, these metals can leach into soil or water sources, potentially affecting plants and small animals. You might want to think about how often you replace the bag and where you place it to minimize impact.

Have you ever wondered if there’s a greener alternative that works just as well? Using reusable containers or natural repellents like herbs can reduce waste and protect your local environment better.

### Safety Precautions

Handling water bags with pennies requires some care. The bag can become heavy, making it easy to drop and break, which might cause injury or create a slipping hazard. Always make sure the bag is securely tied and hung in a safe spot out of reach of children and pets.

Another safety point is the quality of the pennies. Old or corroded coins can release harmful substances, so it's better to use clean, newer pennies. If you notice any discoloration in the water, change the bag promptly to avoid exposure to any toxins.

Think about your surroundings: where you place the bag should not interfere with daily activities or create risks for anyone around. Have you checked if it’s stable and won't fall easily? Small precautions can prevent accidents.
