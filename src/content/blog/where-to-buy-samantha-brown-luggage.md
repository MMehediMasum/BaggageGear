### Model Variations

Samantha Brown offers a range of luggage models designed for various travel needs. Some are carry-on sized, perfect for weekend trips, while others offer larger checked baggage options.

### Material And Durability

The material of your luggage directly impacts its durability and weight. Hard-shell cases usually offer better protection but can be heavier.

Soft-shell bags tend to be lighter and more flexible, fitting into tight spaces easier. However, they might wear out faster.

Check if the luggage uses materials like polycarbonate or ballistic nylon. These are known for resisting scratches and impacts, which can save you money in the long run.

### Warranty And Return Policies

Don't overlook warranty and return policies when comparing prices. A cheaper option might cost more if it lacks a solid warranty.

Samantha Brown luggage often comes with warranties covering manufacturing defects for a certain period. Confirm how long this lasts and what it covers.
