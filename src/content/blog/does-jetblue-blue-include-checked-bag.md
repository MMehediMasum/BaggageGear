## Jetblue Blue Fare Basics

Understanding the JetBlue Blue Fare Basics helps travelers know what to expect. It clarifies what is included and what costs extra. This section breaks down the key details of the Blue Fare.

### Fare Types Explained

JetBlue offers several fare types to fit different needs. Blue Fare is the most basic and budget-friendly option. Other fares include Blue Plus, Blue Extra, and Mint, each with more perks.

Blue Fare is best for travelers who want a low price and can travel light. It is ideal for short trips or those who do not need many extras.

### What Blue Fare Includes

The Blue Fare includes one personal item that fits under the seat. It does not include a free checked bag. Passengers must pay extra for checked luggage.

Other inclusions in Blue Fare:

* Free standard seat selection at check-in
* Complimentary snacks and non-alcoholic drinks
* Free Wi-Fi during the flight
* Earn TrueBlue points on the fare

Knowing what comes with the Blue Fare helps travelers plan their budget. Paying for checked bags separately keeps the base price low.

### Size And Weight Limits

JetBlue allows one carry-on bag per passenger, but size matters. Your carry-on must not exceed **22 x 14 x 9 inches**, including handles and wheels. This fits easily in the overhead bins without any issues.

Weight isn’t explicitly limited, but keep in mind that a heavy bag can slow you down. Think about how easy it will be to lift your bag into the overhead bin yourself. Have you ever struggled with a heavy carry-on only to pay extra for checked luggage later?

### Personal Item Rules

Besides your carry-on, JetBlue permits one personal item per passenger. This could be a small backpack, purse, or laptop bag. Your personal item must fit under the seat in front of you, roughly **17 x 13 x 8 inches**.
