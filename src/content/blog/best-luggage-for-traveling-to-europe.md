## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Luggage Size For Europe Travel?



Carry-on luggage between 20-24 inches is ideal for Europe travel. It fits airline requirements and is easy to carry. Items like tomtoc 40L backpack and Samsonite 20-inch hardside suitcases are perfect choices for space and convenience.



### How Do Vacuum Bags Help Save Luggage Space?



Vacbird vacuum bags compress clothes, reducing volume up to 70%. They come with a rechargeable air pump, ideal for carry-on luggage. This saves space, keeps clothes organized, and makes packing for Europe more efficient and hassle-free.



### Are Tsa Locks Necessary For European Travel Luggage?



Yes, TSA locks are essential for European travel. They allow security checks without damaging luggage. Many recommended bags, like Coolife and Samsonite sets, include TSA-approved locks for safe and secure travel through airport checkpoints.



### Which Travel Backpack Fits A 17-inch Laptop?



Both tomtoc 40L and LOVEVOOK 40L backpacks fit laptops up to 17 inches. They offer water resistance, durability, and multiple compartments, making them suitable for business and leisure trips across Europe.



### Do European Plug Adapters Support Usb Charging?



Yes, adapters like HANYCONY and the European Travel Plug Adapter include USB and USB-C ports. They support charging multiple devices simultaneously, ensuring compatibility with European sockets and convenient device charging on the go.



### What Luggage Materials Are Best For Europe Trips?



Hardshell suitcases like Samsonite Winfield 2 and Amazon Basics offer durability and scratch resistance. Lightweight materials with spinner wheels improve mobility, making travel through European airports and streets easier and more convenient.
