Prices depend on the size and duration of storage. Small bags cost less, while large suitcases cost more. The charges are usually calculated per hour or per day.

* Small bag: $7 per hour, $25 per day
* Medium bag: $10 per hour, $35 per day
* Large bag: $15 per hour, $50 per day

### Booking In Advance

Advance booking is recommended during busy travel seasons. It guarantees space for your luggage and speeds up the drop-off process. You can book online through the Newark Airport website or call the storage service directly.

Storing luggage at Newark Airport requires attention to security and restrictions. The airport ensures that all stored items meet safety standards. Passengers must follow rules to avoid any issues. Understanding these rules helps keep belongings safe and secure.

### What Items Are Allowed

Only personal luggage and bags can be stored. Prohibited items include:

* Weapons or firearms
* Flammable or explosive materials
* Perishable food items
* Illegal substances
* Large sports equipment without prior approval

Check with the storage facility for specific item restrictions before leaving your bags.

### Storage Safety Measures

Luggage storage areas use surveillance cameras for constant monitoring. Access is limited to authorized staff only. Bags are tagged and logged to prevent mix-ups. Facilities are cleaned regularly to avoid damage or pests. Fire safety equipment is installed throughout the storage site.

### Liability And Insurance

Newark Airport storage providers have liability policies for lost or damaged luggage. Customers should read terms carefully before storing bags. It’s advisable to carry insurance for valuable or fragile items. Report any damage or loss immediately to the service desk. Keeping a copy of your claim receipt is important for follow-up.
