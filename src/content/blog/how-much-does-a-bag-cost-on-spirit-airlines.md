## Spirit Airlines Baggage Fees

Spirit Airlines charges fees for bags to keep ticket prices low. Passengers pay separately for carry-on bags and checked luggage. This system helps travelers choose what they need and avoid extra costs.

Knowing Spirit Airlines baggage fees helps plan your trip better. Fees vary by bag size, weight, and when you pay. Early payment online is cheaper than at the airport.

### Carry-on Bag Fees

Spirit allows one personal item for free. This item must fit under the seat. A carry-on bag is larger and costs extra.

* Carry-on bag fees start around $35 if paid online.
* Fees rise to about $65 at the airport.
* Bag size must not exceed 22 x 18 x 10 inches.

### Checked Bag Fees

Checked bags are stored in the plane's cargo hold. These bags cost more than carry-ons.

| Bag Number | Fee Paid Online | Fee Paid at Airport | Max Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First checked bag | $30 to $40 | $45 to $60 | 40 lbs |
| Second checked bag | $40 to $50 | $55 to $70 | 40 lbs |

### Oversized And Overweight Bag Fees

Bags over size or weight limits cost extra. Spirit charges fees for these bags to cover extra handling.

* Overweight bags between 41-70 lbs cost about $75.
* Bags over 70 lbs are not accepted.
* Oversized bags over 62 inches cost about $100.

Carry-on bags on Spirit Airlines come with specific costs and rules. Knowing these helps travelers avoid surprises at the airport. Spirit charges fees based on when and how you book your carry-on bag. Size and weight limits also play a role in whether you pay extra or not.

### Size And Weight Limits

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item free of charge. This item must fit under the seat in front of you. The maximum size is 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

A carry-on bag that goes in the overhead bin must be larger. Its size limit is 22 x 18 x 10 inches. The weight of the bag should not exceed 40 pounds. Bags over these limits will incur extra fees or may not be allowed.

### Fee Breakdown By Booking Stage

| Booking Stage | Carry-On Bag Fee |
| --- | --- |
| During Initial Booking | $35 - $45 |
| Before Online Check-In | $45 - $55 |
| At Airport Check-In | $60 - $65 |

Fees rise as the flight date nears. Booking early saves money on carry-on bags.

### Tips To Avoid Carry-on Fees

* Bring a personal item small enough to fit under your seat.
* Measure your bag before traveling to meet size limits.
* Pay for carry-on bags during booking, not at the airport.
* Pack light to avoid overweight bag fees.

  


## Checked Bag Prices

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=checked-bag-prices-on-spirit-airlines-vary-depending-on-when-and-where-you-pay.-booking-your-checked-bag-online-before-your-flight-is-usually-cheaper-than-paying-at-the-airport.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Checked bag prices on Spirit Airlines vary depending on when and where you pay. Booking your checked bag online before your flight is usually cheaper than paying at the airport. Prices also depend on your route and travel dates. Knowing these costs helps you plan your trip better and avoid surprises.

### First Checked Bag Rates

Spirit Airlines charges for the first checked bag on most flights. The fee ranges from $30 to $45 if paid during booking. Paying at the airport raises the cost to about $65. It’s best to add your first bag early to save money. Some routes or promotions may offer lower fees.

### Additional Checked Bags

Costs increase with each extra checked bag. The second bag usually costs between $40 and $55 online. At the airport, this fee can rise to $75 or more. Third and fourth bags have higher fees, often $100 or above. Spirit allows up to four checked bags per passenger, but fees add up fast.

### Overweight And Oversized Charges

Bags over 50 pounds but under 100 pounds face extra fees. These overweight charges range from $65 to $150. Oversized bags longer than 62 inches also cost extra, usually $100 or more. Spirit does not accept bags over 100 pounds. Pay attention to size and weight limits to avoid fees.

### Other Special Items

Other special items include medical equipment, strollers, and fragile goods. These items may have different fees or rules. Spirit Airlines often requires advance notice for some special baggage. Always confirm policies to ensure smooth travel with these items.
