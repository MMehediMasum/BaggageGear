## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Luggage Size For International Travel?



The ideal luggage size for international travel is 20 to 24 inches for carry-ons. Larger checked bags range from 28 to 30 inches. This ensures compliance with most airline policies while providing enough space for essentials and souvenirs.



### Which Luggage Materials Are Most Durable For International Trips?



Hardshell materials like ABS and polycarbonate offer strong durability. Softside luggage with ballistic nylon is flexible and resistant to tears. These materials protect belongings from rough handling and weather during international travel.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Travel Luggage?



Spinner wheels improve maneuverability by rotating 360 degrees. This makes navigating airports and crowded spaces easier. Double spinner wheels add stability and reduce strain on wrists during long trips.



### Are Tsa Locks Necessary For International Luggage?



Yes, TSA locks are essential for international travel. They allow security officers to inspect luggage without damage. Most airports require TSA-approved locks for smooth security checks and safer travel.



### What Luggage Features Help Maximize Packing Space?



Expandable compartments and multiple organizational pockets optimize packing. Lightweight construction allows more items without exceeding weight limits. Sets with various sizes offer flexibility for different trip lengths.



### Should I Choose A Luggage Set Or Single Suitcase?



Luggage sets provide versatility with multiple sizes for various trip types. Single suitcases are ideal for short trips or carry-on only travelers. Sets often include matching bags, enhancing travel convenience and style.
