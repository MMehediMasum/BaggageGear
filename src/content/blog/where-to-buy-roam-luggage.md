Counterfeit luggage can look nearly identical to the real thing but often lacks quality and durability. Always buy from official Roam stores or authorized retailers listed on their website. If a deal looks too good to be true, it probably is—be cautious of heavily discounted offers from unknown sellers.

Check product details carefully: authentic Roam luggage features precise stitching, quality zippers, and unique serial numbers. Have you ever bought something only to realize it wasn’t genuine? That experience teaches the value of verifying authenticity upfront.

### Checking Return Policies

Before you click “buy,” read the return policy thoroughly. Knowing the timeframe and conditions for returns can save you frustration if your luggage doesn’t meet expectations. Some sellers offer free returns, while others may charge restocking fees or shipping costs.

Ask yourself: can you return the item if it arrives damaged or doesn’t fit your travel needs? Confirming this upfront gives you peace of mind and flexibility. Keep return instructions handy and document your purchase date to avoid surprises.

### Warranty Considerations

Roam luggage often comes with warranties covering defects and repairs. Check the warranty length and what it specifically covers before buying. A solid warranty means the company stands behind its product, reducing your risk.

Make sure you understand how to claim warranty service—some require registering the product online or keeping your receipt. Have you ever ignored warranty details only to regret it later? This small step can protect your investment and extend your luggage’s lifespan.
