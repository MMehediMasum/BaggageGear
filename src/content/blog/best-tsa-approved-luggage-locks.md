## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Tsa Approved Luggage Locks?



TSA approved luggage locks allow TSA agents to inspect your luggage without breaking the lock. They use a universal master key. This ensures the security of your belongings while complying with TSA regulations. These locks are ideal for travel and provide peace of mind during airport security checks.



### How Do Combination Luggage Locks Work?



Combination luggage locks use a sequence of numbers to secure your belongings. You set a code, usually 3-4 digits. To unlock, input the code using the lock's dials. They eliminate the need for keys and are convenient for frequent travelers.



### Why Choose A Lock With An Inspection Indicator?



Inspection indicators notify you if TSA has opened your luggage. The indicator changes color when accessed. This feature helps identify if your bags were inspected during transit. It provides added security and allows you to check for missing or damaged items promptly.



### Are 4-digit Locks More Secure?



Yes, 4-digit locks offer greater security compared to 3-digit ones. They provide 10,000 possible combinations. This makes them harder to crack, enhancing the protection of your luggage. They are ideal for safeguarding valuable items during travel.



### Can Tsa Locks Be Used For Other Purposes?



Absolutely, TSA locks are versatile. They can secure gym lockers, toolboxes, and backpacks. Their robust design ensures safety beyond travel needs. This makes them a practical investment for various security applications in daily life.
