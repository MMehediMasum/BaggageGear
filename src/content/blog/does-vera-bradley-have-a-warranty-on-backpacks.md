## What Warranty Does Not Cover

Normal wear and tear is not covered by Vera Bradley’s warranty. This includes fading, fraying, or minor scuffs that happen over time with regular use. If your backpack’s fabric starts to look tired after months or years, this is expected and not a defect.

Accidents happen, but Vera Bradley’s warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents. This means if your backpack gets ripped, stained, or broken due to drops, spills, or sharp objects, you’re responsible for the repair or replacement.

Imagine dropping your backpack in mud or accidentally tearing it on a fence. These issues are not defects in materials or workmanship but results of unforeseen events. You’ll need to handle these repairs outside the warranty terms.

### Misuse And Neglect

The warranty excludes damage caused by misuse or neglect. Using the backpack for purposes other than carrying personal items or failing to care for it properly can void your warranty.

* Overloading the backpack with heavy items beyond its capacity

## Tips To Maintain Your Backpack

Maintaining your Vera Bradley backpack extends its life and keeps it looking fresh. Regular care prevents damage and helps the backpack serve you longer. Simple habits and good cleaning routines make a big difference.

### Cleaning Guidelines

Use a soft brush or cloth to remove dirt from the surface. Spot clean stains with mild soap and warm water. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage fabric colors and patterns. Let the backpack air dry completely before storing it. Hand wash only if the label allows, and avoid machine drying.

### Storage Advice

Store your backpack in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep it upright or hang it to maintain its shape. Avoid squashing it under heavy items to prevent creases and damage. Use a dust bag or pillowcase to protect it from dust and dirt during long storage.

### Preventive Care

* Do not overload your backpack to avoid stress on seams and zippers.
* Check zippers regularly and keep them clean and lubricated.
* Repair small tears or loose threads quickly to stop further damage.
* Avoid placing backpacks on rough surfaces to prevent abrasions.
