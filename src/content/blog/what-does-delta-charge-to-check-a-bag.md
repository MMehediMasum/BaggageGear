## Delta's Baggage Fees In 2026

Delta's baggage fees in 2026 have specific charges depending on how many bags you check. Knowing these fees can help you avoid surprises at the airport and plan your packing better. Let's break down what you can expect to pay for each checked bag on Delta flights.

### First Checked Bag Charges

Delta usually charges a fee for the first checked bag on most domestic flights. In 2026, this fee typically ranges from $30 to $35 per bag, depending on your route and ticket class.

If you fly often, consider joining Delta’s loyalty program or using a Delta credit card. These options can sometimes waive the first bag fee, saving you money.

### Second Checked Bag Rates

The second checked bag usually costs more than the first. Expect to pay around $40 to $45 for your second bag on domestic flights in 2026.

This fee is higher because airlines want to encourage passengers to pack lighter. If you’re traveling with family or on a long trip, this can add up quickly.

### Fees For Additional Bags

Checking more than two bags? Fees increase significantly after the second bag. Charges can range from $100 to $200 per additional bag, depending on weight and destination.

Heavy or oversized bags may incur extra fees too. Before packing that bulky suitcase, ask yourself if everything inside is essential for your trip.

## Special Baggage Policies

### Oversized And Overweight Fees

If your bag exceeds Delta’s standard size or weight limits, you’ll likely face extra charges. Bags over 50 pounds but less than 70 pounds typically incur a fee, and anything above 70 pounds may not be accepted as checked baggage at all.

Oversized luggage—anything over 62 linear inches (length + width + height)—also comes with additional fees. Think about your golf bags or large suitcases; these can add up quickly if you don’t measure before packing.

Have you ever packed something heavy and got surprised by a hefty fee at the counter? Measuring and weighing your bags beforehand can prevent that shock and save you money.

### Sports And Musical Equipment

Delta treats sports gear and musical instruments differently from regular luggage. Items like skis, surfboards, and bicycles often have their own fee schedules based on size and weight.

Musical instruments can sometimes be carried on if they fit in the overhead bin, but larger cases usually count as checked baggage and may require an additional fee. If your instrument is valuable, consider purchasing extra insurance or a special case.

Have you ever wondered if your guitar could fly with you in the cabin? Checking Delta’s specific rules for your equipment can avoid last-minute hassles and keep your gear safe.

### Carrying Pets And Service Animals

Traveling with pets on Delta involves fees and rules that differ from regular baggage charges. Small pets often travel in the cabin for a fee, while larger pets must be checked or transported in cargo.

Service animals are not considered pets and usually fly free of charge, but you’ll need to provide proper documentation before your flight. Planning ahead is crucial to ensure a smooth experience for you and your companion.

Have you thought about how your furry friend will handle the journey? Knowing the costs and requirements lets you focus on comfort and care rather than unexpected expenses.

### Prepaying Online

Delta lets you pay for checked bags online before you arrive at the airport. Paying in advance usually costs less than paying at the gate. It’s a simple way to save $10 to $20 per bag.

Have you ever waited in line only to be surprised by extra fees? Prepaying removes that stress and speeds up your check-in process. Plus, it helps you budget your travel expenses better.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does Delta Charge For The First Checked Bag?

Delta charges $30 for the first checked bag on most domestic flights. This fee applies to standard economy tickets but may vary by route and fare class.

### Are Checked Bag Fees Different For International Delta Flights?

Yes, Delta often includes the first checked bag free on many international routes. Fees for additional bags or overweight items still apply.

### Can I Avoid Delta Checked Bag Fees?

Delta waives checked bag fees for certain credit card holders, SkyMiles Medallion members, and active military personnel. Otherwise, standard fees apply.

### What Are The Fees For Overweight Bags On Delta?

Delta charges $100 for bags weighing 51-70 pounds and $200 for 71-100 pounds. Oversized fees may also apply based on dimensions.
