## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Fees

Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares but charges for many extras. Carry-on bags are one of these extras. Understanding Spirit Airlines carry-on fees helps travelers avoid surprises at the airport. This section breaks down the costs and rules for carry-on bags on Spirit flights.

### Standard Carry-on Bag Costs

Spirit charges fees for standard carry-on bags. These bags are larger than personal items and must fit in the overhead bin. The cost varies depending on when the bag is added to the booking.

* Paying during booking is the cheapest option.
* Adding a bag at check-in costs more.
* Paying at the gate is the most expensive choice.

Typical fees range from $35 to $65 each way. Prices depend on the route and travel date.

### Personal Item Allowance

Every passenger can bring one free personal item. This item must fit under the seat in front. Examples include a small backpack, purse, or laptop bag. The personal item size limit is 18 x 14 x 8 inches. This item does not incur a fee.

### Fee Variations By Booking Time

Spirit’s carry-on fees change based on when you pay for the bag. Early payment saves money.

| Booking Time | Fee Range |
| --- | --- |
| During Booking | $35 - $45 |
| At Online Check-in | $45 - $55 |
| At Airport Gate | $55 - $65 |

Booking early helps avoid higher fees. Paying at the airport can double the cost.

Let’s break down the key points you need to know about Spirit’s baggage charges.

### Overweight And Oversized Charges

Spirit Airlines charges extra if your bag exceeds their size or weight limits. Carry-on bags must not weigh more than 40 pounds and fit within the 22 x 18 x 10 inches size limit. If your bag is overweight or oversized, expect fees ranging from $65 to $150 depending on how much it exceeds the limits.

Think about this: Is your bag packed with items you truly need? Reducing weight can save you money and hassle. I once avoided a $100 overweight fee just by leaving my laptop charger in my checked luggage.

### Checked Bag Fees

Spirit’s checked bag fees vary based on when and where you pay. Booking your checked bag online before you arrive at the airport can cost as little as $30. Waiting to pay at the airport increases the fee significantly, sometimes up to $65 or more.

Keep in mind the airline allows you to check multiple bags, but each one has its own fee. Planning ahead by pre-paying for bags is a smart move if you want to stick to your budget.

### Special Item Regulations

Spirit Airlines treats special items like sports equipment, musical instruments, and medical devices differently. Some of these can be carried on if they fit within size limits, but others must be checked and may incur extra fees.

* Golf clubs and skis usually require a checked bag fee.
* Medical devices may qualify for fee waivers, but you need proper documentation.
* Fragile items should be packed carefully and declared to avoid damage issues.

Have you ever had to pay extra for a special item last minute? Preparing your gear according to Spirit’s rules can prevent that. Double-check their website for the latest details on what counts as a special item.
