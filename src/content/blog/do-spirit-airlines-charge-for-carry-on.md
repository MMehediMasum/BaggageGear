## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Spirit Airlines has a specific carry-on policy that every traveler must know. Understanding this policy helps avoid extra fees at the airport. Spirit is known for low base fares but charges for many extras, including carry-on bags. Knowing what types of carry-on are allowed and the size limits can save money and time.

### Types Of Carry-on Allowed

Spirit Airlines allows two types of carry-on items without extra charges:

* **Personal Item:**Small bag like a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.
* **Carry-On Bag:**Larger bag that goes in the overhead bin, but usually with a fee.

Only the personal item is free. Carry-on bags require an additional payment unless included in specific fare bundles.

### Size And Weight Limits

Spirit sets clear size limits for carry-on items. The personal item must fit under the seat in front. Its maximum size is 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

Carry-on bags must not exceed 22 x 18 x 10 inches. There is no official weight limit, but bags must be light enough to lift into the overhead bin.

Exceeding these sizes or carrying more bags than allowed leads to extra fees. Checking size before packing helps avoid surprises at the gate.

### Standard Carry-on Charges

Spirit Airlines charges a fee for most standard carry-on bags that go into the overhead bin. This fee varies depending on when you pay—booking early online usually costs less than paying at the airport.

For example, if you add a carry-on bag during booking, the fee might be around $35, but checking in at the gate can push it up to $65 or more. Paying ahead is a smart way to save money and time.

### Personal Item Fees

Spirit allows one personal item free of charge, like a small backpack or purse. This item must fit under the seat in front of you, so size matters here. If your personal item is too large, Spirit will charge you the standard carry-on fee.

Think about what you really need in your personal item. Could your jacket or laptop bag count as one? Keeping it small keeps it free.

### Exceptions And Free Allowances

Some travelers get exceptions. For instance, active-duty military personnel often enjoy free carry-on allowances. Also, Spirit does not charge for items like a diaper bag for parents traveling with infants.

Check the latest Spirit Airlines policies before your trip, as rules can change. Knowing your exceptions can help you pack more without paying extra.

## Additional Baggage Fees To Know

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=spirit-airlines-is-known-for-its-low-base-fares.-but-travelers-should-prepare-for-extra-baggage-fees.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares. But travelers should prepare for extra baggage fees. These fees apply to carry-ons, checked bags, and items that are too heavy or large. Understanding these costs helps avoid surprises at the airport.

### Checked Bags Costs

Checked bags are not free on Spirit Airlines. Charges depend on how many bags you check and when you pay. Paying online before the flight is cheaper than paying at the airport. Prices increase with each additional bag.

* First checked bag: usually costs between $30 and $45.
* Second checked bag: costs between $40 and $65.
* Third bag and beyond: fees rise significantly.

Costs can also change based on your flight route and date. Buying baggage allowance early can save money.

### Overweight And Oversize Charges

Spirit Airlines charges extra for bags that are too heavy or too large. The weight limit for checked bags is 40 pounds. Bags between 41 and 50 pounds have an added fee. Bags over 50 pounds are not allowed.

* Overweight fee for 41-50 pounds: around $70 per bag.
* Oversize bags (over 62 inches total): fees apply, usually $100 or more.
* Bags that exceed size or weight limits may be refused.

Travelers must measure and weigh bags before the airport. This prevents unexpected charges and delays.
