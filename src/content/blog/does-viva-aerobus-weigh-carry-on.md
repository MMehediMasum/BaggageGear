## Carry-on Weight Limits

Carry-on weight limits are important for travelers to know. Airlines set rules to keep flights safe and organized. Knowing these limits helps passengers pack wisely and avoid fees. Each airline has its own policies on how much carry-on luggage you can bring on board.

### Viva Aerobus Policies

Viva Aerobus allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The bag must not weigh more than 10 kilograms (22 pounds). It should fit in the overhead bin or under the seat in front. The size limit is usually 55 x 40 x 25 cm. Bags that exceed weight or size may require extra fees or must be checked in.

### Comparing With Other Airlines

Other budget airlines have similar carry-on weight limits. For example:

* Spirit Airlines: 7 kilograms (15 pounds)
* Ryanair: 10 kilograms (22 pounds)
* EasyJet: 15 kilograms (33 pounds) for standard carry-on

Full-service airlines often allow heavier carry-ons, around 7 to 10 kilograms. Viva Aerobus stands out for offering a decent weight limit for a low-cost airline. Travelers should always check each airline’s rules before packing. This avoids surprises and extra charges at the airport.




## Measuring Your Carry-on

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-your-carry-on-bag-correctly-is-essential-to-avoid-surprises-at-the-viva-aerobus-check-in-counter.-knowing-the-exact-dimensions-ensures-your-bag-complies-with-their-size-limits,-saving-you-time-and-extra-fees.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring your carry-on bag correctly is essential to avoid surprises at the Viva Aerobus check-in counter. Knowing the exact dimensions ensures your bag complies with their size limits, saving you time and extra fees. Taking a moment to measure can make your travel experience smoother and less stressful.

### Tools And Techniques

You don't need fancy equipment to measure your carry-on. A simple tape measure or a ruler works perfectly for checking length, width, and height.

* Place your bag on a flat surface for accuracy.
* Measure the height from the base to the top, including handles or wheels.
* Measure the width at the widest point.
* Measure the depth by checking the thickest part of the bag.

Using a measuring tape with clear markings helps avoid guesswork. If you don’t have one, a piece of string and a ruler can do the trick—just mark the string and measure it afterward.

### Common Mistakes To Avoid

Many travelers overlook a few critical points when measuring their carry-on. One common error is forgetting to include protruding parts like wheels and handles. Viva Aerobus considers the entire external size, so missing these can cause your bag to be rejected.

Another mistake is measuring loosely or at an angle, which can underestimate the true size. Always measure straight and tight against the bag’s edges.

Do you double-check your bag before every trip, or do you rely on memory? Taking a few extra minutes to measure can prevent last-minute panic and unexpected fees at the airport.
