Lightweight suitcases with spinner wheels help move easily through airports and streets. Sets with multiple sizes suit different trip lengths and packing needs. Materials like hard shell or softside offer different levels of protection and flexibility. This guide covers top luggage options that meet these needs, helping you choose the perfect travel companion for your study abroad journey.

Yes, hard shell suitcases like Samsonite Freeform protect belongings from impacts and weather. They are durable and lightweight for easy handling.
