### Are Bluetooth Trackers Reliable For Luggage Tracking?



Bluetooth trackers like Tile and Air Tracker are reliable for short-range luggage tracking. They help find lost items indoors or nearby. However, for long-distance or global tracking, GPS trackers like LandAirSea 54 are more effective.

### How Long Do Luggage Tracker Batteries Last?



Battery life varies by model. Samsung Galaxy SmartTag2 and GEGO PRO trackers offer long battery life, often lasting weeks or months. Replaceable batteries are common, ensuring your luggage tracker stays active throughout your travels.
