Some sales show big discounts that are not real. Sellers may raise prices before a sale, then lower them to the original price. Check previous prices on trusted sites. Compare with other retailers to see if the sale is genuine.

Fake or used luggage may appear in sales. Always buy from official stores or trusted sellers. Look for clear photos and product details. Read reviews to confirm authenticity. Avoid deals that seem too good to be true.

### Understand Restocking Fees And Conditions

Sales often have special return rules. Some sellers charge restocking fees for returned items. Others may limit the return period. Read the return policy carefully before buying. Know your rights to avoid unexpected costs.
