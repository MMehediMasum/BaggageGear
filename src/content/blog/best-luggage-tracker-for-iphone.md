### How Long Does The Apple Airtag Battery Last?



Apple AirTag features a replaceable battery with a lifespan of about 5 to 10 years. This long-lasting battery reduces the need for frequent replacements. When the battery runs low, your iPhone will notify you to replace it promptly.



### Is Apple Airtag Waterproof And Durable For Travel?



Yes, Apple AirTag is IP67 rated, making it water and dust resistant. It can withstand splashes, rain, and brief submersion. This durability makes it ideal for tracking luggage during travel and outdoor adventures without worry.



### Can Apple Airtag Track Luggage In Crowded Places?



Apple AirTag leverages the vast Find My network to locate items even in crowded areas. Nearby iPhones anonymously help detect your AirTag’s location. This crowd-sourced tracking increases the chances of recovering lost luggage quickly.



### Is Apple Airtag Compatible With Android Devices?



No, Apple AirTag is designed exclusively for iOS devices. It works only with iPhone, iPad, and other Apple products through the Find My app. Android users should consider alternative trackers compatible with their devices.



### How Does Apple Airtag Ensure Privacy And Security?



Apple AirTag uses end-to-end encryption to protect location data. Only the owner can see where their AirTag is. It also features anti-stalking measures, such as alerts if an unknown AirTag moves with you. Privacy is a top priority.
