### What Is Backpack Battles

Backpack Battles is a multiplayer game where players compete using items in their backpacks. Each player brings unique tools and weapons to the fight. The goal is to outsmart and outplay the opponent in short, intense rounds. The game encourages quick decision-making and clever use of resources. * Players start with a set of items in their backpack.
* Each round, players choose items to use against their opponent.
* Items have different effects like attack, defense, or special moves.
