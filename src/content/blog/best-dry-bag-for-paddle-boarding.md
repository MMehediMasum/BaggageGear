## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Dry Bag Essential For Paddle Boarding?



Dry bags are crucial for paddle boarding to protect your gear from water. They keep valuables like phones and clothes dry. Their waterproof design ensures peace of mind while enjoying water activities. A dry bag is a reliable companion, especially during unexpected weather changes.



### How Do Roll-top Dry Bags Work?



Roll-top dry bags provide a secure waterproof seal. To use, simply roll the top down three to five times. Then, buckle it tightly. This creates a watertight barrier to protect your gear. Their easy closure system is both efficient and user-friendly, perfect for outdoor adventures.



### Can Dry Bags Float In Water?



Yes, many dry bags are designed to float. When sealed correctly, trapped air inside helps them stay buoyant. This feature is handy if your bag accidentally falls into the water. Floating capability ensures easy retrieval and added safety for your belongings.



### How Do I Choose The Right Dry Bag Size?



Choose a dry bag size based on your activity and gear amount. Smaller bags (2L-5L) suit essentials like phones and snacks. Larger bags (20L-80L) accommodate clothing and bulkier items. Consider your specific needs and the duration of your paddle boarding adventure.



### Are All Dry Bags Waterproof Phone Cases Included?



Not all dry bags come with waterproof phone cases. Some brands offer them as added accessories. Check the product details before purchasing. Having a waterproof phone case is beneficial for extra protection. It ensures your phone remains dry during your paddle boarding trips.
