Many bags, like mommore and Qualyphant, are designed to fit Spectra S1 and S2 pumps. These bags include insulated cooler pockets, waterproof mats, and ample storage for pump accessories. They often have laptop sleeves and stylish designs suitable for work and travel.

Waterproof mats protect surfaces and keep pump parts clean during use. They prevent spills from damaging bags and accessories. Many pump bags include these mats, making them hygienic and convenient for work, travel, and public spaces.



### Can Breast Pump Bags Double As Diaper Bags?



Yes, several breast pump bags like mommore and Qualyphant serve as diaper bags too. They offer large compartments, insulated milk storage, and laptop sleeves. This dual function helps working moms carry essentials efficiently in one stylish bag.
