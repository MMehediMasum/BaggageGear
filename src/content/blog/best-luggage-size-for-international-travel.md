## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Ideal Luggage Size For International Travel?



For international travel, a 20-24 inch carry-on or 28-30 inch checked luggage is ideal. It fits most airline requirements and holds essentials efficiently. Samsonite Freeform 28-inch and Amazon Basics 30-inch suitcases are great options for durability and space.



### Can I Use Carry-on Luggage For International Flights?



Yes, most international flights allow carry-ons sized 20-22 inches. Travelpro Maxlite 5 and LEVEL8 hard shell 20-inch suitcases meet airline size rules. Carry-ons save time at baggage claim and reduce checked bag fees.



### How Does Expandable Luggage Benefit International Travelers?



Expandable luggage offers up to 15% more space, ideal for souvenirs or extra items. Amazon Basics 30-inch and Samsonite Omni PC provide this feature. It helps travelers pack flexibly without buying larger luggage.



### Are Tsa Locks Important For International Luggage?



Yes, TSA-approved locks enhance security and allow airport screening without damage. Samsonite Freeform and SHOWKOO luggage sets include TSA locks. These locks ensure compliance and peace of mind during travel.



### How To Choose Lightweight Luggage For Long Trips?



Choose suitcases made from polycarbonate or durable softside materials for lightness. Samsonite and Travelpro Maxlite 5 are lightweight options. Lightweight luggage reduces strain and makes handling easier during long international trips.



## Conclusion



Choosing the right luggage size for international travel can make your journey easier and more comfortable. Smaller carry-ons, like the Travelpro Maxlite 5 or Samsonite Omni PC, help avoid long waits at baggage claim. Expandable designs add flexibility when you need extra room. Lightweight materials and smooth spinner wheels improve mobility.
