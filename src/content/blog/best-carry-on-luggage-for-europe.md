Traveling across Europe means frequent moves between trains, planes, and buses. Your carry-on needs to be light, sturdy, and easy to roll through airports and city streets. Many travelers prefer hard-shell suitcases for protection, while softside bags offer extra pockets and flexibility.

Popular options include brands like Samsonite, Amazon Basics, and SwissGear, known for quality and airline approval. Whether you want a sleek hard case or a roomy soft bag, picking the right carry-on helps avoid baggage fees and keeps essentials close. This guide reviews top carry-ons designed for European travel, helping you find the perfect fit.
