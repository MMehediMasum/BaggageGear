## Avianca Carry-on Policy

Avianca sets clear rules for carry-on bags to ensure a smooth flight experience. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees or delays at the airport. The airline allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger on most flights. Passengers must follow size and weight limits to fit cabin space and safety rules.

### Size And Weight Limits

Avianca limits carry-on bags to these dimensions:

* Length: 55 cm (21.6 inches)
* Width: 35 cm (13.7 inches)
* Height: 25 cm (9.8 inches)

The maximum weight allowed is 10 kg (22 lbs). Bags heavier or larger than this must be checked in or pay extra fees.

### Included Allowances By Fare Type

Avianca offers different carry-on allowances depending on the ticket type:

| Fare Type | Carry-on Bag | Personal Item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy Light | Not included | One small personal item |
| Economy | One carry-on bag | One personal item |
| Business | One carry-on bag | One personal item |

Personal items include laptops, purses, or small backpacks. Passengers with Economy Light tickets may need to pay for carry-on bags.
