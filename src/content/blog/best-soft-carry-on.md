## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Soft Carry-on Luggage Size?



The ideal size for a soft carry-on is usually 21 to 22 inches. This size fits most airline overhead compartments. It provides enough space for essentials while being compact and lightweight. Always check your airline's specific size restrictions before traveling.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Carry-ons?



Yes, spinner wheels make maneuvering your carry-on much easier. They allow effortless navigation in crowded airports. Spinner wheels offer 360-degree movement, providing more control. They also reduce strain on your arms and shoulders during travel.



### How Does A Tsa Lock Work On Luggage?



A TSA lock allows security to inspect your luggage without damaging it. TSA agents have master keys to open these locks. This ensures your belongings remain secure yet accessible for inspection. It's a recommended feature for added travel security.



### What Features To Look For In Soft Carry-ons?



Look for lightweight materials, spinner wheels, and expandability. These features enhance travel convenience and packing flexibility. A built-in TSA lock and water-resistant fabric add security and durability. USB ports can also be a handy addition for tech-savvy travelers.



### Are Softside Carry-ons Durable?



Yes, softside carry-ons are designed for durability and flexibility. They often use resilient fabrics that withstand travel wear and tear. Many models offer reinforced corners and sturdy zippers. This ensures they last through multiple trips while keeping your items safe.
