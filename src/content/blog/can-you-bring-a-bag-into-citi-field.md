## Bag Policy At Citi Field

Citi Field has clear rules about bags. These rules help keep everyone safe. They also make entry faster and easier. Knowing the bag policy before you go saves time. It ensures you bring the right type of bag. Here is what you need to know about the bag policy at Citi Field.

### Allowed Bag Types

Only certain bags are allowed inside Citi Field. Small purses, clear bags, and backpacks are okay. Bags must fit specific size limits. This helps with quick security checks. Bags larger than the allowed size will be denied entry.

* Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags no larger than 12"x6"x12"
* One-gallon clear plastic freezer bag
* Small clutch bags no larger than 4.5"x6.5"
* Diaper bags and medically necessary bags allowed with inspection

### Prohibited Items

Some items are not allowed in any bags. These items pose safety risks or violate stadium rules. Guests must leave these items outside the stadium. Security will check bags for these prohibited items.

* Weapons of any kind
* Alcohol and illegal drugs
* Outside food and beverages
* Large umbrellas and tripods
* Noise makers and laser pointers

### Clear Bag Rule Details

The clear bag rule makes entry faster. Security can see inside bags without opening them. This reduces wait times and keeps lines moving. Bags must be fully clear or see-through plastic. Solid colors or mesh bags are not allowed. The size limit is strictly enforced at all times.

Yes, you can bring a backpack into Citi Field. It must comply with the stadium’s size and clear bag policies. Bags larger than 16"x16"x8" are not allowed. Clear bags or small clutches are recommended for faster entry.

### Are There Any Prohibited Items In Citi Field Bags?

Yes, Citi Field prohibits items like weapons, outside food, large bags, and alcohol. Check the official policy before your visit. This ensures safety and smooth entry for all guests.

### Is There A Bag Check Service At Citi Field?

Citi Field does not offer a bag check service. Guests must carry their bags with them or use permitted clear bags. Plan accordingly to avoid delays or denied entry.

### Can I Bring A Purse Or Small Bag Inside Citi Field?

Yes, small purses or bags up to 5"x8" are allowed. They should be clear or transparent to meet security requirements. This helps speed up the security screening process.
