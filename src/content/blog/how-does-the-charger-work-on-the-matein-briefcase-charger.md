## Troubleshooting Common Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=troubleshooting-common-issues-with-the-matein-briefcase-charger-helps-keep-your-device-ready.-simple-problems-can-stop-charging-or-cause-confusion-about-charger-status.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Troubleshooting common issues with the Matein Briefcase Charger helps keep your device ready. Simple problems can stop charging or cause confusion about charger status. Understanding these issues saves time and frustration. Below are key points to check and fix usual problems quickly.

### Charging Failures

Check the power source first. A loose plug or dead outlet often causes charging failure. Inspect the USB cable for damage or wear. Use a different cable if possible. Make sure the charger port on your briefcase is clean and free of dust. Avoid using damaged or bent connectors. Try plugging into another device to test the charger. If charging still fails, the internal battery may need service.

### Indicator Light Meanings

The Matein Briefcase Charger uses lights to show status. A solid green light means charging is complete. A blinking green light indicates active charging. A red light warns of a problem, like low battery or connection issues. No light usually means no power or a faulty charger. Always refer to the user manual for exact light codes. Understanding these signals helps identify issues quickly.

### Resetting The Charger

Resetting the charger can fix many problems. Disconnect the charger from all power sources. Wait for about 30 seconds to clear the internal memory. Reconnect the charger and power it on. This simple reset can restore normal function. If problems persist, try a full battery cycle by charging to 100% and then draining it completely. Resetting often resolves glitches without professional help.
