## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Luggage Set Size Is Best For Long Trips?



For long trips, opt for 3 to 5-piece sets with 24" to 30" suitcases. Expandable options add extra space when needed, like the Amazon Basics 3-Piece Set and CONCEPTEN 5-Piece Set, perfect for packing all essentials efficiently.



### Which Luggage Sets Have Tsa-approved Locks?



Many sets include TSA locks for secure travel, such as CONCEPTEN, TILYMOL, and Cosbarn luggage. These locks allow TSA agents to inspect bags without damage, ensuring your belongings stay safe during flights.



### Are Hardshell Or Softside Luggage Sets Better?



Hardshell suitcases, like Amazon Basics and Merax, offer better protection and scratch resistance. Softside sets, such as Rockland Journey, provide flexibility and lighter weight. Choose based on your travel style and durability needs.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Improve Luggage Mobility?



Spinner wheels rotate 360 degrees, allowing smooth and effortless maneuvering in any direction. Sets like Amazon Basics and Travelers Club Chicago feature four multi-directional wheels for easy handling in airports and crowded spaces.



### Which Luggage Sets Are Best For Lightweight Travel?



Lightweight sets like Merax 3-Piece and Cosbarn 5-Piece reduce travel strain without sacrificing durability. These suitcases use ABS or ABS+PC materials to provide toughness while keeping overall weight low for convenient transport.



### Can I Expand These Luggage Sets For Extra Space?



Yes, many sets such as Amazon Basics, Rockland Journey, and TILYMOL are expandable. They offer up to 25% more packing space, ideal for bringing back souvenirs or additional items during your trip.
