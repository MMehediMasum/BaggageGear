## Southwest Baggage Policy

Southwest Airlines offers a straightforward baggage policy that travelers appreciate. Understanding these rules helps avoid extra fees and travel stress. This guide explains how many suitcases you can bring and the size limits.

### Carry-on Allowance

Southwest lets each passenger bring one carry-on bag and one personal item. The carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. The maximum size for carry-on bags is 10 x 16 x 24 inches. Personal items include purses, laptop bags, or small backpacks. These should fit under the seat in front of you.

### Checked Baggage Rules

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. Each bag can weigh up to 50 pounds. The size limit is 62 inches total (length + width + height). Bags over these limits may incur extra fees. Paying attention to weight and size avoids additional charges.

### Oversized And Overweight Bags

Bags weighing between 51 and 100 pounds have fees. The fee increases with the bag’s weight. Oversized bags over 62 inches but under 80 inches also incur charges. Bags larger than 80 inches are not accepted. It’s best to pack smart and keep bags within limits.

This fee can add up quickly if you’re traveling with family or for an extended period. Have you ever packed more than two bags and been surprised at the extra charges at the airport? Planning ahead and consolidating your items can save you money and hassle.

### Oversize And Overweight Fees

If your bag weighs more than 50 pounds or is larger than 62 inches in total dimensions, Southwest charges extra fees. Bags weighing between 51 and 100 pounds will cost $75 each, while overweight bags over 100 pounds are not accepted.

Oversized bags measuring more than 62 inches but less than 80 inches cost $75 each as well. So, it’s smart to weigh and measure your luggage before heading to the airport. Avoiding these fees by packing light or redistributing weight can make your trip smoother and cheaper.

### Lost Or Delayed Baggage Fees

Southwest Airlines doesn’t charge fees for lost or delayed baggage, but the inconvenience is something you want to minimize. If your bag is delayed, Southwest provides reimbursement for essential purchases you make while waiting.
