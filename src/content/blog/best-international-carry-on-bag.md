## Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, TSA-approved toiletry bags simplify security checks by meeting liquid size limits and are leakproof to protect belongings.



### How Important Is Expandability In A Carry-on Bag?



Expandability allows extra packing space when needed, making it perfect for longer trips or bringing souvenirs back.



### Can A Carry-on Bag Fit A Laptop And Essentials?

Spinner wheels offer 360-degree movement, making the bag easy to maneuver in tight airport spaces and crowded areas.
