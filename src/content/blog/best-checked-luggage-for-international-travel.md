## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Luggage Is Best For Durability During International Travel?



Samsonite Freeform and Samsonite Omni 2 offer durable, lightweight polycarbonate shells. They resist scratches and impacts, ideal for rough handling on international flights. Both include TSA locks and spinner wheels for smooth mobility and security.



### What Luggage Offers The Most Expandable Space?



Amazon Basics 30" Hardside luggage expands up to 15% more space. Travelpro Maxlite 5 and Samsonite Centric also provide expandable compartments, allowing extra packing capacity for souvenirs or additional clothing on long trips.



### Which Checked Luggage Is Easiest To Maneuver At Airports?



Samsonite Freeform, Samsonite Omni 2, and Away Large feature 360° spinner wheels. These multi-directional wheels ensure smooth, effortless movement through busy airport terminals and narrow airplane aisles.



### Are Tsa Locks Included In These Luggage Options?



Yes, most models like Samsonite Freeform, Away Large, and Travelpro Platinum Elite come with built-in TSA-approved locks. These locks enhance security while allowing airport officials to inspect your luggage without damage.



### Which Luggage Is Best For Lightweight Travel?



Travelpro Maxlite 5 and Kenneth Cole REACTION Out of Bounds are lightweight options. Their materials reduce overall weight, making them easier to lift and carry during international trips without sacrificing durability.



### Can Softside And Hardside Luggage Both Be Good For International Trips?



Yes, softside luggage like SwissGear Sion offers flexibility and external pockets. Hardside options such as Samsonite Winfield 2 provide better impact protection. Choose based on your packing style and travel needs.
