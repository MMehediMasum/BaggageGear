## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Backpack Airline-approved?



An airline-approved backpack fits carry-on size limits, usually up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. It should fit under the airplane seat or in overhead compartments. Features like a TSA-friendly design and organized compartments enhance convenience during security checks and travel.



### How Does A Usb Charging Port Work?



A USB charging port in backpacks allows you to charge devices on-the-go. You connect a power bank inside the backpack and plug your device into the external USB port. This setup is convenient for charging phones or tablets while traveling or commuting.



### Are These Backpacks Waterproof?



Many travel backpacks are water-resistant, offering protection against light rain or spills. Materials like polyester and nylon are commonly used for water resistance. However, they may not be fully waterproof, so consider additional protection for heavy rain.



### What Is A Tsa-friendly Backpack?



A TSA-friendly backpack allows you to pass through airport security without removing your laptop. It usually features a separate, lay-flat laptop compartment. This design speeds up security checks and reduces hassle for frequent flyers.



### Do These Backpacks Have Anti-theft Features?



Several models come with anti-theft features like RFID-blocking pockets and lockable zippers. These features protect your belongings from theft and unauthorized scanning. They are ideal for travelers concerned about security, especially in crowded areas.
