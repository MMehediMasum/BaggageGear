## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Carry-on Luggage Size?



The best carry-on luggage size is typically 22x14x9 inches. This size is commonly accepted by most airlines. It ensures your luggage fits in overhead compartments. Always check your airline's specific size requirements before traveling.



### Are Hard Shell Carry-ons Better Than Softside?



Hard shell carry-ons offer better protection for fragile items. They are also more durable and scratch-resistant. Softside carry-ons are generally more flexible and expandable. Choose based on your packing needs and travel style.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Benefit Travelers?



Spinner wheels allow for easy maneuverability in tight spaces. They provide effortless movement in any direction. This is especially useful in crowded airports. They reduce strain on your arms and shoulders.



### Why Is A Tsa Lock Important For Luggage?



A TSA lock allows security personnel to inspect your luggage without damaging it. It ensures your belongings remain secure. TSA locks are accepted at all U. S. airports. They offer peace of mind during travel.



### How Does Expandable Luggage Help Travelers?



Expandable luggage provides additional packing space when needed. It's perfect for travelers bringing back souvenirs. This feature adds flexibility to your packing. Ensure the expanded size still meets airline requirements.
