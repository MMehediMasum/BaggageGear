## Jansport Backpack Sizes

Jansport backpacks come in various sizes, which affects whether they fit as a personal item. Knowing the size helps decide if the backpack meets airline or school rules. ### Standard Dimensions

Most Jansport backpacks measure about 16 to 18 inches in height. The width typically ranges from 12 to 14 inches. Depth usually falls between 5 and 9 inches. These sizes fit well under airplane seats and in overhead bins. The standard size suits most personal item requirements for travel.

### Variations By Model

Jansport offers many models with different sizes. The SuperBreak model is smaller and lighter, perfect for light packing. The Big Student model is larger, with more compartments and space. Most airlines allow personal items that fit under the seat in front of you. Typical size limits are around 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Bags must be compact enough to slide easily under the seat. Many Jansport backpacks fall within these dimensions, making them suitable personal items.

### Differences Among Airlines

Each airline has slightly different rules for personal items. Some allow slightly larger bags, while others are stricter. Low-cost carriers often have smaller size allowances to maximize cabin space.

Examples of size limits:

* American Airlines: 18 x 14 x 8 inches
* Delta Air Lines: 18 x 14 x 8 inches
* Southwest Airlines: 18.5 x 13.5 x 8.5 inches
