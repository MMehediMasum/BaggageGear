## Jetblue Baggage Policy

Understanding JetBlue's baggage policy can save you both time and money at the airport. Knowing exactly what you can bring onboard and what costs apply to checked bags helps avoid last-minute surprises. Let’s break down JetBlue’s rules to help you pack smart and travel stress-free.

### Carry-on Allowance

JetBlue lets you bring one carry-on bag and one personal item for free. Your carry-on must fit in the overhead bin, typically up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Personal items, like a purse or laptop bag, should fit under the seat in front of you.

This means you can bring essentials and valuables close by without extra charges. Think about packing a small backpack or tote that fits those measurements. Have you ever had to gate-check your bag because it was too big? JetBlue’s clear size limits help avoid that hassle.

### Checked Bag Basics

Checked bags aren’t included free on most JetBlue fares, so you’ll usually pay to check your luggage. The standard fee for the first checked bag is around $35 each way. The second bag usually costs $45 each way.

Weight limits for checked bags are 50 pounds (23 kg) and size limits are 62 linear inches (length + width + height). Overweight or oversized bags will cost more, sometimes up to $150. It’s worth weighing your suitcase at home to avoid these extra fees.

| Second Checked Bag | $45 | Up to 50 lbs | Up to 62 linear inches |

Knowing these details helps you decide whether to pack light or pay for checked bags. Are you packing for a short trip or a longer vacation? Your packing choices can cut costs and make your journey smoother.

### First Bag Fee

Your first checked bag with JetBlue usually costs $35 on most domestic flights. This fee applies if your fare type doesn’t include a free checked bag. If you have Mosaic status or certain fare classes, you might not pay this fee at all.

Have you ever packed last minute and ended up paying more just because you weren’t aware of these fees? Planning ahead can help you avoid that extra charge.

### Second Bag Fee

The second checked bag will typically cost $45. This fee is a bit higher than the first bag fee, so it’s worth considering if you really need that extra luggage. Sometimes, consolidating your items into one bag can save money.

Think about how often you actually need to check a second bag. Could you rearrange or wear bulkier items instead?

### Additional Bags

For every bag beyond the second, JetBlue charges $150 per bag. This is a significant jump, so carrying more than two bags can get expensive quickly. If you travel with a lot of gear, consider shipping items or upgrading your fare for included baggage.

Ask yourself: is it worth paying for extra bags, or can you find alternative ways to transport your belongings?

JetBlue's checked bag fees vary depending on the fare type you choose. Each fare has its own rules and prices for checked luggage. Understanding these differences helps plan your trip budget better.

Blue Basic is JetBlue's most affordable fare. It does not include any checked bags for free.

### Blue

Blue fare offers more flexibility and includes one free checked bag.

Blue Plus and Mint fares provide the best baggage benefits.

## Weight And Size Limits

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-jetblue's-weight-and-size-limits-for-checked-bags-helps-avoid-extra-fees.-bags-must-meet-specific-rules-to-be-accepted-without-additional-charges.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding JetBlue's weight and size limits for checked bags helps avoid extra fees. Bags must meet specific rules to be accepted without additional charges. Knowing these limits saves time and money at the airport.

### Standard Limits

JetBlue allows checked bags up to 50 pounds (23 kg). The total size must not exceed 62 inches (157 cm). Size is the sum of length, width, and height. Bags larger or heavier than this may cost extra.

### Overweight Charges

Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds (24 to 32 kg) have an overweight fee. This fee is higher than the standard checked bag charge. Bags over 70 pounds are not accepted on most flights.

### Oversized Bag Fees

Checked bags exceeding 62 inches in total size face an oversized fee. The fee applies regardless of the bag’s weight. Oversized bags must still meet the airline’s maximum weight rules.

Paying for your checked bags online before you arrive at the airport is almost always cheaper than paying at the counter. JetBlue’s website or app lets you add bags during booking or anytime before your flight, often at a reduced rate. This simple step can save you money and save time at check-in.

## How To Pay For Checked Bags

Paying for checked bags on JetBlue is simple and offers several options. You can secure your baggage allowance early or handle it later. Each choice affects convenience and sometimes the price. Knowing when and how to pay helps avoid surprises and long waits.

### At Booking

Paying for checked bags during booking is often the easiest way. The website or app shows the baggage fees clearly. Adding your bag here saves time at the airport. You can use credit or debit cards to pay securely. This method often offers the best rates.

### At Check-in

Paying at online or airport kiosk check-in is another option. The system lets you add checked bags before your flight. It may cost a little more than booking in advance. Still, it is faster than paying at the counter. Payment is accepted through cards or digital wallets.

### At The Airport Counter

Paying for checked bags at the airport counter is possible but slower. Lines can be long, especially during busy times. The fee might be higher than other options. Staff accept cash, cards, or mobile payments. This choice suits travelers who decide last minute to check bags.
