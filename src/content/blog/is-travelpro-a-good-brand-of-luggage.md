### Founding And Growth

Travelpro was founded in 1987 by a pilot who wanted luggage that could withstand frequent travel. His own frustrations with flimsy suitcases led him to design durable, lightweight luggage. This practical beginning helped the brand grow steadily as more travelers recognized the value.

Over the years, Travelpro expanded its product line to include various types of bags, from carry-ons to checked luggage. Their focus on quality materials and user-friendly features attracted professionals and casual travelers alike. This growth shows how a clear purpose can lead to lasting success.
