## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hard Shell Luggage Better For Travel?



Hard shell luggage offers superior protection for belongings. It resists impacts, scratches, and weather damage. Its durable exterior ensures safe transport. This is ideal for frequent or rough travel conditions. Models like Samsonite and Amazon Basics are popular choices.



### How Do Expandable Hard Shell Suitcases Work?



Expandable suitcases have zippers that increase packing space. This feature adds up to 15% more room. It’s perfect for extra souvenirs or bulky items. Many models, including Samsonite Freeform and Amazon Basics, offer this convenience. It enhances versatility without compromising durability.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better Than Regular Wheels?



Spinner wheels rotate 360 degrees for easy maneuvering. They reduce wrist strain and improve mobility. Most large hard shell luggage, like Travelpro Maxlite Air, feature spinner wheels. This makes navigation through airports and streets smoother and quicker.



### What Size Luggage Is Airline Approved?



Carry-on luggage is usually 22 inches or smaller. Checked luggage ranges from 24 to 30 inches. Samsonite Freeform (23-inch) fits carry-on requirements. Larger sizes like Amazon Basics 30-inch are for checked baggage. Always check specific airline size restrictions.



### Do Tsa Locks Ensure Luggage Security?



TSA-approved locks allow airport security to inspect without damage. They provide peace of mind and meet travel regulations. Most models listed, such as Samsonite Omni 2, include TSA locks. This feature enhances security while facilitating smooth screening.



### Which Hard Shell Luggage Is Best For Lightweight Travel?



Lightweight hard shell luggage reduces travel fatigue and ease handling. Samsonite Freeform and Travelpro Maxlite Air are excellent lightweight options. They combine durability with low weight for efficient packing. Ideal for travelers seeking convenience and protection.
