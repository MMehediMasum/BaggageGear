## Printing Luggage Tags Step-by-step

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=printing-your-royal-caribbean-luggage-tags-yourself-can-save-time-and-hassle-at-the-port.-it-ensures-your-tags-are-ready-before-your-cruise-day,-letting-you-focus-on-packing-instead-of-waiting-in-lines.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Printing your Royal Caribbean luggage tags yourself can save time and hassle at the port. It ensures your tags are ready before your cruise day, letting you focus on packing instead of waiting in lines. Follow these simple steps to print clear, durable tags that will travel with your bags safely.

### Choosing Printer Settings

Start by selecting the right printer settings to get the best print quality. Use a color printer if possible, as the tags often include color codes or logos that help staff identify your luggage quickly.

Set your printer to the highest quality or photo mode. Choose standard letter size (8.5" x 11") paper or the specific size recommended by Royal Caribbean. Avoid using draft or economy modes as they can produce faint prints that may not scan properly.

### Downloading And Printing Tags

Download your luggage tags directly from the Royal Caribbean website after completing your online check-in. Save the file to your computer to avoid any last-minute internet issues.

Open the downloaded file using a PDF reader and print immediately. Double-check the print preview to ensure all information fits correctly on the page and that barcodes or QR codes are fully visible.

### Tips For Clear And Durable Prints

* **Use thicker paper or card stock:**This prevents tags from tearing or bending during handling.
* **Laminating your tags:**If possible, laminate them or cover with clear tape to protect against moisture.
* **Check ink levels:**Low ink can cause faded barcodes, making scanning difficult.
* **Allow drying time:**Let your tags dry completely before attaching them to your luggage to avoid smudges.

Have you ever experienced a luggage tag that faded or tore during travel? Taking these small steps can save you from that headache and help your bags arrive safely every time.

## Troubleshooting Printing Issues

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=printing-luggage-tags-for-royal-caribbean-should-be-simple-and-quick.-sometimes,-problems-can-happen-during-printing.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Printing luggage tags for Royal Caribbean should be simple and quick. Sometimes, problems can happen during printing. Troubleshooting these issues saves time and stress before your cruise. Understanding common problems helps fix them fast. If problems persist, Royal Caribbean support is ready to assist.

### Common Problems And Fixes

* **Printer not responding:**Check if the printer is on and connected to your device.
* **Paper jams:**Remove any stuck paper carefully. Use the right size and type of paper for tags.
* **Low ink or toner:**Replace cartridges if print quality is poor or faded.
* **Incorrect tag size:**Ensure you select the correct luggage tag size in the print settings.
* **File format issues:**Download the tag file again. Use PDF format for best results.
* **Software glitches:**Restart your computer and printer. Close and reopen the printing page.

### Contacting Royal Caribbean Support

Still cannot print your luggage tags? Reach out to Royal Caribbean support. Use their official website or call their help center. Provide details about your issue and device. Support staff guide you step-by-step. They may resend the tag files or suggest alternate methods.

### Attaching Tags To Your Bags

Start by choosing a visible spot on your suitcase handle or strap to attach the tag. Avoid placing it under straps or inside pockets where it might be missed or damaged. Use the plastic loop or string provided with the tag to secure it firmly.

Make sure the tag’s barcode and your information face outward and are easy to scan. If your bag has multiple handles, pick the one that’s easiest for handlers to access quickly. Have you checked the tag’s orientation before heading to the terminal?

### Ensuring Tags Remain Secure

Double-check the tag’s attachment before you leave for the cruise port. Tug gently to confirm it won’t slip off during transit. It’s wise to carry a spare tag or the printed copy in your carry-on, just in case.

Consider reinforcing the tag’s loop with a small zip tie or clear tape if you expect rough handling. This extra step can prevent losing your tag and avoid baggage confusion. What would happen if your tag got lost—are you prepared with a backup plan?
