## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hardside Luggage Scratch-resistant?



Hardside luggage, like the Amazon Basics 21" Carry-On, features a durable outer shell. This shell is designed to resist scratches and impacts. Its robust material protects your belongings during travel, ensuring longevity and style.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Luggage?



Yes, spinner wheels offer superior maneuverability. They allow the luggage to glide effortlessly in any direction. This feature is especially useful in crowded airports, making travel more convenient and less strenuous.



### How Does Expandable Luggage Benefit Travelers?



Expandable luggage offers additional packing space. This feature is ideal for travelers who shop during trips. It allows for extra souvenirs or clothing, providing flexibility and convenience without needing an extra bag.



### Why Choose Luggage With Tsa Locks?



TSA locks provide added security for your luggage. They allow TSA agents to inspect your bags without damaging locks. This ensures your belongings remain safe while complying with airport security regulations.



### What Is The Advantage Of A Lightweight Suitcase?



Lightweight suitcases, like the Samsonite Freeform 28-Inch, are easier to lift and carry. They reduce the strain of travel, especially when navigating stairs or overhead compartments. This makes them a popular choice for frequent flyers.
