## Backpack Policy At Hershey Park

Visitors often ask about the backpack rules at Hershey Park. Knowing the backpack policy helps plan a smooth visit. It ensures you bring only what is allowed inside the park. Hershey Park has clear guidelines on backpacks and bags to keep everyone safe and comfortable.

### Allowed Bag Types

Hershey Park permits several bag types to bring inside. Backpacks, small coolers, and purses are usually allowed. Clear bags help speed up security checks. Bags should be easy to carry and fit under ride seats.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must not be too large or bulky. The maximum size allowed is generally 12” x 12” x 6”. Oversized bags may be refused entry or require storage outside. Carry only what you need for the day to avoid hassle.

### Prohibited Items

Some items are banned for safety reasons. Sharp objects, weapons, and alcohol are not allowed. Large coolers and glass containers are also prohibited. Check the park’s official list before packing your bag.




## Bag Check Procedures

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=visitors-carrying-backpacks-to-hershey-park-should-know-the-bag-check-procedures.-these-checks-keep-everyone-safe-and-ensure-a-smooth-entry.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Visitors carrying backpacks to Hershey Park should know the bag check procedures. These checks keep everyone safe and ensure a smooth entry. Understanding these steps helps guests prepare their bags and pass through quickly.

### Entry Screening

All backpacks go through entry screening at Hershey Park gates. Staff inspect bags to check for prohibited items. Guests open backpacks for a clear view inside. This process helps keep the park safe for all visitors.

The screening is quick but thorough. Large or bulky bags may take extra time. Guests are advised to pack light and keep only essentials in their backpacks.

### Security Measures

Security staff use metal detectors and visual checks for backpacks. They look for sharp objects, alcohol, and other banned items. If any item is found, it will be confiscated or the guest asked to leave it behind.

Backpacks must not contain outside food or drinks, except for medical needs. Clear bags or smaller bags may speed up the security process. Follow all instructions from security personnel for a smooth check.

## Storage Options Inside The Park

The park offers practical storage options to keep your belongings safe and let you enjoy the attractions hands-free. Understanding these options can help you plan better and avoid carrying unnecessary weight all day.

### Locker Rentals

Lockers are available throughout Hershey Park, providing secure storage for your backpack and other items. These lockers come in various sizes, so you can choose one that fits your needs—whether it’s just a small bag or a larger backpack.

Renting a locker is simple. You can pay at the locker location using cash or card, and the rental fee depends on the locker size and rental duration. Keep in mind, lockers are popular and can fill up quickly on busy days, so arriving early might be a good idea.

### Bag Drop Locations

If you prefer not to carry your backpack around but want quick access to your items, Hershey Park offers designated bag drop areas near major rides and attractions. These spots are staffed and provide a convenient way to leave your backpack securely for a short time.

Bag drop locations work well if you’re hopping between rides and don’t want to repeatedly rent lockers. However, these areas may have size restrictions and limited availability, so check the park map or ask staff about the nearest spots. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Bring A Backpack Into Hershey Park?

Yes, backpacks are allowed at Hershey Park. However, they must comply with the park’s size and security rules. Large or oversized bags may be subject to inspection or restrictions.

### Are There Restrictions On Backpack Contents At Hershey Park?

Yes, certain items like alcohol, weapons, and outside food are prohibited in backpacks. Hershey Park enforces strict security checks to ensure guest safety.

### Is There A Bag Size Limit For Hershey Park Backpacks?

Hershey Park recommends carrying small to medium backpacks. Oversized bags may not be permitted to enter and could require separate storage.

### Can I Bring A Backpack On Hershey Park Rides?

Backpacks are allowed on most rides but must be securely stored or held. Some rides may require guests to leave bags in lockers.
