## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Travel Backpack Flight Approved?



A flight-approved backpack meets airline size restrictions for carry-on luggage. It fits in overhead bins or under seats. The Taygeer and MATEIN backpacks are designed to comply with these requirements. They offer ample storage while ensuring hassle-free boarding.



### How Do Usb Charging Ports Work In Backpacks?



USB charging ports in backpacks allow on-the-go device charging. They connect your power bank inside the bag to a USB port outside. This feature in Taygeer and SHRRADOO backpacks keeps devices charged without holding the power bank.



### Are These Backpacks Suitable For Business Trips?



Yes, these backpacks are ideal for business trips. They feature compartments for laptops and essentials. The LOVEVOOK and ZOMFELT models offer organized storage and professional designs. They cater to both style and functionality needs for business travelers.



### Can These Backpacks Fit A 17-inch Laptop?



Yes, many of these backpacks fit 17-inch laptops. Models like SHRRADOO and LOVEVOOK are designed to accommodate larger laptops. They provide secure compartments to protect your device during travel. Ensure you check product specifications for exact laptop compatibility.



### Are These Backpacks Water-resistant?



Yes, several backpacks are water-resistant, protecting your belongings from rain. MATEIN and ECOHUB offer water-resistant materials for added security. This feature is essential for travelers who encounter various weather conditions. Always verify the product description for water resistance details.



### Do These Backpacks Have Separate Shoe Compartments?



Yes, some backpacks feature separate shoe compartments. Taygeer's design includes a dedicated shoe pouch. This keeps shoes separate from other items, maintaining cleanliness and organization. It's a practical feature for travelers who need to pack footwear separately.
