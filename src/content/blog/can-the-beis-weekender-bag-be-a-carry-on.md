## Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding carry-on size limits is crucial before you pack your Beis Weekender Bag for any trip. Airlines have specific rules about how big your bag can be, and these limits vary widely. Knowing these rules helps you avoid last-minute hassles at the airport.

### Airline Size Restrictions

Each airline sets its own carry-on size restrictions, usually measured in linear inches (length + width + height). Most U.S. airlines allow bags up to around 22 x 14 x 9 inches, but some low-cost carriers have stricter limits.

If your Beis Weekender Bag fits within these dimensions, it should pass as a carry-on. But if it’s even slightly larger, you might be asked to check it in or pay extra fees.

Check the airline’s website before you fly. This can save you from unexpected charges and the frustration of repacking at the gate.

### International Vs Domestic Rules

Carry-on size limits can differ significantly between domestic and international flights. Some international airlines allow smaller bags due to limited overhead bin space on certain aircraft.

Your Beis Weekender Bag might be perfect for a domestic U.S. flight but too large for a European carrier’s cabin requirements. This difference often surprises travelers who assume one size fits all.

Do you usually check your bag last minute because you didn’t check size limits? Taking a few minutes to measure and compare can make your travel experience smoother, especially on international trips.
