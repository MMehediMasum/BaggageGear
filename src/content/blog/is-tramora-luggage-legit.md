### Common Red Flags

One major warning sign is a price that seems too good to be true. If you find Tramora luggage at a steep discount on unofficial sites, pause before buying. Scammers often lure buyers with unbelievably low prices.

Another red flag is vague or missing product details. Authentic sellers provide clear descriptions, materials used, and warranty information. If these are absent or confusing, it might indicate a fake or scam.

Also, watch out for seller reviews. If most reviews mention delayed shipping, poor customer service, or items not matching descriptions, your risk of being scammed rises.

### How To Verify Genuine Products

* **Buy from official stores or authorized retailers.**This is the easiest way to avoid counterfeit items.
* **Check for product serial numbers or authenticity cards.**Tramora often includes these as proof of genuine products.
* **Compare product images and specs on the official Tramora website.**Differences in design or materials can reveal fakes.
* **Contact Tramora customer support with your product details.**They can confirm if the serial number or seller is legitimate.
