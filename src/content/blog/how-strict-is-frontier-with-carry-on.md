## Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding Frontier's carry-on size limits can save you from last-minute hassles at the airport. They have clear rules about the dimensions and weight of carry-on bags, and sticking to these can make your travel experience smoother. Let's break down exactly what you need to know to avoid any surprises.

### Dimensions Allowed

Frontier allows a standard carry-on bag that must fit within **22 x 14 x 9 inches**, including handles and wheels. This means your bag should slide easily into the overhead bin without causing any issues.

Think about your usual travel bag—does it meet this size? I once tried to bring a slightly larger bag and had to pay extra fees at the gate. Don’t let that happen to you. Measure your bag before you pack to be sure.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike some airlines, Frontier does not specify a strict weight limit for carry-on bags. However, your bag must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead bin without assistance.

This practical approach means you should focus more on size than weight. Still, packing too heavy can slow you down and cause frustration at boarding. Ask yourself: can you comfortably lift your bag overhead? If not, consider lightening your load or checking the bag instead.

Frontier Airlines has clear rules about personal items on board. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees at the gate. Personal items must fit under the seat in front of you. This policy ensures safety and comfort for all passengers.

### What Qualifies As A Personal Item

A personal item is a small bag or case. It can be a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. Items like a briefcase, camera bag, or diaper bag also count. The key is that the item must fit under your seat. Larger bags must be checked or paid for as carry-ons.

### Size And Weight Guidelines

Frontier sets strict size limits for personal items. The maximum size allowed is 14 x 18 x 8 inches. Bags larger than these dimensions will not fit under the seat. Weight limits are less clear, but heavy bags may be questioned. It is best to keep your personal item light and compact.

### Prohibited Items

Frontier strictly prohibits certain items in your carry-on for safety reasons. Sharp objects like knives, scissors with blades over 4 inches, and box cutters are not allowed. Firearms and explosives are also banned, no exceptions.

Liquids over the allowed limit and large sporting goods won’t fit in your carry-on either. If you try sneaking in prohibited items, be ready to surrender them or face fines. What’s the one item you always double-check before packing?

### Liquids And Gels Rules

Frontier follows the TSA’s 3-1-1 liquids rule closely. This means you can bring liquids, gels, and aerosols in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less. All containers must fit comfortably in a single, clear, quart-sized zip-top bag.

Remember, this includes makeup, toothpaste, and even hand sanitizer. If your liquids don’t fit these rules, you’ll need to check them or leave them behind. Have you ever had to toss something important because it didn’t meet these rules?
