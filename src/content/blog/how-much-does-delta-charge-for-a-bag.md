## Delta's Baggage Fee Basics

Understanding Delta's baggage fees can save you from unexpected charges at the airport. Knowing the basics helps you pack smarter and budget better for your trip. Let’s break down what you can expect when flying with Delta and how their fees work.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Delta lets you bring one carry-on bag and one personal item onboard for free. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin, typically measuring up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Your personal item, like a backpack or purse, should fit under the seat in front of you.

If you’ve ever scrambled to shove a bag under the seat or gate-checked your carry-on last minute, you know how frustrating it can be. Following these size rules avoids that hassle and keeps your essentials close during the flight.

### Checked Bag Fees

Checked bags aren’t free on most Delta flights. The first checked bag usually costs $30, and the second one costs $40. This applies to domestic flights, but fees may vary on international routes.

Delta waives these fees for certain travelers, such as SkyMiles Medallion members, Delta One passengers, and those with specific credit cards. If you’re planning to check bags, consider if you qualify for these perks to save money.

### Overweight And Oversize Charges

Delta charges extra for bags that exceed weight or size limits. Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds incur a $100 fee, while those between 71 and 100 pounds cost $200. Size limits generally cap at 62 linear inches (length + width + height).

Oversized bags beyond the 62-inch limit face a $200 fee. Imagine packing your bulky winter coat or sports gear—knowing these fees ahead can help you decide if it’s cheaper to ship items or repack.

Delta’s baggage fees change depending on the flight route. Domestic flights usually have standard fees. International flights may have different rules and charges. Certain destinations might allow one free checked bag. Others might charge higher fees due to airport or country regulations. Always check the specific route for accurate baggage costs.

### Class Of Service Impact

The class of service affects baggage fees significantly. Economy class passengers often pay more for checked bags. Premium classes like First or Business usually include free baggage allowance. Delta offers more generous baggage policies for higher ticket classes. This benefit can save money for travelers flying in comfort.

### Frequent Flyer Status Benefits

Delta rewards frequent flyers with baggage fee waivers. Members of the Delta SkyMiles program get perks based on their status level. Higher tiers like Silver, Gold, or Platinum often allow free checked bags. This advantage reduces travel costs for loyal customers. Check your status to see if you qualify for baggage fee discounts.

## How To Avoid Or Reduce Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=delta’s-baggage-fees-can-add-up-quickly.-finding-ways-to-avoid-or-reduce-these-charges-saves-money-and-stress.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Delta’s baggage fees can add up quickly. Finding ways to avoid or reduce these charges saves money and stress. Small changes in planning and payment methods help cut down costs. Here are practical tips to keep your bag fees low.

### Using Delta Credit Cards

Delta offers credit cards that include free checked bags. Cardholders usually get one or two bags checked for free on Delta flights. This benefit applies to the cardholder and sometimes to companions on the same reservation. Using a Delta credit card also earns miles and other perks. Paying for your ticket with the card often unlocks these savings automatically.

### Packing Tips To Save Costs

Pack smart to avoid extra fees for overweight or oversized bags. Use lightweight suitcases to keep total weight low. Check Delta’s size and weight limits before packing. Carry-on bags are free but must fit size rules. Consider packing only essentials to stay within limits. Rolling clothes saves space. Use compression bags for bulky items. Avoid packing heavy shoes and liquids. Plan outfits to mix and match.

### Prepaying For Bags Online

Delta offers lower fees for bags paid online before the airport. Buy your checked bag allowance during booking or before departure. Prepaying saves a few dollars per bag. It also speeds up check-in and boarding. You can add extra bags online too. Keep your confirmation email as proof. Paying later at the airport costs more and causes delays.

Some equipment requires advance notice or approval. Charges often depend on the item's dimensions and weight. Checking Delta’s policy before travel is wise.

### Pet Travel Policies

Traveling with pets needs careful planning. Small pets may travel in the cabin for a fee. Larger pets usually fly in the cargo hold. Delta requires a pet carrier that meets their size standards. Health certificates and vaccination records might be necessary. Booking pet travel early ensures availability and smooth processing.

### International Travel Rules

International baggage rules differ from domestic ones. Weight limits and fees can change depending on the destination. Customs regulations may affect what you can bring. Some countries require special permits for certain items. Checking Delta’s international baggage policy helps avoid surprises. Always verify destination-specific rules before packing.

### Impact Of Covid-19 On Baggage Rules

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Delta adjusted baggage policies for safety. They allowed more flexibility on change and cancellation fees. Some temporary fee waivers for checked bags helped travelers during the crisis.

Today, most COVID-19 baggage exceptions are phased out. However, Delta still encourages contactless check-in and baggage drop-off. These measures reduce touchpoints and speed up airport processes.
