## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Carry-on Luggage For Airlines?



Carry-on luggage for airlines typically measures 22x14x9 inches. This size fits in overhead compartments. It is important to check airline-specific requirements. Some airlines may have slight variations. Always verify dimensions before travel to avoid issues.



### Is A Hardshell Or Softside Suitcase Better For Carry-on?



Hardshell carry-ons offer more protection for fragile items. They are durable and usually water-resistant. Softside suitcases are more flexible and can expand. They are easier to fit in tight spaces. Choose based on your travel needs and preferences.



### Do Carry-on Suitcases Have Tsa Locks?



Many carry-on suitcases come with TSA-approved locks. These locks allow TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. They provide added security for your belongings. Check product descriptions for this feature. It's a useful addition for frequent travelers.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Benefit Carry-on Luggage?



Spinner wheels allow 360-degree movement, making navigation effortless. They reduce strain on your arms and back. These wheels enhance maneuverability in crowded spaces. They are ideal for smooth surfaces, providing a seamless travel experience. Consider spinner wheels for convenience and ease.



### Can Carry-on Luggage Be Expandable?



Yes, many carry-on suitcases have expandable sections. This feature provides extra packing space when needed. It is ideal for longer trips or shopping. Remember to check airline size limits, as expansion may exceed them. Expandable luggage offers flexibility without compromising on space.
