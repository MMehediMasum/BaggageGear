USB charging ports allow easy device charging on the go. You connect a power bank inside the backpack, then charge your phone or tablet externally. This feature is common in backpacks like MATEIN and Volher, offering convenience during long commutes or travel.

Yes, many professional backpacks like MATEIN and KROSER are unisex. They feature neutral colors and versatile designs. These backpacks fit various laptop sizes and offer ample storage, making them ideal for anyone needing a reliable work bag.
