City walking needs a backpack that is small, lightweight, and easy to carry all day. It should have enough space for essentials like your phone, wallet, water bottle, and maybe a light jacket. Breathable materials help keep you cool while you walk.

This guide covers top choices such as SKYSPER, ZOMFELT, and HALOVIE backpacks. Each option balances comfort, style, and function for urban explorers. Find a backpack that supports your daily adventures without weighing you down.
