Medium suitcases range from 24 to 26 inches in height. They offer more packing space than carry-ons but need more trunk room. Usually, two medium suitcases fit comfortably in the Camry’s trunk. Their size balances between packing capacity and trunk space use.

### Large Suitcases

Large suitcases measure 28 inches or more in height. These are perfect for longer trips with more items. The Toyota Camry trunk fits one or two large suitcases, depending on their exact size. Large suitcases may limit room for other bags or items, so packing smart is key.

## Maximizing Space Efficiently

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maximizing-space-efficiently-in-a-toyota-camry-is-key-for-fitting-more-suitcases-comfortably.-the-trunk-space-is-decent-but-limited.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maximizing space efficiently in a Toyota Camry is key for fitting more suitcases comfortably. The trunk space is decent but limited. Smart packing helps use every inch wisely. Organizing bags and clothes properly avoids wasted room. These tips make traveling simpler and stress-free.

### Stacking Techniques

Stack suitcases by size and shape. Place the heaviest and largest bags at the bottom. Smaller suitcases go on top or in gaps. Use soft bags to fill empty spaces between hard cases. This creates a stable and compact stack. Keep the stack balanced to prevent shifting during travel.

### Using Soft Bags

Soft bags are flexible and easier to fit in odd spaces. Use them to fill corners or gaps around suitcases. They can compress to fit tight spots. Soft bags also help protect fragile items inside. Mix soft bags with hard suitcases to maximize space.

### Folding Vs Rolling Clothes

Rolling clothes saves more space than folding. Rolled clothes are compact and less wrinkled. Fold bulky items to keep their shape. Combine both methods depending on the type of clothing. Packing cubes can help keep rolled clothes organized.

Make sure items stored under seats don’t interfere with seat adjustments or comfort. Soft, flexible bags work best for this area. Have you ever found a lost charger or snack under your seat? It’s a great reminder of how valuable this storage space can be.
