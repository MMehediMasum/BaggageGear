## Tsa Rules For Carry-on Items

Understanding the TSA rules for carry-on items helps travelers avoid delays and confiscations. The Transportation Security Administration sets clear security guidelines for all passengers. Knowing what you can and cannot bring ensures smooth airport screening.

### General Security Guidelines

All carry-on items must pass through X-ray machines. Security officers may inspect any item by hand. Liquids in carry-ons must be in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less. These containers should fit in one clear, quart-size bag per passenger. Sharp objects and weapons are not allowed in carry-ons. Electronics like laptops and tablets need separate screening.

### Items Allowed And Prohibited

Personal care items, including vibrators, are usually allowed in carry-ons. They must be free of batteries that can cause safety issues or packed properly. Items with lithium batteries should be carried in the cabin, not in checked bags. Prohibited items include firearms, explosives, and many sporting goods. Always check the TSA website for updated lists before packing.

Battery type matters for TSA regulations. Vibrators powered by standard AA or AAA batteries are straightforward and usually cause no concerns. Devices with rechargeable lithium-ion batteries, like USB-charged models, are also allowed but must comply with battery size limits.
