## Capital One Arena Bag Rules

Capital One Arena enforces clear bag rules to keep all visitors safe. These rules help speed up entry and reduce risks. Knowing the bag policy before your visit saves time and avoids surprises. The arena checks bags carefully at entrances. Understanding allowed items and size limits is important for a smooth experience.

### Allowed Bag Types

* Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”
* One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags (Ziploc or similar)
* Small clutch bags or purses, up to 4.5” x 6.5” (without a strap)
* Diaper bags and medically necessary bags are allowed but subject to inspection

### Prohibited Items

* Backpacks, except for clear bags or medically necessary bags
* Large purses or handbags that exceed size limits
* Food and beverages from outside the arena
* Weapons, sharp objects, and any illegal items
* Selfie sticks, tripods, and professional cameras

### Size Restrictions

All bags must fit within specific size limits. Clear bags must not exceed 12” x 6” x 12”.

Small clutch bags without straps must be no bigger than 4.5” x 6.5”.

Bags larger than these sizes will not be allowed inside. Follow these rules to avoid delays at security checkpoints.
