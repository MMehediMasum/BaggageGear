Also, steer clear of bags with metal studs or chains, as these can trigger additional searches or be considered unsafe.

### Packing Essentials For Game Day

Pack your bag with just the essentials to keep it light and manageable. Bring your ticket, ID, some cash or card, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle (empty before entry).
