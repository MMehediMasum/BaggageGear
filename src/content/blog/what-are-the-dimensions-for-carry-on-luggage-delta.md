## Delta Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding Delta's carry-on size limits helps travelers avoid extra fees and delays. Delta Air Lines sets clear rules for carry-on luggage. Knowing these rules makes packing easier and airport moves smoother. Below are the main details about Delta's carry-on size limits.

### Standard Dimensions

Delta allows carry-on bags up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Bags larger than these may need to be checked in. These dimensions fit most overhead bins on Delta planes.

### Weight Restrictions

Delta does not enforce a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. Still, bags must be light enough to lift into the overhead bin without help. Overly heavy bags may cause problems during boarding.

### Personal Item Allowances

Each passenger can bring one personal item in addition to a carry-on bag. Personal items include purses, laptop bags, or small backpacks. The item should fit under the seat in front. Typical size limit for personal items is 18 x 14 x 8 inches.




## Measuring Your Carry-on Bag

Measuring your carry-on bag accurately is essential for Delta flights. Airlines have strict size limits for carry-on luggage. Proper measurement helps avoid extra fees and hassle at the airport. Use a tape measure or ruler for precise dimensions. Check every part of your bag, including wheels and handles. Follow these guidelines to ensure your bag fits Delta’s requirements.

### Height

Measure from the bottom to the top of your bag. Include any raised parts like handles or wheels. Do not compress or squeeze the bag. Keep it in its natural shape. Delta usually allows carry-ons up to 22 inches tall.

### Width

Measure the widest part of the bag. This is usually the side-to-side distance. Include any pockets or protrusions. Check that the width does not exceed Delta’s 14-inch limit.

### Depth

Measure the front-to-back distance of the bag. Include any bulges or attached items. Delta’s maximum depth is often 9 inches. Make sure your bag fits within this depth.

### Including Wheels And Handles

Always measure wheels and handles as part of the total size. These parts add to the overall dimensions. Extend the handle to measure its full height. Check that wheels do not add extra bulk beyond limits. Delta counts these parts in size restrictions.
