## Allegiant Baggage Weight Limits

Understanding Allegiant baggage weight limits is essential before you pack for your trip. Allegiant Airlines has clear rules on how much your checked bag can weigh without incurring extra fees. Knowing these limits helps you avoid surprise charges at the airport and ensures a smoother travel experience.

### Standard Checked Bag Weight

Allegiant allows each checked bag to weigh up to **40 pounds (18 kilograms)**without additional fees. This weight limit is lower than many other airlines, so you need to pack carefully. If your bag exceeds this limit, you will have to pay extra charges.

Think about what you really need to bring. Could you swap heavy items for lighter alternatives? Planning your packing with this weight limit in mind saves you both money and stress.

### Overweight Baggage Rules

If your checked bag weighs between **41 and 50 pounds (19 to 23 kilograms)**, Allegiant classifies it as overweight. Bags in this range are accepted but come with an overweight baggage fee. This fee varies depending on your route and when you pay it—online or at the airport.

Trying to redistribute items into a carry-on or another checked bag might help you avoid these overweight fees. Have you ever had to repack your luggage at the airport to dodge extra costs? It’s a common headache that’s easy to prevent with a simple scale at home.

### Excessive Weight Charges

Bags weighing more than **50 pounds (23 kilograms)**are considered excessively heavy by Allegiant. These bags are often not accepted at all or require special arrangements and significantly higher fees. In some cases, you may need to ship heavy items separately, which can be more cost-effective.

Before you pack that heavy suitcase, ask yourself if the contents justify the extra fees or hassle. Would it be better to mail some items ahead of time? Thinking through this can save you both money and time at the airport.

## Dimensions And Size Restrictions

Dimensions and size restrictions play a key role in packing for an Allegiant flight. Bags must fit specific limits to avoid extra fees or delays. Knowing these rules helps travelers pack smart and travel stress-free.

### Maximum Bag Dimensions

Allegiant allows checked bags with a total dimension limit of 62 inches. This includes length, width, and height combined. Bags larger than 62 inches may not be accepted or could incur extra charges. It’s important to keep your bag within these size limits for smooth check-in.

### Measuring Your Bag Correctly

Measure your bag by adding its length, width, and height. Use a tape measure for accuracy. Include wheels and handles in your measurements. Check the total size against Allegiant's 62-inch limit. Proper measuring avoids surprises at the airport and helps stay within size rules.

## Fees For Checked Bags

Understanding the fees for checked bags on Allegiant Airlines is crucial before you pack for your trip. These fees can vary based on the number of bags, their weight, and size. Knowing the costs upfront helps you avoid surprises at the airport and manage your travel budget better.

### First Checked Bag Cost

Allegiant charges a fee for your first checked bag, which is usually lower when paid online in advance rather than at the airport. The cost typically ranges from $25 to $30, depending on your route and travel dates.

Booking your first checked bag early can save you money and reduce stress during check-in. Have you ever waited until the last minute and paid more for your luggage? Planning ahead can prevent this.

### Additional Bags Pricing

Fees increase with each additional checked bag. The second bag often costs more than the first, typically around $35 to $45. If you’re traveling with multiple bags, these fees can add up quickly.

Consider how much you really need to bring. Could you consolidate items or wear heavier clothing to avoid extra charges? These small changes can make a big difference in your final bill.

### Overweight And Oversize Fees

Allegiant enforces strict weight and size limits on checked bags, usually 40 pounds for standard bags and 50 pounds for overweight. Bags exceeding these limits face additional fees, which can be quite steep—ranging from $50 to over $100.

Oversize bags, those larger than 62 linear inches (length + width + height), also incur extra charges. If your luggage exceeds these limits, you’ll want to weigh and measure it before heading to the airport.

Have you ever been caught off guard by overweight fees? Investing in a portable luggage scale could save you time and money, making your travel experience smoother.

Checked bags on Allegiant must not exceed 62 linear inches (length + width + height). Oversized bags may incur additional fees or be rejected. Always measure your luggage to ensure compliance with airline policies.
