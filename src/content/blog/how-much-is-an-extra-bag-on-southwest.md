## Southwest Baggage Policy 2026

Southwest Baggage Policy 2026 updates offer clear guidelines for travelers. Understanding these rules helps avoid extra fees and travel stress. Southwest keeps baggage policies simple and traveler-friendly.

### Standard Allowance

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. Each bag can weigh up to 50 pounds. The maximum size for checked bags is 62 inches. This includes length, width, and height combined. Bags exceeding these limits may incur fees.

### Carry-on Rules

Passengers can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item. Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin. Personal items should fit under the seat in front. Typical personal items include purses, laptops, or small backpacks.

### Checked Bag Limits

Each checked bag must not exceed 50 pounds and 62 inches. Overweight bags between 51-100 pounds have an extra charge. Bags over 100 pounds are not accepted. Southwest charges $75 for each extra checked bag beyond two. Paying for extra bags is easy during booking or at the airport.

Southwest Airlines offers generous baggage policies, but extra bags come with fees. Understanding these charges helps travelers plan better. This section breaks down the costs for each additional bag on Southwest flights.

### First Extra Bag Charges

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. The first extra bag means the third checked bag in total. This bag has a fee of $75 each way. Paying this fee applies per flight segment. The fee is the same for all domestic flights.

### Second Extra Bag Costs

The second extra bag is the fourth checked bag. It also costs $75 per flight segment. The fee remains constant regardless of the flight distance. Southwest does not offer any discounts on these fees.

### Additional Bag Fees

Any bag beyond the fourth counts as an additional bag. Each extra bag after two free bags costs $75 per flight. Oversized or overweight bags may have higher fees. Bags over 50 pounds or larger than 62 inches get extra charges.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does Southwest Charge For An Extra Bag?

Southwest Airlines does not charge for the first two checked bags. For a third bag and beyond, fees apply. The fee is typically $75 per additional bag, but fees can vary. Always check Southwest’s official website for the latest bag fee details.

### Are There Weight Limits For Extra Bags On Southwest?

Yes, Southwest sets a weight limit of 50 pounds per checked bag. Bags exceeding this weight incur an overweight fee of $75. Ensure your extra bags meet weight restrictions to avoid extra costs. Overweight fees apply per bag, so pack carefully to save money.

### Can I Bring More Than Two Bags On Southwest?

You can bring more than two bags, but fees apply starting with the third checked bag. Southwest allows unlimited checked bags if you pay the extra fees. Always confirm the latest bag policies and fees before your trip to avoid surprises.

### Is There A Size Limit For Extra Bags On Southwest?

Yes, Southwest allows checked bags up to 62 inches total (length + width + height). Bags exceeding this size are subject to oversized baggage fees of $75. Keep your extra bags within size limits to avoid additional charges and delays.
