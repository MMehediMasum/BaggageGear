## Alaska Airlines Carry-on Policy

Alaska Airlines has clear rules about carry-on bags. Knowing these rules helps you avoid extra fees and travel smoothly. Their policy defines what you can bring on board without paying.

Understanding size and weight limits keeps you prepared. The airline also has specific rules about personal items. Let’s explore these details to plan your trip better.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Each passenger can bring one standard carry-on bag. The bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. Maximum dimensions are 22 x 14 x 9 inches.

The weight limit is usually not enforced strictly. Still, it is best to keep your bag light. Bags that are too large or heavy may need to be checked.

Carry-on bags include suitcases, backpacks, or duffel bags. It is wise to measure your bag before traveling. This prevents surprises at the gate.

### Personal Item Rules

Passengers can also bring one personal item. This item should fit under the seat in front of you. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.

The personal item size limit is about 17 x 13 x 8 inches. Items larger than this may not be allowed as personal items. Keep your personal item small and easy to access.

This policy helps keep aisles clear and boarding fast. Pack your essentials in the personal item for convenience. This way, you have what you need close by.
