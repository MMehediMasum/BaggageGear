## Luggage Tag Requirements

Luggage tags are a key part of your cruise packing process. Knowing the requirements can save you time and hassle at the port. Let’s break down what Royal Caribbean expects from you regarding luggage tags.

### Royal Caribbean's Policy

Royal Caribbean requires all guests to have luggage tags on their bags for check-in and embarkation. These tags help the cruise staff identify and transport your luggage to your stateroom efficiently. Without proper tags, your bags might face delays or get misplaced during loading.

The cruise line usually provides printable tags online after you complete your online check-in. You must print and attach them securely before arriving at the terminal. This ensures a smoother drop-off experience and quicker handling of your luggage.

### When Tags Are Mandatory

You must have printed luggage tags when you drop off your bags at the port. This applies to all checked luggage that you want the crew to handle and deliver to your cabin. If you arrive without tags, staff may ask you to print them on-site, causing delays.

Even if you plan to carry your bags onboard yourself, having tags can speed up the security and check-in process. It’s best to print them in advance and avoid last-minute stress at the terminal.

### Exceptions To The Rule

There are a few situations where printed luggage tags may not be required. For example, if you only bring carry-on bags that you keep with you throughout the boarding process, tags are optional. Also, some private or specialty cruises might have different protocols.

However, it’s wise to check your specific sailing’s instructions. Missing the luggage tag requirement might lead to confusion or delays that could spoil your first moments on board. Would you rather spend time relaxing or sorting out lost bags?




## How To Get Your Luggage Tags

Getting your luggage tags for Royal Caribbean is simple and quick. These tags help identify your bags and ensure they reach your cabin safely. You can get your tags in a few ways, each convenient for different travelers.

### Printing At Home

You can print your luggage tags at home after checking in online. Visit Royal Caribbean’s website and complete your check-in. Download the tags and print them on sturdy paper. Use clear tape to protect the tags from water and tears. Attach them securely to your luggage handles.

### Getting Tags Onboard

If you don’t print tags at home, get them onboard the ship. Head to the guest services desk after boarding. Staff will print and give you the tags for free. This option takes a little extra time but works well if you forget to print tags before sailing.

### Using Mobile Or Digital Tags

Royal Caribbean offers digital luggage tags through their app. Use the app to check in and receive a digital tag. Show the tag on your phone when handing your bags to porters. This option reduces paper use and speeds up the process. Make sure your phone is charged and ready.

## Tips For Attaching Luggage Tags

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=attaching-luggage-tags-correctly-helps-ensure-your-bags-reach-your-cruise-cabin-safely.-proper-placement-and-secure-attachment-prevent-loss-or-mix-ups-during-transit.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Attaching luggage tags correctly helps ensure your bags reach your cruise cabin safely. Proper placement and secure attachment prevent loss or mix-ups during transit. Knowing what to do if tags go missing keeps you prepared.

### Placement On Suitcases

Place tags where they are easy to see. Attach tags to the suitcase handle or a sturdy strap. Avoid placing tags on zippers or fragile parts. Tags should stay visible during loading and unloading.

### Ensuring Tags Are Secure

Use strong ties or loops to fasten luggage tags tightly. Make sure tags do not dangle loosely or fall off. Check tags after packing to confirm they are firmly attached. Consider using plastic covers to protect tags from water or damage.

### What To Do If Tags Are Lost

Report lost tags immediately at the cruise terminal. Provide your name, cabin number, and contact details. Keep copies of your luggage claim forms handy. Stay calm and cooperate with staff to locate your bags quickly.

### What Happens If I Don’t Print My Luggage Tags?

If you don’t print tags, you’ll receive them at the terminal. This may cause minor delays during boarding.

### How Do I Get Royal Caribbean Luggage Tags?

You can print tags from Royal Caribbean’s online check-in portal or pick them up at the cruise terminal.
