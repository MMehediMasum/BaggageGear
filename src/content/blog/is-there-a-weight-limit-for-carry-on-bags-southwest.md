## Southwest Carry-on Policy

Southwest Airlines allows passengers to bring carry-on bags on board. Knowing their carry-on policy helps avoid surprises at the airport. This policy covers size and weight rules for your bags. It ensures your belongings fit in overhead bins or under the seat.

### Size Restrictions

Southwest sets clear size limits for carry-on bags. Your bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. The maximum allowed size is 10 x 16 x 24 inches. This includes wheels and handles. Bags larger than this size may need to be checked.

### Weight Guidelines

Southwest does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. The focus is on size rather than weight. However, your bag should be light enough for you to lift into the overhead bin. Heavy bags that cannot be safely stored may need to be checked. Keep your carry-on manageable to avoid delays and extra fees.

### Overweight Bag Fees

Southwest Airlines allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger without an extra fee, but the carry-on must fit in the overhead bin and meet size restrictions. Unlike checked bags, Southwest does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. However, if your carry-on is too heavy for you to lift into the overhead bin, gate agents may require you to check it in, which could lead to checked bag fees.

Imagine carrying a heavy bag that you can't lift properly—this is often the real issue, not the weight itself. So, the best practice is to pack smart and keep your carry-on manageable. This way, you avoid the inconvenience of being forced to check your bag unexpectedly.

### Checked Bag Alternatives

If your carry-on is too heavy or bulky, Southwest’s checked bag options provide a reliable alternative. Southwest allows the first two checked bags for free, each weighing up to 50 pounds. This can be a great option if you’re traveling with heavier items or longer trips requiring more gear.

Consider this: instead of struggling to keep your carry-on light, you might find it easier to check a bag and enjoy a hassle-free boarding process. Packing heavier items in checked luggage can also free up space in your carry-on for essentials you want to keep close during your flight.
