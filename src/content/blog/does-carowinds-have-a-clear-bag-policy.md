## Carowinds Bag Policy Basics

Understanding Carowinds' bag policy helps visitors prepare for a smooth entry. The park enforces specific rules on bags for safety and convenience. Knowing these basics saves time and avoids surprises at the gate.

### Purpose Of The Clear Bag Policy

The clear bag policy ensures quick security checks. It helps staff see contents without opening bags. This speeds up entry and keeps everyone safe. Clear bags reduce the chance of prohibited items entering the park.

### Types Of Allowed Bags

Carowinds allows only transparent bags. Clear backpacks, purses, and tote bags are accepted. Small clutch bags under a certain size are also allowed. Bags made of mesh or vinyl work best. Non-transparent or large bags are not permitted.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must not exceed 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. This size fits clear backpacks and small totes. Oversized bags cause delays and may be rejected. Visitors should measure bags before arriving to avoid issues.
