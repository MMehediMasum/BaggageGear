## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Spirit Airlines has a specific carry-on policy that every traveler must know. This policy helps keep the boarding process smooth and efficient. Understanding the rules about what you can bring onboard saves time and money. The airline has clear limits on the size and weight of carry-on items.

Knowing these details can help you pack smartly and avoid extra fees at the airport. Spirit Airlines allows one free personal item and charges for larger carry-ons. Let’s break down the key points of their carry-on policy.

### Allowed Carry-on Items

* One personal item is allowed for free.
* Examples: purse, laptop bag, small backpack.
* One additional carry-on bag can be brought with a fee.
* Examples: rolling suitcase, duffel bag.
* Items must fit under the seat or in the overhead bin.

### Size Restrictions

* Personal item size: 18 x 14 x 8 inches.
* Carry-on bag size: 22 x 18 x 10 inches.
* Items larger than these must be checked or pay extra.
* Measure your bags before heading to the airport.

### Weight Limits

* Spirit Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-ons.
* Carry-ons must be light enough to lift into overhead bins.
* Personal items should be manageable and fit under the seat.
* Heavy or oversized bags may be charged or refused.

### What Qualifies As A Personal Item

A personal item is typically a smaller bag that fits under the seat in front of you. This can be a purse, backpack, laptop bag, or a small tote. It’s meant to hold essentials you want within reach, like your phone, wallet, or travel documents.

Keep in mind, Spirit Airlines is strict about what qualifies as a personal item, so avoid using large bags that might be mistaken for carry-ons. Have you ever found yourself struggling to shove an oversized backpack under the seat? That’s exactly what you want to avoid.

### Size And Weight Limits For Personal Items

| Dimension | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 18 inches (45 cm) |
| Width | 14 inches (35 cm) |
| Depth | 8 inches (20 cm) |

Spirit Airlines does not specify a strict weight limit for personal items, but keep it reasonable. A heavy personal item can be tough to handle and might slow you down during boarding. Light and compact is the way to go.

### Tips For Choosing A Personal Item

* Pick a bag with sturdy handles and a comfortable strap to avoid shoulder strain.
* Choose one with multiple compartments to keep your essentials organized and easy to find.
* Consider a bag with a hard bottom to keep your items safe and upright under the seat.
* Test the bag’s size by trying to slide it under a chair at home to simulate the under-seat space.

Have you ever grabbed a bag only to realize it barely fits under the seat? Selecting the right personal item can prevent that frustration. Your goal should be to carry what you need without feeling weighed down or risking extra fees.

These fees kick in during different stages:

* At booking: You can add carry-on baggage for a lower fee.
* Before online check-in: Fees increase slightly.
* At the airport or gate: Fees rise sharply and can be frustrating.

* Pack light with just a personal item that fits under the seat.
* Pay for your carry-on bag when you book your ticket—this is the cheapest option.
