## United's Baggage Policies

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-united's-baggage-policies-is-essential-before-you-add-a-checked-bag-to-your-flight.-these-rules-determine-the-size,-weight,-and-fees-associated-with-your-luggage.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding United's baggage policies is essential before you add a checked bag to your flight. These rules determine the size, weight, and fees associated with your luggage. Knowing the details helps you avoid surprises at the airport and ensures a smoother travel experience.

### Weight And Size Limits

Your checked bag must meet specific weight and size limits set by United Airlines. The standard weight limit is 50 pounds (23 kg) per bag for most economy tickets. Bags exceeding this weight may incur additional fees, so weighing your luggage before heading to the airport can save you money.

The maximum size allowed is 62 linear inches (length + width + height). If your bag is larger, it may be considered oversized and subject to extra charges. It’s a good idea to measure your suitcase at home to avoid any last-minute hassles.

### Fees And Allowances

United’s baggage fees vary based on your ticket type and destination. For most domestic flights, the first checked bag costs $35, and the second bag is $45. Some fare classes, like United Premier members or those with certain credit cards, may have free checked bags included.

Additional bags or overweight luggage will increase your costs. For example, bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds usually cost $100 each, while those over 70 pounds can go up to $200. Checking your baggage allowance before booking can help you plan and budget better.

## At The Airport

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=adding-a-checked-bag-at-the-airport-with-united-is-simple-and-quick.-you-have-several-options-to-choose-from-based-on-your-convenience-and-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Adding a checked bag at the airport with United is simple and quick. You have several options to choose from based on your convenience and time. Each method ensures your luggage is properly tagged and sent to your flight without hassle. Here are ways to add a checked bag once you arrive at the airport.

### Self-service Kiosks

United offers self-service kiosks in most airports. These kiosks allow you to check in and add a checked bag by yourself. Simply enter your booking details or scan your boarding pass. Follow the on-screen prompts to select your checked bag option and pay any fees. After payment, the kiosk prints your bag tag. Attach it to your luggage and drop the bag at the designated bag drop area. This option saves time and avoids long lines.

### Checking Bags At The Counter

Visit the United check-in counter to add a checked bag. A United agent will assist you with the process. Provide your flight details and the bag you want to check. The agent will weigh your luggage and print the bag tag. Pay any checked bag fees directly at the counter. This method is helpful if you have questions or need extra help. It may take a little longer than kiosks but ensures everything is done right.

### Last-minute Bag Addition

Sometimes you decide to check a bag just before boarding. United allows last-minute bag check during the bag drop period. Head to the bag drop counter as soon as possible. Inform the staff that you want to add a checked bag. They will weigh and tag your luggage. Pay the fee using a credit card or other accepted methods. Keep in mind, this option might cost more than pre-paying online. Arrive early to avoid missing your flight.
