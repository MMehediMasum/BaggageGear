## Delta Carry-on Rules

Delta Air Lines has clear rules for carry-on luggage. Knowing these rules helps avoid surprises at the airport. Carry-on rules include size, weight, and personal item limits. These rules ensure your items fit safely in the cabin. Understanding them lets you pack smart and travel stress-free.

### Carry-on Size Limits

Delta allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The bag must fit in the overhead bin. Maximum size is 22 x 14 x 9 inches. This size includes wheels and handles. Oversized bags may need to be checked at the gate.

### Weight Restrictions

Delta does not have a specific carry-on weight limit. Still, bags must be light enough to lift into bins. Passengers should be able to handle their bags alone. Heavy bags may cause delays or require checking.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Each passenger can bring one personal item. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. The item must fit under the seat in front. Typical size is about 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Personal items should be easy to access during the flight.

## Garment Bag Types

Garment bags come in different styles to suit various travel needs. Choosing the right type helps protect clothes and fits airline rules. Some bags focus on flexibility, while others offer strong protection or ease of carrying. Understanding the main types makes packing easier and keeps clothes neat.

### Soft Vs Hard Garment Bags

Soft garment bags are lightweight and easy to fold. They usually have fabric or nylon material. These bags fit well in overhead bins but may offer less protection.

Hard garment bags have a solid shell, like plastic or hard-sided materials. They protect clothes from damage and keep the shape intact. These bags can be heavier and take more space.

### Foldable Options

Foldable garment bags save space when not in use. They fold down into a smaller size for easy storage. These bags are great for short trips or travelers with limited luggage space.

They often have compartments to keep clothes wrinkle-free. Folding helps fit them inside larger suitcases or carry-ons.

### Wheeled Garment Bags

Wheeled garment bags come with built-in wheels and a handle. They make moving through airports easier, especially with heavy clothes inside.

These bags combine the convenience of a suitcase with garment protection. They often meet carry-on size limits but check airline rules before flying.

Yes, Delta Airlines allows garment bags as carry-on items if they meet size requirements. The bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat.

### Can I Use A Garment Bag As A Personal Item On Delta?
