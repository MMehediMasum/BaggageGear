Choosing the best backpack for hiking and travel depends on your needs. Size, weight, and waterproof features matter a lot. Backpacks like the Maelstrom 40L and FENGDONG 40L offer good space and durability. For lighter trips, options like WATERFLY 20L or SKYSPER 20L are great.
