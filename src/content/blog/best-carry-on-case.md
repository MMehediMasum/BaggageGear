## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Best Features Of Carry-on Luggage?



Carry-on luggage boasts features like durable hardshells, smooth spinner wheels, and TSA-approved locks. Many models are expandable, offering up to 25% more space. Scratch-resistant surfaces and lightweight designs enhance travel convenience. Some even include USB ports for charging devices on the go.



### How Do Expandable Carry-ons Benefit Travelers?



Expandable carry-ons allow travelers to pack more by increasing the luggage's capacity. This feature is perfect for bringing back souvenirs or accommodating last-minute items. It offers flexibility without compromising on size restrictions, making it an ideal choice for frequent travelers.



### Are Hardshell Suitcases Better Than Softside Ones?



Hardshell suitcases offer enhanced protection for fragile items and are generally more water-resistant. They are often scratch-resistant and maintain their shape. Softside suitcases, however, are more flexible and can be easier to fit into tight spaces. The choice depends on individual travel needs.



### How Important Are Tsa Locks On Carry-ons?



TSA locks provide security while allowing TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. They are crucial for protecting valuables and ensuring peace of mind. When choosing a carry-on, opt for one with a built-in TSA lock for added security during travel.



### What Should I Consider When Choosing Carry-on Wheels?



Choose carry-ons with multi-directional spinner wheels for easy maneuverability. These wheels allow smooth, 360-degree movement, reducing strain on your arms. Consider the wheel material and durability, as these factors impact the luggage's longevity and performance on different surfaces.
