RFID blocking technology prevents unauthorized scanning of your cards' information. It creates a barrier that blocks electromagnetic signals. This protects your personal data from electronic pickpocketing. Wallets like the HERO Neck Wallet and DAITET Money Belt include this feature for enhanced travel security.

Neck wallets, like the Lewis N. Clark RFID Blocking Stash, provide easy access and concealment. They are worn around your neck, making it difficult for pickpockets to reach. These wallets are also lightweight and compact, offering convenience without sacrificing security.
