## Southwest Baggage Policies

Understanding Southwest's baggage policies can save you a lot of stress at the airport. Knowing what you can bring, how much it costs, and the timing for checking your bags helps you prepare better. Let’s break down the essentials so you won’t be caught off guard during your next Southwest flight.

### Checked Bag Allowance

Southwest allows you to check up to two bags for free on most fares. Each bag must not exceed 50 pounds in weight and 62 inches in total size (length + width + height). If your bags are bigger or heavier, additional fees will apply.

Remember, this generous policy means you don’t need to worry about paying for your first two checked bags, unlike many other airlines. Have you ever packed last minute and been surprised by extra bag fees? Southwest’s approach can be a real relief.

### Carry-on Bag Rules

You can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item on board without any charge. The carry-on should fit in the overhead bin, while your personal item must fit under the seat in front of you. This setup makes it easy to keep your essentials close during the flight.

Make sure your carry-on meets the size limits: 10 x 16 x 24 inches for the carry-on and smaller for the personal item. Thinking about what to pack in your personal item? It’s the perfect spot for valuables and items you’ll need mid-flight.

### Fees And Charges

Southwest is known for having fewer fees compared to other airlines. However, if you check more than two bags or have overweight or oversized luggage, expect to pay extra. The fees vary but typically start around $75 for the third bag and go up for weight or size violations.

Want to avoid surprise fees? Weigh your bags at home and check Southwest’s latest fee schedule before you leave. This way, you can adjust your packing or budget accordingly and keep your trip smooth and affordable.

### Online Check-in Cutoff

Southwest allows online check-in up to 24 hours before your flight's scheduled departure. This is the best way to secure a good boarding position and avoid long lines. However, you cannot check in online after this 24-hour window closes.

Have you ever missed out on a preferred boarding group just because you checked in late online? That’s a common scenario. Setting a reminder to check in exactly 24 hours before can make a big difference.

### Airport Check-in Times

At the airport, Southwest recommends arriving at least 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international ones. The airline stops accepting checked bags 45 minutes before departure. If you miss this deadline, you might have to carry your bags on board or risk missing your flight.

Imagine rushing through security only to find out you can’t check your bag anymore. It’s frustrating and completely avoidable if you plan ahead. Arriving early gives you time to handle unexpected delays.

### Mobile App Check-in

The Southwest mobile app offers a convenient way to check in starting 24 hours before your flight. The app allows you to manage your boarding pass and even add bags without standing in line. However, just like online check-in, the mobile app closes 1 hour before departure.

Southwest has clear guidelines on when to drop off your bags. These timelines depend on airport rules and flight type. Following these rules helps prevent delays and missed flights.

### Recommended Bag Drop Time

Southwest suggests dropping bags at least 45 minutes before domestic flights. For international flights, aim for 60 minutes early. Early bag drop helps avoid long lines and last-minute rushes. It also allows extra time for security checks.

### Last Chance For Bag Drop

The final bag drop usually closes 30 minutes before departure. After this time, Southwest will not accept checked bags. Missing this deadline means carrying your bags onto the plane or leaving them behind. Airport staff strictly enforce this cutoff to keep flights on schedule.

### Impact Of Flight Type On Bag Drop

Flight type affects bag drop times significantly. Domestic flights have shorter timelines than international trips. International flights require more time due to customs and extra security. Southwest advises checking your specific flight details for exact times. Planning ahead based on flight type reduces travel stress.
