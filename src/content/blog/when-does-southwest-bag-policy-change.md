## Southwest's Current Bag Policy

Understanding Southwest's current bag policy can save you time and money on your next trip. Southwest Airlines is well-known for its customer-friendly approach, especially when it comes to baggage. Knowing the details of their allowance and fees helps you pack smarter and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Carry-on Allowance

Southwest allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger at no extra charge. Your carry-on can be a small suitcase or a backpack, as long as it fits in the overhead bin. The personal item should fit under the seat in front of you, like a purse or laptop bag.

One tip is to measure your bags before you fly. I once tried to bring a slightly oversized backpack as a carry-on and had to check it last minute, which added an unexpected fee. Don’t let this happen to you—stick to the size limits to breeze through boarding.

### Checked Bag Limits

Southwest stands out by allowing two free checked bags per ticketed passenger. Each checked bag must not exceed 50 pounds in weight and 62 inches in combined length, width, and height. This generous limit means you can pack more without worrying about extra charges.

Think about your last trip: did you have to leave things behind or pay extra for baggage? With Southwest, you often don’t need to make that tough decision. But if you’re traveling with a lot of gear, keep the weight and size restrictions in mind to avoid fees.

### Fees And Charges

While Southwest offers two free checked bags, fees apply if you exceed the allowance or if your bags are overweight or oversized. For example, a third checked bag comes with a fee, and overweight bags between 51 and 100 pounds have an additional charge.
