**Choosing the best backpack for thru hiking makes your journey easier and more enjoyable. The right pack fits well, carries your gear comfortably, and lasts through tough trails.**



Thru hiking demands a backpack that balances weight, space, and durability. You need a pack that holds essentials like water, food, clothes, and gear without feeling heavy. Features like waterproof fabric, hydration compatibility, and adjustable straps improve comfort and convenience.



Different hikers prefer different sizes—from small daypacks like the SKYSPER 15L to large multiday packs like the Kelty Glendale 85L. Some backpacks, such as the Maelstrom 40L and Mountainsmith Zerk 40L, offer lightweight designs with rain covers for added protection. This guide covers top backpacks that suit various hiking styles and needs, helping you pick the perfect companion for your long-distance trek.
