## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Spirit Airlines has a strict policy for carry-on bags. Knowing the rules helps avoid extra fees at the airport. This policy separates personal items from carry-on bags. Each has its own size and weight limits. Understanding these limits saves money and time during your trip.

### Size And Weight Limits

Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin. The maximum size allowed is 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This includes wheels and handles. Spirit does not have a weight limit for carry-on bags. But the bag must be easy to lift into the bin by yourself.

### Personal Item Vs Carry-on Bag

Every passenger gets one free personal item. This item must fit under the seat in front. The size limit is 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Common personal items include backpacks, purses, and laptop bags.

Carry-on bags are larger and stored in the overhead bin. Spirit charges extra for carry-on bags. Personal items do not have a fee if they meet size rules. This difference is important to avoid surprise charges.

### Booking Online

Spirit charges the lowest fees for carry-ons during online booking. You can add a carry-on bag while buying your ticket. The fee usually ranges from $35 to $45. Paying online saves money and time at the airport. It also guarantees your bag allowance in advance.

### At The Airport

Paying for a carry-on bag at the airport is the most costly option. Fees can rise to $65 or more. Spirit charges extra for last-minute bag additions. This fee applies if you decide to bring a carry-on without prior payment. Airport staff will ask you to pay before boarding.

### During Check-in

Checking in online or at a kiosk allows mid-range carry-on fees. The cost is higher than online booking but lower than airport payment. Fees during check-in range from $45 to $55. You can still add a carry-on bag before reaching the gate. This option gives some flexibility while saving money.

Spirit’s carry-on fees are typical of budget airlines, but they can still feel steep if you’re not prepared. For example, Spirit charges around $45-$65 for a standard carry-on bag purchased at the airport, which can quickly add up if you travel last minute.

Other low-cost carriers like Frontier and Allegiant also charge for carry-ons, but their fees vary. Frontier’s carry-on fee ranges from $30 to $60, often increasing closer to departure. Allegiant’s fees tend to be slightly lower, around $25 to $50, depending on timing.

These fees encourage travelers to pack light or pay upfront online, which is usually cheaper. Have you ever been surprised by a last-minute carry-on fee? Planning ahead with low-cost carriers can save you money and stress.

### Traditional Airlines

Traditional airlines generally include at least one carry-on bag for free, which sets them apart from Spirit and other budget carriers. Airlines like American, Delta, and United allow a carry-on and a personal item without extra fees, making them more convenient if you travel with luggage.

However, if you need to check bags, traditional airlines often charge higher fees than Spirit. So while carry-ons may be free, checked luggage can cost $30-$60 per bag, depending on the route and fare class.

Consider your packing habits. Do you prefer carrying everything onboard or checking bags? If you usually travel with just a carry-on, Spirit’s fees might add up, but traditional airlines offer a no-fee carry-on that could be worth the ticket price difference.

If your carry-on bag exceeds Spirit's size limits, you’ll face extra fees. The airline measures your bag at the gate, and if it’s too large, it might need to be checked. This means you could pay a higher checked bag fee, which is often more expensive than the carry-on charge.

Think about your last trip—did you ever try squeezing in an extra item? It’s better to measure your bag beforehand. Avoid surprises by sticking to Spirit’s size rules: 22 x 18 x 10 inches including handles and wheels.

### Are Fees Refundable?

Spirit’s carry-on fees are generally non-refundable. Once you pay, the charge is final, even if you decide not to bring your bag on board. This policy can feel strict, but it encourages you to plan carefully and pay for only what you need.

Have you ever paid for a carry-on and then changed your mind? Unfortunately, you won’t get that money back. So it’s wise to decide ahead whether you really need a carry-on or if a personal item will do.

### How To Pay Carry-on Fees

You can pay Spirit carry-on fees in several ways, and timing makes a big difference. The cheapest option is to pay online during your booking or before online check-in. Paying at the airport gate is the most expensive choice.

* Pay during booking on Spirit’s website or app
* Pay during online check-in (24 hours before flight)
* Pay at the airport kiosk or counter
* Pay at the gate (highest fee)

Planning your payment early can save you a lot. Ask yourself—are you ready to deal with a higher fee just because you waited until the last minute?
