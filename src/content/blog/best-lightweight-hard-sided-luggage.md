## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hard-sided Luggage Lightweight And Durable?



Lightweight hard-sided luggage uses materials like polycarbonate for durability without extra weight. It resists impact, scratches, and protects belongings well. Models like Samsonite Freeform and Amazon Basics offer a strong yet light design ideal for travel convenience and long-term use.



### How Does Expandable Luggage Add Travel Convenience?



Expandable luggage provides up to 15% extra space by unzipping an additional compartment. This feature suits travelers needing flexibility for souvenirs or packing changes. Amazon Basics and LEVEL8 Grace offer expandable options that maximize storage while maintaining a sleek, hard-sided exterior.



### Are Tsa Locks Standard On Best Hard-shell Suitcases?



Yes, most top hard-shell suitcases, including Coolife and Sweetcrispy, come with built-in TSA-approved locks. These locks allow airport security to inspect bags without damage, ensuring safety and compliance with travel regulations.



### Why Choose Multi-directional Spinner Wheels On Luggage?



Four multi-directional spinner wheels enable smooth, 360-degree movement for easy maneuvering. This feature reduces travel strain and helps navigate crowded airports effortlessly. Popular sets like Kenneth Cole REACTION and Hanke 24 Inch feature spinner wheels for traveler comfort.



### What Sizes Are Best For Carry-on Versus Checked Luggage?



Carry-on luggage typically ranges from 20 to 22 inches, fitting airline overhead bins. Checked luggage sizes start at 24 inches and up, ideal for longer trips. Samsonite Freeform (21-inch) fits carry-on rules, while Coolife 3-piece sets cover both carry-on and checked needs.



### How To Choose Luggage Color For Easy Identification?



Bright colors like orange or apricot white improve luggage visibility on carousels. Options like Amazon Basics and Coolife offer vibrant hues that reduce mix-ups and add personal style. Darker colors like navy or black offer classic looks but are less distinctive.
