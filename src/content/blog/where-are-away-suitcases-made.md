## Away Suitcases Brand Background

Away suitcases have gained a solid reputation for blending style, durability, and smart features. Understanding the brand’s background helps you see why they stand out in a crowded luggage market. The story behind Away reflects a focus on solving real traveler problems through thoughtful design.

### Away’s Founding Story

Away was founded in 2015 by two friends who wanted better luggage for their frequent travels. They noticed many suitcases were either too bulky or lacked practical features. This sparked the idea to create a suitcase that was sleek, functional, and affordable.

The founders started by designing a hard-shell suitcase with a built-in charger. This feature alone changed how travelers kept their devices powered on the go. Their approach proved that innovation doesn’t need to be complicated to improve your travel experience.

### Design Philosophy And Innovation

Away focuses on simplicity and usefulness, avoiding unnecessary extras that add weight or cost. Their suitcases often feature:

* Durable polycarbonate shells
* 360-degree spinner wheels for easy movement
* Removable battery packs for charging devices
* Organized interiors with compression systems

These details show a commitment to making travel easier and more comfortable. If you’ve ever struggled with a heavy or clunky suitcase, Away’s design might feel like a breath of fresh air.

### Where Away Suitcases Are Made

You might wonder where such thoughtfully designed suitcases are manufactured. Away products are primarily made in China, leveraging advanced factories that meet strict quality standards. This helps keep the price reasonable without compromising on build quality.

Knowing this can shift your perspective on “made in” labels. It’s less about location and more about the standards a brand enforces. Would you prioritize quality and innovation over the country of manufacture when choosing your next suitcase?
