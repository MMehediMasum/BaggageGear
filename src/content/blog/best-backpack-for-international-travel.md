## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Backpack Suitable For International Travel?



A good travel backpack should be spacious, durable, and comfortable. Look for features like expandable compartments, water resistance, and secure zippers. A TSA-approved design and USB charging ports can add convenience. Ensure it fits carry-on dimensions and has padded straps for comfort during long journeys.



### How Do I Choose The Right Size Backpack?



Consider your travel duration and packing needs. For short trips, a 30-40L backpack is ideal. For longer adventures, a 50L capacity may be more suitable. Ensure it fits airline carry-on regulations if you plan to avoid checked baggage. Always check the dimensions before purchasing.
