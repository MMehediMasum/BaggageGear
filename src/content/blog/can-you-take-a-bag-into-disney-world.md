## Bag Policies At Disney World

Understanding Disney World's bag policies can save you a lot of hassle at the entrance. Knowing what you can bring and how to pack your bag ensures a smooth start to your magical day. Let’s dive into the key points about size limits, restricted items, and the security screening process.

### Size Limits And Restrictions

Disney World allows guests to bring one bag per person, but it must fit within specific dimensions. Your bag cannot be larger than 24” long x 15” wide x 18” high. Oversized bags may be denied entry or require you to check them at Guest Relations.

Many visitors find that a standard backpack or a medium-sized tote works best. Rolling suitcases are generally not allowed inside the parks. Think about what you truly need for the day to avoid carrying extra weight or having to leave items behind.

### Prohibited Items

Disney has a strict list of items you cannot bring into the park. These include weapons of any kind, alcohol, illegal substances, and large coolers. Glass containers and selfie sticks are also banned for safety reasons.

For families with little ones, note that strollers are allowed but must meet size guidelines. Frozen food or ice packs are permitted only if they are completely frozen solid when entering. Have you ever packed snacks only to be asked to discard them? Being aware of these rules can prevent such disappointments.

### Security Screening Process

Every guest and their bags go through a security screening before entering. This includes bag checks and metal detectors. Security staff are thorough but friendly, aiming to keep everyone safe without causing delays.

Arriving early can help you avoid long lines at the screening points. Keep your bag organized for a quicker inspection—having pockets and compartments easily accessible speeds up the process. Remember, a little preparation here means more time enjoying rides and attractions.

## Best Bags For Disney World

Choosing the right bag for Disney World can make your day smoother and more enjoyable. You need something comfortable, easy to carry, and with enough space for essentials like snacks, water, and souvenirs. Let’s look at some of the best bag types to help you decide what fits your style and needs best.

### Backpacks Vs. Tote Bags

Backpacks offer hands-free convenience and distribute weight evenly, which is great for a long day of walking. They usually have multiple compartments, so organizing your items like sunscreen, maps, and chargers is easier. On the downside, they can feel bulky in crowded spaces or on rides.

Tote bags are stylish and easy to access, especially if you want to grab something quickly without taking the bag off. However, they often lack structure and can become uncomfortable if overloaded. Think about how much you plan to carry—if you’re packing light, a tote might work well.

### Crossbody Bags And Fanny Packs

Crossbody bags keep your belongings secure and close to your body, reducing the risk of losing anything. They’re perfect if you want something small but still roomy enough for essentials like your phone, wallet, and park tickets. Fanny packs, or waist bags, provide even easier access and keep your hands completely free.

Both options are great for quick movement through crowds and can be more comfortable in hot weather. Would you prefer to have your hands free for photos and snacks? If yes, these might be your best bet.

### Water-resistant Options

Florida weather is unpredictable, and a sudden rain shower can soak your belongings. Water-resistant bags protect your stuff from moisture and spills, which means less worry about your phone or important documents getting wet. Materials like nylon or coated canvas are excellent choices.

Some water-resistant bags also dry quickly, so you won’t be carrying a soggy bag all day. If you plan to enjoy water rides, consider a bag that can handle splashes without soaking through. How often have you had to dig through a wet bag at the end of the day?

Families visiting Disney World have unique needs when bringing bags into the park. Planning ahead helps keep everyone happy and stress-free. Parents must balance packing essentials with park rules. ### Packing For Kids

Pack light but include key items for comfort and fun. Bring snacks, water bottles, sunscreen, and hats. Include extra clothes for spills or weather changes. Small toys or books keep kids busy during waits. Use clear bags to speed up security checks. Keep medicines and wipes in an easy-to-reach spot.

### Managing Multiple Bags

Use backpacks or small totes for each child. Label bags with names or colors for quick ID. Distribute items evenly to avoid heavy loads. Consider a single large bag for shared items. Keep valuables and tickets in one parent’s bag. Use bag clips or carabiners to attach bags together.

### Stroller Storage Guidelines

Disney allows most strollers but limits size to 31” wide and 52” long. Use stroller pockets for small bags and essentials. Park strollers in designated areas during rides. Avoid leaving valuable items in the stroller. Fold strollers during security checks to ease entry. Consider a stroller tag for easy identification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Bring A Backpack Into Disney World?

Yes, you can bring a backpack into Disney World. It must meet size restrictions and pass security checks at entry points.

### What Items Are Prohibited In Disney World Bags?

Prohibited items include weapons, alcohol, glass containers, drones, and large tripods. Check Disney’s official list for full details before visiting.

### Are There Size Limits For Bags At Disney World?

Yes, bags must be no larger than 24" x 15" x 18". Oversized bags may be denied entry or require storage.

### Can I Bring Food And Drinks In My Bag?

You can bring small snacks and non-alcoholic drinks. Large coolers and glass bottles are not allowed inside the park.
