## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make Luggage Scratch-resistant?



Scratch-resistant luggage typically features a hard shell made of durable materials like polycarbonate or ABS. These materials are resilient against abrasions and impacts. Additionally, some luggage comes with textured surfaces or special coatings to minimize visible scratches. This ensures your suitcase remains looking new, even after multiple travels.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Enhance Luggage Mobility?



Spinner wheels provide 360-degree rotation, allowing effortless maneuverability in any direction. This feature reduces strain on your arms and shoulders by making navigation through crowded areas easy. With spinner wheels, you can glide your luggage smoothly beside you, ensuring a stress-free travel experience.



### Why Is Tsa Lock Important For Checked Luggage?



A TSA lock allows airport security to inspect your luggage without damaging it. This is crucial for maintaining the security and integrity of your belongings. TSA-approved locks ensure that only authorized personnel can open your suitcase, providing peace of mind during air travel.



### What Are The Benefits Of Expandable Luggage?



Expandable luggage offers additional packing space, accommodating up to 15% more items. This feature is ideal for travelers who shop or carry gifts. It provides flexibility and convenience, ensuring you can pack essentials without worrying about space limitations. Expandability maximizes your luggage's utility without compromising its structure.



### How Does Luggage Weight Impact Travel?



Lightweight luggage is easier to handle and maneuver, reducing fatigue during travel. It allows you to maximize the weight of your packed items without exceeding airline limits. Choosing lightweight options helps avoid additional fees and ensures a comfortable travel experience.



Travel becomes more enjoyable with less physical strain.
