## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hardside Luggage Better For Checked Bags?



Hardside luggage offers superior protection against impacts and rough handling. Its rigid shell prevents damage to contents. It’s also more water-resistant and durable than softside options. These features make it ideal for checked bags that face tough travel conditions.



### Are Expandable Hardside Suitcases Worth Buying?



Yes, expandable hardside suitcases provide extra packing space when needed. They offer flexibility for souvenirs or additional items. Most models expand up to 12-15% more volume. This feature enhances convenience without sacrificing durability or protection during travel.



### How Important Is Tsa-approved Locking On Checked Luggage?



TSA-approved locks allow airport security to inspect bags without damage. They provide peace of mind and comply with travel regulations. Many hardside suitcases come with built-in TSA locks for enhanced security and convenience during checked baggage screening.



### Which Wheel Type Is Best For Hardside Checked Luggage?



Multi-directional spinner wheels are best for hardside checked luggage. They offer smooth, 360-degree movement and better maneuverability. Four or eight spinner wheels reduce strain and improve travel comfort in busy airports and tight spaces.



### How Lightweight Are These Hardside Checked Luggage Options?



Most featured hardside checked luggage weighs between 7 to 12 pounds. Lightweight materials like ABS and polypropylene keep cases easy to handle. This reduces airline weight fees and makes travel less tiring, especially for larger 24 to 30-inch suitcases.
