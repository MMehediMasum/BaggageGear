American Airlines allows carry-on bags with maximum dimensions of 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including handles and wheels. This means your Beis carry-on must fit within these measurements to be accepted without any issues.

From my own experience, even a slight oversize can lead to gate check fees. So, it’s smart to measure your bag carefully before your trip.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike some international carriers, American Airlines does not have a strict weight limit for carry-on bags. However, you should be able to lift and store your bag in the overhead bin without assistance.

If your Beis carry-on is packed too heavy, it may be cumbersome to handle, especially during busy boarding times. Ask yourself: can you comfortably lift your bag above your head? If not, consider lightening your load.

### Additional Personal Items

Besides your carry-on, American Airlines allows one additional personal item such as a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. This item must fit under the seat in front of you.

Many travelers overlook this and try to pack everything in one bag. Having a separate personal item can give you more flexibility and easy access to essentials during the flight.
