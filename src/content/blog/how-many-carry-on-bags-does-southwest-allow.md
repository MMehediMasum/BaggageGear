## Southwest Carry-on Allowance

Southwest Airlines offers a clear carry-on allowance for travelers. Knowing these rules helps avoid surprises at the airport. It also ensures a smooth boarding experience. This guide explains Southwest’s carry-on bag policies in simple terms.

### Carry-on Bag Size Limits

Southwest allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The bag must not exceed 10 x 16 x 24 inches. This size includes handles and wheels. Bags larger than this may need to be checked.

### Personal Item Dimensions

In addition to the carry-on bag, passengers can bring one personal item. This item should fit under the seat in front. The recommended size is 18.5 x 8.5 x 13.5 inches. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.

### Number Of Allowed Bags

* One carry-on bag
* One personal item

Both items are free of charge. Keep in mind, these items must fit size limits. Exceeding these limits may result in extra fees or checking the bag.

## Tips For Packing Carry-on Bags

Packing your carry-on bag smartly can make your Southwest flight much more comfortable and hassle-free. You want to fit everything you need without exceeding the airline’s size and quantity limits. The trick lies in organizing efficiently and choosing essentials wisely.

### Maximizing Space

Use packing cubes or compression bags to squeeze more into your carry-on without creating a mess. Rolling clothes instead of folding them saves space and reduces wrinkles. Place heavier items at the bottom and fill gaps with smaller things like socks or chargers.

Have you ever struggled to close your carry-on last minute? A personal trick is to wear your bulkiest shoes or jacket on the plane to free up room inside the bag. Also, keep liquids in clear, resealable bags for easy access during security checks.

### Essential Items To Pack

Focus on what you truly need during the flight and at your destination. Always pack your travel documents, a phone charger, and a small toiletry kit. A lightweight jacket or scarf can be a lifesaver if the cabin gets cold.

Think about comfort and convenience: noise-canceling headphones, a neck pillow, and snacks can improve your travel experience. Don’t forget a reusable water bottle—Southwest allows empty bottles through security, so you can fill up after the checkpoint.
