## Amtrak Luggage Policy

Understanding Amtrak's luggage policy is crucial before you pack for your train journey. It affects what you can bring on board and how you prepare your bags. This can save you from last-minute hassles and extra fees, making your trip smoother.

### Carry-on Allowance

You can bring two carry-on items on Amtrak trains for free. Each item should not weigh more than 50 pounds (23 kg). The size limit is 28 x 22 x 14 inches (71 x 56 x 36 cm), which fits most standard suitcases or backpacks.

In addition to the two items, you can also carry one personal item like a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. Keep these items within easy reach since you’ll store them in overhead racks or under your seat.

### Checked Baggage Rules

Checked baggage service is available on many long-distance Amtrak routes, but not all. It’s important to check if your route allows checked bags before you travel. You can check up to two bags per passenger, each weighing no more than 50 pounds.

Checked bags must be tagged and dropped off at the station before boarding. You can’t carry liquids or fragile items in checked baggage, so keep those in your carry-on. Remember, some stations don’t have checked baggage facilities, so plan accordingly.

### Special Items And Restrictions

Amtrak has specific rules for special items like bicycles, musical instruments, and pets. For example, bicycles are allowed on certain trains but require a reservation and sometimes a fee. Musical instruments up to a certain size can be carried on board, but larger cases may need to be checked.

Hazardous materials, weapons, and large sports equipment like surfboards have restrictions or may be prohibited. Always check the latest Amtrak guidelines to avoid surprises. ## Packing Tips For Amtrak Trips

Packing for an Amtrak trip can be a bit different than preparing for a plane flight or a road trip. You want to balance comfort, convenience, and the specific luggage rules Amtrak has. Knowing what to bring and how to pack it ensures your journey is smooth and stress-free.

### Choosing The Right Luggage

Think about your travel style and how much space you’ll need on the train. Soft-sided bags are often easier to fit in overhead racks or under seats compared to hard-shell suitcases.

Carry-ons with wheels help navigate stations and platforms easily. If you plan to store your luggage in the baggage car, larger suitcases are fine, but remember you’ll need to check them in advance.

### Weight And Size Limits

Amtrak allows each passenger to bring up to two carry-on bags, each weighing no more than 50 pounds and measuring up to 28 x 22 x 14 inches. Checked baggage has similar weight limits but must be checked at least 45 minutes before departure.

Exceeding these limits can mean extra fees or having to repack at the station. Have you ever tried lifting a heavy bag onto a high overhead rack? Keeping your luggage manageable saves you from that hassle.

### Essentials To Pack

Pack items you’ll need during the trip in a small daypack or backpack. This can include snacks, a refillable water bottle, your phone charger, a book, and a travel pillow.

Think about the length of your trip and the amenities available onboard. For longer journeys, bring comfortable clothes, toiletries, and any medications you might need.

## Luggage Handling On Board

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=traveling-with-luggage-on-amtrak-is-designed-to-be-simple-and-convenient.-understanding-how-luggage-is-handled-on-board-helps-you-prepare-better-for-your-trip.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Traveling with luggage on Amtrak is designed to be simple and convenient. Understanding how luggage is handled on board helps you prepare better for your trip. Amtrak offers clear options for storing your bags, keeping them secure, and accessing them during your journey. This makes your ride comfortable and stress-free.

### Storage Options

Amtrak provides several places to store luggage. Large suitcases go in the baggage car. You must check these bags before boarding. Smaller bags and carry-ons fit in overhead racks above your seat. Some trains have storage areas near the entrances. Keep your important items close by at your seat.

### Security Measures

Your luggage is safe while traveling on Amtrak. Checked bags are locked in the baggage car. Amtrak staff monitors these areas regularly. Carry-on bags stay with you, reducing risk of loss. Always keep your bag locked or zipped. Label your bags with your name and contact info.

### Accessing Your Bags During Travel

Access to checked luggage is not allowed during the trip. You get your checked bags at the end of the journey. Carry-on bags remain with you, so you can reach them anytime. Keep essential items like snacks, medication, and electronics in your carry-on. This ensures you have what you need during travel.




## Booking With Extra Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=booking-your-amtrak-trip-with-extra-luggage-requires-some-planning.-you-need-to-know-the-rules-and-options-so-your-bags-travel-smoothly-with-you.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Booking your Amtrak trip with extra luggage requires some planning. You need to know the rules and options so your bags travel smoothly with you. Understanding how to handle additional luggage can save you stress and unexpected fees.

### Additional Fees

Amtrak allows two carry-on bags and two checked bags for free, but what if you have more? Extra luggage usually comes with fees that vary by route and bag size. It's important to check the latest fee schedule on Amtrak’s website before booking.

For example, if you bring a third checked bag, you might pay around $20 to $25 per bag. Oversized or heavy items often have higher fees, sometimes doubling the cost. Knowing this upfront helps you budget better for your trip.

### How To Reserve Extra Space

You can’t just show up with extra bags expecting space. Amtrak requires you to reserve additional luggage space in advance, especially for checked bags. This can be done online during ticket booking or by contacting customer service.

Booking extra space early improves your chances of securing room for all your bags. It also prevents last-minute hassles at the station. Have you tried booking extra luggage and found the process straightforward or tricky?

### Alternatives For Oversized Items

If your luggage is too big or heavy for Amtrak’s limits, consider alternative shipping services. Companies like FedEx, UPS, or even specialized baggage shippers can deliver your oversized items to your destination.

This can be cheaper and less stressful than paying Amtrak’s heavy or oversized baggage fees. Another option is to split your belongings into smaller bags if possible. Think about your options before packing to avoid surprises at check-in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Bring Carry-on Luggage On Amtrak Trains?

Yes, Amtrak allows carry-on luggage with size limits of 28 x 22 x 14 inches. Each passenger can bring two carry-on bags free of charge.

### Are There Weight Limits For Amtrak Checked Baggage?

Checked bags must weigh under 50 pounds each. Overweight fees may apply, so pack accordingly to avoid extra charges.

### How Many Bags Can I Check On Amtrak?

Passengers can check up to two bags per ticket. Additional bags may incur fees and require advance notice.

### Is There A Fee For Bringing Luggage On Amtrak?

No fees are charged for standard carry-on or checked bags within Amtrak’s size and weight limits.
