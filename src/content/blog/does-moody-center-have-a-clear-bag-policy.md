## Moody Center's Bag Policy Basics

The Moody Center has a clear bag policy to speed up entry and keep everyone safe. Visitors must follow specific rules about what bags they can bring inside. Knowing these basics helps avoid delays and ensures a smooth visit. The policy focuses on bag size, type, and exceptions for special needs. Understanding these points makes attending events easier and more enjoyable.

### Types Of Bags Allowed

Only clear bags are allowed at Moody Center. These bags must be see-through and made of plastic, vinyl, or PVC. Small clutches without a strap are allowed if they are under a certain size. Bags that are not clear, like backpacks or large purses, are not permitted. This rule helps security quickly check bags at the entrance.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must not be larger than 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. Clutches without straps can be up to 4.5 inches by 6.5 inches. These limits keep lines moving and make security checks faster. Oversized bags will need to be returned to the car or left outside. Visitors should measure their bags before arriving.

### Exceptions To The Policy

Some exceptions exist for medical or diaper bags. Visitors with special needs can bring larger bags with proper identification. These exceptions require a separate inspection at the gate. Food containers or cameras may also have special rules. It is best to check Moody Center’s website before attending an event.
