### What Is Suitcase Piercing

Suitcase piercing is a piercing that goes through the cartilage of your ear, specifically the flat part called the tragus or the area nearby. Unlike common lobe piercings, this one passes through a thicker part of your ear, making it more noticeable and edgy.

This piercing is often chosen by people who want something different but subtle enough to wear daily. It can hold unique jewelry, from hoops to studs, letting you express your style with ease.

### Origin Of The Trend

The suitcase piercing trend gained popularity on social media platforms where influencers share their bold ear styles. It’s thought to have emerged in the late 2010s as part of a broader interest in unconventional ear piercings.

Many who first tried it were looking for a fresh way to stand out without going for extreme facial piercings. The name "suitcase" might come from the piercing’s resemblance to a handle or a small latch, adding a quirky touch to your ear’s look.

### Unique Placement

This piercing’s placement sets it apart from others. It’s positioned through the ear’s cartilage in a spot that’s not easily pierced, requiring precision and care.

The location means healing can take longer than a simple lobe piercing, so you’ll need to follow aftercare instructions carefully to avoid infection. The unique placement also means your jewelry choice can dramatically change how the piercing looks, allowing for creative expression.

## Suitcase Piercing Process

The suitcase piercing process involves careful steps to ensure safety and comfort. This unique piercing requires precision and proper hygiene. Understanding each phase helps prepare you mentally and physically. The process includes preparation, the actual piercing, and aftercare. Each step plays a key role in healing and appearance.

### Preparation Steps

* Choose a professional piercer with good reviews.
* Clean the area with an antiseptic solution.
* Discuss any allergies or health concerns with the piercer.
* Ensure the piercing studio uses sterile tools.
* Avoid alcohol or blood thinners before the appointment.
* Stay hydrated and eat a light meal.

### Piercing Procedure

The piercer marks the exact spot for the suitcase piercing. They use a sterilized needle to create a small hole. The needle passes smoothly through the skin or cartilage. Jewelry is inserted carefully into the new piercing. The piercer checks the fit and comfort of the jewelry. The process usually takes less than 10 minutes.

### Aftercare Tips

* Clean the piercing twice daily with saline solution.
* Avoid touching the piercing with dirty hands.
* Do not remove or change jewelry before healing.
* Avoid swimming pools and hot tubs during healing.
* Watch for signs of infection like redness or swelling.
* Follow your piercer’s advice for the best healing.

## Risks And Considerations

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-suitcase-piercing-is-a-unique-ear-piercing-that-requires-careful-thought.-understanding-risks-and-considerations-helps-you-make-a-safe-choice.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A suitcase piercing is a unique ear piercing that requires careful thought. Understanding risks and considerations helps you make a safe choice. This piercing goes through the ear's cartilage, which can be sensitive. Proper care and knowledge reduce problems during healing. Here are important points to keep in mind.

### Potential Complications

* Infection is common if the piercing is not clean.
* Swelling and redness may last several days.
* Allergic reactions to metal jewelry can occur.
* Cartilage damage might cause pain or scarring.
* Migration or rejection of the piercing is possible.

### Healing Time

Healing takes longer for cartilage piercings like the suitcase piercing. Expect about 3 to 6 months for full healing.

During this time:

* Keep the area clean and dry.
* Avoid touching or twisting the jewelry.
* Watch for signs of infection.

### Choosing A Professional

Select a piercer with experience in cartilage piercings. Check for cleanliness and proper tools. A skilled piercer reduces risks and ensures correct placement.

Ask about:

* Sterilization procedures.
* Jewelry quality and material.
* Aftercare instructions.

Suitcase piercing stands out among other piercings due to its unique style and placement. It offers a distinct look that differs from more common ear piercings. Comparing suitcase piercing with other types helps understand its special features.

### Differences In Placement

Suitcase piercing goes through the ear cartilage horizontally near the helix. Other piercings like the helix or tragus are vertical or at the edge of the ear. This horizontal placement creates a bold, eye-catching effect.

### Pain Levels

Pain varies by piercing location and individual tolerance. Suitcase piercing may feel more intense because it passes through thick cartilage. Earlobe piercings usually cause less pain due to softer tissue.

### Maintenance Requirements

Suitcase piercing needs careful cleaning to prevent infection. Healing takes several months with regular saline rinses. Other piercings might heal faster but still require daily care to stay healthy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is A Suitcase Piercing Exactly?

A suitcase piercing is a unique ear cartilage piercing. It involves two holes on the outer edge of the ear. Both holes are connected by a single barbell. This piercing resembles a suitcase handle, hence the name.

### How Painful Is A Suitcase Piercing?

Pain levels vary by person but generally, suitcase piercings cause moderate pain. The cartilage area is sensitive, so expect some discomfort during piercing. Healing time and aftercare also influence pain duration.

### How Long Does A Suitcase Piercing Take To Heal?

Suitcase piercings typically take 6 to 12 months to fully heal. Cartilage piercings heal slower than earlobe piercings. Proper cleaning and care are essential to avoid infections and speed up healing.

### Can I Swim After Getting A Suitcase Piercing?

It's best to avoid swimming for at least 2 weeks after piercing. Pools and natural water bodies contain bacteria that can cause infections. Always consult your piercer for personalized aftercare advice.
