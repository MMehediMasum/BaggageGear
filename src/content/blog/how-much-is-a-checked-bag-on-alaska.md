## Alaska Airlines Baggage Policy

Alaska Airlines has clear rules for baggage. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees and delays. You can carry bags on the plane or check them in. Each type of bag has size and weight limits. Follow these rules to make your trip smooth.

### Standard Checked Bag Allowance

Alaska Airlines allows one checked bag for free on most flights. The first bag usually costs $30 for many routes. The second checked bag costs more, around $40. Some tickets or memberships may include free checked bags. Always check your ticket for specific details.

### Weight And Size Limits

Each checked bag must weigh less than 50 pounds (23 kg). The total size of the bag should not exceed 62 inches (157 cm). Size means the sum of length, width, and height. Bags over these limits may have extra fees. Heavy or large bags need extra payment at the airport.

### Carry-on Bag Rules

Alaska Airlines allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. The carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. It can be no larger than 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm). Personal items should fit under the seat in front of you. Examples include a purse, backpack, or laptop bag.

## Checked Bag Fees Breakdown

Understanding Alaska Airlines' checked bag fees helps you plan your trip budget. Fees depend on the number of bags and your ticket type. Knowing these charges avoids surprises at the airport.

### First Bag Fee

The first checked bag on Alaska Airlines usually costs $30. This fee applies to most economy tickets. Some fare classes and loyalty members may get this bag free. Bags must weigh under 50 pounds and meet size limits.

### Second Bag Fee

The second checked bag fee is higher, typically $40. This charge also applies to economy passengers. Just like the first bag, size and weight rules apply. Paying this fee lets you bring more luggage without hassle.

### Additional Bags Charges

For a third bag or more, the fee rises to $100 per bag. Oversized or overweight bags may have extra charges. Always check Alaska Airlines’ guidelines for exact fees. Planning ahead saves money and stress at check-in.
