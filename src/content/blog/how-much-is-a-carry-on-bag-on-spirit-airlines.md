## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Fees

Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares, but they often charge extra fees for carry-on bags. Understanding these fees can help you avoid surprises at the airport and save money. Let's break down exactly what you might pay depending on when and where you book your carry-on.

### Fee Breakdown By Booking Time

The cost of a carry-on bag on Spirit Airlines changes depending on when you add it to your booking. If you pay for your carry-on during the initial booking, the fee is usually the lowest.

Adding a carry-on after booking but before check-in costs more. Waiting until you get to the airport to pay can almost double the fee compared to booking early.

For example, booking your carry-on at the time of ticket purchase might cost around $35, but paying at the gate can be as high as $65. Have you ever waited until the last minute only to regret it?

### Online Vs Airport Charges

Paying for your carry-on online is much cheaper and quicker. Spirit encourages travelers to add bags through their website or app before arriving at the airport.

At the airport, fees are higher and you might face longer lines just to pay. The difference can be significant—sometimes $20 or more extra.

Planning ahead saves not only money but also stress. Wouldn’t you prefer to breeze through the airport rather than stand in line to pay unexpected fees?

### Carry-on Fee Exceptions

Not every carry-on bag requires a fee. Spirit Airlines allows certain personal items to fly free, such as a small backpack or purse that fits under the seat.

Also, active-duty military members traveling on orders may be exempt from carry-on fees. Children under two years old traveling on a lap usually don’t have to pay either.

If your bag fits Spirit's personal item size restrictions, you might avoid fees altogether. Have you checked your bag’s size before packing?

## Carry-on Size And Weight Limits

Understanding Spirit Airlines' carry-on size and weight limits can save you from unexpected fees and hassle at the airport. Knowing exactly what you can bring onboard helps you pack smarter and avoid last-minute repacking. Let’s break down the rules so you can travel confidently without surprises.

### Maximum Dimensions Allowed

Spirit Airlines allows a carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of **22 x 18 x 10 inches**(including handles and wheels). This size fits in the overhead bin, which is essential for most travelers.

If your bag exceeds these dimensions, you will likely be charged a fee or asked to check it. I once tried bringing a slightly bigger bag and ended up paying an extra $65 at the gate—definitely not worth it!

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike many airlines, Spirit does not specify a strict weight limit for carry-on bags. However, your bag must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead bin by yourself.

This means you should keep your carry-on manageable—aim for under 40 pounds to avoid struggling or drawing extra attention from gate agents. Have you ever had to awkwardly heave a heavy bag overhead? Avoid that stress by packing smart and light.

### Personal Item Rules

In addition to your carry-on, Spirit allows one personal item like a backpack, purse, or laptop bag. The personal item must fit under the seat in front of you with maximum dimensions of **18 x 14 x 8 inches**.

Even if you have a carry-on, your personal item is complimentary and can hold essentials like electronics, documents, or snacks. Remember, keeping your personal item compact helps you stay organized and keeps your hands free during boarding.
