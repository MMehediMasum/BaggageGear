## What Is A Colostomy Bag?

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=a-colostomy-bag-is-a-medical-device-used-by-people-who-have-had-surgery-on-their-colon-or-large-intestine.-it-collects-waste-from-the-body-after-the-normal-route-through-the-rectum-is-not-possible.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

A colostomy bag is a medical device used by people who have had surgery on their colon or large intestine. It collects waste from the body after the normal route through the rectum is not possible. This device helps maintain hygiene and allows people to continue their daily activities with confidence.

Understanding what a colostomy bag is can clear up many common questions and concerns. It is important to learn how it works and why some people need it.

A colostomy bag is a pouch attached to the abdomen. It collects stool from the body through an opening called a stoma. The stoma is created during surgery and is connected to the colon.

The bag sticks to the skin using a special adhesive. It is designed to be leak-proof and odor-resistant. People can empty or change the bag as needed.

### Why Do People Need A Colostomy Bag?

Some health conditions require part of the colon to be removed or bypassed. This can happen due to diseases like cancer, injury, or infections. A colostomy bag allows waste to leave the body safely when the colon cannot function normally.

### How Does A Colostomy Bag Work?

* Waste exits the body through the stoma.
* The bag collects waste and prevents spills.
* Users change or empty the bag regularly.
* The bag protects the skin from irritation.
