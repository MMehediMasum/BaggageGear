## Booking Your Flight

Booking your flight with American Airlines sets the stage for a smooth travel experience. Knowing how to select the right fare and understanding baggage rules will help you avoid surprises at the airport. Planning ahead saves time and money.

### Selecting Fare Types

American Airlines offers several fare types, each with different baggage policies. Basic Economy fares usually do not include a free checked bag. Main Cabin and higher fares often include at least one checked bag without extra fees. Choosing the right fare depends on your travel needs and budget.

### Baggage Allowance Basics

Standard baggage allowance varies by route and ticket type. Most domestic flights allow one checked bag for free with Main Cabin fares. Oversized or additional bags incur extra charges. It helps to review baggage rules before booking to avoid unexpected fees.




## Adding Checked Bags Online

Adding a checked bag online saves time at the airport. It lets you pay for baggage before your flight. This process is simple and fast. You can add bags using the American Airlines website or mobile app. Both options guide you step-by-step.

### Using The American Airlines Website

Start by visiting the official American Airlines website. Log in with your booking reference and last name. Find your flight details in the “Manage Trips” section. Click the option to add checked bags.

The website shows baggage fees and limits clearly. Choose the number of bags you want to check. Confirm your selection and proceed to payment. You can pay with a credit or debit card. After payment, you receive a confirmation email.

Print your receipt or save it on your phone. This helps during check-in at the airport. The website is user-friendly and works on all devices.

### Through The Mobile App

Open the American Airlines app on your smartphone. Sign in using your booking information. Tap “Manage Trips” to find your flight. Select the option to add checked bags.

The app displays baggage fees and rules clearly. Choose how many bags to check and confirm your choice. Use the app to pay securely with your card.

You get an instant confirmation and receipt in the app. Save it or screenshot for easy access. The app is fast and works well on both Android and iOS devices.

## Baggage Fees And Policies

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-american-airlines'-baggage-fees-and-policies-helps-avoid-surprises-at-the-airport.-knowing-the-rules-lets-travelers-plan-their-packing-and-budget-wisely.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding American Airlines' baggage fees and policies helps avoid surprises at the airport. Knowing the rules lets travelers plan their packing and budget wisely. This section explains the fee structure, weight and size limits, and charges for extra baggage.

### Fee Structure

American Airlines charges fees for checked bags based on the flight route and ticket class. Domestic flights usually have a standard fee for the first and second checked bags. Some fare types include one or more free checked bags. Payment can be made online, during check-in, or at the airport.

* First checked bag fee typically ranges from $30 to $35.
* Second checked bag fee is usually higher, around $40 to $45.
* Some premium tickets may allow free checked bags.

### Weight And Size Limits

Each checked bag must meet specific weight and size limits. Overweight or oversized bags face additional charges. The standard weight limit is 50 pounds per bag. The maximum size allowed is 62 inches in total dimensions (length + width + height).

* Bags over 50 pounds but under 70 pounds have extra fees.
* Bags exceeding 70 pounds are not accepted as checked baggage.
* Bags larger than 62 inches may be considered oversized.

### Excess Baggage Charges

Charges apply for bags beyond the allowed number or those overweight and oversized. Excess baggage fees can add up quickly. Paying for extra bags in advance often costs less than at the airport.

| Type | Fee Range |
| --- | --- |
| Third checked bag and beyond | $150 - $200 per bag |
| Overweight bag (51-70 lbs) | $75 - $100 |
| Oversized bag (over 62 inches) | $100 - $200 |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Add A Checked Bag On American Airlines?

You can add a checked bag during online booking or manage your reservation later. Visit American Airlines’ website, log in, and select “Add Bags” to pay and confirm.

### What Is The Cost To Add A Checked Bag On American Airlines?

The first checked bag usually costs $30, and the second $40 on domestic flights. Fees may vary for international flights or elite status members.

### Can I Add A Checked Bag After Check-in On American Airlines?

Yes, you can add a checked bag after check-in by visiting the airport counter or kiosk, but fees remain the same.

### Are There Weight Limits For Checked Bags On American Airlines?

Yes, each checked bag must not exceed 50 pounds. Overweight bags incur additional fees, so check the limits before packing.
