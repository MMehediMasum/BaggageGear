## Luggage Policies On Shinkansen

Understanding the luggage policies on Shinkansen trains is essential to ensure a smooth and stress-free journey. These rules are designed to keep the aisles clear and provide enough space for everyone’s belongings. Knowing what you can bring, where to store it, and any extra costs will help you avoid surprises.

### Size And Weight Limits

Each passenger is allowed to bring luggage that fits within specific size and weight limits. The maximum size for carry-on bags is typically around 250 cm in total dimensions (length + width + height) and up to 30 kg in weight.

If your bag is larger or heavier, it may not fit in the overhead racks or under the seat. Have you checked your suitcase dimensions recently? Measuring your luggage before packing can save you from last-minute hassles.

### Reserved Large Luggage Spaces

For bigger bags, Shinkansen offers reserved spaces at the end of certain cars. These areas can hold luggage that exceeds the standard size limits but still fits within the allowed maximum dimensions (usually up to 160 cm in one dimension and 250 cm total).

Booking your seat with a large luggage reservation ensures your bag has a safe spot during the trip. This service is especially useful if you’re traveling with sports equipment or multiple large suitcases. Have you ever missed out on these spots by not reserving in advance?

### Additional Fees For Oversized Bags

If your luggage exceeds the reserved space limits, Shinkansen may require an additional fee. This surcharge helps manage space and maintain comfort for all passengers.

Be prepared to pay this fee when buying your ticket or during boarding. It’s a good idea to check the latest fee schedule, as costs can vary by route and season. Would you rather pay a small fee upfront or struggle with your luggage on the platform?




## Main Storage Areas On Shinkansen

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-where-to-store-your-luggage-on-the-shinkansen-can-make-your-journey-much-smoother.-the-train-offers-several-main-storage-areas-designed-to-keep-your-bags-safe-and-accessible.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing where to store your luggage on the Shinkansen can make your journey much smoother. The train offers several main storage areas designed to keep your bags safe and accessible. Understanding these spots helps you plan better, especially during busy travel times.

### Overhead Racks

Overhead racks run above the seats and are perfect for smaller suitcases and backpacks. They’re easy to reach, so you can quickly grab your bag when the train stops. However, if your luggage is large or heavy, lifting it up might be tricky without help.

Think about the size of your bag before relying on this space. If you’ve ever struggled to hoist a bulky suitcase overhead, you know it’s best for lighter items.

### Behind The Last Row Seats

At the end of each car, there’s a dedicated space behind the last row of seats for larger luggage. This area is ideal for big suitcases that won’t fit in the overhead racks. It keeps your bags out of the way but still within sight.

Be aware that these spots fill up fast during peak travel times. Arriving early or choosing a car with available space can save you from a last-minute scramble.

### Under-seat Storage

Some Shinkansen trains offer limited storage under the seats. This area works best for small bags and personal items like purses or laptops. It’s convenient because your belongings stay right next to you throughout the trip.

However, don’t expect to fit large suitcases here. If you’ve ever tried to cram a big bag under a seat, you know it’s usually not possible.

## Maximizing Space For Carry-on Bags

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=traveling-on-the-shinkansen-means-managing-limited-space-for-carry-on-bags.-efficient-packing-helps-keep-your-luggage-organized-and-easy-to-store.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Traveling on the Shinkansen means managing limited space for carry-on bags. Efficient packing helps keep your luggage organized and easy to store. Knowing how to maximize space ensures a smooth journey without hassle.

### Packing Tips For Easy Storage

* Use compact packing cubes to save room.
* Roll clothes tightly to reduce bulk.
* Place heavier items at the bottom of your bag.
* Limit liquids to small, travel-sized containers.
* Keep essentials in a small, accessible pouch.

### Using Soft Bags Vs Hard Cases

Soft bags fit better in tight spaces. They can squeeze into overhead racks or under seats. Soft bags are lighter and easier to handle.

Hard cases protect fragile items well. They keep shape but take more space. Choose hard cases only if necessary for protection.

### Organizing Personal Items

* Keep tickets, passport, and money in an easy-to-reach pocket.
* Use small pouches for chargers and cables.
* Separate clean and dirty clothes in different compartments.
* Keep snacks and water handy but compact.




## Handling Large Or Bulky Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handling-large-or-bulky-luggage-on-the-shinkansen-requires-some-planning.-space-is-limited,-and-not-all-trains-offer-the-same-storage-options.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handling large or bulky luggage on the Shinkansen requires some planning. Space is limited, and not all trains offer the same storage options. Knowing how to manage your luggage can make your trip smoother and more comfortable.

Large suitcases may not fit in the overhead racks. Some trains have designated areas for bigger bags, but these spots are limited. Finding the best way to store your luggage helps avoid stress during your journey.

### Booking Reserved Spaces In Advance

Many Shinkansen trains allow passengers to reserve spaces for large luggage. These spots are near the end of each car. You must book them when buying your ticket. It ensures your bag has a safe place on the train. Without a reservation, you might have to keep your luggage in the aisle or under your seat.

### Using Delivery Services

Delivery services offer a convenient way to manage bulky luggage. You can send your bags ahead to your destination. This service is called “takkyubin” in Japan. It’s reliable and fast. Using delivery reduces the hassle of carrying heavy bags on the train. You travel light and pick up your luggage after arriving.

### Storing Luggage At Stations

Many Shinkansen stations have coin lockers and luggage storage counters. Coin lockers vary in size but can be small for very large bags. Luggage storage counters handle bigger items for a fee. Storing bags at the station lets you explore without your heavy suitcases. Check opening hours and availability before relying on this option.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where Can I Store Large Luggage On Shinkansen?

Large luggage can be stored in designated spaces at the end of each car. Some Shinkansen trains also provide overhead racks and under-seat space for smaller bags. It’s best to arrive early to secure a good spot for your luggage.

### Is There A Luggage Size Limit On Shinkansen Trains?

Yes, luggage must not exceed 250 cm in total dimensions (length + width + height). Oversized bags may require advanced reservation or separate arrangements. Always check the specific train’s policy before traveling to avoid issues.

### Can I Keep My Luggage With Me On Shinkansen Seats?

Small luggage can be kept on overhead racks or under your seat. Large suitcases should be stored in the designated luggage area. Keeping bulky bags on seats is discouraged as it blocks other passengers.

### Are There Luggage Storage Services At Shinkansen Stations?

Yes, many Shinkansen stations offer coin lockers and luggage storage services. These services are ideal if you want to explore without carrying heavy bags. Availability varies by station, so check in advance.
