## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Rules

Spirit Airlines has specific rules for carrying bags onboard. Understanding these rules helps avoid extra fees and delays. Passengers must follow size and weight limits for their carry-ons. The airline also has a clear policy on personal items. Knowing these details ensures a smooth travel experience with Spirit Airlines.

### Allowed Bag Dimensions

Spirit Airlines allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size for this bag is 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Bags larger than this size must be checked in. Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat.

### Weight Restrictions

Spirit Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. However, passengers must be able to lift their bags into the overhead bin. Bags that are too heavy to lift may be checked at the gate. It is best to pack light to avoid any issues.

### Personal Item Policy

Each passenger may bring one personal item free of charge. This item must fit under the seat in front. Common personal items include small backpacks, purses, or laptop bags. The maximum size for personal items is 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Personal items do not count as the carry-on bag.

### Personal Item Fees

Good news: Spirit allows one free personal item, such as a backpack or purse, that fits under the seat in front of you. This means you can bring essentials without extra charges. However, if your personal item is larger or doesn’t fit the size requirements, you might be charged a carry-on fee instead.

Remember, the personal item must fit within 18 x 14 x 8 inches. If it doesn’t, Spirit treats it like a carry-on bag, which means you’ll pay a fee. Have you measured your bag recently to avoid unexpected costs?

### Checked Bag Pricing

Checked baggage on Spirit Airlines comes with fees that vary based on when you purchase and your route. Buying [checked bags](https://baggagegear.com/can-you-bring-any-size-liquid-in-a-checked-bag/) during booking is the cheapest option. Prices can range from $30 to $65 per bag each way.
