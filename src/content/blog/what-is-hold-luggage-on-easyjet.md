## Hold Luggage Basics

Hold luggage is the baggage you check in before your EasyJet flight. It travels in the plane's cargo hold, not with you in the cabin. Understanding hold luggage basics helps avoid extra fees and airport stress.

EasyJet offers clear rules about hold luggage size, weight, and cost. Knowing these rules makes your trip smoother and packing easier.

### What Counts As Hold Luggage

Hold luggage includes any bags you cannot carry on the plane. These are usually bigger and heavier than cabin bags. Examples include:

* Large suitcases
* Sports equipment
* Musical instruments
* Extra bags beyond your cabin allowance

These items must be checked in at the airport counter or online before boarding.

### Easyjet's Luggage Policies

EasyJet sets limits on hold luggage weight and size. Each bag can weigh up to 23kg. Oversized or overweight bags may cost extra.

You can buy hold luggage allowance during booking or later. Prices depend on the route and season. Some fare types include free hold luggage, but most do not.

Keep in mind these rules:

* Maximum bag size: 275cm (length + width + height)
* Weight limit: 23kg per bag
* Extra fees apply for additional or heavy bags

Checking luggage online is usually cheaper than paying at the airport.

Booking hold luggage before your flight date is the best way to save money.

## Luggage Size And Weight Limits

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-easyjet's-luggage-size-and-weight-limits-is-essential-before-you-pack-for-your-trip.-these-limits-help-you-avoid-unexpected-fees-and-ensure-a-smooth-airport-experience.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding EasyJet's luggage size and weight limits is essential before you pack for your trip. These limits help you avoid unexpected fees and ensure a smooth airport experience. Knowing exactly what you can bring in your hold luggage can save you time and stress at the check-in desk.

### Maximum Dimensions

EasyJet allows hold luggage with a maximum size of 275cm when you add together the length, width, and height. This means your suitcase's total dimensions must not exceed this limit.

To visualize, if your bag is 70cm long, 50cm wide, and 55cm high, it perfectly fits the allowed size since 70 + 50 + 55 = 175cm, well within the 275cm limit. Oversized bags could be refused or charged extra, so measuring your luggage beforehand is a smart move.

### Weight Restrictions

Each piece of hold luggage on EasyJet can weigh up to 23kg by default. You can buy additional bags, but each must also follow this weight limit.

Imagine carrying a 25kg suitcase; you would likely face additional charges or be forced to repack at the airport. It’s better to use a luggage scale at home to avoid surprises.

### Excess Baggage Charges

If your bag exceeds size or weight limits, EasyJet applies extra fees. These charges can add up quickly and vary depending on your route and how much over the limit your luggage is.

For instance, an overweight bag could cost you an additional £50 or more. Have you ever been caught off guard by baggage fees? Planning your luggage carefully can help you skip these extra costs and keep your travel budget intact.




## Packing Tips For Hold Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-your-hold-luggage-for-an-easyjet-flight-requires-a-bit-of-strategy-to-make-sure-you-meet-airline-rules-and-avoid-unnecessary-fees.-knowing-exactly-what-to-pack,-what-to-leave-out,-and-how-to-arrange-your-items-can-save-you-time-and-stress-at-the-airport.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing your hold luggage for an EasyJet flight requires a bit of strategy to make sure you meet airline rules and avoid unnecessary fees. Knowing exactly what to pack, what to leave out, and how to arrange your items can save you time and stress at the airport.

### What To Pack

Focus on essentials that you won’t need during the flight but will want at your destination. Clothes, shoes, toiletries, and any bulkier items fit perfectly in hold luggage.

Think about your trip length and activities. If you’re heading to a beach, pack swimwear and sunscreen. For a city break, bring comfortable walking shoes and a light jacket.

Don’t forget chargers, adapters, and any medications, but keep these in your carry-on just in case.

### Prohibited Items

EasyJet has strict rules about what you cannot pack in hold luggage. Avoid sharp objects like knives or scissors, flammable items, and any liquids over 100ml unless they’re securely packed.

Remember, batteries and electronic cigarettes are often restricted or need special packaging. Check EasyJet’s official list before packing.

Have you ever faced delays because of prohibited items found in your bag? Taking a moment to review these rules can prevent that hassle.

### Packing Efficiently

Use packing cubes or compression bags to save space and keep your luggage organized. Rolling clothes instead of folding can reduce wrinkles and create more room.

Place heavier items at the bottom near the wheels of your suitcase for better balance. Keep fragile or valuable items in the middle, cushioned by softer clothing.

Try to leave a little extra space for souvenirs or last-minute purchases. Overpacking can lead to extra fees or the need to repack at the airport, which nobody enjoys.

## Checking In Hold Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=checking-in-hold-luggage-with-easyjet-is-a-straightforward-process-designed-to-save-you-time-and-stress-before-your-flight.-whether-you-prefer-to-handle-things-online-or-prefer-the-airport-experience,-knowing-the-steps-can-make-your-journey-smoother.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Checking in hold luggage with EasyJet is a straightforward process designed to save you time and stress before your flight. Whether you prefer to handle things online or prefer the airport experience, knowing the steps can make your journey smoother. Here’s what you need to know about checking in your hold luggage.

### At The Airport

If you haven't checked in online, you can do so at the airport. EasyJet has dedicated check-in desks where you can drop off your hold luggage after printing your boarding pass.

Arrive early to avoid long queues, especially during busy travel times. You’ll need to present your ID and flight details at the desk before handing over your bags.

Remember, there are size and weight limits for hold luggage, so weigh your bags before you get to the airport to avoid extra charges.

### Online Check-in

EasyJet allows you to check in online up to 30 days before your flight, which is a great way to skip lines at the airport. During this process, you can also add hold luggage to your booking if you didn’t include it when you first purchased your ticket.

Once checked in, print your boarding pass or download it to your mobile device. This step speeds up your journey and helps you move quickly through security and to the baggage drop-off.

Have you ever forgotten to add hold luggage online? It can lead to higher fees at the airport, so it’s worth handling this early.

### Drop-off Procedures

After checking in online or at the airport, the next step is dropping off your hold luggage at EasyJet’s designated bag drop counters. These counters are usually clearly marked and separate from the general check-in desks.

Make sure your luggage is tagged correctly and keep your baggage receipt safe—it’s your proof in case your bag goes missing. The staff will scan your boarding pass and tag your hold luggage for the flight.

Want to speed things up? Pack your bags neatly and avoid overpacking to stay within weight limits, reducing the chance of delays or extra fees at drop-off.




## Handling Lost Or Damaged Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=handling-lost-or-damaged-luggage-on-easyjet-can-feel-stressful.-the-airline-has-clear-steps-to-help-passengers-report-and-resolve-such-issues.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Handling lost or damaged luggage on EasyJet can feel stressful. The airline has clear steps to help passengers report and resolve such issues. Knowing these steps can save time and reduce frustration during travel.

### Reporting Issues

Report lost or damaged luggage as soon as possible. Go to the EasyJet baggage desk at the airport. Provide your flight details and bag description. The staff will help you fill out a Property Irregularity Report (PIR). Keep a copy of this report for your records.

### Compensation Policies

EasyJet follows specific rules for luggage compensation. Compensation depends on the bag’s value and the damage. Submit a claim within the time limits set by EasyJet. Keep receipts for any necessary purchases due to lost luggage. Compensation may cover essentials while waiting for your bag.

### Tracking Your Bag

EasyJet offers a tracking system for lost luggage. Use the reference number from your PIR to check updates online. The system shows the location and estimated delivery of your bag. Stay in touch with EasyJet’s customer service for further help. Tracking helps you stay informed and plan accordingly.

Hold luggage on EasyJet refers to checked bags stored in the aircraft’s cargo hold. Passengers pay extra to check these bags. It allows carrying larger or heavier items not allowed in cabin bags.
