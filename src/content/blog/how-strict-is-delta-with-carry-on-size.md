## Delta Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding Delta's carry-on size limits can save you from last-minute hassles at the airport. Delta Airlines sets clear boundaries on what you can bring onboard as carry-on luggage. Knowing these limits helps you pack smart and avoid unexpected fees or having to check your bag unexpectedly.

### Maximum Dimensions Allowed

Delta allows carry-on bags with a maximum size of **22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm)**, including handles and wheels. This size is fairly standard among major airlines but can feel restrictive if you tend to pack bulky items.

Think about your favorite carry-on suitcase. Does it fit within these dimensions? If not, you might face gate-checking your bag, which can be inconvenient and slow down your travel.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike some airlines, Delta does not impose a specific weight limit on carry-on bags. This means you can pack heavier items as long as your bag fits the size requirements and you can lift it into the overhead bin.

However, consider your own strength and comfort. Struggling to lift a heavy bag could slow you down when boarding or deplaning. Also, be mindful that gate agents may ask you to check bags that are too heavy or bulky for overhead compartments.
