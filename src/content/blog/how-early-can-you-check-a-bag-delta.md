## Delta Baggage Policies

Delta Airlines has clear rules for baggage. Knowing these rules helps travelers avoid extra fees and delays. Understanding what you can bring and how much it costs is important. This section explains Delta’s baggage policies in simple terms.

### Checked Baggage Allowance

Most Delta flights allow at least one checked bag free for certain ticket types. The standard weight limit is 50 pounds (23 kg) per bag. Size must not exceed 62 inches (length + width + height). Excess bags or heavier bags may cost extra. Always check your ticket to see your allowance.

### Carry-on Rules

Delta lets passengers bring one carry-on and one personal item on board. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin. Size limits are usually 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm). Personal items include backpacks, purses, or laptops. These must fit under the seat in front.

### Oversize And Overweight Fees

Bags over 50 pounds or 62 inches may face additional fees. Fees vary by route and ticket class. Large items like sports equipment or musical instruments may have special rules. It is best to measure and weigh bags before arriving at the airport. This avoids surprises at check-in.
