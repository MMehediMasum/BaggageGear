## Aer Lingus Carry-on Size Rules

Aer Lingus has clear rules about carry-on luggage size. These rules help ensure smooth boarding and safe storage on the plane. Passengers must follow these size and weight limits strictly. Airlines check bags at the gate, and oversized bags may need to be checked in. Understanding Aer Lingus carry-on size rules saves time and avoids extra fees.

### Allowed Dimensions

Aer Lingus allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size is 55 cm x 40 cm x 24 cm. This size includes handles and wheels. Bags larger than this may not fit in overhead bins.

### Weight Limits

Carry-on bags must weigh no more than 10 kg. This limit helps passengers lift and store bags safely. Overweight bags may require checking in or paying fees.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Besides the carry-on bag, each passenger can bring one personal item. This can be a handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack. The personal item must fit under the seat in front. Typical size is about 40 cm x 25 cm x 15 cm.

### Security Check Procedures

At security checkpoints, officers inspect carry-on bags closely. Bags that look too large or bulky may be measured or flagged. All liquids and electronics must be easily accessible for inspection. Passengers should pack neatly to speed up the process.

Gate agents may check your carry-on size before boarding. If the bag is too big, it might be gate checked. This means the bag goes in the aircraft hold instead of the cabin. Aer Lingus may charge a fee for gate-checked bags. Paying attention to size limits avoids extra charges.

### Handling Oversized Bags

If a carry-on is oversized, staff will ask to check it in. This can cause delays or risk of damage. Packing within the allowed dimensions helps keep bags with you. Some airports have measuring devices at the gate for quick checks. Following the rules makes travel smoother and stress-free.
