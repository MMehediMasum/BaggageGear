Alternatively, some local businesses and nearby luggage storage services partner with the station to provide drop-off points. These can be a good backup if lockers are full, though they might cost a bit more.

Pick storage spots that are well-reviewed and have visible security measures like cameras or staff supervision. Look for places inside busy areas such as train stations or trusted luggage storage services with clear policies.

Some services include insurance in their price, while others offer it for an extra fee. Don’t hesitate to ask for details upfront—understanding the terms can save you headaches.

## Cost Comparison Of Storage Options

Choosing where to store your luggage in Boston means weighing different costs carefully. Storage fees can vary widely depending on how long you need the service and the type of facility you select. Understanding these costs helps you avoid surprises and get the best value for your money.

### Hourly Vs Daily Rates

Many luggage storage spots offer both hourly and daily rates. Hourly rates are great if you only need storage for a few hours between flights or appointments. They typically range from $5 to $10 per hour.

Daily rates can be more economical if you plan to leave your bags for a whole day or more. These usually cost between $10 and $20 per day, depending on the location and security features.

Think about your schedule carefully: Will you need your bags back within a couple of hours, or will you be out exploring Boston all day? Choosing the right rate can save you a surprising amount.

### Hidden Fees To Watch

Some storage services may charge extra fees that you might not notice right away. These can include:

* Insurance fees for valuable items
* Late pick-up penalties
* Size-based pricing if your luggage is oversized
* Reservation or booking fees

I once booked a spot that seemed cheap until I was hit with a $5 “late pick-up” fee because my flight was delayed. Always ask about these charges before you commit. It’s better to know upfront than be surprised later.
