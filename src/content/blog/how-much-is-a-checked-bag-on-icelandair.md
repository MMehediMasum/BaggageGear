## Icelandair Baggage Policy

Icelandair’s baggage policy is designed to be straightforward, but understanding the details can save you time and money. Whether you’re packing for a quick trip or a longer adventure, knowing what you can carry on and check in is crucial. Let’s break down the essentials so you can plan your luggage without surprises.

### Carry-on Allowance

You are allowed one carry-on bag and one personal item on Icelandair flights. The carry-on bag must not exceed **22 x 18 x 10 inches (56 x 46 x 25 cm)**. Your personal item, like a laptop bag or handbag, should fit under the seat in front of you.

Keep in mind the weight limit for carry-ons is generally **22 lbs (10 kg)**. If you’ve ever struggled lifting a heavy bag onto the overhead bin, this limit is a welcome relief. Have you packed your essentials in a way that fits these restrictions?

### Checked Bag Basics

When it comes to checked baggage, Icelandair’s fees depend on your ticket type and destination. Most economy fares include a fee for the first checked bag, usually around **$30 to $50 USD**. However, Saga Premium and Saga Class tickets often include at least one checked bag for free.

| Ticket Type | First Checked Bag Fee | Weight Limit | Dimensions Allowed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Economy (Saver, Flex) | $30 - $50 | 50 lbs (23 kg) | 62 inches (157 cm) total |
| Saga Premium / Saga Class | Included | 70 lbs (32 kg) | 62 inches (157 cm) total |

Oversized or overweight bags come with additional fees, so it’s smart to weigh your luggage at home. I once had to pay extra for a heavy ski bag after a winter trip, which was an unexpected cost. Avoid this by checking your bag’s weight before heading to the airport.

Additional charges and restrictions apply to checked bags on Icelandair. Knowing these rules helps avoid surprises at the airport. Fees vary by weight, size, and type of item.

### Overweight And Oversized Bags

Bags over the standard weight limit face extra fees. The usual weight limit is 23 kg (50 lbs) per bag. Bags weighing between 24 kg and 32 kg have an additional charge. Any bag over 32 kg may be refused or charged at a higher rate.

Oversized bags also incur fees. The maximum size allowed is 158 cm (length + width + height). Bags larger than this face extra costs or may not be accepted.

### Excess Baggage Fees

Excess baggage fees apply when you exceed the free baggage allowance. The cost depends on your route and ticket class. Paying online before the flight is usually cheaper than paying at the airport.

Charges can add up quickly, so pack carefully. Check your ticket for included baggage allowance to avoid extra fees.

### Sports Equipment

Sports gear like skis, golf clubs, or bicycles require special handling. Icelandair allows sports equipment but charges fees depending on the item. Some equipment counts as part of your free allowance; others need extra payment.

Packing sports equipment properly avoids damage and delays. Contact Icelandair for specific rules and fees for your gear.
