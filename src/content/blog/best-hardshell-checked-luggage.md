## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hardshell Luggage Better For Checked Bags?



Hardshell luggage offers superior protection against impacts and rough handling. It resists scratches and water better than softshell bags. Many models are expandable, increasing packing space. Durable materials like polycarbonate provide lightweight strength. Multi-directional spinner wheels enhance maneuverability for easy travel.



### How Do Tsa Locks Improve Luggage Security?



TSA locks allow airport security to inspect bags without damage. They meet Transportation Security Administration standards for safe access. Using TSA locks prevents forced openings and keeps belongings secure. Most hardshell luggage models include these locks for traveler peace of mind.



### Are Expandable Suitcases Worth Buying For Travel?



Expandable suitcases provide extra packing space when needed. They offer flexibility for souvenirs or additional items. Expansion features usually add up to 15% more capacity. This makes them ideal for longer trips or variable packing needs. Many hardshell models include this convenient feature.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Checked Luggage?



Spinner wheels improve mobility by rotating 360 degrees. They reduce strain by allowing smooth, multi-directional movement. Most premium hardshell luggage has four or eight spinner wheels. This feature makes navigating airports and hotels easier and faster. Durable wheels also enhance suitcase longevity.



### Which Materials Are Best For Durable Hardshell Luggage?



Polycarbonate and ABS plastics are popular for hardshell luggage. Polycarbonate is lightweight, impact-resistant, and flexible under pressure. ABS is sturdy but slightly heavier and more rigid. Some high-end luggage combines materials for optimal durability and weight. Quality materials protect belongings during rough travel conditions.



### How Do I Choose The Right Luggage Size For Checked Bags?



Choose luggage size based on trip length and airline limits. Large bags (28-30 inches) suit long trips or family travel. Medium bags (24-26 inches) work for shorter trips or lighter packing. Always check airline weight and size restrictions to avoid fees.



Expandable options add packing flexibility.
