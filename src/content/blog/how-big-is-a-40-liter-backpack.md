## How To Measure Your Backpack Size

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=measuring-your-backpack-size-helps-you-pick-the-right-pack-for-your-needs.-it-ensures-you-carry-enough-items-without-extra-bulk.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Measuring your backpack size helps you pick the right pack for your needs. It ensures you carry enough items without extra bulk. Understanding the size also aids in comfort and fit during travel or hiking.

### Checking Volume

Volume measures how much space the backpack holds. It is usually given in liters. To check the volume yourself, measure the height, width, and depth of the main compartment in centimeters.

* Height: Measure from top to bottom
* Width: Measure across the widest part
* Depth: Measure from front to back

Multiply these three numbers to get the cubic centimeters. Divide by 1000 to convert to liters. This gives a close estimate of the backpack’s volume.

### Evaluating Fit And Comfort

Size alone does not guarantee a good fit. The backpack should match your torso length and body type. Look for adjustable straps to customize fit.

* Shoulder straps should rest comfortably without digging in
* Hip belt should sit on your hips to transfer weight
* Check for padding in key areas to avoid pressure points

Try the backpack with some weight to feel how it carries. A well-fitted backpack improves comfort and reduces fatigue.

## Common Misconceptions About Backpack Size

Many people misunderstand what a 40 liter backpack can actually hold. This leads to frustration when their gear doesn’t fit or when the backpack feels heavier than expected. Clearing up these misconceptions helps you choose the right pack and pack smarter.

### Overestimating Capacity

A 40 liter backpack might sound huge, but it’s not as big as you think. It’s designed for about two to three days of gear, not a full week’s worth of supplies. Trying to cram everything in just because the label says "40 liters" often results in an overstuffed, uncomfortable pack.

Think about your last trip—did you pack more than necessary just because you had the space? Many hikers do this, assuming bigger means better. But packing efficiently is more important than just having a large volume.

### Ignoring Weight Distribution

Size alone doesn’t determine comfort. How you distribute weight inside your 40 liter backpack makes a huge difference. Placing heavy items at the bottom or front can throw off your balance and strain your back.

Have you ever felt your pack pulling you backward or causing shoulder pain? That’s often because the weight isn’t centered properly. Use compartments and compression straps to keep weight close to your spine and evenly spread out.
