The term for a small bag worn around the waist varies across English-speaking countries. In Britain, this item is commonly known as a "bum bag." This phrase might puzzle non-Brits who are used to hearing "fanny pack," especially in the US. Understanding these British terms helps in solving crossword clues and enhances cultural knowledge.

### Fanny Pack Vs Bum Bag

The words "fanny pack" and "bum bag" describe the same accessory. Both mean a small pouch attached with a strap worn around the waist. The key difference lies in geography and language use.

"Fanny pack" is mainly used in the United States. The word "fanny" in American English refers to the front of the body. Meanwhile, in British English, "fanny" is a slang term for female anatomy, so it is rarely used for the bag.

Brits prefer "bum bag" because "bum" means buttocks in the UK. The name reflects the bag's position on the body, often resting near the lower back or hips.

### Regional Variations

Within the UK, some regions may use different slang or terms for the bum bag. Although "bum bag" is the most common, other words include:

* Waist bag
* Hip bag
* Fanny pack (rare and usually influenced by American English)

Outside Britain, similar terms exist too. In Australia, people might call it a "bumbag" or "fanny pack," depending on the area.

These regional variations can confuse crossword solvers. Knowing the British preference for "bum bag" is a helpful clue for puzzles and quizzes.
