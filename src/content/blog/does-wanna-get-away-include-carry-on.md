## Wanna Get Away Fare Basics

The Wanna Get Away fare is a popular choice for travelers looking for budget-friendly flights without sacrificing flexibility entirely. Understanding the basics of this fare can help you make smarter choices when booking. ### Fare Types And Benefits

The Wanna Get Away fare is Southwest Airlines’ most affordable ticket option. It offers significant savings compared to Anytime or Business Select fares, making it ideal if you’re price-conscious.

This fare comes with these key benefits:

* Refundable in the form of travel credit if you cancel before departure
* Earn points toward Southwest’s Rapid Rewards program
* Access to free inflight snacks and drinks

However, it doesn’t include some perks of higher fares, such as priority boarding or full cash refunds. Knowing this helps you weigh if the price savings are worth the trade-offs.

### Baggage Policies Overview

One of the most common questions is whether the Wanna Get Away fare includes a carry-on bag. The good news is that Southwest’s baggage policy is the same across all fare types.

Southwest allows you to bring one carry-on bag and one personal item for free. This applies to Wanna Get Away tickets as well, so you won’t pay extra just for a carry-on. You can pack your laptop bag, purse, or small backpack without worrying about fees.

Checked bags, however, are treated differently:

* Wanna Get Away includes two free checked bags per passenger.
* Additional bags beyond these two will incur fees.

Knowing this can save you money. Many airlines charge for both carry-ons and checked bags, but Southwest’s policy is more generous. Have you ever been surprised by baggage fees at the airport? This fare can help you avoid that hassle.
