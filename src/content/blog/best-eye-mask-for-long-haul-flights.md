## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Eye Mask For Long Haul Flights?



The best eye mask for long haul flights offers 100% light blocking. It should be comfortable, breathable, and adjustable. Masks like MABAO and LitBear provide 3D contoured designs to prevent eye pressure. Pairing with travel pillows enhances sleep quality during flights.



### How Do 3d Contoured Eye Masks Improve Sleep?



3D contoured eye masks create space around the eyes, avoiding pressure on lashes and eyelids. They block light completely, promoting deeper sleep. This design suits side sleepers and those with lash extensions, offering comfort and reducing irritation on long flights.



### Can Eye Masks With Adjustable Straps Fit All Head Sizes?



Yes, most travel eye masks come with adjustable straps. This feature ensures a secure fit for all head sizes. Adjustable straps prevent masks from slipping during sleep, enhancing comfort on long haul flights.



### Are Weighted Eye Masks Effective For Flight Naps?



Weighted eye masks provide gentle pressure, which can reduce anxiety and improve relaxation. This helps passengers fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer. Weighted masks like the FACEMOON model are ideal for long haul flights needing restful naps.



### What Additional Accessories Improve In-flight Sleep Quality?



In-flight sleep improves with travel pillows, earplugs, and contoured eye masks. Kits like Proglobe’s include these essentials for complete comfort. Using these together reduces noise, light, and neck strain, making long haul flights more restful.



### How To Choose An Eye Mask For Side Sleepers On Planes?



Choose a 3D contoured eye mask with deep cups to avoid eye pressure. Masks like LitBear and MABAO offer soft, breathable fabrics and adjustable straps. These features ensure comfort and effective light blocking for side sleepers on flights.
