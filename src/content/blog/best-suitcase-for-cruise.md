## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Suitcase Size Is Best For A Cruise Trip?



A 20 to 28-inch suitcase fits most cruise luggage requirements. It balances capacity with ease of handling onboard. Carry-ons like the TRAVELARIM 22 Inch are ideal for essentials and quick access. Larger checked bags like Samsonite Freeform provide ample space for longer voyages.



### Are Hard-shell Suitcases Better For Cruises?



Yes, hard-shell suitcases offer more protection against rough handling and water exposure on cruises. Models like Coolife Luggage 3 Piece Set and Amazon Basics 30" Hardside provide durability and scratch resistance. They keep belongings safe and dry during embarkation and excursions.



### Do Cruise Suitcases Need Tsa Locks?



TSA locks are highly recommended for cruise luggage. They allow security staff to inspect bags without damage. Most cruise ports require locked luggage, and sets like Coolife and Samsonite include TSA-approved locks for safe travel.



### Can Lightweight Luggage Hold Enough For Cruises?



Lightweight luggage like Coolife and Samsonite Omni 2 is designed to maximize packing space without extra weight. This helps avoid airline overweight fees and eases mobility on the ship. They are durable yet light, perfect for cruise travelers.



### Is Expandable Luggage Useful On Cruises?



Expandable luggage is very useful for cruise trips. It offers extra space for souvenirs and additional clothing. Options like Amazon Basics and Samsonite Freeform expand up to 15%, making packing flexible and convenient for varying trip lengths.



### What Features Matter Most In Cruise Suitcases?



Key features include durable hardshell material, TSA locks, spinner wheels, and lightweight design. These enhance security, ease of movement, and longevity. Sets like Coolife and American Tourister combine these traits for an optimal cruise packing experience.
