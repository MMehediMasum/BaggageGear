## Seaworld's Bag Policy Basics

Seaworld has clear rules about bags visitors can bring inside the park. These rules help keep everyone safe and speed up entry. Understanding the basics of Seaworld's bag policy makes your visit easier and more fun. This section explains the main points of the policy, including the purpose, allowed bags, and size limits.

### Purpose Of The Clear Bag Rule

The clear bag rule helps security staff check bags quickly. It stops harmful items from entering the park. Clear bags make the screening process faster and safer. This rule protects guests, staff, and animals at Seaworld. It also reduces long lines at the entrance.

### Types Of Bags Allowed

Seaworld allows only clear bags made of plastic, vinyl, or PVC. These bags must be fully transparent. Mesh and cloth bags are not allowed. Small clutches or purses are okay if they meet size rules. Backpacks and large handbags must follow the clear bag rule.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must be no larger than 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. This size fits most clear tote bags. Small bags like clutch purses can be up to 4.5 inches by 6.5 inches. Bags bigger than these sizes will not be allowed inside. Check your bag size before arriving to avoid issues.

### Medical And Accessibility Needs

If you have a medical condition or require accessibility accommodations, SeaWorld makes exceptions to their clear bag policy. You’re allowed to bring non-clear bags that contain essential medical supplies, such as insulin, inhalers, or mobility aids.

It’s a good idea to carry documentation or a doctor’s note explaining your need for these items. This can help avoid delays at the entrance and make the security process easier for you.

Do you carry special medical equipment? Plan ahead by packing only what’s necessary and informing the staff if needed. This approach ensures you won’t be caught off guard at the gate.

### Stroller And Diaper Bag Policies

Visiting SeaWorld with little ones? The park understands that families often need to bring strollers and diaper bags, which do not always fit the clear bag rule.

Strollers are allowed, but they may be subject to inspection. Diaper bags can exceed the clear bag size but must contain only baby necessities like diapers, wipes, bottles, and snacks.

* Keep diaper bags organized to speed up security checks.
* Consider using smaller clear bags inside your diaper bag for easier inspection.
* Check stroller dimensions in advance to avoid surprises at the gate.
