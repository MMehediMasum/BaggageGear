## Knott's Berry Farm Bag Policy

Planning a visit to Knott’s Berry Farm often raises questions about what you can bring along, especially when it comes to backpacks. Understanding the park’s bag policy helps you avoid surprises at the gate and ensures a smooth entry. Here’s a clear guide to what types of bags are allowed, the size restrictions, and the items you should leave at home.

### Allowed Bag Types

You can bring various types of bags into Knott’s Berry Farm, but there are limits. Small backpacks, purses, and clear bags are generally acceptable. Many visitors find that a clear backpack not only meets the rules but also speeds up security checks.

Consider a backpack with multiple compartments. It helps keep your essentials organized and easy to find. If you prefer something smaller, a crossbody bag or fanny pack works well too, especially on busy days.

### Size Restrictions

Knott’s Berry Farm enforces size limits to keep walkways clear and ensure safety. Backpacks larger than 24” x 15” x 10” are not permitted. Think about packing only the essentials to stay within these dimensions.

Have you ever struggled with a bulky backpack at a theme park? Choosing a compact bag makes your visit more comfortable and lets you move freely through crowds. If your bag is too large, you might be asked to leave it at a designated area or return it to your vehicle.

### Prohibited Items

Knowing what you can’t bring is just as important as knowing what you can. Knott’s Berry Farm bans weapons, outside alcohol, large coolers, drones, and glass containers. This policy protects everyone’s safety and enjoyment.

Think twice before packing items like selfie sticks or folding chairs, as some of these may also be restricted.
