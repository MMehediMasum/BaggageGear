## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Hard Shell Carry-on Luggage?



The best hard shell carry-on offers durability and ample space. Options like Samsonite and Amazon Basics are popular for their robust designs and practical features. Consider factors like weight, expandability, and wheel quality when choosing. These aspects ensure ease of travel and protect your belongings.



### Are Hard Shell Carry-ons Scratch-resistant?



Many hard shell carry-ons, like the Amazon Basics model, boast scratch-resistant surfaces. This feature helps maintain the luggage's appearance during travel. It is essential for frequent travelers who want their luggage to stay looking new over time.



### Do Hard Shell Carry-ons Have Tsa Locks?



Yes, many hard shell carry-ons, including models from Samsonite and Sweetcrispy, feature TSA-approved locks. These locks offer security while ensuring easy access for TSA officers during inspections. Always check for this feature if security is a priority for your travels.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Benefit Travel?



Spinner wheels offer excellent maneuverability, allowing smooth navigation through crowded airports. They rotate 360 degrees for effortless control in tight spaces. Luggage with spinner wheels, like those from KROSER and Wrangler, enhances travel convenience by reducing the effort needed to move your suitcase.



### Can Hard Shell Luggage Be Expandable?



Yes, many hard shell carry-ons, such as the Amazon Basics model, are expandable. This feature provides up to 25% more packing space. It is ideal for travelers needing extra room for souvenirs or additional items. Always check for expandability to maximize packing efficiency.
