### Can I Use A Us Plug In Portugal?



No, you cannot use a US plug directly in Portugal. Portugal uses Type C outlets, which differ from US plugs. You need a travel adapter like the TESSAN or HANYCONY models. These adapters allow you to connect US plugs to Portuguese outlets safely.



### Do Travel Adapters Work In All European Countries?



Most travel adapters work in many European countries, including Portugal. Look for adapters labeled as Type C or multi-country. Brands like TESSAN or EPICKA offer international compatibility. Always check specific country requirements before traveling to ensure your adapter will work.



### How Many Devices Can I Charge At Once?



Travel adapters like TESSAN and HANYCONY can charge multiple devices simultaneously. They offer several outlets and USB ports. Some models have up to four outlets and multiple USB ports. This allows for efficient charging of phones, tablets, and more during your travels.



### Do Travel Adapters Convert Voltage?



No, travel adapters do not convert voltage. They only change plug shapes to fit different outlets. Ensure your device supports 220-240V used in Portugal. Otherwise, use a voltage converter alongside your adapter to prevent damage to your devices.



### Are Usb-c Ports Available On Travel Adapters?



Yes, many travel adapters now include USB-C ports. Brands like HANYCONY and TESSAN offer models with USB-C charging. USB-C ports provide faster charging for compatible devices. They are ideal for modern electronics like smartphones and tablets during international travel.
