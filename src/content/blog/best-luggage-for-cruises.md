## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Luggage Size Is Best For Cruise Travel?



Medium-sized luggage, around 24 to 28 inches, is ideal for cruises. It fits in cabins and meets airline requirements. Sets with carry-ons and checked bags offer versatility for different cruise lengths and activities.



### Are Tsa Locks Necessary For Cruise Luggage?



Yes, TSA locks are recommended for cruise luggage. They allow security to inspect bags without damage. Many featured luggage sets include TSA-approved locks for secure, hassle-free travel on cruises and flights.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Cruise Suitcases?



Spinner wheels are very important for cruise luggage. They provide easy 360-degree maneuverability through airports, terminals, and ship corridors. Spinner wheels reduce strain and make transporting heavy bags smoother and more convenient.



### Should Cruise Luggage Be Lightweight And Expandable?



Yes, lightweight and expandable luggage is best for cruises. Lightweight cases are easier to carry and store. Expandable luggage offers extra packing space for souvenirs and essentials during your trip.



### What Are The Benefits Of Packing Cubes For Cruises?



Packing cubes improve organization and save space in cruise luggage. They separate clothes, shoes, and toiletries, making unpacking easier. Many cruise travelers find packing cubes essential for efficient cabin storage and quick access.
