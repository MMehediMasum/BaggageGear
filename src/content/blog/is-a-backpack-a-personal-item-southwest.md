## Southwest Carry-on Rules

Understanding Southwest's carry-on rules can save you from last-minute hassles at the gate. Knowing what counts as a personal item and what fits in the overhead bin is crucial. Let’s break down the essentials so you can pack smart and breeze through your next Southwest flight.

### Carry-on Size Limits

Southwest allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size for this bag is 10 x 16 x 24 inches, including handles and wheels. If your bag fits within these dimensions, it should fit comfortably in the overhead bin.

Think about your last trip—did your carry-on ever feel too bulky? Measuring your bag before you fly can prevent surprises. Even a small difference in size might mean your bag has to be checked at the gate.

### Personal Item Size Limits

Besides the carry-on, you’re allowed one personal item. This must fit under the seat in front of you. The maximum dimensions are 18.5 x 8.5 x 13.5 inches.

Personal items include things like backpacks, purses, or laptop bags. If your backpack fits these size limits, it counts as your personal item. Have you ever struggled to find space for your personal item under the seat? Choosing the right size can make a big difference.

### Bag Types Allowed

Southwest is flexible with bag types for both carry-ons and personal items. Common carry-ons include rolling suitcases and larger backpacks. Personal items often are smaller backpacks, tote bags, or briefcases.

* **Carry-on examples:**wheeled luggage, duffel bags, larger backpacks
* **Personal item examples:**small backpacks, laptop bags, purses

### Gate Check Procedures

If your backpack is too large to fit under the seat, Southwest might require you to gate check it. This means handing it over before boarding, and you’ll pick it up at the jet bridge upon arrival. The gate agent will tag your backpack, so it’s important to keep an eye on the process to avoid mix-ups.

Remember, gate checking is different from checked baggage. Your backpack stays with the plane but not with you, so pack valuables accordingly. Have you ever had to gate check something unexpectedly? It can feel like losing control of your belongings in a busy airport.

### Fees And Charges

One of the perks of Southwest is their free first and second checked bags. However, gate-checked personal items like backpacks don’t usually incur extra fees. That said, if your backpack doesn’t qualify as a personal item and you need to check it as regular baggage, fees will apply.

Understanding the size limits for personal items (typically 10 x 16 x 24 inches) helps you avoid these fees. Do you know the exact dimensions of your backpack? Measuring it before you pack can prevent unexpected charges.

### Tips To Avoid Issues

* **Measure Your Backpack:**Ensure it fits Southwest’s personal item size limits before your trip.
* **Pack Smart:**Keep essentials and valuables in your backpack since gate checking means temporary separation.
* **Arrive Early:**Give yourself time to address any gate check requests without stress.
