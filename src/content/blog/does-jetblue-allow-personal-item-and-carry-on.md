JetBlue lets you bring one personal item free of charge. This could be a backpack, purse, laptop bag, or briefcase. The item must fit under the seat in front of you, with maximum dimensions of 17 x 13 x 8 inches.

Think about what you carry daily—your laptop bag or a small backpack usually fits perfectly. I once tried to bring a slightly larger bag and had to gate-check it, which delayed my arrival to the gate. Avoid surprises by measuring your bag beforehand.

### Carry-on Baggage Rules

In addition to a personal item, JetBlue allows one carry-on bag. The carry-on must not exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including handles and wheels. It should fit in the overhead bin comfortably.

If you’re traveling with a bulky carry-on, consider how quickly you want to board. Overhead bin space can fill up fast, especially on busy flights. Arriving early increases your chances of storing your bag overhead.

### Weight Limits And Fees

JetBlue does not enforce a specific weight limit for carry-ons, but your bag must be manageable by you. Oversized or overweight bags may require gate check, which can be inconvenient.

Checked bags have fees depending on your fare type and loyalty status. However, personal items and carry-ons usually stay free if they meet size requirements. Do you really want to pay extra for a bag you can carry on?
