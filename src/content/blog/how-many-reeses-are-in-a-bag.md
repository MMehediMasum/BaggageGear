## How To Estimate Reese's Count

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=estimating-the-number-of-reese's-in-a-bag-can-be-simple-and-quick.-it-helps-when-you-want-to-share-or-plan-portions.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Estimating the number of Reese's in a bag can be simple and quick. It helps when you want to share or plan portions. Two easy ways can guide you: weighing and visual counting. These methods save time and avoid opening the whole bag.

### Weighing Method

Use a kitchen scale for this method. First, find the total weight of the bag on the scale. Then, weigh one Reese's peanut butter cup separately. Divide the total bag weight by the single cup's weight. This gives an estimate of how many pieces are inside. Remember to subtract the bag's weight if you can.

### Visual Counting Tips

Look closely at the bag’s shape and size. Sometimes, you can see the outline of the cups through the packaging. Count the visible pieces on one side. Multiply by the number of similar layers inside the bag. This gives a rough idea of the total count. Use this with the weighing method for better accuracy.
