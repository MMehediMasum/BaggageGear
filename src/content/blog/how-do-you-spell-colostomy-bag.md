### How Do You Correctly Spell Colostomy Bag?

The correct spelling is "colostomy bag. " It combines "colostomy," referring to a surgical opening in the colon, and "bag," used to collect waste. Always spell it as one word per part, with no extra letters or spaces.

### What Is The Meaning Of A Colostomy Bag?

A colostomy bag collects waste from the body after colostomy surgery. It attaches to the stoma, a surgically created opening in the abdomen. The bag helps manage bowel movements when the colon cannot function normally.
