## Southwest's Luggage Policy

Southwest Airlines has a luggage policy that often surprises travelers in a good way. Unlike many other airlines, Southwest offers a more generous approach to baggage fees, which can save you money and hassle during your trip. Knowing the details of their policy helps you pack smarter and avoid unexpected charges.

### Free Baggage Allowance

Southwest allows you to check in **two bags for free**on every flight. This is a major benefit if you travel with family or carry extra gear. Each bag must weigh less than 50 pounds and be under 62 inches in total size (length + width + height).

Carry-on bags and personal items like backpacks or purses do not count toward this free checked bag limit. This means you can bring quite a bit without paying extra. Have you ever paid for luggage on other airlines and wished you flew Southwest instead?

### Types Of Luggage Covered

Southwest’s free baggage policy covers most types of standard luggage. This includes:

* Suitcases and duffel bags
* Sporting equipment like skis or golf clubs (with special packaging)
* Musical instruments that fit size and weight limits

Oversized or overweight bags will incur additional fees, so weigh and measure your luggage before you arrive at the airport. Southwest’s clear guidelines make it easier to avoid surprises at the check-in counter.




## Changes In Luggage Fees

Southwest Airlines has made notable changes to its luggage fee policies over time. These changes affect how travelers plan their trips and pack their bags. Understanding the evolution of these fees helps passengers avoid surprises at the airport.

### Historical Fee Introduction

Southwest Airlines was once known for not charging fees for the first two checked bags. This policy made it stand out among U.S. carriers. The no-fee luggage policy began as a way to attract more customers and simplify travel.

Over the years, Southwest maintained this approach while other airlines started charging for checked bags. This helped Southwest build a loyal customer base. The first fees for excess or overweight bags appeared later but the first two bags remained free for a long time.

### Current Fee Structure

Today, Southwest still offers two free checked bags per passenger on most fares. This policy is rare among major airlines. Passengers pay only for additional bags beyond the first two.

Fees apply for overweight and oversized bags. These charges vary depending on the size and weight of the luggage. Southwest also charges fees for certain special items like pets and sports equipment.

Knowing the current fee rules can save money and reduce stress. Travelers should check Southwest’s website for the latest updates before flying.

## When Fees Apply

Southwest Airlines offers free checked bags, but fees apply in specific situations. Knowing when these charges occur helps travelers avoid surprises. Fees are mainly for extra bags or items that don’t meet standard size and weight limits.

### Excess Baggage Charges

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. Charges start with the third bag and any beyond. Each extra bag costs a fixed fee, payable at check-in or online. Overweight bags also trigger fees if they exceed 50 pounds. Bags over 100 pounds are not accepted.

* Third and additional bags cost a fee per piece.
* Bags weighing 51 to 100 pounds have extra charges.
* More than two free bags only apply to standard checked luggage.

### Special Items And Oversized Bags

Special items like sports equipment or musical instruments may need extra fees. Oversized bags that exceed size limits also incur charges. Southwest sets clear size rules for checked bags. Items beyond these limits require extra fees or special handling.

* Golf bags, bicycles, and surfboards often have fees.
* Oversized bags larger than 62 inches total dimensions cost extra.
* Special handling fees may apply for fragile or heavy items.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When Does Southwest Start Charging For Checked Luggage?

Southwest began charging for checked bags on certain fare types starting in 2023. However, two free checked bags remain for most passengers. Charges apply mainly to the Wanna Get Away Plus and Business Select fares.

### How Much Does Southwest Charge For First Checked Bag?

Southwest Airlines still offers the first two checked bags free on most fares. Charges typically apply only for additional bags beyond the first two or for certain upgraded fare types.

### Are Carry-on Bags Charged By Southwest Airlines?

No, Southwest does not charge for carry-on bags. Passengers can bring one carry-on and one personal item free of charge.

### Can I Avoid Southwest Luggage Fees?

Yes, by booking Wanna Get Away or Business Select fares, you get two free checked bags. Upgrading fare type may lead to luggage fees.
