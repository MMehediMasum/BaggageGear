Yes, a voltage converter is necessary if your device runs on 110V. Europe uses 220-240V. Converters like Key Power 230-Watt or DOACE 350W safely step down voltage. They protect your US appliances such as hair straighteners and laptops from damage.



### Can I Charge Usb-c Devices With European Adapters?



Absolutely. Many European adapters come with USB-C ports. For example, TESSAN and FOVAL adapters include USB-C charging options. These ensure fast and safe charging for modern smartphones, tablets, and laptops while traveling across Europe.
