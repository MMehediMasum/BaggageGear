## Packing Contacts For Travel

Packing your contacts for travel requires more thought than just tossing your case into your bag. You need to consider how to keep your lenses safe, clean, and accessible throughout your journey. Proper packing ensures you avoid discomfort and maintain eye health even in unfamiliar environments.

### Types Of Contact Lens Solutions

Not all contact lens solutions are the same. You have options like:

* **Multipurpose solutions:**Clean, rinse, disinfect, and store lenses all in one bottle.
* **Hydrogen peroxide solutions:**Great for deep cleaning but require neutralization before use.
* **Saline solutions:**Used mainly for rinsing lenses but don’t disinfect.

Choosing the right solution depends on your lens type and sensitivity. Do you know which one suits your eyes best? Carrying the correct solution is crucial when traveling, especially if you can’t easily buy replacements.

### Why Liquids Bag Matters

Your contact lens solution is considered a liquid by airport security. That means it must go into your liquids bag if you’re flying. This bag typically has a 100ml size limit for each container.

Failing to pack your solution correctly can mean delays or having to discard your essential lenses care items. I once had to replace my solution mid-trip because I didn’t pack it in the liquids bag. It was inconvenient and costly.

Using a clear, resealable liquids bag keeps everything visible and organized. It also helps you comply with security rules, making your travel smoother. Have you ever thought about how this small step can save you from a big hassle?




## Airport Security Rules

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=airport-security-rules-are-strict-about-what-passengers-can-carry-in-their-bags.-these-rules-keep-everyone-safe.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Airport security rules are strict about what passengers can carry in their bags. These rules keep everyone safe. Liquids have special limits at security checkpoints. Understanding these limits helps avoid delays and confusion.

### Liquid Restrictions Explained

Liquids in carry-on bags must follow the 3-1-1 rule:

* Containers no larger than 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters)
* All containers fit in one clear, quart-sized plastic bag
* One bag per passenger

This includes eye drops, gels, lotions, and contact lens solutions. Liquids exceeding these sizes must go in checked baggage.

### Contacts And Solution In Carry-on

Contact lenses themselves do not need to be in the liquids bag. They are solid items and allowed outside the bag. The contact lens solution, however, must follow liquid rules. It should be in a container of 3.4 ounces or less and placed inside the clear plastic bag.

Carrying your contact lenses in their case separately is fine. Keep the solution in the quart-sized bag for easy screening. This prevents problems at security and keeps your eyes comfortable during travel.

## Best Practices For Carrying Contacts

Carrying your contact lenses while traveling or going through airport security can be tricky. Knowing the best ways to handle your contacts ensures you won’t face unexpected hassles or risk damaging them. Here’s how to keep your lenses safe and make security checks smoother.

### Using Travel-sized Containers

Always opt for travel-sized contact lens cases and solution bottles. Most airports require liquids to be 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less if you’re carrying them in your carry-on. Using smaller containers means you can keep your lenses and solution in your liquids bag without any issues.

I once tried carrying a full-size bottle of solution in my carry-on and had to throw it away at security. Since then, I refill small bottles at home before each trip. This simple step saves money and avoids frustration.

### Labeling And Packaging Tips

Label your containers clearly with “contact lens solution” to prevent confusion during security checks. Place your lens case and solution together in a clear, resealable plastic bag along with other liquids. This keeps everything organized and easy to access.

Have you ever struggled to find your lenses in a crowded bag? Keeping your contacts in a separate, labeled pouch helps you avoid that panic. Plus, it speeds up your security line process when you can quickly show what you’re carrying.

Many travelers try to fit all their contact lens solutions and eye drops into one small liquids bag. This often leads to exceeding the allowed volume, causing security delays or having to discard items. Instead, check the size limits carefully and choose travel-sized bottles that meet the requirements.

Think about what you truly need for your trip. Carrying extra bottles “just in case” might seem smart, but it can backfire if you can’t fit them all into the allowed bag. Prioritize essentials and consider buying replacements at your destination if necessary.

### Forgetting Spare Supplies

It’s easy to forget packing spare lenses or an extra solution bottle when focusing too much on liquids. Yet, running out of these during your trip can cause real inconvenience. Always double-check your kit before leaving, ensuring you have backups of everything critical.

Have you ever been stuck without spare lenses or solution? That experience highlights the importance of preparing thoroughly. A simple checklist before packing can help you avoid this common oversight and keep your eyes comfortable throughout your travels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do Contact Lenses Count As Liquids In Security Checks?

Contact lenses themselves are not liquids, but their solution is. The solution must go in the liquids bag during security screening. This helps comply with airport security rules and avoid delays.

### Can I Carry Contact Lens Solution In A Liquids Bag?

Yes, contact lens solution must be in a clear, resealable liquids bag. The container should not exceed 100ml for air travel. This ensures smooth security checks and follows liquid restrictions.

### Are Contact Lenses Allowed In Carry-on Luggage?

Contact lenses can be carried in your hand luggage safely. The lenses do not need to be in the liquids bag. Only the solution requires placement in the liquids bag.

### Why Must Contact Lens Solution Be In A Liquids Bag?

Contact lens solution is a liquid and subject to airport liquid rules. Placing it in the liquids bag prevents security issues. It ensures compliance with liquid quantity and packaging limits.

## Conclusion

Contact lens solution does not count as a liquid for travel bags. Only liquids in bottles or containers need to go in the liquids bag. Carry your contact lenses and solution carefully to avoid spills. Keep your lenses clean and safe during flights.
