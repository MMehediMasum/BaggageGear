## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Ideal For A Mid-sized Suitcase?



A mid-sized suitcase typically ranges from 23 to 25 inches. This size balances ample packing space with easy maneuverability, fitting most airline checked baggage requirements. It suits week-long trips and offers expandability for extra items without being bulky.



### Which Suitcase Brand Offers The Best Durability?



Samsonite is renowned for durable luggage. Their hardside models like Winfield 2 and Omni PC provide strong impact resistance. SwissGear also offers reliable softside and hardside suitcases with sturdy materials, ensuring long-lasting use and protection for your belongings.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Mid-sized Suitcases?



Yes, spinner wheels offer 360-degree rotation, improving suitcase mobility. Models like Samsonite Freeform and SwissGear 7366 include multi-directional spinners, making it easier to navigate crowded airports and tight spaces with less effort and strain.



### How Important Is Tsa-approved Locking In Suitcases?



TSA-approved locks are crucial for secure and hassle-free travel. They allow TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. Many mid-sized suitcases, such as Coolife and Samsonite Freeform, feature these locks for enhanced safety and convenience.



### Can Mid-sized Suitcases Be Expandable?



Yes, many mid-sized suitcases like SwissGear Sion and Samsonite Omni PC include expandable compartments. This feature increases packing capacity by up to 15%, ideal for bringing back souvenirs or additional clothing during your trip.



### What Materials Are Best For Mid-sized Luggage Shells?



Hardshell luggage made of polycarbonate or ABS offers excellent impact resistance and scratch protection. Models like Samsonite Octiv and Amazon Basics use these materials, providing lightweight yet durable suitcases that safeguard your belongings during travel.
