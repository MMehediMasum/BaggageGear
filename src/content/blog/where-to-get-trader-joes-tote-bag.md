## Caring For Your Trader Joe's Tote Bag

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=caring-for-your-trader-joe's-tote-bag-helps-it-last-longer-and-stay-fresh.-proper-care-keeps-the-fabric-strong-and-colors-bright.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Caring for your Trader Joe's tote bag helps it last longer and stay fresh. Proper care keeps the fabric strong and colors bright. A well-maintained tote bag looks good and works well for many trips.

### Cleaning Instructions

Use cold water to hand wash your tote bag. Mild soap works best to avoid damage. Gently scrub any stains with a soft brush or cloth. Avoid bleach or harsh chemicals that can weaken fabric. Air dry your bag flat, away from direct sunlight. Do not put it in the dryer to prevent shrinking.

### Storage Tips

Store your tote bag in a dry, cool place. Keep it away from damp areas to prevent mold. Fold the bag neatly to avoid creases and wrinkles. Use a drawer or shelf to keep it clean. Avoid placing heavy items on top to keep its shape.
