## Bag Policy At Metlife Stadium

MetLife Stadium enforces a strict bag policy to ensure safety and speed up entry. Visitors must follow clear rules about what bags they can bring. Knowing these rules helps avoid delays and denied entry. The policy aims to keep everyone safe without causing inconvenience.

### Allowed Bag Types

Only certain bags are allowed inside MetLife Stadium. Clear bags are the safest and most common. Small clutch bags without straps are also permitted. Backpacks and large purses are generally not allowed. Fans should bring only essentials to avoid issues at the gate.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must fit within specific size limits. The maximum size for clear bags is 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. Clutch bags can be no larger than 4.5 inches by 6.5 inches. Oversized bags will be rejected. Measuring your bag before arrival saves time and stress.

### Clear Bag Rule

The Clear Bag Rule requires all bags to be see-through. This rule helps security check bags quickly. Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags work best. Solid colored bags or backpacks are not allowed. Following this rule speeds up entry and keeps lines moving.
