## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Luggage Size Is Best For 3 Weeks In Europe?



A 20 to 28-inch suitcase is ideal for a 3-week Europe trip. It offers enough space for clothes and essentials without being bulky or heavy.



### Are Spinner Wheels Important For European Travel Luggage?



Yes, spinner wheels provide smooth 360-degree movement. They make navigating airports and cobblestone streets in Europe easier and reduce travel strain.



### Is Tsa Lock Necessary For International Luggage?



TSA locks are recommended for international travel. They allow security to inspect bags without damage, ensuring your belongings stay safe and secure.



### Should I Choose Hardside Or Softside Luggage For Europe?



Hardside luggage offers better protection and water resistance. Softside luggage is lighter and expandable. Choose based on your packing style and durability needs.



### How Important Is Luggage Expandability For A 3-week Trip?



Expandable luggage adds up to 15% more space. It’s useful for souvenirs or extra items collected during your European trip.



### What Luggage Brands Are Reliable For Long European Trips?



Coolife, SwissGear, Travelpro, and Amazon Basics offer durable, lightweight sets. They include TSA locks and spinner wheels, ideal for extended European travel.
