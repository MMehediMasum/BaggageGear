## Delta's Carry-on Policy

Understanding Delta's carry-on policy can save you time and avoid unexpected fees at the airport. Knowing exactly what you can bring onboard helps you pack smarter and travel stress-free. Let’s break down the key details you need to know about Delta’s carry-on rules.

### Standard Allowance Details

Delta allows each passenger to bring one carry-on bag and one personal item onboard for free. This applies to most ticket types, including Main Cabin and Delta Comfort+. However, Basic Economy tickets come with some restrictions—you may only bring a personal item unless you pay for a carry-on bag or check a bag.

Thinking about how much you usually pack? You might want to reconsider if you’re flying Basic Economy, as the carry-on fee can add up. Have you ever been caught off guard by these rules at the gate?

### Size And Weight Limits

Your carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. The maximum size allowed is 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), including handles and wheels. Delta doesn’t specify a weight limit for carry-ons, but keep in mind you’ll need to lift it yourself into the overhead compartment.

This means even if your bag fits the size limit, an overly heavy bag could slow you down or cause issues boarding. Have you ever struggled to lift a heavy bag on a crowded plane?

### Personal Item Rules

Your personal item can be a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. It must fit under the seat in front of you, with typical dimensions around 18 x 14 x 8 inches (45 x 35 x 20 cm). This item is in addition to your carry-on bag and does not count toward the carry-on size limits.

Many travelers underestimate the space their personal item requires. Have you ever tried to fit a bulky laptop case under the seat only to realize there wasn’t enough room? Choosing a compact, soft-sided bag can help you avoid this hassle.

### When Fees Apply

Delta Airlines usually allows one free carry-on bag per passenger. This includes items like backpacks or small suitcases. Fees apply mainly to checked bags, not carry-ons. Yet, certain ticket types or routes might have different rules. If the carry-on is oversized or overweight, extra charges could occur. Always check your ticket details to confirm.

### Exceptions To Fees

Some passengers do not pay carry-on fees at all. Delta allows free carry-ons for all main cabin passengers. Military personnel traveling on orders get free carry-on and checked bags. Delta SkyMiles Medallion members also enjoy extra baggage allowances. Children under two years old can bring a small bag without fees. These exceptions help certain travelers save money.

### Fee Amounts And Variations

Carry-on fees are rare but possible in special cases. Overweight or oversized bags can cost between $75 and $200. These fees vary by route and ticket class. International flights may have different baggage policies. Delta's website provides the latest fee details. Knowing these fees helps passengers pack smartly and avoid extra costs.

## Tips To Avoid Carry-on Charges

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=carry-on-charges-can-sneak-up-on-you-and-add-unexpected-costs-to-your-delta-flight.-however,-with-a-few-smart-moves,-you-can-keep-your-carry-on-fees-at-bay.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Carry-on charges can sneak up on you and add unexpected costs to your Delta flight. However, with a few smart moves, you can keep your carry-on fees at bay. Understanding how to pack smartly, leverage your ticket perks, and use loyalty rewards can save you both money and hassle.

### Packing Strategies

Think small and light. Delta allows a personal item like a backpack or purse for free—use that space wisely to avoid paying for an extra carry-on.

Roll your clothes tightly to maximize space and fit more in your personal item. Use packing cubes to stay organized and compact.

Ask yourself, do you really need that extra pair of shoes or bulky jacket? Cutting down just one item can mean the difference between free and paid baggage.

### Ticket Class Benefits

Check your ticket class before packing. Delta’s Main Cabin Basic fares often charge for carry-ons, but Main Cabin and higher classes usually include one free carry-on.

If you’re booking a trip, consider if upgrading your ticket could save money on baggage fees over time. Sometimes, the extra cost upfront is cheaper than paying for bags separately.

Have you noticed how often you travel? If it’s frequent, those class benefits can add up quickly in savings.

### Loyalty Program Perks

Delta’s SkyMiles members enjoy perks that can waive carry-on charges. Silver Medallion status and above typically get free carry-ons on Delta flights.

Even without elite status, credit cards affiliated with Delta often include baggage fee waivers. Using the right card at booking can save you a significant amount.

Think about signing up for SkyMiles if you fly Delta regularly. The perks can offset the cost of your flights and luggage over time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Delta Airlines Charge For Carry-on Bags?

No, Delta Airlines generally does not charge for one standard carry-on bag. Passengers can bring a carry-on and a personal item free of charge on most flights.

### What Size Is Allowed For Delta Carry-on Luggage?

Delta allows carry-on bags up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including handles and wheels. Oversized bags may incur fees or require checked baggage.

### Are There Exceptions For Carry-on Fees On Delta?

Yes, some basic economy tickets may have restrictions or fees for carry-ons. Always check your ticket type and fare rules before traveling.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item For Free On Delta?

Yes, passengers can bring one personal item like a purse or laptop bag free of charge, in addition to their carry-on.
