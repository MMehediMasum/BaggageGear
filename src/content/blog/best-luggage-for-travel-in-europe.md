## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Luggage Size For Traveling In Europe?



Carry-on luggage around 20-22 inches is ideal for Europe. It fits overhead bins and suits short trips. Brands like Samsonite and SwissGear offer durable, lightweight options perfect for European travel.



### How Do Vacuum Bags Save Space In Travel Luggage?



Vacuum bags compress clothes by removing air, saving up to 50% space. They keep clothes organized and wrinkle-free. Vacbird’s rechargeable vacuum bags are great for efficient packing and maximizing luggage space.



### Why Use A Luggage Scale For European Trips?



A portable luggage scale prevents overweight baggage fees at airports. It ensures your suitcase meets airline weight limits. Travel scales like Travel Inspira are compact and easy to use for peace of mind.



### Are Hardshell Or Softside Suitcases Better For Europe?



Hardshell suitcases offer better protection and water resistance. Softside bags like SwissGear Sion provide flexibility and extra pockets. Choose based on your travel style and luggage handling preferences.



### How Do Packing Cubes Improve European Travel Packing?



Packing cubes organize clothes and essentials, making packing efficient. They reduce mess and speed up security checks. Veken’s packing cube sets include multiple sizes and additional laundry bags for convenience.



### What Features Should A European Travel Backpack Have?



A good travel backpack should be airline-approved, water-resistant, and have multiple compartments. LOVEVOOK’s 40L backpack fits a 17" laptop and includes packing cubes, perfect for carry-on and day trips in Europe.
