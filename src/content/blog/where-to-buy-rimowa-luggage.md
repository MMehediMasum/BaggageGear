## Tips For Authenticity

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=buying-rimowa-luggage-requires-careful-checks-to-ensure-authenticity.-fake-products-look-very-similar-but-lack-quality-and-durability.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Buying Rimowa luggage requires careful checks to ensure authenticity. Fake products look very similar but lack quality and durability. Knowing how to spot fakes and verify details protects your investment.

### Spotting Fake Rimowa Luggage

Look closely at the material and finish. Genuine Rimowa uses high-quality aluminum or polycarbonate shells. Fake bags often feel lighter or have rough surfaces.

* Check the logo. It should be clear and precisely engraved.
* Inspect the zippers and wheels. They run smoothly on authentic bags.
* Look inside for neat stitching and labels.
* Be cautious of unusually low prices or suspicious sellers.

### Checking Serial Numbers

Every Rimowa luggage piece has a unique serial number. It appears on a metal plate inside the suitcase. This number helps confirm authenticity.

* Compare the serial number with the purchase receipt.
* Contact Rimowa customer service to verify the number.
* Do not accept luggage without a visible serial number.
