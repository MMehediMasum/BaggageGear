## Spirit Airlines Baggage Policy

Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares, but understanding their baggage policy is key to avoiding unexpected fees. Their approach to baggage is quite strict, and the costs can add up quickly if you're not careful. Knowing exactly what’s allowed and what costs extra helps you pack smart and save money on your trip.

### Carry-on Allowance

Spirit Airlines allows one standard carry-on bag per passenger, but there’s a catch: you must pay an additional fee to bring it onboard unless you have purchased the “Bundle It” option or a higher fare class. This carry-on must fit within the size limit of 22 x 18 x 10 inches.

If you try to bring a carry-on without prepaying, Spirit will charge you a hefty fee at the gate. I once forgot to pay for my carry-on and ended up paying more than my ticket cost just to bring my bag onboard. It’s a small step that can save you a lot of money if you plan ahead.

### Personal Item Rules

Each passenger can bring one personal item free of charge. This item must fit under the seat in front of you, with maximum dimensions of 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Examples include a backpack, purse, or laptop bag.

Spirit is very strict about these size limits. I’ve seen travelers have their personal items measured at the gate and be forced to check them — which comes with a fee. Make sure your personal item fits the rules to avoid surprises.

### Checked Baggage Limits

Checked bags on Spirit Airlines start at a fee, which varies by route and when you pay. The maximum weight per checked bag is 40 pounds, and the size limit is 62 linear inches (length + width + height).

Going over these limits means extra charges: overweight bags can cost an additional $65 or more, and oversized bags may face similar fees. Booking your checked baggage online before arriving at the airport can save you significant money compared to paying at the counter or gate.

### Fee Breakdown By Booking Time

Booking early usually means lower carry-on fees on Spirit Airlines. Prices increase closer to your flight date and at the airport.

* Booking during initial purchase: Lowest fees
* Adding carry-on during online check-in: Higher fees
* Paying at the airport: Highest fees

Plan ahead and add your carry-on bag early to pay less.

### Size And Weight Restrictions

Spirit allows one personal item for free, but carry-on bags cost extra. Carry-ons must fit in the overhead bin.

* Maximum size: 22 x 18 x 10 inches (including handles and wheels)
* Weight limit: No official limit, but bag must fit safely overhead

Larger or overweight bags may be checked and charged additional fees.

### Ways To Avoid Carry-on Fees

* Bring only a personal item that fits under the seat
* Purchase a bundle or “The Big Front Seat” package that includes a carry-on
* Use a smaller backpack or tote as your free personal item

Travel light to avoid extra charges and speed up boarding.

Checked bag costs on Spirit Airlines vary based on when and how you pay. Prices can change depending on your flight route and booking time. Paying early usually means lower fees. Understanding these costs helps you avoid surprises at the airport.

### First Bag Pricing

The first checked bag fee starts low if paid during booking. Prices rise at the airport. Typically, the first bag costs between $30 and $45. This fee applies for most domestic flights. Booking online saves money.

### Second And Additional Bags

Second checked bags cost more than the first. The fee usually ranges from $40 to $65. Fees increase for every extra bag after the second. Carrying multiple bags can add up quickly. Planning ahead reduces extra expenses.

### Overweight And Oversize Charges

Bags over 40 pounds face overweight fees. These charges start around $65 and go up. Oversized bags larger than 62 inches also have extra fees. These costs can be as high as $150. Checking bag size and weight prevents high fees.

Musicians face unique challenges flying with instruments on Spirit. Small instruments may fit as carry-ons, but larger ones often require special handling and fees. Spirit typically charges a fee similar to checked baggage for bigger instruments.

Have you ever struggled to find safe transport for your favorite instrument? Booking early and informing Spirit about your instrument can make the process smoother. Also, investing in a sturdy case protects your gear and may prevent additional costs.

Checked bag fees on Spirit range from $30 to $65 per bag each way. Early online payment is cheaper. Fees increase with bag weight and payment timing, so pay in advance to avoid high costs.
