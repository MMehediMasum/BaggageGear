## Tiger Woods' Driver

Tiger Woods' driver is more than just a club; it’s a finely tuned instrument that plays a crucial role in his game. The right driver can add yards, improve accuracy, and boost confidence off the tee. ### Model And Specifications

Tiger Woods uses a TaylorMade driver, often customized from the popular SIM or Stealth series. The clubhead is typically around 9 degrees of loft, which suits his powerful and controlled swing.

His driver shaft is usually a Fujikura Ventus Black, designed to provide stability and optimize launch conditions. The length of the shaft is slightly shorter than standard, around 44.5 inches, to help with control and consistency.

### Customization Details

Tiger’s driver is far from stock. His club is finely adjusted to match his swing tempo and ball flight preferences. He prefers a stiffer shaft to maintain control at high swing speeds, reducing unwanted side spin.

The clubhead’s weight distribution is customized to optimize forgiveness and promote a slight draw bias. This helps Tiger keep the ball in play, especially under pressure.

Grip size and texture are also tailored to his preference, ensuring a secure hold and better feel throughout the swing. These small details can make a big difference in performance.

* **Increased Distance:**The combination of loft, shaft stiffness, and optimized clubhead design helps Tiger generate maximum ball speed.
* **Improved Accuracy:**Custom weighting reduces slices and hooks, allowing more fairways hit even on challenging courses.
* **Consistent Launch:**Tiger’s driver setup promotes a stable launch angle, giving him control over trajectory and spin.
