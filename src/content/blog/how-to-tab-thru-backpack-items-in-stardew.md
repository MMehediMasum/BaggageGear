### Navigating Your Inventory

Your backpack in Stardew Valley is divided into slots arranged in rows and columns. When you press the Tab key, the selection jumps from one item slot to the next in a left-to-right, top-to-bottom order. This means you don’t have to use your mouse to pick items; your keyboard does the work for you.

Think about how many times you’ve had to scroll endlessly to find seeds or tools. Tabbing makes that unnecessary. It lets you quickly locate the item you want without breaking your rhythm.

### Key Controls For Tabbing

* **Tab Key:**Moves the selection forward through each item slot.
* **Shift + Tab:**Moves the selection backward, so you can go back if you miss an item.
* **Enter or Spacebar:**Selects or uses the currently highlighted item.
* **Arrow Keys:**Offer precise movement within the inventory grid if you want to jump faster.

Using these controls feels natural once you get the hang of them.
