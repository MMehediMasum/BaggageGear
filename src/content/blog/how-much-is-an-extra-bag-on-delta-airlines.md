## Delta Airlines Baggage Policies

Understanding Delta Airlines baggage policies can save you from unexpected fees and travel hassles. Whether you're packing light or bringing extra gear, knowing what is allowed and what costs extra is key. Let’s break down the essentials so you can plan your trip with confidence and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Delta Airlines allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger at no extra charge. Your carry-on should fit in the overhead bin, while the personal item can slide under the seat in front of you. Common personal items include a backpack, purse, or laptop bag.

Carry-on size limits are 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), including handles and wheels. You might wonder, “Can I bring a larger bag if I’m willing to pay?” The answer is no—oversized carry-ons usually must be checked and may incur extra fees. Planning your carry-on size carefully can keep your boarding process smooth and stress-free.

### Checked Bag Basics

Checked baggage policies vary based on your ticket type, destination, and frequent flyer status. Typically, the first checked bag costs $30, and the second bag costs $40 on domestic flights. Prices tend to increase for additional bags or overweight items.

| Checked Bag | Fee (Domestic Flights) |
| --- | --- |
| First checked bag (up to 50 lbs) | $30 |
| Second checked bag (up to 50 lbs) | $40 |
| Third bag and beyond | $150 per bag |
| Overweight bags (51-70 lbs) | $100 per bag |

Thinking about bringing an extra bag? It pays to check if your ticket or credit card offers free checked bags. Sometimes, paying upfront for extra bags online is cheaper than at the airport. Have you ever had to pay surprise fees at check-in? Planning ahead helps you avoid those moments.

## Oversized And Overweight Bag Charges

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=delta-airlines-charges-extra-for-bags-that-are-too-big-or-too-heavy.-these-fees-help-cover-the-extra-handling-and-space-needed-for-such-luggage.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Delta Airlines charges extra for bags that are too big or too heavy. These fees help cover the extra handling and space needed for such luggage. Knowing these charges can save money and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Size Limits And Fees

Delta accepts checked bags with a maximum size of 62 linear inches. This means the total of length, width, and height must not exceed 62 inches. Bags larger than this are considered oversized.

Oversized bags face additional fees, which vary by destination. Common fees range from $150 to $200 per bag. These charges apply whether the bag is just over the limit or much bigger.

### Weight Restrictions And Penalties

Delta allows checked bags up to 50 pounds without extra fees. Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds incur a fee of $100. Bags over 70 pounds have a higher fee, usually $200 per bag.

Heavy bags are harder to handle and can cause delays. Paying the overweight fee ensures safe and smooth handling of your luggage. Always weigh your bags before arriving at the airport to avoid these charges.
