## Alaska Airlines Baggage Policy

Alaska Airlines sets clear rules for baggage to help travelers pack smart. Knowing these rules saves time and avoids extra fees. The airline has limits on the size and weight of bags. Fees apply based on your ticket type and route. Understanding these details helps you plan your trip better.

### Checked Bag Allowance

Most travelers can bring one or two checked bags. Each bag must not exceed 50 pounds. The size limit is 62 inches total (length + width + height). Fees depend on your ticket class and destination. Some passengers, like Mileage Plan elites, get free checked bags.

* First checked bag fee: Usually $30
* Second checked bag fee: Usually $40
* Additional bags: Higher fees apply
* Oversized or overweight bags: Extra charges

Check your ticket details before packing. Paying baggage fees online is cheaper than at the airport.

### Carry-on Bag Rules

Each passenger can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item. Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin. The maximum size is 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Personal items include purses, laptops, or small backpacks. Items must fit under the seat in front of you.

* Carry-on weight limit: No official limit, but must be manageable
* Personal item examples: Laptop bag, purse, briefcase
* Prohibited items: Check Alaska Airlines’ list before travel

Carry-on bags save time and fees at the airport. Pack light to avoid delays during boarding.

## Oversized And Overweight Baggage Fees

Handling oversized and overweight baggage with Alaska Airlines can impact your travel budget more than you expect. Knowing the fees upfront helps you avoid surprises at the airport. Let's break down the key details so you can pack smart and save money.

### Weight Limits And Charges

Alaska Airlines sets a weight limit of 50 pounds (23 kg) for checked bags without extra fees. If your bag weighs between 51 and 100 pounds (24 to 45 kg), you’ll face an overweight baggage fee.

* Overweight bags (51-100 lbs): $100 per bag
* Bags over 100 lbs are not accepted

Think about the items you pack. Could you redistribute weight between bags or carry some items onboard? This strategy can help you avoid the $100 overweight fee.

### Size Restrictions And Costs

Alaska Airlines allows checked bags up to 62 linear inches (length + width + height). Bags exceeding this size are subject to oversized baggage fees.

* Oversized bags (63-115 linear inches): $100 per bag
* Bags larger than 115 linear inches are not accepted

Have you ever tried fitting your suitcase through a measuring frame at the airport? A quick check at home with a tape measure can save you that $100 charge. When packing bulky items, consider if they can be shipped separately or broken down to meet size limits.
