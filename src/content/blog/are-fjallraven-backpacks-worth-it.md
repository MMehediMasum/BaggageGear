### Origins And Evolution

Fjallraven began in 1960 in Sweden, founded by Åke Nordin, who was just 14 years old. He wasn’t happy with the heavy, uncomfortable backpacks available, so he designed his own lightweight frame pack. This simple innovation sparked the brand’s focus on functional, practical outdoor gear.

Over the years, Fjallraven expanded its range but stayed true to its roots. The iconic Kånken backpack was introduced in 1978 to help prevent back problems in school children. Its clean design and durability quickly made it popular beyond Sweden, transforming it into a global symbol of quality.

### Core Values And Mission

Fjallraven’s mission goes beyond making backpacks. They aim to help people get closer to nature while protecting the environment. This focus is clear in their use of eco-friendly materials like G-1000 fabric, which is tough and repairable, reducing waste.

## Care And Maintenance

Taking good care of your Fjallraven backpack ensures it stays durable and looks great over time. These backpacks are built tough, but proper care will help maintain their iconic fabric and structure. Let’s dive into some simple steps to keep your backpack in top shape.

### Cleaning Instructions

Cleaning your Fjallraven backpack doesn’t have to be complicated. Use a soft brush or cloth to remove dirt and dust from the surface regularly. For deeper cleaning, hand wash it with cold water and mild soap—avoid harsh detergents that can damage the fabric.

Resist the urge to machine wash or tumble dry your backpack. Instead, rinse thoroughly and let it air dry naturally, away from direct sunlight or heat sources. Have you noticed how gentle cleaning keeps the waxed canvas looking fresh without losing its water-resistant qualities?

### Storage Tips

Proper storage can extend your backpack’s life significantly. Store it in a cool, dry place to prevent mildew and fabric degradation. Avoid stuffing it into tight spaces where it can lose shape or get crushed.
