## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Trunk Is Best For Sleepaway Camp?



A trunk around 30-32 inches long fits most sleepaway camp needs. It offers ample space without being too bulky. Models like the Rhino Trunk 32"x18"x14" provide a good balance of storage and portability for campers.



### Are Rolling Trunks Better For Camp Storage?



Yes, rolling trunks like the Pacific Coast Signature 30" Rolling Duffel bag ease transport. Wheels reduce strain when carrying heavy gear. Rolling trunks also simplify moving your belongings across campgrounds or dorms.



### How Durable Are Heavy-duty Soft Trunks For Camps?



Heavy-duty soft trunks, such as Gilbins 1800 Denier models, are highly durable. They resist tears and water, protecting your gear well. Their strong materials suit rugged camp environments and frequent travel.



### Can Trunks With Embroidered Names Prevent Mix-ups?



Yes, personalized trunks with embroidered names reduce mix-ups. They help campers quickly identify their belongings. Customization adds a personal touch and ensures your trunk stands out in shared storage areas.



### What Capacity Should A Camp Trunk Have?



A capacity of around 1500+ cubic inches is ideal for sleepaway camp. It holds clothes, supplies, and gear securely. Trunks like the Rhino Trunk & Case models offer over 1000 lbs load capacity, ensuring strength and space.



### Are Mesh Shower Caddies Useful For Camp Hygiene?



Mesh shower caddies like famichan are great for camp hygiene. They keep toiletries organized and dry with good ventilation. Lightweight and portable, they fit well in shared bathroom setups at camps.
