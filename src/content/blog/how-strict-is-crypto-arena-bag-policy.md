## Crypto Arena Bag Policy Basics

The Crypto Arena enforces a clear bag policy to maintain safety and speed at events. Visitors must follow specific rules about the types and sizes of bags allowed. Understanding these basics helps guests prepare and avoid delays during entry.

### Permitted Bag Types

Only certain bags are allowed inside Crypto Arena. Clear bags made of plastic, vinyl, or PVC are accepted. Small clutches without a strap can also be carried. Backpacks, totes, and large purses are not permitted unless they meet the size rules.

* Clear plastic bags
* Clear vinyl or PVC bags
* Small clutches without straps (no larger than 4.5" x 6.5")

### Size And Dimension Limits

The arena limits bag size to speed up security checks. Bags must not exceed 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. Clutches must be smaller than 4.5 inches by 6.5 inches. Bags larger than this will be denied entry or must be returned to the car or home.
