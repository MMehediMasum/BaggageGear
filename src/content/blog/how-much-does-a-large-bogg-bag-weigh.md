## How To Weigh Your Bogg Bag

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=knowing-the-weight-of-your-large-bogg-bag-helps-with-packing-and-travel-plans.-weighing-it-is-simple-and-takes-little-time.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Knowing the weight of your large Bogg Bag helps with packing and travel plans. Weighing it is simple and takes little time. You can use a scale or estimate the weight without tools. Both ways give you a good idea of how heavy your bag is.

### Using A Scale

A scale gives the most accurate weight for your Bogg Bag. Use a bathroom or luggage scale for best results. Follow these steps:

* Place the empty scale on a flat surface.
* Turn the scale on and wait for it to show zero.
* Lift your Bogg Bag and hold it steadily on the scale.
* Read the weight shown on the scale display.

This method works well for full or empty bags. It helps you avoid overpacking or exceeding weight limits.

### Estimating Weight Without Tools

If you lack a scale, you can estimate weight by comparing with known objects. This method is less exact but still useful.

* Think of items you have weighed before, like a gallon of water (about 8.3 pounds).
* Compare how heavy your Bogg Bag feels against those items.
* Use your body strength as a guide—does the bag feel light, medium, or heavy?

Ask a friend to help by lifting the bag and sharing their opinion. Estimating is handy when no scale is nearby.
