Wrigley Field requires fans to carry clear bags for entry. This means your bag must be transparent enough for security to see what’s inside without digging through it. The rule helps speed up security checks and reduces wait times.

Size matters at Wrigley Field. Bags must be no larger than 12" x 6" x 12". This size restriction is strict, so even if your bag is clear, it still has to fit within these dimensions.

Why does size matter? Smaller bags mean less chance of prohibited items sneaking in and easier inspection for security staff. Before heading out, measure your bag. If it’s bigger, consider switching to a smaller clear tote or a clutch.

Not all bags are created equal at Wrigley Field. The approved types include:
