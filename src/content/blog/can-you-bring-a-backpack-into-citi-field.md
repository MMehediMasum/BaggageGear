### Citi Field Bag Size Limits

Only small bags are allowed inside Citi Field. Backpacks must be no larger than 16x16x8 inches. Larger bags are not permitted. Clear bags are preferred for faster security checks.

### Types Of Bags Allowed

* Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags
* One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
* Small clutch bags no larger than 4.5x6.5 inches
* Small backpacks within size limits

### Prohibited Items In Bags

* No large backpacks over size limits
* No duffel bags or purses larger than allowed
* No outside food or drinks
* No weapons or sharp objects

### Tips For Bringing A Backpack

Choose a clear backpack to save time at security. Pack only essentials to stay within size rules. Avoid bringing prohibited items to prevent delays. Check bag size before arrival to ensure compliance.
