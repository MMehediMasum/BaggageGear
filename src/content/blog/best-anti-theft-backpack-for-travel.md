## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Backpack Anti-theft?



Anti-theft backpacks include features like lockable zippers, RFID-blocking pockets, and hidden compartments. These features help protect valuables from pickpockets and electronic theft. Some models also offer slash-resistant material and integrated USB charging ports for added convenience during travel.



### Are Anti-theft Backpacks Water-resistant?



Yes, many anti-theft backpacks are water-resistant. They are designed to protect your belongings from rain and spills. Materials like waterproof nylon or polyester are commonly used. Always check the product specifications to confirm water resistance before purchasing.



### Can Anti-theft Backpacks Fit A 17-inch Laptop?



Yes, several anti-theft backpacks can accommodate a 17-inch laptop. Models like the BANGE and KROSER backpacks offer spacious compartments. Always verify the backpack's dimensions to ensure it fits your specific laptop model securely.



### Do Anti-theft Backpacks Have Usb Charging Ports?



Yes, many anti-theft backpacks come with USB charging ports. These allow you to charge devices on the go. You will need a portable power bank, as it is not included. This feature is particularly useful for travelers needing quick device charging.



### Are Anti-theft Backpacks Suitable For Business Travel?



Yes, anti-theft backpacks are ideal for business travel. They offer security features like RFID-blocking pockets and lockable zippers. Additionally, they often include laptop compartments and USB charging ports. These features ensure safety and convenience during business trips.



### How Do Rfid-blocking Pockets Work?



RFID-blocking pockets prevent unauthorized scanning of your credit cards and passports. They use a special material that blocks radio waves. This protects personal information from electronic pickpocketing, ensuring your data remains secure while traveling. Always check if your backpack includes this feature for added security.
