Rockland uses materials like polycarbonate, ABS, and fabric. Polycarbonate is strong and flexible. It can take rough handling without cracking. ABS is lighter but less flexible. The choice of material impacts the luggage’s weight too. Hard-shell cases protect contents better. Soft-shell cases offer more pockets and expandability. Rockland’s materials balance strength and lightness for travelers.
