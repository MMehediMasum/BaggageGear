## Delta Carry-on Size Limits

### Standard Dimensions

Delta allows carry-on bags with a maximum size of **22 x 14 x 9 inches**including handles and wheels. This size fits in the overhead bin, so your bag stays with you during the flight.

Think about your last trip—did you check a bag because your carry-on was too big? Sticking to these dimensions can help you skip the baggage line altogether.

### Weight Restrictions

Delta does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on luggage, but your bag must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead bin without assistance.

This is practical because heavier bags slow down boarding and can cause damage if dropped. If your carry-on feels heavy, consider shifting some items to your personal item.

### Personal Item Allowance

Along with your carry-on bag, Delta lets you bring one personal item like a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. This item must fit under the seat in front of you.

Typical personal item dimensions are about **18 x 14 x 8 inches**. Have you ever struggled to find space for your laptop bag? Keeping it within this size means easy access and no gate-checking.
