## Away Luggage Warranty Basics

Away luggage offers a warranty that covers specific issues with their products. Understanding the basics of this warranty helps buyers know what protection they have. This section explains key points about the Away luggage warranty.

### Coverage Details

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover damage from normal wear and tear. Problems caused by accidents or misuse are excluded. The warranty applies only to the original owner. It protects the hard shell, zippers, wheels, and handles.

### Warranty Duration

Away offers a limited warranty that lasts for five years. This period begins from the date of purchase. The warranty does not extend beyond this time. Buyers should keep their receipts as proof of purchase. Repairs or replacements are available only within this timeframe.

### What’s Included

* Repair or replacement of broken wheels
* Fixing faulty zippers or handles
* Correction of manufacturing defects
* Repair of the hard shell if it cracks under normal use

Damage from airline mishandling is not covered. Cosmetic scratches and dents are excluded. The warranty does not cover lost or stolen luggage.

## Tips For Maintaining Away Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=maintaining-away-luggage-extends-its-life-and-keeps-it-looking-fresh.-proper-care-avoids-damage-and-saves-money.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Maintaining Away luggage extends its life and keeps it looking fresh. Proper care avoids damage and saves money. Simple habits make a big difference in how long your luggage lasts. Follow these tips to keep your bag strong and clean for every trip.

### Cleaning Recommendations

Use a soft cloth to wipe the outside of your luggage. Mild soap and water work best for removing dirt. Avoid harsh chemicals that can weaken the material. Let your bag dry fully before storing it. Clean the inside with a handheld vacuum or a damp cloth. Regular cleaning prevents stains and odors from setting in.

### Storage Best Practices

Store your luggage in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Avoid stacking heavy items on top to prevent dents or cracks. Keep zippers closed to maintain shape and protect from dust. Use dust bags or covers for extra protection. Make sure your bag is empty and dry before storing to stop mold and mildew.

### Preventing Common Damage

* Handle your luggage gently to avoid scratches and dents.
* Do not overload your bag; follow the weight limits.
* Use luggage straps to secure your bag during travel.
* Check wheels and handles regularly for wear and tear.
* Repair small issues early to prevent bigger problems later.
