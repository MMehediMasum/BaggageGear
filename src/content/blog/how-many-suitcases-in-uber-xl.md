### Carry-on Vs Checked Bags

Carry-on bags are usually smaller and designed to fit in overhead compartments on airplanes. Their typical dimensions are about 22 x 14 x 9 inches. These bags often fit easily into an Uber XL, allowing you to bring more items without crowding the car.

Checked bags, on the other hand, are larger and meant to be stored in the plane’s cargo hold. They can measure up to 27 x 21 x 14 inches or more. These bigger bags take up more space, so you might only fit two or three in an Uber XL, depending on the vehicle and other passengers' luggage.

### Measuring Your Suitcase

Before your ride, measure your suitcase to ensure it fits comfortably in an Uber XL. Use a tape measure to check the length, width, and height.

* Length: Measure from one end of the suitcase to the other.
* Width: Measure the side-to-side dimension.
* Height: Measure from the base to the top, including wheels and handles.

Keep in mind that Uber XL can fit about 3 large suitcases or 4-5 carry-ons. If your bags are oversized, consider how many people and bags will be sharing the ride. Have you ever squeezed in too many bags and made the ride uncomfortable? Measuring beforehand helps avoid that problem.

### Packing Tips

Choose soft-sided bags instead of hard-shell suitcases when possible. They’re easier to squeeze into tight spaces and can flex around other items. Rolling your clothes instead of folding them saves space and reduces wrinkles.

Consider packing cubes to keep things organized and compact. You might be surprised how much extra room these small organizers can create. Also, avoid overpacking bulky items; wear your heaviest clothes during the ride to free up suitcase space.

### Arranging Bags Efficiently

Place the largest suitcases flat on the floor of the Uber XL’s trunk to build a stable base. Stack smaller bags on top, filling gaps with soft items like backpacks or jackets. This method helps you use the vertical space wisely without risking damage to your luggage.

Think about the shape of each bag and how they can nest together. For example, a duffel bag can often fit into the curve of a suitcase. If you’re traveling with others, coordinate your packing styles to avoid wasted space—sometimes just swapping one bag for another can make a big difference.
