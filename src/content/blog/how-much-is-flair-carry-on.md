## Flair Carry On Basics

Understanding the basics of Flair's carry on policy is essential before you pack your bags. Knowing the size and weight limits can save you from unexpected fees at the airport. It also helps you pack smarter and travel lighter.

### Size And Weight Limits

Flair Airlines allows carry on bags that must fit within specific dimensions. Your bag cannot be larger than **21 inches x 15 inches x 9 inches**, including handles and wheels.

The weight limit for carry on luggage is typically **15 pounds (7 kg)**. If your bag exceeds this weight, you may have to check it in or pay extra fees.

Have you ever struggled to fit your bag in the overhead bin? Sticking to these limits will help avoid that hassle and speed up your boarding process.

### Included Carry On Allowance

Flair includes one free carry on bag per passenger on most fares. This allowance covers your main bag and a personal item like a purse or laptop bag.

Your personal item must fit under the seat in front of you, with typical dimensions around **17 inches x 13 inches x 7 inches**. This gives you extra space for essentials without worrying about extra charges.

It’s smart to pack a small bag with valuables and documents as your personal item. This way, you keep important things close and avoid digging through your main carry on during the flight.

Yes, Flair strictly enforces carry-on size limits of 21 x 15 x 9 inches. Bags exceeding these dimensions may be checked and charged fees. It’s best to measure your bag before travel to avoid surprises.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item For Free On Flair?

Yes, Flair permits one free personal item like a purse or laptop bag. It must fit under the seat and meet size limits of 16 x 12 x 6 inches. This is separate from the carry-on allowance.

### What Happens If My Carry-on Is Overweight On Flair?

If your carry-on exceeds the 10 kg weight limit, Flair may require you to check it. Overweight bags can incur fees, which vary by flight. Weigh your bag beforehand to avoid extra charges.
