## Checked Bag Weight Limits

Knowing the checked bag weight limits on American Airlines can save you from unexpected fees and travel hassles. Each airline sets specific rules, and American Airlines is no exception. Understanding these limits helps you pack smartly and avoid last-minute repacking or extra charges.

### Standard Allowance

American Airlines allows a checked bag to weigh up to **50 pounds (23 kg)**for most economy tickets without extra fees. If you’re flying in business or first class, the limit often increases to **70 pounds (32 kg)**.

Each passenger typically can check one or two bags, depending on the fare type and destination. Keep in mind, the size also matters—bags should not exceed **62 inches (158 cm)**in total dimensions (length + width + height).

### Oversized And Overweight Fees

What happens if your bag tips the scales over the limit? American Airlines charges overweight fees starting at **$100**for bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds. Bags over 70 pounds can cost up to **$200**per bag.

Oversized bags—those exceeding 62 inches in total dimensions—also come with additional fees. These can range from **$150 to $200**depending on the exact size and destination.

Have you ever had to pay these fees unexpectedly? Preparing ahead by weighing your bags at home can prevent surprises at the airport. Do you keep a small luggage scale in your suitcase? It’s a simple tool that can save you a lot of money and stress.

Plus, the size limit remains the same at 62 linear inches, but you usually get to check two bags without extra fees.
