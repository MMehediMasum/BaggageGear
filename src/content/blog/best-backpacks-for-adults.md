## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Features Make A Backpack Travel-friendly?



Travel-friendly backpacks have water resistance, TSA approval, and spacious compartments. USB charging ports and anti-theft designs add convenience and security during trips.



### Which Backpacks Fit 17 To 18-inch Laptops Best?



Backpacks like YOREPEK, KROSER, and Travel Extra Large models fit laptops up to 18. 4 inches. They offer padded sleeves and secure compartments for protection.



### Are Usb Charging Ports Useful In Backpacks?



Yes, USB charging ports provide easy device charging on the go. They connect to internal power banks, making travel and daily use convenient.



### How Do Anti-theft Backpacks Protect Your Belongings?



Anti-theft backpacks use hidden zippers, lockable compartments, and RFID-blocking pockets. These features safeguard valuables from theft and unauthorized scanning.



### What Backpacks Suit Both Men And Women For Work?



Unisex backpacks like KROSER, LARIPOP, and YOREPEK combine style and functionality. They offer ergonomic straps, multiple compartments, and professional looks suitable for all.



### Can Large Backpacks Be Airline Carry-ons?



Yes, backpacks with 50-55L capacity like YOREPEK and Travel Extra Large meet airline carry-on size limits. They are designed for easy boarding and storage.
