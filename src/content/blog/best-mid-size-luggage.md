## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Ideal For Mid-size Luggage?



Mid-size luggage typically ranges from 23 to 26 inches. This size fits most checked baggage requirements. It offers ample space without being too bulky. Models like the SwissGear 25-inch and Samsonite 24-inch fit this ideal size for medium trips.



### Are Expandable Luggage Options Worth It?



Yes, expandable luggage adds 10-15% extra packing space. It’s useful for souvenirs or last-minute items. Many mid-size options, such as Samsonite Omni PC, feature expandable designs. This flexibility ensures you pack efficiently without needing a larger suitcase.



### Which Luggage Has The Best Wheel System?



Spinner wheels offer smooth 360-degree mobility. Most mid-size luggage, like Samsonite Winfield 2 and Away Medium, feature durable spinner wheels. These wheels reduce strain and make airport navigation easier, especially on tiled or carpeted floors.



### Is Tsa Lock Important For Checked Luggage?



Yes, TSA locks provide security and comply with airport regulations. They allow TSA agents to inspect luggage without damage. Many mid-size models, including Away and KROSER, come with built-in TSA locks for safer travel.



### How Durable Are Hardshell Suitcases?



Hardshell suitcases are highly durable and protect belongings well. Materials like polycarbonate resist scratches and impacts. Models like Samsonite Freeform and Amazon Basics offer tough, lightweight shells ideal for frequent travelers. They handle rough handling better than softside cases.



### Do Mid-size Suitcases Fit Airline Checked Baggage Rules?



Most mid-size suitcases, between 23-26 inches, meet airline checked baggage standards. They usually comply with weight and dimension limits for domestic and international flights. Always verify airline-specific rules, but models listed here are generally airline-approved.
