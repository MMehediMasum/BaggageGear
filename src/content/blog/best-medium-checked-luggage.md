## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Best For Medium Checked Luggage?



Medium checked luggage typically ranges from 24 to 28 inches. This size fits most airline requirements and offers ample packing space. It balances capacity and portability, ideal for week-long trips or business travel.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Suitcases Better For Durability?



Hardside luggage offers superior protection against impacts and scratches. Softside suitcases are more flexible and lightweight. For durability, hardshell models like Samsonite Omni PC are preferred by frequent travelers.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Medium Luggage?



Spinner wheels enhance maneuverability by rotating 360 degrees. They reduce wrist strain and make navigation through airports easier. Most top models, including Amazon Basics and Travelpro Maxlite, feature multi-directional spinner wheels.



### Can Medium Luggage Be Expandable For Extra Space?



Yes, many medium suitcases offer expandable features. This allows up to 15% more packing space when needed. Expandable luggage like Amazon Basics 26" provides flexibility for souvenirs or additional items.



### Do Medium Checked Bags Come With Tsa Locks?



Most medium checked luggage includes built-in TSA-approved locks. These locks ensure security and comply with airport regulations. Brands like Samsonite and Amazon Basics integrate TSA locks for traveler safety.



### Which Material Is Best For Lightweight Medium Luggage?



Polycarbonate and polypropylene are popular for lightweight, durable hardshell luggage. Softside fabrics like ballistic nylon also offer lightness and flexibility. Samsonite and Kenneth Cole models use these materials effectively.
