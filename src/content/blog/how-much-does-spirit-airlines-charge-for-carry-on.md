## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Fees

Spirit Airlines charges fees for carry-on bags, unlike some other airlines. Passengers must pay to bring a larger carry-on bag onboard. The price varies depending on when and how you pay. Knowing these fees helps you plan better and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Spirit allows one personal item for free. This item must fit under the seat in front of you. Examples include a small backpack, purse, or laptop bag. If your bag is bigger and goes in the overhead bin, you must pay a fee.

### Fee Breakdown By Booking Time

| When You Pay | Typical Fee Range |
| --- | --- |
| During Booking | $35 - $45 |
| Before Check-In | $45 - $50 |
| At Airport | $60 - $65 |

Paying early saves money. Fees rise as the flight day approaches.

### Online Vs Airport Charges

* Online payments are cheaper than airport payments.
* Paying at the airport can cost up to double.
* Spirit recommends paying online to avoid high fees.
* Online check-in opens 24 hours before the flight.

Plan ahead to pay less for your carry-on bag. Avoid last-minute airport fees by adding your bag online.

## Personal Item Policy

Spirit Airlines has a specific personal item policy that passengers must follow. A personal item is usually a small bag you can carry on board without extra charge. This policy helps ensure every passenger has space for their belongings.

Knowing the rules for personal items can save you money. It also makes your travel experience smoother and less stressful.

### Size And Weight Limits

Your personal item must fit under the seat in front of you. The maximum size allowed is 18 x 14 x 8 inches. This includes handles and wheels.

Spirit Airlines does not have a strict weight limit for personal items. However, the item should be easy to lift and store safely under the seat.

### Cost For Additional Items

Only one personal item is free with your ticket. If you bring a carry-on bag larger than the personal item, you will pay extra fees.

Additional carry-on bags can cost between $35 to $65 or more. The price depends on when you pay—online, at the airport, or at the gate.

To avoid fees, pack only one personal item that meets the size limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does Spirit Airlines Charge For A Carry-on?

Spirit Airlines charges $32 to $65 for a carry-on bag. The exact fee depends on when you pay—online, at the airport, or at the gate. Booking your carry-on online is the cheapest option.

### What Is Spirit Airlines' Carry-on Size Limit?

Spirit Airlines allows carry-on bags up to 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This size includes handles and wheels. Bags exceeding this size may be subject to additional fees or checked as regular luggage.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item For Free On Spirit?

Yes, Spirit Airlines lets you bring one free personal item. It must fit under the seat in front of you. Typical items include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.

### Are Carry-on Fees Refundable On Spirit Airlines?

No, Spirit Airlines' carry-on fees are generally non-refundable. Once paid, the fee cannot be canceled or reimbursed. Make sure to purchase carry-on allowance before your trip to avoid extra charges.
