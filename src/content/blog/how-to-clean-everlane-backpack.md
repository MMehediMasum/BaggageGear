**You've invested in an Everlane backpack, a stylish and sustainable choice that's perfect for your everyday adventures. But after a few months of use, you might notice it doesn't look as fresh as it once did.**

The thought of cleaning it might seem daunting, but don't worry—keeping your backpack in pristine condition is easier than you think. In this guide, you'll discover simple, effective methods to clean your Everlane backpack without compromising its quality. Stick around to learn the secrets of maintaining its sleek appearance and extending its life, so it continues to be your trusty companion for many journeys to come.




## Materials Needed

Cleaning your Everlane backpack requires a few simple materials. These items help keep your bag fresh without causing damage. Use gentle tools to protect the fabric and zippers. Gather everything before starting for a smooth cleaning process.

### Soft Cloth Or Sponge

A soft cloth or sponge cleans dirt gently. Avoid rough materials that can scratch the fabric. Choose something absorbent for better cleaning results.

### Mild Detergent

Use a mild detergent to remove stains and grime. Harsh chemicals can damage the backpack’s material. A gentle soap keeps the fabric safe and clean.

### Cold Water

Cold water works best for cleaning the backpack. It prevents colors from fading. Avoid hot water to keep the fabric strong.

### Soft Brush

A soft brush helps remove tough dirt from seams and corners. Use light pressure to avoid damage. This tool is perfect for deep cleaning.

### Towel For Drying

A clean towel dries the backpack after washing. Pat the fabric gently to remove moisture. Avoid direct sunlight for drying to protect color.




## Preparation Before Cleaning

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=preparation-is-key-before-cleaning-your-everlane-backpack.-it-helps-protect-the-fabric-and-keeps-the-cleaning-process-smooth.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Preparation is key before cleaning your Everlane backpack. It helps protect the fabric and keeps the cleaning process smooth. Taking time to prepare ensures better results and prevents damage. Follow these simple steps to get your backpack ready for cleaning.

### Emptying The Backpack

Remove all items from every pocket and compartment. Check small sections carefully for hidden objects. This step prevents damage to belongings and helps clean every spot inside. Shake the backpack gently to dislodge loose crumbs and debris.

### Removing Loose Dirt

Use a soft brush or cloth to wipe off dirt on the surface. Focus on seams, zippers, and corners where dirt collects. This reduces stains and prepares the fabric for deeper cleaning. Avoid harsh scrubbing to protect the material’s quality.

  


## Spot Cleaning Techniques

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=spot-cleaning-is-a-quick-and-effective-way-to-keep-your-everlane-backpack-looking-fresh.-this-technique-targets-small-stains-without-soaking-the-entire-bag.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Spot cleaning is a quick and effective way to keep your Everlane backpack looking fresh. This technique targets small stains without soaking the entire bag. It saves time and protects the fabric from damage. Using gentle cleaning methods preserves the backpack’s color and shape.

### Using Mild Detergent

Choose a mild detergent that is safe for delicate fabrics. Mix a small amount with warm water to create a gentle cleaning solution. Dip a soft cloth or sponge into the solution. Gently dab the stained area without rubbing hard. Avoid soaking the fabric to prevent water spots. After cleaning, wipe the area with a clean damp cloth to remove detergent residue. Let the backpack air dry completely away from direct sunlight.

### Dealing With Stubborn Stains

For tough stains, apply a small amount of undiluted mild detergent directly on the spot. Use a soft brush or toothbrush to gently scrub in circular motions. Be careful not to damage the fabric fibers. Rinse the area with a clean damp cloth to remove soap and loosened dirt. Repeat the process if needed, but avoid over-wetting the fabric. Allow the backpack to dry naturally in a ventilated space to prevent mold and odors.

## Hand Washing Method

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=cleaning-your-everlane-backpack-by-hand-is-a-simple-and-effective-way-to-keep-it-looking-fresh-without-risking-damage-from-harsh-machine-cycles.-this-method-lets-you-control-the-cleaning-process-carefully,-ensuring-every-spot-is-treated-gently.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Cleaning your Everlane backpack by hand is a simple and effective way to keep it looking fresh without risking damage from harsh machine cycles. This method lets you control the cleaning process carefully, ensuring every spot is treated gently. Ready to see how easy it can be to refresh your backpack?

### Filling The Basin

Start by filling a clean basin or sink with lukewarm water. Avoid hot water, as it can weaken the fabric or fade colors.

Add a small amount of mild detergent—something designed for delicate clothes works best. You want just enough to create gentle suds without overwhelming the fabric.

### Gentle Scrubbing

Dip a soft cloth or sponge into the soapy water and gently scrub the backpack's surface. Focus on stains or dirt spots but be careful not to scrub too hard to avoid damaging the material.

Pay special attention to the straps and bottom, where grime tends to build up. Use a soft-bristled brush for stubborn areas, but keep your movements light and controlled.

### Rinsing Properly

After scrubbing, rinse the backpack thoroughly with clean, lukewarm water. Make sure all soap residue is washed away, as leftover detergent can attract more dirt.

Shake off excess water and gently press the fabric with a towel to absorb moisture. Avoid wringing or twisting the backpack to keep its shape intact.

## Machine Washing Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=machine-washing-your-everlane-backpack-can-save-time-and-effort,-but-it-requires-careful-handling-to-avoid-damage.-knowing-the-right-steps-ensures-your-backpack-stays-clean-without-losing-its-shape-or-color.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Machine washing your Everlane backpack can save time and effort, but it requires careful handling to avoid damage. Knowing the right steps ensures your backpack stays clean without losing its shape or color. Here’s what you need to keep in mind before tossing it in the washer.

### Checking Care Labels

Always start by reading the care labels inside your Everlane backpack. These labels provide specific instructions that can prevent costly mistakes. Ignoring them might lead to fabric fading or structural damage.

Look for symbols indicating whether machine washing is safe or if hand washing is preferred. If the label warns against machine washing, don’t risk it—spot clean instead. Your backpack’s fabric and hardware need special attention based on these guidelines.

### Choosing The Right Cycle

Selecting the correct washing cycle is crucial to protect your backpack’s fabric and zippers. Use a gentle or delicate cycle with cold water to minimize wear and tear. Hot water can shrink or fade materials, so avoid it.

If your washer has a “hand wash” option, that’s even better for delicate backpacks. Avoid heavy-duty or spin cycles that might stress the fabric or damage stitching. Think about how you wash your clothes—would you put your favorite jacket through a rough cycle?

### Protecting The Backpack

Place your Everlane backpack inside a pillowcase or laundry bag before washing. This simple step shields the backpack from friction and prevents straps or zippers from getting tangled. It also keeps the washing machine drum safe from scratches.

Remove any detachable parts or accessories beforehand. Empty all pockets to avoid washing debris inside the machine. After washing, reshape the backpack and air dry it—never use a dryer, as heat can warp the materials.




## Drying The Backpack

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=drying-the-everlane-backpack-properly-is-key-to-keeping-it-in-good-shape.-using-the-right-drying-methods-stops-damage-and-keeps-the-fabric-fresh.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Drying the Everlane backpack properly is key to keeping it in good shape. Using the right drying methods stops damage and keeps the fabric fresh. Quick drying helps avoid bad smells and mold growth. Take care to dry the backpack gently and naturally for the best results.

### Air Drying Techniques

Hang the backpack in a well-ventilated spot. A shaded area works best to prevent sun damage. Use a clothes hanger or a hook to keep its shape. Open all compartments and pockets to let air flow inside. Pat the backpack gently with a towel to remove extra water before hanging. Leave it to dry for several hours or until completely dry. Avoid crumpling or folding the backpack while drying.

### Avoiding Heat Damage

Keep the backpack away from direct heat sources like radiators. Do not use a dryer or hairdryer; heat can ruin the fabric. Hot air may cause shrinking or warping of materials. Avoid ironing the backpack even if it looks wrinkled. Letting it air dry naturally keeps the fabric strong and durable. Patience is important for preserving the backpack’s look and function.

## Maintaining Backpack Freshness

Keeping your Everlane backpack fresh extends its life and keeps it looking new. Dirt and odors build up quickly without care. Simple habits help maintain cleanliness and freshness daily. A clean backpack feels pleasant and lasts longer.

### Regular Cleaning Schedule

Set a routine to clean your backpack every 1-2 weeks. Wipe the outside with a damp cloth to remove dust and stains. Empty the backpack completely before cleaning. Use mild soap and water for tougher spots. Avoid harsh chemicals that damage the fabric. Let it air dry fully before use. Check zippers and pockets for crumbs or dirt. This simple schedule prevents grime and odors from settling in.

### Storage Tips

Store your backpack in a cool, dry place to avoid moisture build-up. Keep it upright to maintain its shape. Avoid stuffing it tightly with other items. Use a breathable bag or cover for long-term storage. Remove items after each use to prevent odors. Open all compartments during storage to allow air circulation. These steps keep your backpack fresh between uses and ready to carry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Clean The Exterior Of An Everlane Backpack?

Use a soft brush or cloth to remove dirt gently. Mix mild soap with warm water and wipe the surface. Avoid harsh chemicals to protect the material and maintain the backpack’s look.

### Can I Machine Wash My Everlane Backpack?

No, machine washing is not recommended. It may damage the fabric and structure. Instead, clean by hand using mild soap and water for best results.

### How Do I Dry My Everlane Backpack After Cleaning?

Air dry the backpack naturally in a well-ventilated area. Avoid direct sunlight or heat sources, which can cause fabric damage or color fading.

### What Cleaning Products Are Safe For Everlane Backpacks?

Use mild, non-abrasive soaps or detergents. Avoid bleach, strong chemicals, or fabric softeners to prevent damage to the fabric and colors.

## Conclusion

Cleaning your Everlane backpack keeps it fresh and lasts longer. Use gentle soap and a soft cloth for best results. Avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the fabric. Dry it fully before using again to prevent smells. Regular care protects your backpack from dirt and stains.

Small steps make a big difference in keeping it neat. Take time to clean it right, and enjoy your backpack every day. Simple habits help your bag stay new and ready for any trip.
