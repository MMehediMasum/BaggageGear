## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Suitcase Size For Family Travel?



For family travel, a mix of 20 to 30-inch suitcases works best. Larger cases like the Amazon Basics 30" offer ample space. Smaller carry-ons help manage essentials. Sets like the LONG VACATION 6 Piece cover all travel needs efficiently.



### Which Luggage Materials Offer Best Durability For Families?



ABS+PC hardshell materials, found in LONG VACATION and Coolife sets, provide great durability. These materials resist impacts and scratches. They ensure long-lasting protection for your belongings during family trips. Lightweight hardshell options also ease transport.



### Are Tsa Locks Necessary For Family Suitcases?



Yes, TSA locks are essential for secure family travel. They allow airport security to inspect bags without damage. Most sets here, like Coolife and TRAVELEO, include TSA-approved locks, enhancing safety and convenience during your journey.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels For Family Luggage?



Spinner wheels are very important for easy maneuverability. They allow 360-degree rotation, reducing strain while moving through airports. Most featured sets, such as Joyway and CONCEPTEN, include spinner wheels for smooth, effortless handling.



### Can Expandable Luggage Sets Benefit Family Vacations?



Expandable luggage offers flexible packing space. Sets like Joyway and Amazon Basics provide up to 15% more room. This helps accommodate extra items or souvenirs, making it ideal for family travel needs. Expansion also allows better organization.



### What Additional Accessories Improve Family Travel Luggage?



Accessories like YAMIU travel shoe bags add convenience. They keep shoes separate and protect clothes. Multi-pocket designs in the Travelers Club duffel bag improve organization. These extras enhance packing efficiency and family travel comfort.
