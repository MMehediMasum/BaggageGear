## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Garment Bag Is Best For Long Dresses?



A garment bag around 165-183 cm long fits long dresses perfectly. It prevents wrinkles and protects delicate fabrics during travel or storage.



### Are Transparent Dress Garment Bags Useful For Travel?



Yes, transparent garment bags help quickly identify contents. They are breathable and allow easy inspection without opening the bag.



### Which Garment Bag Is Ideal For Multiple Suits?



A large 40L garment bag like MATEIN fits up to three suits. It offers waterproof protection and shoulder straps for convenient carrying.



### How Do Garment Bags Protect Wedding Dresses?



Specialized wedding garment bags have extra length, padding, and shoe pockets. They keep dresses wrinkle-free, clean, and secure during travel or storage.



### Can Garment Bags Store Shoes And Accessories?



Yes, many garment bags include separate shoe compartments or accessory pockets. This keeps everything organized and prevents damage during transport.



### Are Garment Bags Durable And Tear-resistant?



High-quality bags use ripstop or reinforced fabric. This ensures durability, tear resistance, and long-term protection for your garments.
