### History Of The Velvet Bag

The velvet bag has a royal story. Crown Royal was created in 1939 to honor King George VI and Queen Elizabeth. The bag reflects this royal connection. It shows respect for the whisky and the drinker.

The bag’s design is both practical and stylish. Velvet feels soft and smooth, adding a special touch. The purple color matches the brand’s royal theme. ### Royal Connection And Canadian Heritage

The bag tradition started as a tribute to royalty. Crown Royal was created in 1939 to honor the visit of King George VI and Queen Elizabeth to Canada. The soft velvet bag was designed to reflect the luxury and elegance befitting a royal gift.

This purple bag, with gold drawstrings, symbolizes regal colors often associated with monarchy. It’s not just about appearance—this bag ties the whisky directly to its Canadian roots and the royal visit that inspired it. When you see the bag, you’re reminded of the story behind the drink.

### Early Marketing Strategies

The bag also served a practical and marketing purpose. Back then, whisky was often sold in simple bottles without much flair. The bag made Crown Royal stand out on store shelves, giving it a unique identity.

Offering the whisky in a bag suggested exclusivity and care. It made the bottle feel like a special gift, something worth showing off or sharing. This approach helped Crown Royal build a loyal following by appealing to customers’ desire for something memorable and different.
