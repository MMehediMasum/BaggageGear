## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are Tsa Approved Luggage Locks?



TSA approved locks allow airport security to inspect luggage without damaging locks. They use a special key for access. These locks provide security and ensure compliance with TSA regulations. They are essential for travelers who want peace of mind during air travel.



### How Do I Reset A Combination Lock?



To reset a combination lock, open the lock and find the reset tool. Insert it into the designated slot. Turn the dials to your desired combination. Remove the tool and test your new combination. Always refer to the manufacturer's instructions for specific guidance.



### What Is An Inspection Indicator On Locks?



An inspection indicator alerts you if your luggage was opened by TSA. It shows a red alert when accessed. This feature adds transparency and assurance. It helps travelers know if their belongings were inspected during transit.



### Are Cable Shackle Locks Secure For Luggage?



Cable shackle locks are flexible and secure. They are designed to withstand tampering and cut attempts. The cable allows for easier locking of multiple zippers. These locks are ideal for travelers needing robust and adaptable security options for their luggage.



### How Many Combinations Can A 4-digit Lock Have?



A 4-digit lock offers 10,000 possible combinations. This makes it highly secure and difficult to crack. Such locks provide strong protection for personal belongings. The numerous combinations add an extra layer of security for travelers using these locks on their luggage.



### Why Choose Zinc Alloy For Luggage Locks?



Zinc alloy provides strength and durability in luggage locks. It resists tampering and wear over time. This material is lightweight yet robust, making it ideal for travel. Zinc alloy locks offer travelers reliable security for their belongings.
