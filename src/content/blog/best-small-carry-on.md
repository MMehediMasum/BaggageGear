## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Carry-on Luggage Size?



The best carry-on luggage size is typically 20-22 inches. This size fits most airline overhead compartments. For example, the Amazon Basics 21" and Wrangler 20" are popular choices. They are compact, yet spacious enough for essentials. Always check your airline's specific size restrictions before traveling.



### Are Hard Shell Carry-ons Durable?



Yes, hard shell carry-ons are durable and protect your belongings. They are made from materials like ABS or polycarbonate. These materials resist scratches and impacts. Options like the LEVEL8 Grace and Sweetcrispy models offer both durability and style. They are great for frequent travelers.



### Do Carry-on Suitcases Have Tsa Locks?



Many carry-on suitcases come with TSA locks for security. Models like the LEVEL8 Grace and Sweetcrispy provide built-in TSA locks. These locks allow TSA agents to inspect your luggage without damage. Always look for this feature for added peace of mind during travel.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Carry-ons?



Spinner wheels offer better maneuverability for carry-ons. They allow 360-degree movement, making navigation through airports easier. Options like the Amazon Basics and Ruausine feature four multi-directional wheels. This design provides smooth and effortless rolling, ideal for busy travel environments.



### Can Carry-ons Expand For More Space?



Yes, many carry-ons are expandable for extra space. For instance, the Amazon Basics and Travelers Club Chicago models offer expandable features. This provides up to 25% more packing room. It's a useful option for travelers needing flexibility with their luggage capacity.
