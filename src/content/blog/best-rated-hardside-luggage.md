## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hardside Luggage Scratch-resistant?



Hardside luggage often features a durable polycarbonate or ABS shell. This material is designed to resist scratches and impacts. The scratch-resistant surface helps maintain its sleek appearance even after rough handling. This feature is ideal for frequent travelers who want their luggage to look new longer.



### How Do Spinner Wheels Enhance Maneuverability?



Spinner wheels rotate 360 degrees, allowing effortless movement in any direction. This feature helps navigate crowded airports and tight spaces easily. Multi-directional wheels provide smooth and stable movement, reducing strain on the user's wrist and arm. They make traveling with heavy luggage more convenient and stress-free.



### Why Is Expandable Luggage Beneficial?



Expandable luggage offers additional packing space when needed. It allows travelers to accommodate extra items without overpacking. This flexibility is helpful for shopping trips or long vacations. The expandable feature usually provides up to 15% more space, making it versatile for different travel needs.



### What Is A Tsa Lock, And Why Is It Important?



A TSA lock allows TSA agents to inspect luggage without damaging it. It ensures security and peace of mind for travelers. These locks are approved by the Transportation Security Administration. They help prevent unauthorized access while allowing for easy inspection at airports.



### How Does A Hardside Suitcase Protect Belongings?



Hardside suitcases have a rigid shell that absorbs impacts. This feature minimizes the risk of damage to contents. The sturdy material also protects against moisture and dirt. It keeps belongings safe and secure during travel, making it suitable for transporting fragile items.
