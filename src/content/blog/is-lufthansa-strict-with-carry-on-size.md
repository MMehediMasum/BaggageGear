## Lufthansa Carry-on Size Rules

Understanding Lufthansa's carry-on size rules is essential before you pack your bags. Their regulations can feel strict, but knowing the details helps you avoid last-minute hassles at the gate. Let’s break down what you need to know about size, weight, and personal item guidelines.

### Allowed Dimensions

Lufthansa allows one carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of **55 x 40 x 23 cm**(21.6 x 15.7 x 9 inches). This includes wheels and handles, so be sure to measure your suitcase carefully.

If your bag exceeds these limits, you may be asked to check it in, which could cost extra and slow you down. Think about your usual carry-on—does it fit easily in the overhead bin on Lufthansa flights?

### Weight Limits

The maximum weight for your carry-on depends on your travel class. For Economy and Premium Economy, the limit is **8 kg (17.6 lbs)**. For Business and First Class, you can bring up to **16 kg (35 lbs)**.

Many travelers don’t realize how quickly small items add up on the scale. Have you ever struggled with a bag that felt light but tipped the scales? Weigh your carry-on before heading to the airport to stay within limits and avoid surprises.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Besides your carry-on, Lufthansa allows one personal item, such as a handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack. This item must fit under the seat in front of you, usually around **40 x 30 x 10 cm**.

Keep essentials like your passport, wallet, and electronics here for easy access. Ask yourself—does your personal item fit comfortably under the seat, or will you struggle to store it during boarding?
