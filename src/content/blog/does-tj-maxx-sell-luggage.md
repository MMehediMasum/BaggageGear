### Price Comparison Tips

Before you buy, check prices online and at other stores. Use your phone to quickly search the same luggage brand and model. This helps you see if T.J. Maxx really offers the best price or if you can find a better deal elsewhere.

Keep a note of typical prices for the luggage you want. Sometimes, what looks like a discount might be close to the regular price. Have you ever bought something thinking it was a steal, only to find it cheaper later? Avoid that by doing a quick comparison.

### Spotting Genuine Discounts

T.J. Maxx often sells items at reduced prices because they buy overstock or past-season goods. Look for tags that clearly show the original price and the discounted price. This makes it easier to know if you’re getting a true bargain.

Also, check the quality of the luggage carefully. A low price might mean a hidden defect or wear. If the bag looks perfect and the price is way lower than usual, you’ve likely found a genuine discount.

### Loyalty Programs And Coupons

Sign up for T.J. Maxx’s email list or download their app to get exclusive coupons and updates on sales. These deals often aren’t advertised in-store and can add extra savings on top of already reduced prices.

Combine coupons with loyalty rewards or seasonal promotions for even more discounts. Have you tried stacking a coupon with a sale price? It’s a smart way to stretch your budget further.
