### Visual Estimation Tricks

Start by looking through the bag carefully. Squashies usually come in similar sizes, so count how many fit into a small section of the bag, then multiply by how many of those sections fill the entire bag.

Try breaking the bag into imaginary parts—like quarters or thirds—and estimate the number in one section. This trick works well if the bag is clear or partially transparent.

* Use your hand or a small container to measure how many squashies fit in a handful or cup.
* Estimate the total based on how many handfuls or cups fill the bag.

Have you noticed some bags have oddly shaped or different-sized squashies? Adjust your count by considering the average size to avoid overestimating.

### Using Weight For Accuracy

If you want a more exact estimate, weighing the bag is a smart move. First, weigh a single squashy or a small group to get an average weight per piece.

Then, weigh the whole bag and divide that number by the average weight of one squashy. This gives you a close estimate of how many pieces are inside.

| Item | Weight (grams) |
| --- | --- |
| Single Squashy | 15 |
| Full Bag | 450 |
| Estimated Count | 450 ÷ 15 = 30 squashies |

This method works best if squashies in the bag are consistent in size and weight. Have you tried using your kitchen scale for this? It’s a handy tool that many overlook.
