## Tips To Maintain Your Backpack

Proper care helps your backpack last longer and stay functional. Small actions keep the fabric strong and zippers working well. These tips help you maintain your North Face backpack and protect your investment. Follow simple routines to keep it clean and ready for use.

### Cleaning Advice

* Use a soft brush to remove dirt and dust.
* Spot clean stains with mild soap and water.
* Avoid harsh detergents that can damage fabric.
* Do not machine wash or dry; air dry only.
* Empty all pockets before cleaning.
* Let the backpack dry completely before storing.

### Storage Tips

* Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
* Keep the backpack loosely packed to maintain shape.
* Avoid placing heavy items on top of the backpack.
* Use a breathable bag or cover to prevent dust buildup.
* Check zippers and straps regularly for wear.
* Fix small tears or loose threads promptly.
