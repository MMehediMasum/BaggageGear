## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Materials Are Best For Durable Hard Suitcase Sets?



Durable hard suitcase sets often use ABS, PC, or a PC+ABS blend. These materials provide impact resistance and lightweight strength. They protect your belongings while traveling. For example, SHOWKOO uses PC+ABS for durability and scratch resistance. This ensures long-lasting performance and easy handling.



### How Do Tsa Locks Enhance Suitcase Security?



TSA locks allow airport security to inspect luggage without damage. They keep your belongings secure during travel. Most hard suitcase sets, like Coolife and Cosbarn, include TSA-approved locks. This feature is essential for hassle-free security checks and peace of mind when flying.



### Are Expandable Suitcases Useful For Extra Packing Space?



Yes, expandable suitcases offer up to 25% more packing space. They are ideal for trips with varying luggage needs. Amazon Basics and Westridge sets include expandable features. This flexibility helps you pack souvenirs or additional items easily without needing extra bags.



### What Benefits Do Spinner Wheels Provide In Hard Suitcase Sets?



Spinner wheels rotate 360 degrees, allowing smooth and easy maneuvering. They reduce strain on your arms and improve mobility. Most sets like Amazon Basics and TILYMOL include multi-directional spinner wheels. This makes navigating airports and crowded spaces effortless.



### Which Suitcase Sizes Are Best For Different Trip Lengths?



Carry-on sizes (20"-21") suit short trips or weekend getaways. Medium (24"-26") and large (28"-30") sizes fit longer vacations. Sets like Cosbarn and Westridge offer multiple sizes for varied travel needs. Choosing the right size helps avoid overweight fees and improves packing efficiency.



### How Lightweight Are These Hard Suitcase Sets For Travel Convenience?



Most hard suitcase sets use lightweight materials like ABS and PC. Sets such as Coolife and Cosbarn are designed to be easy to carry. Lightweight luggage reduces travel fatigue and meets airline weight limits. This enhances comfort and convenience on trips.
