## Spirit Carry-on Fee Basics

Understanding Spirit's carry-on fee is key if you want to avoid surprises at the airport. Knowing what you’re allowed to bring onboard and when you’ll be charged can save you both money and stress. Let’s break down the basics so you can plan your packing smartly.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item per passenger for free. This item must fit under the seat in front of you. Think of small backpacks, purses, or laptop bags.

If you want to bring a larger carry-on bag, like a roller suitcase, you’ll need to pay a fee. This larger bag must fit in the overhead bin. The size limit for this bag is usually around 22 x 18 x 10 inches.

### Fee Structure Overview

Spirit’s carry-on fee varies depending on when and how you pay. Paying for your carry-on during booking is cheaper than paying at the airport.

* **Booking online:**Usually the lowest fee.
* **Before online check-in closes:**Slightly higher fee.
* **At the airport or gate:**Highest fee, sometimes double the online price.

Fees also depend on your route and the specific flight. Prices can range from about $35 to $65 or more for a carry-on bag. Checking Spirit’s website before your trip is crucial to get the exact fee.

### When Fees Apply

If you only bring a personal item, you won’t pay a carry-on fee. But if your bag exceeds the personal item size, fees kick in.

Have you ever thought your small backpack was just fine only to be told it’s too big at the gate? It happens often. Spirit is strict about size limits, so measure your bags before you pack.

Also, if you decide to add a carry-on after booking, expect to pay more. Planning ahead is your best bet to keep costs low.

## Additional Baggage Fees To Consider

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=beyond-the-carry-on-fee,-travelers-face-several-additional-baggage-costs-with-spirit-airlines.-these-charges-can-quickly-add-up-and-affect-your-travel-budget.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Beyond the carry-on fee, travelers face several additional baggage costs with Spirit Airlines. These charges can quickly add up and affect your travel budget. Knowing about these fees ahead can help you plan smarter and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Checked Bag Costs

Spirit Airlines charges for checked bags based on when and where you pay. Prices are usually lower if you pay online before your flight. Fees can range from $30 to over $60 per bag, depending on the route and timing. Each passenger can bring up to two checked bags, but more bags mean more fees.

* Paying at the airport is often the most expensive option.
* Booking early saves money on checked bag fees.
* Weight limits for checked bags typically max out at 40 pounds.

### Oversized And Overweight Charges

Spirit also charges extra for bags that are too heavy or too large. Overweight bags cost more, usually starting at $75 for weights over 40 pounds. Bags over 50 pounds can have even higher fees. Oversized bags, larger than 62 linear inches, also incur extra charges.

* Check Spirit’s size and weight limits before packing.
* Fees increase sharply for bags beyond limits.
* Consider redistributing items to avoid these fees.
