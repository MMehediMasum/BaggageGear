Storing Hi Chews correctly keeps them fresh and tasty. Proper storage helps preserve their chewy texture and fruity flavor. A few simple tips can make your Hi Chews last longer and maintain their quality.

### Shelf Life

Hi Chews usually stay fresh for about 6 to 9 months. Check the expiration date on the bag to know the exact time. After this period, the candy may lose flavor and become hard. Keep an eye on the texture and taste before eating old Hi Chews.

### Best Storage Practices

* Store Hi Chews in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
* Keep the bag tightly sealed to avoid air exposure.
* Avoid storing Hi Chews in the refrigerator; moisture can affect texture.
* If the bag is opened, use an airtight container for better freshness.
* Do not expose Hi Chews to heat to prevent melting.
