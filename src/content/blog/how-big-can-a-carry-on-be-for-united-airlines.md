## Carry-on Size Limits

Understanding carry-on size limits helps avoid extra fees and delays. United Airlines has clear rules on how big your carry-on bag can be. These rules ensure your bag fits in the overhead bin or under the seat. Knowing these limits saves time at the airport and makes your trip smoother.

### United Airlines Dimensions

United Airlines allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. The maximum size for a carry-on bag is:

* Length: 9 inches (22 cm)
* Width: 14 inches (35 cm)
* Height: 22 inches (56 cm)

Your bag must fit in the overhead bin. Personal items like backpacks or purses must fit under the seat. They should be smaller than the carry-on bag.

### Weight Restrictions

United Airlines does not have a strict weight limit for carry-on bags. Still, the bag must be light enough to lift into the overhead bin. Most travelers keep carry-on bags under 15 pounds (7 kg). Overweight bags may need to be checked in, which can cost extra.

Think about your last flight—did your laptop bag or small backpack slide easily under the seat? If it didn’t, it might have been too big. Checking your bag’s dimensions before you pack can save you last-minute hassles at the gate.

### Size Differences

United Airlines allows carry-ons up to 9 x 14 x 22 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Some airlines have stricter or more generous size rules. For example:

* American Airlines: 9 x 14 x 22 inches, same as United.
* Delta Air Lines: 9 x 14 x 22 inches, matches United’s limit.
* Southwest Airlines: Slightly larger, 10 x 16 x 24 inches allowed.
* JetBlue: Smaller, 8 x 13 x 22 inches maximum.

These size differences change what fits as a carry-on. Southwest’s larger allowance suits bigger bags. JetBlue’s smaller size means tighter packing. United’s size falls in the middle, balancing convenience and space.

### Policy Variations

Carry-on policies vary beyond size limits. United Airlines permits one carry-on bag plus one personal item. Other airlines may differ:

* American Airlines: One carry-on bag and one personal item allowed.
* Delta Air Lines: Same policy as United.
* Southwest Airlines: Allows two free checked bags but limits carry-ons.
* Spirit Airlines: Charges fees for carry-ons and personal items on some fares.

Some airlines charge for carry-on bags on basic fares. United usually includes carry-ons in ticket price. Airline rules on bag weight, gate check, and overhead bin space also vary. These policy details impact travel comfort and cost.

## Checking Your Bag At The Gate

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=checking-your-bag-at-the-gate-happens-when-your-carry-on-is-too-big-or-the-flight-is-full.-united-airlines-staff-will-ask-you-to-check-the-bag-right-before-boarding.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Checking your bag at the gate happens when your carry-on is too big or the flight is full. United Airlines staff will ask you to check the bag right before boarding. This helps make space in the overhead bins for all passengers’ bags. Knowing this process can save time and avoid surprises at the gate.

### When Carry-ons Are Too Large

United Airlines allows carry-ons up to 9 x 14 x 22 inches. Bags larger than this may not fit in the overhead bins. If your bag is too big or heavy, gate agents may ask you to check it. This often happens on crowded flights or smaller planes with limited space.

Try to measure your bag before you go to the airport. A bag that is even slightly larger can be gate checked. This means you must give your bag to the staff before boarding. Your bag will travel in the plane’s cargo hold.

### Gate Check Process

At the gate, staff will tag your bag for gate check. You will carry it onto the plane but leave it before you sit down. The bag is placed in the cargo area during the flight. After landing, you pick up your bag at the gate or baggage claim.

Gate checking is free on United Airlines. It helps avoid delays caused by overhead bin space. Just be ready to part with your bag for a while during the flight. Keep important items with you in a smaller personal bag.
