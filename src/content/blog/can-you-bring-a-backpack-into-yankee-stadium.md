## Yankee Stadium Bag Policy

The Yankee Stadium bag policy ensures safety and smooth entry for all visitors. It sets clear rules about which bags are allowed inside. Knowing these rules helps avoid delays at the gates. Fans should prepare their bags according to these guidelines before arriving.

### Allowed Bag Types

Yankee Stadium permits only certain types of bags. Small purses, clear bags, and approved backpacks are allowed. Large backpacks or bags with hard sides are not permitted. All bags must be easy for security to inspect.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must meet specific size limits to enter the stadium. The maximum size allowed is 16 inches by 16 inches by 8 inches. Bags larger than this will be denied entry. Carry only what fits inside these dimensions.

### Clear Bag Rule

The clear bag rule is strictly enforced at Yankee Stadium. Bags must be see-through and made of plastic, vinyl, or PVC. This rule speeds up security checks and keeps everyone safe. Non-clear bags must fit within the size limits.

Arriving at Yankee Stadium early gives you a buffer against unexpected delays at security. Lines get longer closer to game time, and rushing can make the whole experience stressful. Give yourself at least 30 to 45 minutes before the gates open.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I Bring A Backpack Into Yankee Stadium?

Yes, you can bring a backpack into Yankee Stadium. However, it must comply with the stadium’s clear bag policy. Backpacks should be transparent or inside a clear bag not exceeding 12” x 6” x 12”. Non-compliant bags may be denied entry or require inspection.

### What Size Backpack Is Allowed At Yankee Stadium?

Backpacks must fit inside a clear bag no larger than 12” x 6” x 12”. Any backpack exceeding these dimensions is not allowed. Smaller backpacks that fit these size requirements or clear backpacks are permitted. It ensures faster entry and enhanced security for all guests.

### Are Backpacks Subject To Security Checks At Yankee Stadium?

Yes, all backpacks undergo thorough security screening. Stadium staff inspect backpacks for prohibited items before entry. This process maintains safety and prevents banned objects from entering the venue. Cooperation speeds up the screening and entry process.

### Can I Carry A Non-clear Backpack Into Yankee Stadium?

Non-clear backpacks are generally not allowed. Only clear backpacks or those fitting inside a clear bag are permitted. Exceptions may apply for medical or diaper bags but must be declared and inspected. Check the latest stadium rules before your visit.
