## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Hard Shell Suitcase Lightweight?



Lightweight hard shell suitcases use materials like polycarbonate or ABS. These materials balance durability with reduced weight for easy travel. Models like Samsonite Freeform and Sweetcrispy offer strong, light construction. This helps travelers avoid heavy luggage fees and improves mobility.



### Are Expandable Suitcases Worth Buying?



Expandable suitcases increase packing space by up to 15%. Amazon Basics and OLIXIS models feature this option. They offer flexibility for extra items without needing a larger bag. Expandability is ideal for travelers who pack variably or return with more belongings.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Luggage?



Spinner wheels provide 360-degree movement, making maneuvering easier. Nearly all listed suitcases, including Kenneth Cole and Coolife, have spinner wheels. They reduce wrist strain and improve stability on various surfaces. Spinner wheels enhance travel comfort and convenience.



### Do Tsa Locks Improve Suitcase Security?



TSA locks allow security to inspect luggage without damage. Many suitcases listed, like LEVEL8 and LUGGEX, come with TSA-approved locks. This feature ensures your bag stays secure while complying with airport regulations. It is essential for safe, hassle-free travel.



### Which Suitcase Sizes Are Airline Approved?



Carry-ons are typically 20-23 inches, like Samsonite Freeform and Sweetcrispy. Checked luggage often ranges from 24 to 30 inches, such as Amazon Basics and Kenneth Cole sets. Choosing airline-approved sizes avoids extra fees and ensures smooth boarding. Always check specific airline restrictions.
