## United Carry-on Allowance

Understanding United's carry-on allowance helps travelers pack smart and avoid extra fees. United Airlines lets you bring one carry-on bag plus one personal item onboard. These rules apply to most flights, making it easier to plan your trip.

### Size And Weight Limits

Your carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. The maximum size allowed is 9 inches high, 14 inches wide, and 22 inches long. The weight limit is generally not enforced, but keeping it light is best for easy handling.

Check these dimensions before you pack to avoid gate checks. Soft-sided bags can sometimes fit better in tight spaces.

### Personal Item Policy

Along with your carry-on, you may bring one personal item. This could be a backpack, purse, or laptop bag. The personal item must fit under the seat in front of you.

Typical size for a personal item is 9 inches high, 10 inches wide, and 17 inches long. Keep your essentials handy by packing them in this smaller bag.

## Packing Tips For United Flights

Packing smart is key for smooth travel on United flights. Knowing what fits in your carry-on saves time and stress at the airport. Efficient packing helps you bring essentials without extra fees.

Focus on using space wisely and following rules about what you can bring. Packing light and right means a better travel experience.

### Maximizing Carry-on Space

Choose a carry-on bag that fits United’s size limits. Soft-sided bags can compress better than hard cases. Roll clothes instead of folding to save space. Use packing cubes to keep items organized. Place heavier items at the bottom to balance weight. Fill shoes with socks or small items to avoid wasted space. Keep liquids in a clear, quart-sized bag for easy security checks.

### Items To Avoid Carrying Onboard

United restricts certain items in carry-ons. Sharp objects like knives or scissors are not allowed. Large liquids over 3.4 ounces (100 ml) must go in checked bags. Avoid carrying flammable items such as lighters or aerosols. Guns and ammunition are strictly forbidden onboard. Check for any specific rules on sports equipment or tools. Electronics must be easily accessible for security screening.

## Carry-on Fees And Upgrades

Understanding United Airlines' carry-on fees and upgrade options helps travelers save money and enjoy smoother boarding. Knowing when fees apply prevents surprises at the gate. Upgrading to priority boarding offers extra convenience and peace of mind. Both choices impact your travel experience significantly.

### When Fees Apply

United Airlines generally allows one free carry-on bag per passenger. The bag must meet size and weight limits. Fees apply if:

* Your carry-on exceeds size limits.
* You travel on a basic economy ticket.
* You want to bring an additional carry-on bag.

Basic economy passengers usually cannot bring a full-sized carry-on for free. They may only bring a personal item. Other ticket types include a free carry-on allowance.

Fees vary by route and can range from $30 to $60 per bag. Checking United's official website before your trip avoids surprises.

### Upgrading To Priority Boarding

Priority boarding lets you board early and secure overhead bin space. This upgrade is available for purchase or included with certain ticket classes.

* Early boarding reduces stress and saves time.
* You can bring your carry-on without worrying about space.
* Priority boarding often includes other perks, such as access to better seats.

Purchasing priority boarding is a smart choice for travelers with heavy or multiple bags. It helps avoid last-minute bag gate checking.

## Security And Carry-on Bags

Security checks at the airport can be stressful, especially when you're unsure about what you can carry in your United Airlines carry-on bag. Understanding the security rules can save you time and hassle. Knowing exactly how to pack your carry-on will help you breeze through TSA checkpoints without surprises.

### Tsa Rules For Carry-ons

TSA allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger on United flights. Your carry-on must fit in the overhead bin, typically around 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Personal items include backpacks, purses, or laptop bags that fit under the seat.

Items prohibited in carry-ons include sharp objects, large scissors, and most sporting goods. Always check the TSA website before packing to avoid delays. Remember, security officers have the final say on what can pass through.

### Handling Liquids And Electronics

Liquids in your carry-on must follow the 3-1-1 rule: containers no larger than 3.4 ounces (100 ml), all fitting in one clear quart-sized bag, with one bag per passenger. This means your shampoo or lotion bottles should be travel-sized.

Electronics like laptops and tablets must be removed from your bag and placed in separate bins during screening. This helps TSA scan them more clearly. Packing these items in an easy-to-access spot speeds up your security process.

Have you ever been caught off guard at security because of a forgotten large liquid or a packed laptop? Preparing your carry-on with these rules in mind will make your travel smoother and less stressful.

### Medical And Mobility Devices

United permits passengers to bring medical and mobility devices without extra fees. Items like wheelchairs, walkers, and crutches count as carry-on or checked baggage. These devices usually do not need to fit in the overhead bin.

Passengers should inform the airline ahead about these items. Proper packing and notification ensure smooth boarding and handling.

### Sports Equipment And Oversized Items

Sports gear often requires special attention. United accepts some sports equipment as carry-on if it fits size limits. Oversized items like skis or golf clubs usually need to be checked.

Fees may apply for checked sports gear. Packing these items carefully protects them during travel. Always check United's latest guidelines for specific sports equipment rules.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do United Airlines Tickets Include A Free Carry-on Bag?

Yes, United Airlines allows one free carry-on bag per passenger. It must meet size limits of 9 x 14 x 22 inches, including handles and wheels.

### What Are United Airlines Carry-on Baggage Size Restrictions?

Carry-on bags must not exceed 22 inches tall, 14 inches wide, and 9 inches deep. Personal items like purses or laptops must fit under the seat.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item Along With My Carry-on?

Yes, United permits one personal item plus one carry-on bag. Personal items include briefcases, backpacks, or laptop bags that fit under the seat.

### Are There Any Carry-on Fees On United Airlines?

Most standard tickets include a free carry-on. Basic Economy tickets allow a personal item only; carry-ons may incur fees for these fares.
