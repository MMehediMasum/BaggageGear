## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Fees

Spirit Airlines is known for its ultra-low fares, but that often means paying extra for things many airlines include for free—like carry-on bags. Understanding Spirit's carry-on fees can save you from unexpected costs and hassle at the airport. Let’s break down what you need to know about their carry-on policies and fees.

### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Spirit allows one personal item on board for free, such as a small backpack or purse that fits under the seat. However, if you want to bring a larger carry-on bag that goes in the overhead bin, you will likely face a fee.

This personal item must fit within dimensions of 18 x 14 x 8 inches. If your bag exceeds this size, Spirit charges for a standard carry-on, so measuring your bag before you pack can help you avoid fees.

### Fee Structure By Booking Time

When you book your ticket affects how much you pay for a carry-on. Adding a carry-on during your initial booking is cheaper than paying for it later.

| Booking Time | Typical Carry-On Fee |
| --- | --- |
| During Booking | $35 - $45 |
| Before Online Check-In | $45 - $55 |
| At Airport | $60 - $65 |

Waiting until you reach the airport to pay for your carry-on can cost you more than double. Have you ever felt rushed at the airport only to face a higher fee? Planning ahead can save you both money and stress.

### Impact Of Fare Type

Spirit’s different fare types also influence carry-on fees. Their “Bare Fare” is the most basic option and does not include a carry-on in the price.

* **Bare Fare:**Personal item free, carry-on and checked bags cost extra.
* **Bundle It:**Includes one carry-on and one personal item, saving you money if you know you’ll need more space.
* **Big Front Seat:**Also includes carry-on allowance plus extra legroom.

Choosing a fare that includes your carry-on might seem like a bigger upfront cost, but it often turns out cheaper than adding bags later. Have you checked which fare type fits your travel needs best before you book?

## Personal Item Rules

Understanding Spirit Airlines' personal item rules can save you time and money at the airport. Your personal item is the small bag you’re allowed to bring on board without an extra fee, but it must meet specific size and weight requirements. Knowing these rules helps you pack smart and avoid surprises during boarding.

### Size And Weight Limits

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item per passenger. This item must fit under the seat in front of you, so size matters. The maximum dimensions allowed are 18 inches long, 14 inches wide, and 8 inches tall.

Weight restrictions are less strict for personal items, but it’s best to keep it light for ease of carrying. Think of a small backpack, purse, or laptop bag that fits comfortably under the seat. I once tried bringing a slightly larger bag and ended up having to check it last minute, which cost me extra.

### Fee Differences From Carry-ons

Unlike carry-on bags, personal items do not have an additional fee. Spirit Airlines charges for most carry-on bags, which can add up quickly. Carry-ons must be stored in the overhead bin, while personal items fit under your seat, making the distinction important.

If you’re traveling light and can fit everything into your personal item, you avoid carry-on fees altogether. Have you ever packed so efficiently that you didn’t need a carry-on? It’s a great feeling to breeze through boarding without extra charges.

## Ways To Avoid Carry-on Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=carry-on-fees-can-quickly-add-up,-especially-with-airlines-like-spirit-known-for-their-budget-friendly-base-fares-but-extra-charges.-you-don’t-have-to-pay-more-if-you-plan-smartly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Carry-on fees can quickly add up, especially with airlines like Spirit known for their budget-friendly base fares but extra charges. You don’t have to pay more if you plan smartly. Understanding how to avoid these fees can save you money and stress at the airport.

### Booking Strategies

Booking your ticket early often unlocks options that help you skip carry-on fees. Spirit Airlines sometimes offers bundles or options that include a carry-on allowance if you pay during booking, which is cheaper than paying at the gate.

Check the fare details carefully before finalizing your purchase. Some fares come with a personal item included for free—knowing the size limits can help you avoid charges if you stick to these dimensions.

### Membership And Perks

Spirit’s $9 Fare Club membership offers perks like discounted or waived carry-on fees. If you travel frequently, this membership can quickly pay for itself by cutting down your extra costs.

Also, credit cards affiliated with airlines might offer free carry-on bags as a perk. Have you checked if your card gives you this benefit? It’s an easy way to bypass fees without changing your packing habits.

### Packing Tips

Try fitting everything into a personal item that meets Spirit’s size limits—usually a backpack or a large purse. This can save you from paying for a full carry-on bag.

Use packing cubes and roll your clothes to maximize space. Small changes like these can make your bag fit the personal item criteria, helping you avoid fees completely.

Ask yourself: can you really travel comfortably without that extra bag? Often, trimming down your carry-on saves you money and makes moving through airports faster and easier.




## Additional Baggage Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=additional-baggage-fees-can-quickly-add-up-and-surprise-travelers-who-aren’t-prepared.-spirit-airlines-is-known-for-its-low-base-fares,-but-the-cost-for-extra-bags-beyond-your-carry-on-can-be-significant.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Additional baggage fees can quickly add up and surprise travelers who aren’t prepared. Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares, but the cost for extra bags beyond your carry-on can be significant. Knowing these fees ahead of time helps you avoid last-minute charges and make smarter packing decisions.

### Checked Bags Pricing

Spirit charges for checked bags separately, and the price varies based on when you pay and your route. Booking your checked bags online before you arrive at the airport is usually cheaper than paying at the gate.

* First checked bag typically costs between $30 and $45 if purchased online in advance.
* Second checked bag usually runs from $40 to $55.
* Paying at the airport can add $10 or more to these fees.

Think about how many items you really need to check. Sometimes, it’s more cost-effective to pay a bit more for a larger carry-on than to check multiple bags. Have you ever ended up paying double because you didn’t prepay for your checked luggage?

### Overweight And Oversized Charges

Spirit also charges extra if your bag exceeds their weight or size limits. This is where many travelers get caught off guard, especially if you packed heavy souvenirs or bulky winter coats.

| Weight/Size Limit | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Over 40 lbs (18 kg) | $65 - $150 |
| Over 50 lbs (23 kg) | $150 |
| Oversized (over 62 inches total dimensions) | $150 |

To avoid these charges, weigh your bags at home and measure their dimensions. Can you redistribute weight between bags or wear heavier items on the plane? These small tricks can save you a lot of money and hassle at the airport.

## Tips For Smooth Boarding

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=boarding-your-spirit-airlines-flight-with-a-carry-on-can-be-a-hassle-free-experience-if-you-prepare-smartly.-small-actions-taken-before-and-during-boarding-can-save-you-time-and-stress.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Boarding your Spirit Airlines flight with a carry-on can be a hassle-free experience if you prepare smartly. Small actions taken before and during boarding can save you time and stress. Here are some tips to help you breeze through the process and keep your carry-on with you.

### Early Check-in Benefits

Checking in early is more than just a good habit; it can make a real difference with Spirit Airlines. By checking in online 24 hours before your flight, you secure a better chance of selecting your preferred seat and confirming your carry-on allowance.

Early check-in also means you avoid last-minute fees or surprises at the airport counter. You can print your boarding pass or download it to your phone, which speeds up your time at security and gate.

Have you noticed how much smoother boarding feels when you’re already checked in? It’s not just luck—it’s smart planning.

### Gate Checking Options

If your carry-on is slightly larger or the flight is crowded, Spirit may ask you to gate check your bag. This means you hand over your carry-on just before boarding, and it’s returned to you at the jet bridge or baggage claim.

Gate checking can save you the headache of paying extra for a larger carry-on or trying to squeeze your bag into overhead bins. However, keep valuables and essentials in a personal item you keep with you.

Here’s a quick checklist for gate checking:

* Remove any fragile items or electronics from your carry-on
* Label your bag with your contact information
* Have a small bag ready for items you want to keep close

Would you rather risk gate checking or pay for a checked bag upfront? It’s worth weighing your options before you board.

Other budget airlines like Southwest include a carry-on for free. This makes Southwest more attractive for travelers who want to avoid extra baggage fees.

Choosing an airline depends on how many bags you bring. If you travel light, Spirit can be cheaper overall. For those needing a carry-on, Southwest or Frontier might offer better value. Always compare total costs, not just ticket prices.
