## Frequently Asked Questions



### Which Luggage Brands Offer The Best Lifetime Warranty?



Away, Travelpro, Coolife, and Kenneth Cole offer strong lifetime warranties. These warranties cover manufacturing defects and ensure long-term durability.



### Does The Lifetime Warranty Cover Damage From Travel Mishandling?



No, most lifetime warranties exclude damage from airline handling or misuse. They mainly cover defects in materials and workmanship.



### How Do I Claim A Luggage Warranty For Repairs?



Contact the brand’s customer service with your proof of purchase. Follow their instructions for repair or replacement under warranty.



### Are Spinner Wheels Covered Under Lifetime Warranties?



Yes, spinner wheels are usually included if they have manufacturing defects. Normal wear and tear or damage from abuse is excluded.



### Do All Sizes In A Luggage Set Have The Same Warranty?



Typically, yes. Brands like Coolife and Away offer the same lifetime warranty across all sizes in a set for consistency.
