## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Suitcase Is Best For European Travel?



A 20 to 28-inch suitcase is ideal for Europe. Carry-ons like the Samsonite Omni PC fit overhead bins. Larger checked options like the Samsonite Omni 2 28-inch offer extra packing space for longer trips.



### Are Hard-shell Or Soft-shell Suitcases Better For Europe?



Hard-shell suitcases like Samsonite’s models provide better protection and durability. Soft-shell options, such as SwissGear Sion, offer flexibility and extra pockets. Choose based on your travel style and needs.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels For European Trips?



Spinner wheels are crucial for smooth navigation through airports and streets. Models like Samsonite Freeform and Amazon Basics come with 360° spinner wheels for easy handling and better mobility.



### Can I Weigh My Suitcase With A Travel Luggage Scale?



Yes, the Travel Inspira Luggage Scale is portable and accurate. It helps avoid overweight fees by measuring your suitcase weight up to 110 pounds before your flight.



### What Features Should I Look For In European Carry-on Luggage?



Look for TSA-approved locks, expandability, lightweight materials, and smooth spinner wheels. Samsonite Freeform and LOVEVOOK backpacks meet these needs, ensuring secure and convenient travel.



### Are Packing Cubes Helpful For Organizing European Trips?



Yes, packing cubes like those included with LOVEVOOK backpacks keep clothes organized and maximize space. Compression bags also help save room for souvenirs and essentials.



## Conclusion



Choosing the right suitcase makes your trip to Europe easier and more enjoyable. Durable options like Samsonite and Amazon Basics offer strong protection and smooth wheels. Lightweight and expandable suitcases help you pack smart and move quickly. Backpacks from LOVEVOOK provide flexibility and extra space with packing cubes.



Don’t forget a luggage scale to avoid overweight fees at the airport. Compression bags save space and keep your clothes organized. Whether you prefer hardshell or softside luggage, pick one that fits your travel style. This way, you stay comfortable and prepared on your journey.
