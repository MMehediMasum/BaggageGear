## How To Use Lockers

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=using-luggage-lockers-at-amsterdam-central-station-is-a-straightforward-way-to-explore-the-city-hands-free.-knowing-how-to-use-them-correctly-can-save-you-time-and-hassle.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Using luggage lockers at Amsterdam Central Station is a straightforward way to explore the city hands-free. Knowing how to use them correctly can save you time and hassle. Below is a detailed guide to help you store your belongings securely and access them with ease.

### Step-by-step Guide

* Find the lockers located near the main entrance or the ticket hall. They are usually grouped together and clearly marked.
* Choose a locker size that fits your luggage. Sizes range from small lockers for backpacks to large ones for suitcases.
* Pay the locker fee using coins, credit card, or contactless payment, depending on the machine's options.
* Place your luggage inside the locker and close the door securely.
* Remember or write down your locker number and keep the key or code safe. Losing this means you won’t be able to access your items.
* When you return, insert your key or enter your code to open the locker and retrieve your belongings.

### Access Hours And Availability

Lockers at Amsterdam Central Station are accessible daily from early morning until late at night, typically between 6 AM and midnight. However, exact hours can vary, so check the posted signs or the station’s website before planning your visit.

During peak tourist seasons, lockers may fill up quickly. Consider arriving early or using alternative storage facilities if you have large or multiple bags. Have you ever found all lockers full just when you needed one the most? Planning ahead can save you that stress.

### Security Tips

Always double-check that your locker is securely locked before walking away. If the locker uses a key, keep it somewhere safe but accessible—like a zipped pocket or a wrist strap.

Avoid storing valuables like passports, wallets, or electronics in the lockers. Instead, carry them on you or use a hotel safe. It’s tempting to leave everything behind, but your peace of mind is worth carrying a small bag.

Report any malfunctioning lockers to station staff immediately. Your safety depends not only on your vigilance but also on the station’s maintenance. Have you noticed unattended or damaged lockers? Letting staff know helps everyone stay secure.

## Traveler Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=storing-your-luggage-at-amsterdam-central-station-can-make-your-trip-smoother-and-more-enjoyable.-however,-knowing-how-to-use-the-lockers-wisely-can-save-you-time-and-frustration.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Storing your luggage at Amsterdam Central Station can make your trip smoother and more enjoyable. However, knowing how to use the lockers wisely can save you time and frustration. Here are some practical traveler tips to help you make the most of luggage storage during your visit.

### Best Times To Store Luggage

Lockers at Amsterdam Central Station fill up quickly during peak hours, especially in the morning and late afternoon when many travelers arrive or depart. Aim to store your bags early in the day to secure a spot and avoid waiting.

Weekends and holidays tend to be busier. If your schedule allows, try to store your luggage during weekday afternoons when the station is less crowded.

### Avoiding Common Issues

Always check the size limits of lockers before storing your bags. Oversized luggage may not fit, so look for alternative storage options nearby if needed.

Keep your locker ticket or code safe and easily accessible. Losing it can cause unnecessary stress and delays. Consider taking a photo of the locker number and your receipt right after storing your items.

Watch out for lockers that require coins or specific payment methods. Some travelers get caught without the right change, so bring small bills or digital payment options if available.

### Combining Storage With Sightseeing

Storing your luggage early can free you up to explore Amsterdam’s nearby attractions without dragging your bags around. The station is close to places like the Anne Frank House and Dam Square, so you can plan quick visits in between your travel schedule.

Think about the timing of your sightseeing. Can you drop off your bags, enjoy a couple of hours exploring, and then return to collect them before heading to your next destination?

This approach lets you enjoy the city stress-free and makes your travel day more productive.
