## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Are The Benefits Of No Zipper Luggage?



No zipper luggage offers enhanced security and durability. It uses aluminum frames and locks, reducing risks of theft and damage. These suitcases are lightweight, sturdy, and often TSA-approved, making them ideal for frequent travelers. They also provide a sleek, modern look.



### Are No Zipper Suitcases Airline Approved?



Yes, many no zipper suitcases like LUGGEX and VentureVault models meet airline carry-on size requirements. They are designed with TSA locks and sturdy frames to comply with airline regulations. Always check dimensions before flying to ensure approval for cabin or checked baggage.



### How Durable Is Aluminum Frame No Zipper Luggage?



Aluminum frame no zipper luggage is highly durable and impact-resistant. It protects belongings better than soft cases. The hard shell withstands rough handling during travel. These suitcases also resist water and scratches, extending their lifespan and maintaining a polished appearance.



### Do No Zipper Luggage Cases Have Tsa Locks?



Most premium no zipper luggage models include TSA-approved locks. These locks allow airport security to inspect bags without damage. They enhance security and travel convenience. Brands like Krute and AnyZip ensure travelers can lock their belongings safely.



### Can No Zipper Luggage Fit In Overhead Compartments?



Yes, many no zipper luggage options are sized for overhead compartments. Models like the 20-inch LUGGEX and 22-inch VentureVault fit standard airline dimensions. Always verify your suitcase size with airline policies to avoid check-in issues or additional fees.



### Are No Zipper Suitcases Lightweight For Travel?



No zipper suitcases often feature lightweight materials like polycarbonate and aluminum. This reduces overall bag weight while maintaining strength. Lightweight designs improve maneuverability and reduce travel fatigue. Brands like ROAMING and AIWEINY balance durability with easy handling.
