## Step-by-step Guide To Self Tagging

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=self-tagging-your-luggage-at-heathrow-saves-time-and-makes-your-travel-smoother.-follow-these-clear-steps-to-tag-your-bags-correctly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Self tagging your luggage at Heathrow saves time and makes your travel smoother. Follow these clear steps to tag your bags correctly. Each step is simple and quick. This guide helps you avoid delays and enjoy a stress-free airport experience.

### Checking In Online

Start by checking in on your airline’s website or app. Enter your booking details and select your flight. Choose the number of bags you want to check. Pay any baggage fees if needed. After completing check-in, download or print your boarding pass. This step prepares you for self tagging at the airport.

### Using The Self Tagging Kiosk

Find the self tagging kiosks near the check-in counters. Scan your boarding pass or enter your booking reference. The kiosk will display your flight and baggage details. Confirm the information is correct. The machine prints your luggage tags. Pick up the printed tags carefully from the slot.

### Attaching The Tag Securely

Remove the backing from the sticky side of the tag. Wrap the tag around the handle of your suitcase. Press the sticky part firmly to seal it. Ensure the barcode and your name are visible. Double-check the tag is tight and won’t fall off. Secure tagging helps your luggage reach the right destination.

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=self-tagging-luggage-at-heathrow-can-save-time-and-effort.-avoiding-common-mistakes-ensures-a-smooth-process.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Self tagging luggage at Heathrow can save time and effort. Avoiding common mistakes ensures a smooth process. Small errors cause delays or lost bags. Pay attention to these common pitfalls to keep your travel stress-free.

### Incorrect Tag Placement

Placing the tag in the wrong spot can cause problems. Always attach the tag where the airline instructs. Tags should be clearly visible and securely fastened. Avoid placing tags under straps or inside pockets. This helps scanners read the tag easily.

### Skipping Airline Requirements

Each airline has specific rules for self tagging. Ignoring these rules may lead to rejected tags. Check your airline’s website for detailed instructions. Use only approved tags and follow size guidelines. Meeting airline requirements speeds up baggage processing.

### Forgetting To Confirm Tagging

After tagging, confirm your bag is registered correctly. Use the airline’s app or kiosk to check status. Without confirmation, bags might not be tracked. Always ensure your bag shows as tagged before leaving. This step prevents lost or delayed luggage.

## Troubleshooting Tagging Issues

### What To Do If The Kiosk Fails

Sometimes the self-tagging kiosk may stop working. The screen might freeze or fail to print your luggage tag. Stay calm and try these steps:

* Restart the touchscreen by following on-screen prompts.
* Check if the printer has paper and is ready.
* Try using a different kiosk nearby.
* Ensure your booking reference or passport details are correct.

If the kiosk remains unresponsive, do not waste time. Move on to find airport staff for help.

### Seeking Assistance At The Airport

Airport staff are ready to assist with self-tagging issues. You can find help desks near the kiosks or check-in areas. Staff can:

* Manually print your luggage tags.
* Guide you through the tagging process.
* Provide alternative check-in options if needed.

Look for signs directing you to the airline counters. Arrive early to allow extra time for assistance. This prevents stress and ensures your bags are tagged correctly.
