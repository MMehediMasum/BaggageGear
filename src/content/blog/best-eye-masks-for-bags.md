## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Ingredients Help Reduce Under Eye Bags Effectively?



Ingredients like 24K gold, hyaluronic acid, collagen, aloe vera, and retinol are proven to reduce eye bags. They hydrate, soothe, and improve skin elasticity, minimizing puffiness and dark circles. These ingredients also boost collagen production for firmer, youthful under-eye skin.



### How Often Should I Use Under Eye Masks For Best Results?



For optimal results, use under eye masks 3-4 times a week. Regular use helps reduce puffiness, dark circles, and wrinkles. Consistency maximizes hydration and skin repair, giving a refreshed and brightened under-eye area over time.



### Are These Eye Masks Suitable For Sensitive Skin?



Yes, many under eye masks like those with natural extracts and aloe vera are gentle on sensitive skin. Look for non-irritating formulas without harsh chemicals. Always patch test first to ensure no allergic reactions occur.



### Can These Masks Help With Wrinkles And Fine Lines?



Yes, eye masks containing collagen, retinol, and hyaluronic acid help reduce wrinkles and fine lines. They promote skin elasticity and hydration, smoothing the delicate under-eye skin for a youthful appearance.



### Are Gel Eye Masks Reusable Or Single-use?



Most gel eye masks like cold therapy gel beads are reusable and washable. However, patches with ingredients like gold or collagen are usually single-use. Reusable masks provide ongoing relief, while single-use patches offer targeted treatment.



### Do These Eye Masks Work For Both Men And Women?



Yes, under eye masks are effective for both men and women. They address common concerns like puffiness, dark circles, and wrinkles regardless of gender. Formulas are designed to suit all skin types and tones.
