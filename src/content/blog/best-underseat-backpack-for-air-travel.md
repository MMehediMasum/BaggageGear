## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Backpacks Fit Under Airplane Seats?



Most underseat backpacks range from 15 to 20 liters capacity. Dimensions usually fit airline personal item rules, like 40x20x25 cm. Models like Taygeer, ECOHUB, and HOMIEE comply with common airline size limits. Always check specific airline requirements before travel.



### Are These Backpacks Airline Approved For Laptops?



Yes, many backpacks such as HOTOR and MATEIN fit laptops up to 15. 6 or 17 inches. They have padded compartments to protect laptops during travel. These bags meet airline carry-on and personal item policies for business and leisure trips.



### How Do Vacuum Seal Backpacks Save Space?



Backpacks like MATEIN use vacuum seal compression to reduce bulk. This feature compresses clothes and essentials, maximizing underseat space. It helps travelers pack efficiently without exceeding airline size restrictions. Ideal for longer trips with limited cabin space.



### Are These Backpacks Waterproof And Durable?



Yes, backpacks like ECOHUB, Kono, and wepadre are made with water-resistant materials. They protect belongings from rain and spills. Durable fabrics ensure long-lasting use for outdoor, hiking, and travel needs. Waterproof features add extra security for valuable items.



### What Extra Features Do These Backpacks Offer?



Many have multiple pockets, shoe compartments, USB ports, and anti-theft designs. For example, ECOHUB includes a luggage sleeve and USB charging port. Taygeer offers a separate shoe compartment, while Fmeida provides anti-theft zippers. These features enhance convenience and travel organization.



### Can These Backpacks Be Used For Work And Weekend Trips?



Yes, backpacks like HOTOR and WEPLAN suit business, college, and weekend travel. They combine professional design with spacious compartments for work essentials. Lightweight and easy to carry, they serve as versatile bags for daily use and short getaways.
