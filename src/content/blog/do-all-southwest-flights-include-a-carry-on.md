## Southwest Carry-on Policy

Understanding Southwest's carry-on policy can save you from last-minute hassles at the gate. Southwest Airlines offers a straightforward approach, but there are specific rules you need to know to avoid unexpected fees or having to check your bag. Let’s break down exactly what you can bring aboard and how to prepare your carry-on items for a smooth travel experience.

### Carry-on Allowance Details

Southwest allows each passenger to bring one carry-on bag and one personal item on board at no extra charge. This policy applies to all Southwest flights, so you don’t have to worry about paying for overhead bin space. However, the airline expects you to manage the size and number of your bags to fit their guidelines.

Have you ever had to gate-check a bag because it was too big? Southwest's policy aims to minimize that by clearly defining what counts as a carry-on. Knowing this ahead of time helps you pack smarter and keep your belongings with you during the flight.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Your personal item can be anything that fits under the seat in front of you. Common choices include a backpack, purse, laptop bag, or small tote. This item should be easy to reach during the flight and must not exceed Southwest’s size restrictions.

Think about what you really need during your flight—do you need your laptop handy or a book to read? Packing a personal item that fits the rules lets you avoid delays and stress at boarding.

### Size And Weight Limits

| Bag Type | Maximum Dimensions | Weight Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carry-On Bag | 10 x 16 x 24 inches (including handles and wheels) | No specific weight limit, but must be liftable into overhead bin |
| Personal Item | Must fit under the seat, typically around 18.5 x 8.5 x 13.5 inches | No specific weight limit |

Southwest doesn’t enforce a strict weight limit for carry-ons, but you need to be able to lift your bag into the overhead bin without help. Oversized bags can lead to gate checks, which might delay your trip or cause inconvenience.

Have you checked your bag’s dimensions before packing? Using a measuring tape can prevent surprises at the airport. This small step can make your travel day much smoother and less stressful.

### Carry-on Across Fare Classes

Southwest generally allows one carry-on bag and one personal item for free on every flight. This applies to all fare classes, giving you peace of mind no matter which ticket you buy.

However, the size and weight limits remain consistent, so don’t expect extra perks on carry-ons just because you picked a cheaper fare. The key takeaway: your carry-on rights are protected regardless of fare, but always double-check the size rules to avoid surprises.

### Effect Of Wanna Get Away Fares

The Wanna Get Away fare is Southwest’s most budget-friendly option. While it’s tempting to pick this low-cost fare, some travelers wonder if it means tighter restrictions on carry-on luggage.

The good news? You still get the standard carry-on allowance without additional charges. But be aware that Wanna Get Away fares don’t include perks like priority boarding, which means you might struggle to find overhead bin space if the flight is full.

### Business Select And Anytime Benefits

Choosing Business Select or Anytime fares brings more than just flexible travel dates. These fares grant you early boarding privileges, which can be a game-changer when it comes to stashing your carry-on bag comfortably.

With early boarding, you can secure overhead bin space easily and avoid the stress of last-minute gate checks. Plus, Business Select passengers earn more rapid rewards points, adding value beyond just carry-on convenience.

## Exceptions And Special Cases

While Southwest Airlines generally includes a carry-on bag with every ticket, certain exceptions and special cases might affect this policy. Understanding these nuances can save you stress and unexpected fees at the airport. Let’s dive into some of the unique situations where carry-on allowances might differ.

### Military And Senior Discounts

If you qualify for Southwest's military or senior discounts, your carry-on allowance typically stays the same as regular passengers. However, active military members traveling on orders may receive additional baggage benefits. These can include extra checked bags rather than changes to your carry-on rules.

It’s worth checking your ticket details carefully. Sometimes, discounts don’t automatically update your baggage allowance. Have you ever noticed extra perks after showing your military ID at check-in?

### Unaccompanied Minors

Traveling alone as a child comes with its own set of rules. Southwest allows unaccompanied minors to bring a carry-on bag, but there are strict size and weight limits. Parents should prepare their kids to manage only one small carry-on item.

In addition, some items like sharp objects or liquids over a certain volume are prohibited, even if packed in a carry-on. Does your child’s bag meet these requirements? Double-checking can avoid last-minute hassles.

### International Flight Policies

Southwest’s carry-on policy changes when you fly internationally. While carry-on bags are still included, customs and security regulations might restrict certain items. You might also face stricter size limits depending on your destination.

For example, flights to Mexico or the Caribbean might require you to remove liquids or electronics at security checkpoints differently than domestic flights. Have you ever packed differently for an international trip? Adjusting your carry-on contents ahead of time is a smart move.

## Additional Baggage Options

Southwest Airlines offers several baggage options beyond the standard carry-on. These choices help travelers manage luggage needs for trips of any length. Understanding these options can save time and money at the airport.

### Checked Bag Allowance

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. Each bag can weigh up to 50 pounds. The maximum size is 62 inches in total dimensions. This generous policy helps avoid extra costs for most travelers.

### Fees For Extra Bags

Passengers pay a fee for bags beyond the two free checked ones. The fee is usually $75 per extra bag. Overweight or oversized bags also require additional fees. Paying ahead online can reduce some charges.

### Upgrading To Priority Boarding

Priority boarding offers early access to the overhead bins. This helps secure space for carry-ons and personal items. Passengers can buy this upgrade during booking or later. Early boarding reduces stress about finding room for bags.

## Tips For Packing Smart

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-smart-can-transform-your-travel-experience,-especially-when-flying-with-southwest-airlines.-knowing-how-to-use-your-carry-on-allowance-effectively-saves-time-and-reduces-stress.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing smart can transform your travel experience, especially when flying with Southwest Airlines. Knowing how to use your carry-on allowance effectively saves time and reduces stress. It also helps you avoid unexpected fees and last-minute repacking.

### Maximizing Carry-on Space

Think about how you arrange your items before you even start packing. Rolling clothes instead of folding can save a surprising amount of space. Use packing cubes to keep things organized and compress your items tightly.

Place heavier items at the bottom and lighter ones on top to prevent crushing. Don’t forget to fill small gaps with socks or chargers to use every inch. Have you ever tried packing shoes inside your carry-on? Stuffing socks or small items inside shoes can free up extra space.

### Avoiding Common Mistakes

One mistake is overpacking beyond the carry-on size limits. Southwest allows one carry-on and one personal item, so know the dimensions before you pack. Another common error is forgetting prohibited items, which can delay you at security.

Leaving liquids in large containers or not sealing them properly is a frequent problem. Keep your liquids under 3.4 ounces and place them in a clear, quart-sized bag for quick inspection. Are you packing items that you won’t actually need? Be ruthless—every unnecessary item adds weight and bulk.

### Security Checkpoint Advice

Organize your carry-on so you can easily access electronics and your liquids bag. This speeds up the screening process and reduces the chance of delays. Keep your ID and boarding pass handy to avoid fumbling at the checkpoint.

Wear slip-on shoes to make the shoe removal step faster. Also, avoid wearing belts or jewelry that might set off alarms. How often have you been stuck behind a passenger struggling with their bag? Being prepared helps you move through security smoothly and keeps the line moving.




## Impact Of Carry-on On Boarding

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=carry-on-bags-affect-the-boarding-process-on-southwest-flights.-passengers-with-carry-ons-often-need-to-find-space-in-overhead-bins-quickly.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Carry-on bags affect the boarding process on Southwest flights. Passengers with carry-ons often need to find space in overhead bins quickly. This can slow down boarding and cause delays. Understanding how carry-ons interact with boarding groups and gate-checked bags helps passengers prepare better. It also improves the overall boarding experience for everyone.

### Boarding Groups And Carry-on

Southwest uses a unique boarding system with groups A, B, and C. Passengers in group A board first and have the best chance to find overhead bin space. Those in groups B and C may have limited space left. Carry-on size and number can impact how quickly passengers settle in. Bringing only one small bag increases the chance of smooth boarding.

### Gate-checked Bags Explained

When overhead bins are full, some carry-ons must be gate-checked. Gate-checked bags go under the plane in the cargo hold. Passengers pick them up at the baggage claim. Gate-checking helps speed up boarding since fewer bags need to fit inside the cabin. This process protects bags but adds a step after landing.

### Handling Overhead Bin Space

Overhead bin space is limited on every Southwest flight. Passengers with large or multiple carry-ons may struggle to find room. Early boarding groups have better access to bins. To save space:

* Bring a small, soft-sided bag
* Use personal items that fit under the seat
* Be ready to gate-check if asked

Clear overhead bins help keep boarding fast and orderly. Planning carry-ons carefully benefits all travelers on board.

### Do All Southwest Flights Allow A Free Carry-on Bag?

Yes, Southwest Airlines permits one free carry-on bag per passenger on every flight. This includes a standard-sized bag and one personal item.

### What Are Southwest Airlines’ Carry-on Size Limits?

Carry-on bags must not exceed 10 x 16 x 24 inches. Personal items should fit under the seat in front.

### Can I Bring A Carry-on And A Personal Item On Southwest?

Yes, Southwest allows one carry-on bag plus one personal item like a purse or laptop bag.

### Are There Extra Fees For Carry-on Bags On Southwest Flights?

No, Southwest does not charge fees for carry-on bags. This policy applies to all fare types.
