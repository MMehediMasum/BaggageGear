## Southwest Carry-on Policy

Southwest Airlines is known for its customer-friendly policies, but understanding their carry-on rules can save you time and avoid surprises at the gate. Their carry-on policy is straightforward, yet specific, allowing passengers to bring both a carry-on bag and a personal item without extra fees. Knowing the details helps you pack smart and breeze through security.

### Allowed Carry-on Items

You’re allowed to bring one carry-on bag plus one personal item on board with Southwest Airlines. Common carry-on items include small suitcases, backpacks, and duffel bags. Your personal item can be something like a purse, laptop bag, or briefcase.

Items that fit under the seat in front of you typically qualify as personal items. Think about what you need easy access to during the flight, like your phone charger or a book. Have you ever had to gate-check a bag because it was too big? Avoid that hassle by sticking to these rules.

### Size And Weight Limits

Southwest allows carry-on bags up to 10 x 16 x 24 inches, including handles and wheels. There’s no specific weight limit for carry-ons, but the bag must fit in the overhead bin. If your bag is too large or heavy, you might be asked to check it at the gate.

Measuring your bag before you leave can prevent last-minute stress. A lightweight, well-packed carry-on can make your travel experience smoother. Have you ever struggled fitting your bag into the overhead bin? Southwest’s dimensions help avoid that problem.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Your personal item should fit under the seat in front of you, with typical dimensions around 18.5 x 8.5 x 13.5 inches. Common personal items include small backpacks, laptop bags, and handbags. This item must be small enough to store safely and not block the aisle or other passengers.

Choosing the right personal item can improve your flight comfort. A well-packed personal item keeps essentials close without crowding your space. What do you usually carry as your personal item to stay organized during flights?

### Free Carry-on Allowance

Southwest Airlines allows you to bring one carry-on bag and one personal item on board at no extra cost.

* Your carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin and meet size limits of 10 x 16 x 24 inches.
* The personal item should fit under the seat in front of you, like a purse or laptop bag.

This policy means you can travel light without paying extra, unlike many other airlines that charge for carry-ons. Have you ever been surprised by an airline’s carry-on fees? Southwest’s free allowance might be a relief if you’re used to paying.

### Excess Baggage Fees

If your carry-on exceeds the allowed size or weight, Southwest will gate-check your bag for free, so you won’t pay a fee upfront.

However, if you bring more than one carry-on bag, you may be charged for the additional item as checked luggage. Checked bag fees start at $75 per bag, with higher fees for overweight or oversized items.

It’s important to measure your bags before arriving at the airport to avoid surprises. Would you rather adjust your packing or pay extra fees?

## Packing Tips For Carry-on

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-your-carry-on-for-a-southwest-airlines-flight-can-be-a-game-changer-for-your-travel-experience.-knowing-how-to-pack-smartly-saves-you-time-at-the-airport-and-keeps-your-essentials-within-easy-reach.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing your carry-on for a Southwest Airlines flight can be a game-changer for your travel experience. Knowing how to pack smartly saves you time at the airport and keeps your essentials within easy reach. Let’s dive into some practical tips that will help you make the most of your carry-on allowance.

### Maximizing Space

Start by choosing a soft-sided bag that fits Southwest’s size limits. Soft bags can squeeze into overhead bins more easily than hard-shell luggage. Rolling your clothes instead of folding them creates extra room and reduces wrinkles.

Use packing cubes or compression bags to organize items and compress bulky clothes. Place heavier items like shoes at the bottom, near the wheels, for better balance. Don’t forget to tuck smaller items like chargers and toiletries into shoes or corners to avoid wasted space.

### Prohibited Items

Southwest follows TSA rules closely, so avoid packing liquids over 3.4 ounces (100 ml) in your carry-on. Sharp objects such as knives, scissors, or tools aren’t allowed, even if packed carefully. Check for any unusual items like large batteries or sporting gear that might need special handling.

Always review Southwest’s latest prohibited items list before packing. Have you ever been surprised by an item you thought was allowed? Double-checking saves you from last-minute hassles at security checkpoints.

### Smart Packing Hacks

* Wear your bulkiest shoes and jacket onto the plane to free up space inside your bag.
* Use empty spaces inside hats or shoes to stash socks, underwear, or chargers.
* Keep a small, clear bag for toiletries that fits TSA liquid rules and place it on top for easy access.
* Pack a reusable water bottle to fill after security—you’ll stay hydrated without carrying extra weight.

Think about how you unpack at your destination. Packing with easy access to daily essentials like your phone charger or medications can save frustration. What’s one item you always wish you had quick access to during a flight?

Choosing between checking bags and carrying on depends on your trip needs. Carry-on luggage lets you keep belongings close and avoid waiting at baggage claim. ### Benefits Of Carry-on

* Skip waiting for bags at your destination.
* Keep important items within reach.
* Save money by avoiding checked bag fees.
* Move faster through airport security and boarding.
* Reduce the risk of lost or delayed luggage.

### When To Check Bags

* Traveling with bulky or heavy items.
* Taking long trips requiring more clothes.
* Carrying liquids or items not allowed in carry-on.
* Wanting to avoid overhead bin space issues.
* Transporting sports or special equipment.
