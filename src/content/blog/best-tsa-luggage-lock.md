## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Lock Tsa Approved?



A TSA approved lock can be opened by airport security using a master key. This allows security to inspect luggage without damaging the lock. These locks are essential for travelers as they ensure security and compliance with airport regulations. They are identifiable by a red diamond logo.



### How Do You Reset A Combination Lock?



To reset, open the lock with the current combination. Turn the reset button or shackle to a reset position. Set your desired combination and ensure it clicks into place. Return the reset button or shackle to its original position. Always test the new combination before locking your luggage.



### Why Choose A 4-digit Lock Over A 3-digit One?



A 4-digit lock provides 10,000 possible combinations, making it more secure than a 3-digit lock. This higher security level is beneficial for protecting valuable items during travel. The extra digit significantly reduces the chance of an unauthorized person guessing the combination.



### How Does An Inspection Indicator Work?



An inspection indicator alerts you if TSA has opened your luggage. It changes color or position when accessed. This feature provides peace of mind, allowing travelers to know if their luggage was inspected. It's a valuable tool for identifying potential tampering or mishandling.



### Are Cable Locks Better Than Rigid Locks?



Cable locks are flexible and easier to use on multiple zippers. They can secure bags of different sizes and shapes. This versatility is beneficial for travelers with various luggage types. Cable locks are also more durable and less prone to breaking under pressure compared to rigid locks.
