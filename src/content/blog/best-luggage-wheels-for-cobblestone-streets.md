## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Luggage Wheels Suitable For Cobblestone Streets?



Luggage wheels for cobblestones need shock absorption and durability. Heavy-duty PVC and silicone covers reduce noise and damage. Larger, dual-spinner wheels improve stability and maneuverability on uneven surfaces. These features ensure smooth travel over rough, uneven terrain like cobblestone streets.



### How Do I Measure Luggage Wheels For Replacement?



Measure the screw holes and wheel diameter precisely. Use a ruler or caliper to check width and height. Compare measurements with replacement kits to ensure compatibility. Accurate measuring prevents fitting issues and ensures the wheels function properly.



### Are Universal Swivel Casters Compatible With Most Suitcases?



Yes, universal swivel casters fit many suitcase models. They usually include multiple screws for secure installation. However, always check the screw hole size and wheel dimensions before buying. Compatibility ensures easy installation and smooth rolling performance.



### How Do Silicone Wheel Covers Protect Luggage Wheels?



Silicone wheel covers absorb shocks and reduce noise. They protect wheels from wear, scratches, and damage on rough surfaces. These covers also prevent wheel falls and increase luggage lifespan. Silicone is flexible, durable, and easy to install.



### Can I Install Luggage Replacement Wheels Myself?



Yes, most luggage wheels are easy to install with basic tools. Follow manufacturer instructions for removing old wheels and securing new ones. Measure screw holes before installation for a perfect fit. DIY installation saves time and money.



### Which Luggage Wheels Are Best For Heavy-duty Travel?



Heavy-duty PVC wheels with shock absorption are ideal. Look for dual-spinner wheels for stability and smooth movement. Brands like Samsonite and KROSER offer durable, lightweight options. These wheels handle rough terrains and heavy loads efficiently.
