## Herschel Brand Origins

The Herschel brand has a rich history rooted in family tradition and quality craftsmanship. It began with a desire to create backpacks that combine style and function. The story behind Herschel is about passion, heritage, and dedication to detail.

### Founding Story

Herschel Supply Co. started in 2009 in Vancouver, Canada. Brothers Jamie and Lyndon Cormack named the brand after their hometown. The company grew from a small family business into a global accessory brand. They focused on making backpacks with a classic look and durable build. Their goal was to bring back simple, timeless designs.

### Brand Philosophy

Herschel believes in creating products that last long and look good. They value craftsmanship and pay attention to every detail. The brand mixes modern trends with vintage styles. Their backpacks are practical and stylish for everyday use. Herschel also cares about sustainability and responsible production.

## How To Identify Genuine Herschel Backpacks

Knowing how to spot a genuine Herschel backpack can save you from wasting money on knockoffs. Authentic Herschel products have specific details that counterfeit versions often miss. Paying attention to these details will help you confidently identify the real deal.

### Authenticity Markers

Check the Herschel label carefully. Genuine backpacks have a clean, clear logo with sharp fonts and no spelling mistakes.

* The tag usually shows “The Finest Quality” and “Trade Mark” clearly printed.
* Look inside for a sewn-in label with a unique serial number or batch code.
* The zippers should be smooth and often branded with “YKK” or another high-quality manufacturer.
* Stitching is even and strong, with no loose threads or irregular patterns.

Have you ever seen a backpack with cheap plastic zippers or fuzzy, uneven stitching? That’s a red flag.

### Avoiding Counterfeits

Buy only from trusted retailers or directly from Herschel’s official website. Third-party sellers on unknown sites often sell fakes.

Compare prices—if the deal looks too good to be true, it probably is. Genuine Herschel backpacks have consistent pricing across major outlets.

* Inspect the packaging; authentic Herschel backpacks come with branded tags and care instructions.
* Check customer reviews and seller ratings before purchasing.
* Ask for close-up photos if buying secondhand to verify the details mentioned above.

Have you checked the seller’s return policy? Genuine products usually come with clear return options, unlike many counterfeit sellers.
