## Allegiant Checked Bag Size Limits

Understanding Allegiant's checked bag size limits can save you from unexpected fees and travel hassles. Knowing exactly how big your bag can be ensures smoother airport experiences and helps you pack smarter. Let's break down what Allegiant allows so you can travel with confidence.

### Maximum Dimensions Allowed

Allegiant sets a clear size limit for checked bags: your luggage's total dimensions (length + width + height) must not exceed **62 inches (157 cm)**. This means your suitcase should comfortably fit within this combined measurement to avoid extra charges.

Think about your usual suitcase—does it seem bulky? Measuring it before your trip can prevent surprises at check-in. A bag that's just a few inches over can lead to overweight or oversize fees, so double-check those numbers.

### Weight Restrictions

Besides size, weight is a crucial factor. Allegiant typically allows checked bags to weigh up to **40 pounds (18 kg)**without additional fees, but this can vary depending on your fare or route.

If your bag exceeds this limit, expect to pay an overweight fee. Have you ever struggled with packing light? Consider weighing your bag at home or the airport before checking in to avoid last-minute repacking.

### Measurement Tips

Measuring your bag accurately can be tricky. Use a tape measure to get the length, width, and height, including wheels and handles—don’t leave any part out.

Lay the bag flat, then measure each side. Add those three numbers to confirm you’re under the 62-inch limit. If your bag is close to the edge, try rearranging items to reduce bulges.

Have you tried using a luggage scale? It’s a handy tool that can give you peace of mind before heading to the airport.

## Packing Strategies For Size Compliance

### Choosing The Right Luggage

Select luggage that fits within Allegiant’s size rules. Look for bags labeled with dimensions under 62 linear inches (length + width + height). Hard-shell suitcases protect your items but can be less flexible. Soft-sided bags allow slight compression and can fit better in tight spaces. ### Efficient Packing Techniques

Organize your items to use every inch of space inside your bag. Roll clothes instead of folding to reduce wrinkles and save space. ### Using Compression Bags

Compression bags squeeze air out of clothes to reduce volume. They can shrink bulky sweaters and jackets to fit better. These bags also protect clothes from spills and dirt inside your suitcase. Use vacuum-seal bags for the best compression but remember the bag itself adds some thickness. Check the final size of your bag after compression to stay within Allegiant’s rules.
