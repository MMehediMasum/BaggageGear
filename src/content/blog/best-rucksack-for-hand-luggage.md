## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Rucksack Fits As Hand Luggage On Flights?



Most airlines accept carry-on bags up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Rucksacks like tomtoc 40L and ECOHUB 25L fit these dimensions perfectly. Always check your airline’s specific size rules before traveling to avoid hassles at the gate.



### Are These Rucksacks Tsa Friendly For Airport Security?



Yes, models like tomtoc and Tolaccea are TSA friendly. They feature lay-flat designs that speed up security checks. This saves time and reduces the need to remove laptops or electronics during screening.



### Which Backpacks Offer Laptop Protection For Travel?



Backpacks such as Z-MGKISS, WITZMAN, and KEQIKAI provide padded compartments for laptops up to 17. 3 inches. They ensure your device stays secure and protected from shocks or scratches while on the move.



### Are These Travel Backpacks Water-resistant?



Yes, most listed rucksacks like ECOHUB, Z-MGKISS, and Tolaccea have water-resistant materials. This helps protect your belongings during light rain or accidental spills, making them reliable for travel in various weather conditions.



### Do These Backpacks Have Anti-theft Features?



The Z-MGKISS backpack includes anti-theft features like hidden pockets and lockable zippers. Such security measures help keep valuables safe during crowded travel situations or daily commutes.



### Which Backpacks Include Extra Compartments Like Shoe Storage?



Backpacks like WITZMAN and Taygeer offer dedicated shoe compartments. These separate storage areas keep shoes away from clean clothes, enhancing organization and convenience during trips or gym visits.
