## United Carry-on Allowance

Understanding United's carry-on allowance helps travelers pack smart. Knowing what and how much you can carry saves time at the airport. United allows passengers to bring both a carry-on bag and a personal item on board.

Each type of item has its own rules. Size and weight limits ensure safety and comfort for everyone. Let’s break down United’s carry-on allowance in detail.

### Standard Carry-on Bags

Passengers may bring one standard carry-on bag free of charge. This bag fits in the overhead bin during the flight. Typical examples include small suitcases and larger backpacks.

United requires the bag to fit within these dimensions:

* 9 inches x 14 inches x 22 inches (22 cm x 35 cm x 56 cm)

Keep your carry-on compact to avoid gate check fees.

### Personal Item Rules

Along with the carry-on bag, each passenger may bring one personal item. This item fits under the seat in front of you. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.

The personal item must be smaller than the carry-on bag. It should be easy to store and access during the flight.

### Size And Weight Limits

United focuses on size rather than weight for carry-ons. The maximum size is 22 x 14 x 9 inches for the carry-on bag.

Personal items should fit under the seat, usually smaller than 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

There is no official weight limit, but heavy bags may be hard to lift or stow.

Choose bags with multiple compartments for better organization. Roll clothes instead of folding to save space. Place smaller items inside shoes or corners of the bag. Use packing cubes to separate and compress clothes. Fill every gap to avoid wasted space.

### Packing Smart

Pack only essentials to avoid heavy bags. Wear your bulkiest shoes and jacket on the plane. Keep liquids in containers under 3.4 ounces and place them in a clear bag. Use travel-sized toiletries to meet airline rules. Keep important documents and electronics in an easy-to-reach pocket.

### Avoiding Fees

Check United’s carry-on size and weight limits before packing. Stay within the allowed number of bags to avoid extra costs. Weigh your bag at home to prevent surprises at the airport. If your bag is close to the limit, remove non-essential items. Consider a personal item like a backpack to increase your carry-on space.

### Mileageplus Members

## Handling Oversized And Overweight Bags

Handling oversized and overweight bags on United Airlines can be tricky. The airline has strict rules about bag size and weight. Exceeding these limits means extra fees. Knowing the costs and smart packing tips helps travelers avoid surprises. Sometimes, shipping bags is a better choice.

### Fees For Extra Or Heavy Bags

United charges fees for bags that are too large or too heavy. Oversized bags usually cost more than standard checked bags. Overweight bags also have extra fees based on weight ranges.

| Bag Type | Weight Limit | Fee Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Checked Bag | Up to 50 lbs (23 kg) | Included with ticket or standard fee |
| Overweight Bag | 51 to 70 lbs (23 to 32 kg) | $100 - $200 |
| Heavy Bag | 71 to 100 lbs (32 to 45 kg) | $200 - $400 |
| Oversized Bag | More than 62 inches (157 cm) total dimension | $200 - $400 |

Fees vary by route and ticket class. Check your ticket details before packing.

### Packing Strategies

Pack smart to avoid overweight or oversized bags. Use lightweight suitcases to save weight. Distribute heavy items evenly in multiple bags. Roll clothes instead of folding to save space. Wear your heaviest clothes on the plane. Use compression bags to reduce bulk.

* Check bag weight before leaving home
* Use a luggage scale for accuracy
* Leave extra space for souvenirs or gifts
* Consider carry-on for heavier items

### When To Ship Bags Instead

Shipping bags may be cheaper for heavy or large luggage. It avoids airline fees and long waits at baggage claim. Shipping works well for long trips or special equipment. Use reliable shipping services with tracking options. Plan ahead to allow delivery time before your trip.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Many Carry-on Bags Does United Allow?

United permits one carry-on bag plus one personal item per passenger. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin and meet size limits of 9 x 14 x 22 inches.

### What Is The Weight Limit For United Carry-on Bags?

United does not impose a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. However, passengers must be able to lift their bags into the overhead bin unaided.

### Can I Bring More Than One Personal Item On United?

No, United allows only one personal item such as a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. It must fit under the seat in front of you.

### Are There Size Restrictions For United Checked Bags?

Yes, United's checked bags must not exceed 62 linear inches (length + width + height). Oversized bags may incur extra fees.
