## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes A Power Strip Cruise-approved?



A cruise-approved power strip lacks surge protection, meeting cruise ship safety standards. It often includes multiple outlets and USB ports. This ensures you can charge multiple devices efficiently. Always check cruise line policies before traveling to confirm compliance.



### Why Avoid Surge Protectors On Cruise Ships?



Surge protectors pose a fire risk on cruise ships due to sensitive onboard electrical systems. Non-surge options are safer and meet cruise line regulations. They still provide multiple outlets and USB ports for charging.



### How Many Usb Ports Are Ideal For Cruising?



Four USB ports are ideal for cruising, allowing simultaneous charging of multiple devices. Look for models with USB-C ports for faster charging. This ensures you stay connected and powered up throughout your trip.



### Are Flat Plug Extension Cords Better For Cruises?



Flat plug extension cords are better for cruises due to limited cabin space. They fit snugly against walls and furniture. This design prevents tripping hazards and maximizes room for your belongings.



### Can I Use A Power Strip For International Travel?



Yes, many cruise-approved power strips work internationally. Look for models with universal adapters. Ensure compatibility with different outlet types and voltages. This versatility makes them a great travel companion beyond cruises.
