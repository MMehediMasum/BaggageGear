### Standard Carry-on Allowance

Spirit Airlines allows one standard carry-on bag per passenger, but there’s a catch: you have to pay for it. This carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin, and you’ll need to add it during booking or before you reach the gate.

Skipping this step can lead to higher fees at the airport, so it’s better to plan ahead. Think about whether you really need that extra bag or if your personal item can hold everything.

### Personal Item Dimensions

Everyone gets one free personal item, like a backpack or small purse. But it must fit under the seat in front of you, so Spirit has strict size limits: 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

Make sure you measure your bag before packing because even a small difference can mean your item won't fit. When I flew last time, I underestimated the size and had to pay extra to check my bag—something you definitely want to avoid.

### Fees For Carry-on Bags

Spirit’s fees for carry-on bags vary depending on when you add them and your route. Adding a carry-on during booking is cheapest, but if you wait until check-in or the gate, fees increase significantly.

To save money, always add your carry-on as early as possible. Have you ever been surprised by a hefty baggage fee? Planning ahead can keep your trip budget-friendly.

## How To Check Carry-on At The Airport

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=checking-your-carry-on-at-the-airport-can-save-you-time-and-stress.-spirit-airlines-has-clear-rules-for-carry-on-bags.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Checking your carry-on at the airport can save you time and stress. Spirit Airlines has clear rules for carry-on bags. Knowing how to check your bag helps you avoid extra fees or delays. Follow these tips to manage your carry-on smoothly.

### Self-check Options

Spirit offers self-check kiosks at most airports. These kiosks let you print your boarding pass and bag tags. You can tag your carry-on for checked status here. The process is quick and easy. Look for signs pointing to self-check kiosks. Follow the instructions on the screen carefully. After tagging, drop your bag at the designated counter.

### Gate Check Procedures

If your carry-on is too large or the overhead bins are full, gate check is an option. Present your bag to the gate agent before boarding. They will tag your bag and place it in the cargo hold. You get your bag back at the plane’s exit. Gate check is usually free but confirm with the agent. Keep valuable items with you as gate-checked bags are not accessible during flight.

### Dealing With Overhead Bin Space

Spirit planes have limited overhead bin space. Boarding early increases your chance to store your carry-on overhead. If bins fill up, flight attendants may ask you to gate check your bag. Have a smaller personal item on hand to keep with you. Pack smart to fit under the seat if needed. Arrive at the gate early to secure space for your carry-on.
