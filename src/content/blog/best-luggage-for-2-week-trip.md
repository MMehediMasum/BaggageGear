## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Luggage Size Is Best For A 2-week Trip?



A 25-30 inch suitcase is ideal for a 2-week trip. It offers ample space without being too bulky. Expandable models provide extra room if needed, making packing flexible and convenient for longer travels.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Suitcases Better For Long Trips?



Hardside luggage offers superior protection and scratch resistance. Softside bags provide flexibility and extra pockets. For a 2-week trip, choose based on your packing style and durability preferences.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels For Travel Luggage?



Spinner wheels enhance maneuverability and reduce strain on your arms. Four multi-directional wheels allow smooth rolling in any direction. They are highly recommended for ease during long trips.



### Can Expandable Luggage Really Increase Packing Space?



Yes, expandable luggage can increase capacity by up to 15%. This feature is useful for souvenirs or extra items on a 2-week trip. It adds versatility without compromising suitcase size.



### What Are The Benefits Of Using Packing Cubes?



Packing cubes organize clothes efficiently and save space. They simplify unpacking and keep items wrinkle-free. For a 2-week trip, cubes help separate outfits and accessories neatly.



### Is A Duffle Bag With Wheels Suitable For Long Trips?



Rolling duffle bags combine flexibility and ease of transport. Durable materials and multiple compartments make them practical. They are a great alternative for travelers who prefer casual style and convenience.
