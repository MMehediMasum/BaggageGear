## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Sizes Do Vacuum Storage Bags Come In?



Vacuum storage bags come in various sizes like jumbo, large, medium, and small. Some sets include roll bags for custom sizes. This variety helps store different items such as clothes, blankets, and pillows efficiently.



### How Much Space Can Vacuum Bags Save?



Vacuum bags can save up to 80% of storage space. They compress bulky items like comforters and clothes by removing air. This makes storage easier and more organized, ideal for closets, luggage, or seasonal storage.



### Are Hand Pumps Or Electric Pumps Better For Vacuum Bags?



Hand pumps are portable and convenient for travel or small jobs. Electric pumps save time and effort for large storage needs. Choose based on your usage frequency and the volume of items to compress.



### Can Vacuum Storage Bags Protect Against Moisture And Pests?



Yes, vacuum storage bags create airtight seals that protect contents from moisture, dust, and pests. This keeps clothes and bedding fresh and clean during long-term storage or travel.



### Are Vacuum Bags Reusable And Durable?



Most vacuum storage bags are reusable and made from durable plastic. Proper use and careful handling ensure long-term use for multiple storage cycles, providing excellent value and sustainability.



### Can Vacuum Bags Be Used For Travel Packing?



Vacuum bags are ideal for travel packing. They reduce luggage bulk and help organize clothes efficiently. Many sets include different sizes to fit various travel needs and maximize suitcase space.
