### Spotting Genuine Bogg Bags

Genuine Bogg Bags have distinct features. Check the material first. Authentic bags use thick, durable EVA plastic. The texture feels sturdy, not flimsy or soft.

Look for the logo carefully. It is usually embossed or printed clearly. Colors should be vibrant and consistent. Handles have a unique design with comfortable grips.

Inside the bag, tags and labels provide details. Authentic tags include the brand name and care instructions. Stitching should be even and neat without loose threads.

### Avoiding Counterfeit Products

Buy only from official stores or trusted sellers. Avoid very cheap offers that seem too good. Fake bags often have spelling mistakes on labels or packaging.

Check reviews from other buyers. Negative feedback about quality or delivery can be a warning. Use payment methods that offer buyer protection.

Beware of unusual colors or patterns not listed on the official site. Counterfeit products often copy popular styles but miss small details.
