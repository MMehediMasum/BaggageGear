Choosing a good eye mask makes a big difference during travel. It blocks out light, helping you sleep better on planes. Many masks have soft, breathable fabrics and adjustable straps for comfort. Some designs reduce pressure on eyes, perfect for side sleepers or those with lash extensions.



Cooling masks can soothe tired eyes, while contoured shapes avoid eye contact and pressure. Lightweight and compact masks fit easily in carry-ons. A quality eye mask creates a dark, quiet space, letting you relax despite noisy cabins. Whether for short naps or long flights, these masks improve your rest and reduce jet lag. Comfortable eye masks make flying more peaceful and refreshing.

### Do Sleep Masks With Cooling Features Improve Travel Rest?
