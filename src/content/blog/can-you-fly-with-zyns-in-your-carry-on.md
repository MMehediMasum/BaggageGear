## Zyns And Air Travel Rules

Traveling with Zyns requires understanding air travel rules. Knowing these rules helps avoid problems at security checkpoints and during flights. Let’s explore what Zyns are and how air travel regulations affect carrying them.

### What Are Zyns?

Zyns are nicotine pouches. They contain nicotine but no tobacco leaf. Users place them between the gum and lip. Nicotine releases slowly without smoke or vapor. Zyns come in small, discreet packages. They are popular for being smoke-free and spit-free.

### Tsa Regulations On Nicotine Pouches

The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) allows nicotine pouches in carry-on bags. They consider Zyns safe and legal to carry. Keep Zyns in original packaging for easy inspection. Do not open large quantities to avoid suspicion. The TSA prohibits liquid nicotine in carry-on luggage over 3.4 ounces. Solid pouches like Zyns do not fall under liquid rules.

### International Flight Considerations

Rules vary by country for nicotine products. Some countries ban nicotine pouches completely. Others allow limited amounts for personal use. Check the destination’s regulations before packing Zyns. Declare nicotine products at customs if required. Carry only small quantities to avoid fines or confiscation. Airline policies might also restrict nicotine use onboard.
