## How To Request Tags Online

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=requesting-luggage-tags-online-for-your-celebrity-cruise-is-simple-and-fast.-it-helps-keep-your-bags-organized-and-ready-for-your-trip.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Requesting luggage tags online for your Celebrity Cruise is simple and fast. It helps keep your bags organized and ready for your trip. You can do it from home using your cruise account. This saves time and avoids confusion at the port. Follow the easy steps below to get your tags.

### Logging Into Your Cruise Account

Start by visiting the official Celebrity Cruises website. Find the login button at the top right corner. Enter your username and password carefully. If you forgot your password, use the reset option. Once logged in, you will see your cruise details on the dashboard.

### Navigating The Tag Request Section

Look for the section named “Manage Booking” or “My Cruise.” Click on it to open your reservation details. Find the link or tab labeled “Luggage Tags” or “Baggage.” Click on that to enter the tag request area. Here, you can enter the number of tags you need.

### Printing Or Downloading Tags

After requesting, the system will show your luggage tags. You can choose to print them directly from the page. Or, download the tags as a PDF file to print later. Make sure your printer has enough ink and paper. Attach the printed tags securely to your bags before arriving at the port.

The luggage tag pickup area is usually set up near the cruise terminal entrance or the check-in counters. Look for clear signage or ask a staff member if you’re unsure where to go. Most terminals open the tag pickup desk about two hours before the ship’s boarding time and close roughly an hour after boarding begins.

Arriving early lets you avoid long lines and gives you enough time to tag your bags properly. Have you ever waited in a long queue only to miss your boarding window? Getting your tags early prevents that hassle.

### What To Bring For Verification

To get your luggage tags, you’ll need to show your cruise documents. This usually includes your boarding pass and a valid photo ID like a passport or driver’s license. Staff will verify your identity to ensure the tags are assigned correctly to your stateroom.

## Tips For Attaching Luggage Tags

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=attaching-luggage-tags-correctly-is-crucial-to-ensure-your-bags-arrive-safely-on-your-celebrity-cruise.-a-poorly-attached-tag-can-easily-get-lost,-causing-unnecessary-stress-and-delays.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Attaching luggage tags correctly is crucial to ensure your bags arrive safely on your Celebrity Cruise. A poorly attached tag can easily get lost, causing unnecessary stress and delays. Here are some practical tips to help you secure your luggage tags effectively and keep your belongings safe throughout your journey.

### Securing Tags Properly

Make sure your luggage tag is firmly fastened to a sturdy part of your bag, like the handle or a strap. Avoid attaching it to zippers or weak loops that might break during handling.

Use a strong plastic loop or a metal clasp to attach the tag. If the tag’s string feels flimsy, replace it with a more durable option before your trip.

Have you checked if your tag’s information is clearly visible and waterproof? Smudged or torn tags can make it hard for staff to identify your luggage quickly.

### Double-tagging For Extra Safety

Adding a second tag inside your luggage can be a lifesaver. If the external tag falls off, the internal one still carries your contact details.

Use a printed copy of your itinerary or a business card inside a clear plastic pouch. This extra step gives you peace of mind and helps the cruise line return your luggage faster if it gets misplaced.

Think about combining a traditional tag with a modern solution like a QR code tag or luggage tracker for enhanced security. How confident are you that your bag will always be identifiable?

### Lost Or Missing Tags

If your luggage tag goes missing, act quickly. Visit the guest services desk as soon as you notice the issue. They can often track your bags using your cruise ID and cabin number.

Keep a copy of your tag information or a photo on your phone before handing over your luggage. This simple step can speed up recovery if your tag is lost. Have you ever lost something important and wished you had a backup plan? This is your chance to avoid that frustration.

### Damaged Or Illegible Tags

Tags can get wet, torn, or smudged, making them hard to read. If your tag looks damaged, notify the staff immediately. They’ll provide a replacement or update the information to prevent misplacement.

Try to use a clear plastic cover for your tags to protect them from damage. Small actions like this make a big difference. Do you usually check your gear before trips? This is a perfect moment to start.
