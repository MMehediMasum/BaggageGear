## Southwest's Current Luggage Policy

Southwest Airlines is known for its customer-friendly luggage policy. It offers generous baggage allowances compared to many other airlines. Passengers can bring bags without paying extra fees in most cases. This policy helps travelers save money and avoid surprises at the airport.

Understanding Southwest's current luggage rules is important before booking a flight. Knowing what you can bring for free and the limits can make your travel smoother. Here is a clear look at Southwest's baggage policies.

### Free Checked Bags

Southwest allows two free checked bags per passenger. Each bag can weigh up to 50 pounds. The maximum size is 62 inches in total dimensions (length + width + height). If a bag is heavier or larger, extra fees will apply. This free baggage offer is one of the airline’s biggest perks.

### Carry-on Allowance

Passengers may bring one carry-on bag and one personal item on board. The carry-on bag must fit in the overhead bin. The personal item should fit under the seat in front. Examples of personal items include purses, laptops, or small backpacks. There are no extra fees for carry-on bags.

### Exceptions And Special Items

Some items require special handling or fees. Musical instruments, sports equipment, and pets have different rules. Medical devices and assistive equipment are usually allowed without extra charge. Southwest recommends checking specific rules for these items before flying. It helps avoid delays or surprises at the airport.
