## Spirit Airlines Baggage Policy

Spirit Airlines has a unique baggage policy that travelers must know before flying. The airline often charges for checked bags, making it important to understand the rules. Knowing the baggage policy helps avoid surprises at the airport. Here is a clear overview of Spirit Airlines baggage rules and fees.

### Checked Bag Size And Weight Limits

Spirit Airlines allows checked bags up to 62 inches in total size. This means the length, width, and height combined must not exceed 62 inches. The weight limit for each checked bag is 40 pounds. Bags heavier than this will face extra fees. Oversized or overweight bags cost more to check.

Spirit Airlines charges fees for checked bags depending on when you pay. Paying online before your flight is cheaper than paying at the airport. The first checked bag usually costs between $30 and $45 if paid in advance. The fee rises to about $65 if paid during check-in or at the gate. The second checked bag costs more, around $40 to $55 online and up to $75 at the airport.

### How To Pay For Checked Bags

Passengers can pay for checked bags during booking on Spirit Airlines’ website. Adding bags before arriving at the airport saves money. You can also pay during online check-in or at the airport counter. Paying at the gate is the most expensive option. It is best to plan ahead and pay early for checked bags.

### Carry-on Baggage Rules

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item for free, like a purse or small backpack. Carry-on bags that do not fit under the seat must be checked and may have fees. Carry-on bags have size limits of 22 x 18 x 10 inches. Larger carry-ons must be checked and charged as a checked bag.

Bags must meet size and weight limits. Oversized or heavy bags cost more. Below are the details.

### Standard Weight Allowance

The maximum weight for a checked bag is 40 pounds (18 kg). Bags over this weight need extra fees. Dimensions must not exceed 62 inches (length + width + height). This size includes wheels and handles. Bags larger than this face additional charges.

### Oversized And Overweight Charges

Bags weighing between 41 and 100 pounds have extra fees. Bags over 100 pounds are not accepted. If your bag is larger than 62 inches, Spirit charges an oversized fee. This fee is separate from the regular bag fee. Always measure and weigh your bag before travel to avoid surprises.
