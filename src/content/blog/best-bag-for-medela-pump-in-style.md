Yes, many breast pump bags come with cooler compartments. These compartments help keep breast milk fresh during travel or work. Bags like the LIVEAGAIN and Jaffzora models include cooler sections with ice packs. This feature is essential for maintaining milk quality and convenience.

### What Should I Consider When Choosing A Pump Bag?



When choosing a pump bag, consider size, compartments, and additional features like cooler sections or waterproof mats. Compatibility with your specific Medela model is crucial. Look for durable materials and ease of access to ensure convenience during use.
