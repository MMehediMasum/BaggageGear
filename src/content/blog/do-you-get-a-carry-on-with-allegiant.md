## Allegiant Carry-on Allowance

Understanding Allegiant's carry-on allowance can save you from last-minute hassles at the airport. Knowing exactly what size and weight limits you must follow helps you pack smarter and travel lighter. Let's break down the key rules for carry-on luggage so you can breeze through your next trip with Allegiant.

### Size Limits For Carry-ons

Allegiant allows one carry-on bag per passenger, but it must fit within specific dimensions to qualify. Your carry-on should not exceed **22 x 14 x 9 inches**, including handles and wheels.

This size limit is pretty standard, but if your bag is even slightly larger, you may be asked to check it at the gate, which can add extra fees. Have you ever had to repack your bag in a rush? Sticking to these dimensions avoids that stress.

### Weight Restrictions

Unlike some airlines, Allegiant does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. However, your bag must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead bin without assistance.

This means practicality matters more than a strict number. If your carry-on is too heavy, it could slow down boarding or cause inconvenience to other passengers.

### Personal Item Rules

In addition to your carry-on, you can bring one personal item onboard. This could be a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack.

Your personal item must fit under the seat in front of you, generally measuring no more than **18 x 14 x 8 inches**. Keep this in mind when choosing your bag.

Think about what you really need easy access to during your flight. A well-packed personal item can hold essentials and save you from digging through your carry-on mid-flight.

## Packing Tips For Allegiant Flights

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=packing-for-allegiant-flights-requires-a-smart-approach-to-make-the-most-of-your-carry-on-allowance.-since-allegiant-has-specific-size-and-weight-limits,-knowing-how-to-pack-efficiently-can-save-you-both-time-and-money.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Packing for Allegiant flights requires a smart approach to make the most of your carry-on allowance. Since Allegiant has specific size and weight limits, knowing how to pack efficiently can save you both time and money. Let's dive into some practical tips that help you travel light without leaving behind essentials or risking extra fees.

### Maximizing Carry-on Space

Think about packing your carry-on like a puzzle. Rolling clothes instead of folding them reduces wrinkles and saves space. Use packing cubes or compression bags to keep your items organized and compact.

Wear your heaviest shoes and jacket on the plane. This frees up room in your bag and keeps you comfortable during the flight. Also, fill every nook—socks inside shoes, small items inside hats—so no space goes wasted.

### Essential Items To Pack

Focus on items that make your trip smoother and more comfortable. Always include your travel documents, phone charger, and a small toiletry kit with essentials like toothpaste and deodorant.

Consider a lightweight reusable water bottle to stay hydrated after security. Don’t forget a book or headphones for entertainment; these small additions make a big difference on flights that might have limited amenities.

### Prohibited Items To Avoid

Allegiant follows TSA rules strictly, so avoid packing liquids over 3.4 ounces in your carry-on. Sharp objects like scissors or knives are also banned, even if they seem small or harmless.

Electronics with lithium batteries should be carried in your hand luggage, but double-check battery size limits to prevent confiscation. Asking yourself, “Is this item necessary for the flight?” can help you skip banned or bulky items that cause delays.




## Special Considerations

Allegiant Airlines allows carry-on bags but some special rules apply. These rules help keep travel smooth and safe for everyone. Understanding these special considerations can save time and avoid extra fees at the airport.

### Traveling With Kids

Parents can bring a diaper bag or stroller in addition to their carry-on. These items do not count toward the carry-on limit. Small children may also carry a personal item like a backpack or small bag. It helps to pack snacks and toys to keep kids calm during the flight.

### Medical And Mobility Devices

Devices such as wheelchairs, crutches, and oxygen tanks are allowed free of charge. They do not count as carry-on items. Passengers should inform Allegiant ahead of time about any medical equipment. This ensures proper assistance and storage during the flight.

### Sporting Equipment And Oversized Items

Large items like golf clubs, skis, or surfboards usually cannot fit in the overhead bin. Allegiant requires these to be checked in, often with an extra fee. Some smaller items may qualify as carry-on if they fit within size limits. Confirm with Allegiant before packing to avoid surprises.
