## Fenway Park Bag Policy

Heading to Fenway Park for a game or event? Knowing the bag policy before you arrive can save you time and hassle. Fenway Park has clear rules about what types of bags you can bring inside, helping keep the experience safe and smooth for everyone.

### Allowed Bag Types

Fenway Park permits several types of bags, but they must meet specific criteria. You can bring:

* Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC bags
* One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags
* Small clutch bags, about the size of a hand (no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”)

Any bag that doesn’t fit these categories might be denied entry, so it’s best to plan ahead and choose wisely.

### Size Restrictions

Size matters at Fenway Park. The maximum dimensions for clear bags are 12 inches by 6 inches by 12 inches. Clutch bags must be smaller than 4.5 inches by 6.5 inches.

If your bag is larger than these limits, you’ll likely have to leave it behind or use the park’s bag check, if available. Have you ever faced long waits because you had to return to your car for your bag? Avoid that frustration by checking your bag size before you get there.

### Clear Bag Policy Details

The clear bag policy at Fenway Park is designed to speed up entry and boost security. All clear bags must be transparent enough to see the contents clearly. This makes security checks faster and lets you get inside quicker.
