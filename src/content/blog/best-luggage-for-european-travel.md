Backpacks with large space and water resistance work well for short trips or business travel. This guide covers top luggage options that suit different travelers and styles across Europe. ## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Luggage Is Best For European Travel?



For European travel, carry-ons between 20-26 inches are ideal. They fit airline cabins and handle cobblestone streets well. Larger checked luggage like 30 inches is great for longer trips but less convenient in cities.



### Which Luggage Is Best For Durability And Scratch Resistance?



Amazon Basics 30" Hardside luggage offers a scratch-resistant surface and durable hardshell design. Samsonite Omni PC and Coolife sets also provide strong, hard shells that resist wear and tear on European trips.



### Are Expandable Suitcases Useful For Europe Trips?



Yes, expandable suitcases like SwissGear Sion and Samsonite Freeform offer extra space, perfect for souvenirs or packing flexibility. They adapt well to changing luggage needs during European travel.



### What Luggage Features Improve Mobility In European Cities?



Look for four multi-directional spinner wheels and lightweight designs like those on Samsonite Freeform and Hanke 26 Inch suitcases. They ensure smooth rolling on uneven surfaces like cobblestones.



### Is A Carry-on Backpack Suitable For European Travel?



Carry-on backpacks, such as the 40L Flight Approved Bag, are ideal for short trips or city exploration. They are lightweight, water-resistant, and fit airline requirements, offering hands-free convenience.



### Do Tsa Locks Matter For European Luggage Security?



Yes, TSA-approved locks on Samsonite and imiomo luggage provide security and allow airport screenings without damage. They are essential for hassle-free travel through European airports.
