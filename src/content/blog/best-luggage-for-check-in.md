Size matters too—large enough for your needs but within airline limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Luggage For Checked Bags?



The Samsonite Freeform and Travelpro Maxlite 5 are top choices. They offer durability, lightweight design, and TSA locks. Both have spinner wheels for easy maneuvering and expandable compartments for extra packing space. These features ensure convenience and security during checked baggage travel.



### Are Hardshell Or Softside Suitcases Better For Check-in?



Hardshell suitcases like Samsonite Omni 2 protect belongings from impacts. Softside luggage like SwissGear Sion offers flexibility and external pockets. Choose hardshell for durability and water resistance, softside for lighter weight and expandability. Both types come with spinner wheels for smooth airport handling.



### How Important Is Tsa Lock In Checked Luggage?



TSA locks are essential for checked luggage security. They allow TSA agents to inspect bags without damage. Most luggage in this list, like Samsonite Freeform and Travelpro Maxlite Air V2, include TSA-approved locks. This feature provides peace of mind during travel.



### Which Luggage Brand Offers The Most Lightweight Checked Bags?



Travelpro Maxlite 5 and Samsonite Freeform are known for lightweight designs. They reduce strain when lifting or carrying checked bags. Despite being lightweight, they remain durable and spacious. Lightweight luggage improves travel comfort and airport navigation.



### Can Expandable Luggage Fit More Items For Check-in?



Yes, expandable luggage like Amazon Basics 30" and Kenneth Cole REACTION Renegade increases packing space by up to 15%. This flexibility is perfect for souvenirs or extra clothes. Expandable suitcases maintain durability while offering extra capacity for checked baggage.



### What Spinner Wheel Type Is Best For Checked Luggage?



Dual 360° spinner wheels, found on Samsonite Freeform and Travelpro Maxlite Air V2, offer smooth, effortless movement. They enhance stability and allow easy navigation through busy airports. Spinner wheels reduce wrist strain and improve travel convenience.
