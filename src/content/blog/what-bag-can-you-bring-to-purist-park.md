The park allows only specific types of bags. Small backpacks, clear bags, and purses are accepted. Clear bags must be see-through for easy inspection. Avoid bulky or oversized bags to prevent delays. Keep your bag simple and easy to check.

### Size Restrictions

Bags must fit within certain size limits. The maximum allowed size is usually 12 x 6 x 12 inches. Bags larger than this size will be denied entry. Carry compact bags to avoid hassle at the gate. Measure your bag before you come to the park.

### Prohibited Items

Some items cannot be brought inside Purist Park. No weapons, alcohol, or illegal substances are allowed. Large coolers and outside food are also banned. Sharp objects and drones are forbidden too. Check the list of banned items before packing your bag.
