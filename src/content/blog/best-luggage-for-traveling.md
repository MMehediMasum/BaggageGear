## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Makes Hardside Luggage Sets Durable?



Hardside luggage sets are crafted from sturdy materials like polycarbonate and ABS. These materials offer excellent impact resistance. They protect your belongings from rough handling during travel. Additionally, TSA locks enhance security. Spinner wheels provide easy mobility. These features collectively ensure durability and convenience for frequent travelers.



### How To Choose The Right Luggage Size?



Consider your travel duration and destination when choosing luggage size. For short trips, a 20-inch carry-on is ideal. Longer vacations may require 24-inch or 28-inch suitcases. Check airline size restrictions to avoid extra fees. Packing efficiently can also help maximize space in your chosen luggage size.



### Why Use A Digital Luggage Scale?



A digital luggage scale ensures you stay within airline weight limits. It prevents unexpected baggage fees. Lightweight and portable, it easily fits in your carry-on. Accurate readings help you pack efficiently. Knowing your luggage weight in advance saves time at check-in, making your travel stress-free.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Travel?



Yes, spinner wheels offer superior maneuverability. They rotate 360 degrees, making navigation through airports easy. Unlike two-wheel luggage, spinner wheels require less effort to push. They reduce strain on your arms and shoulders. This feature is especially beneficial for travelers with multiple bags or heavy luggage.



### How Do Vacuum Bags Save Space?



Vacuum bags compress clothes, saving up to 80% space. They protect clothing from moisture and odors. Perfect for packing bulky items like jackets. Use the portable electric pump for easy compression. Vacuum bags are reusable and durable, making them a practical solution for maximizing suitcase space.
