## American Airlines Carry-on Dimensions

Understanding the American Airlines carry-on dimensions helps avoid travel hassles. Each airline sets specific size and weight rules for carry-on bags. American Airlines has clear limits to make boarding smooth for all passengers.

Knowing these dimensions saves time at the gate and prevents extra fees. Pack smart by following the airline’s guidelines for carry-ons and personal items.

### Standard Size Limits

American Airlines allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size is:

* 22 inches (56 cm) in height
* 14 inches (36 cm) in width
* 9 inches (23 cm) in depth

The bag must fit in the overhead bin. Bags larger than these dimensions may need to be checked.

### Weight Restrictions

American Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-ons. The main rule is that passengers must be able to lift the bag into the overhead bin unassisted.

Keep your bag light to avoid delays or extra charges. Overweight bags might be checked or charged at the gate.

### Personal Item Allowances

In addition to the carry-on, each passenger can bring one personal item. This includes:

* Purse
* Briefcase
* Laptop bag
* Small backpack

The personal item must fit under the seat in front of you. Typical size limits are about 18 x 14 x 8 inches (45 x 35 x 20 cm).

Carry-on size limits usually stay the same. But the number of allowed bags and extra benefits vary by ticket class.

### Economy Class

Economy passengers can bring one carry-on bag plus one personal item. The carry-on bag must not exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Personal items include purses, laptops, or small backpacks. Both items must fit in overhead bins or under the seat.

### Business And First Class

Business and First Class travelers have more carry-on flexibility. They can bring two carry-on bags plus one personal item. The size limit for each carry-on remains 22 x 14 x 9 inches. This allows extra space for important belongings or work gear.

### Basic Economy Restrictions

Basic Economy passengers face stricter rules. Most Basic Economy tickets allow only one personal item. Carry-on bags that go in the overhead bin usually require an additional fee. The personal item must fit under the seat in front. This rule helps keep boarding fast and organized.
