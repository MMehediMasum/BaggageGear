## Delta Baggage Policy Basics

Understanding Delta’s baggage policy is key to avoiding surprise fees and stress at the airport. Knowing what you can bring without extra charges helps you pack smarter and travel lighter.

Let’s break down the basics of what Delta allows, including your standard baggage allowance, carry-on rules, and limits on size and weight.

### Standard Allowance

Delta typically lets you check one bag for free on many domestic flights, but this depends on your ticket type and destination. Basic economy tickets often don’t include a free checked bag, so you’ll want to check your fare details carefully.

Each standard checked bag can weigh up to 50 pounds (23 kg) and measure no more than 62 inches (157 cm) in total dimensions—length plus width plus height. If you travel frequently, keep your baggage allowance in mind to avoid unexpected fees.

### Carry-on Rules

You’re allowed one carry-on bag and one personal item like a purse or laptop bag on Delta flights. The carry-on must fit in the overhead bin and meet size limits of 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm).

Try packing your essentials in your carry-on to skip the wait at baggage claim. Ever had to wait forever for your bag? Traveling with just a carry-on can save you time and hassle.

### Oversize And Overweight Limits

If your bag exceeds 50 pounds or the 62-inch size limit, Delta charges extra fees. Bags weighing between 51 and 70 pounds usually incur a fee, and anything over 70 pounds costs even more.

Oversized bags—those larger than 62 inches but less than 115 inches in total—also carry additional fees. Think about whether it’s worth paying for an extra bag or packing lighter to avoid these charges.




## Fees For Extra Bags

Delta Airlines charges fees for extra bags beyond the free allowance. These fees vary based on flight type and destination. Knowing the costs helps avoid surprises at the airport. Here is a breakdown of fees for extra baggage on Delta flights.

### Domestic Flight Charges

For most domestic flights within the U.S., Delta charges $40 for the first extra bag. The second extra bag costs $50. Fees apply per bag and per direction. Each bag must weigh less than 50 pounds and measure under 62 inches.

### International Flight Charges

Extra bag fees vary by destination on international flights. For flights to Europe, the fee usually starts at $100 for the first extra bag. For flights to Asia or Latin America, fees can be higher, often $150 or more.

Weight limits and size rules remain the same as domestic flights. Some routes may allow heavier bags for a higher fee. Always check Delta’s website for current fees before travel.

### Special Items And Sports Equipment

Delta treats special items like sports gear differently. Fees depend on the item’s size and weight. Some equipment, such as skis or golf clubs, may have a flat fee around $150.

Oversized or overweight bags cost extra. Items like bicycles often require advance notice. Packing these items properly avoids damage and extra charges.

  


## Ways To Save On Baggage Fees

Paying for extra bags on Delta can add up quickly, but there are smart ways to reduce or even avoid those fees. Knowing where to look for savings can help you keep more money in your pocket without sacrificing convenience. Let’s dive into some effective strategies you can use to save on baggage fees.

### Delta Medallion Benefits

If you fly often with Delta, earning Medallion status can be a game-changer for baggage costs. Medallion members usually get at least one free checked bag, sometimes more depending on the tier. This perk alone can cover the fee for an extra bag on many trips.

Think about how much you spend on checked bags yearly. Could it be worth aiming for status if you fly regularly? Many frequent travelers find the savings on baggage fees quickly add up, making Medallion status a practical goal.

### Credit Card Perks

Delta co-branded credit cards offer valuable baggage fee waivers as part of their benefits. Cardholders often get their first checked bag free for themselves and sometimes for companions traveling on the same reservation. This perk can save you $30 to $60 per bag per flight.

Besides the free bag, these cards usually come with other travel perks like priority boarding or discounts on in-flight purchases. If you fly Delta a few times a year, carrying a Delta credit card might pay for itself just with baggage fee savings.

### Booking Tips

How you book your ticket can affect baggage fees. Booking directly on Delta’s website sometimes offers better baggage fee policies than third-party sites. Also, bundling your ticket with extras like seat upgrades or bundles that include checked bags can be cheaper than paying for bags separately.

Consider traveling with carry-on bags only when possible. Many travelers underestimate how much they can fit into a carry-on, avoiding checked bag fees altogether. Have you tried packing lighter to skip the extra baggage cost?

This method lets you avoid long lines at the airport and often comes with slightly lower fees compared to paying at the airport. Have you checked your baggage allowance while booking? It’s worth confirming to avoid last-minute charges.

## Common Baggage Fee Questions

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=many-travelers-have-questions-about-delta's-baggage-fees.-understanding-these-common-concerns-helps-avoid-surprises-at-the-airport.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Many travelers have questions about Delta's baggage fees. Understanding these common concerns helps avoid surprises at the airport. Here are answers to some frequent questions about extra bags on Delta.

### What Counts As An Extra Bag?

An extra bag is any piece of luggage beyond your allowed limit. Delta usually lets you bring one or two checked bags depending on your ticket. If you carry more, Delta charges an extra fee. Oversized or overweight bags may also cost more.

### Can Fees Change After Booking?

Yes, baggage fees can change after booking. If you add bags later, expect to pay current rates. Delta updates fees based on season, route, or policy changes. Paying for bags before your flight often costs less than at the airport.

### How To Handle Lost Or Damaged Bags

Report lost or damaged bags immediately at the airport. Delta staff will help you file a claim. Keep your baggage claim ticket and receipts. Compensation depends on the bag’s value and situation. Follow up with customer service if needed.

Delta charges $30 for the first extra bag and $40 for the second on domestic flights. Fees may vary on international routes. Always check Delta’s official baggage policy before traveling for the most accurate fees and updates.

### Are Overweight Bags Charged Extra On Delta?

Yes, Delta charges additional fees for bags weighing between 51-70 pounds. The fee is typically $100 per overweight bag. Bags over 70 pounds may incur higher fees or be prohibited. Check Delta’s specific weight limits and fees before packing.

### Can I Prepay For Extra Bags On Delta?

Yes, you can prepay for extra bags during online check-in or on Delta’s website. Prepaying often saves time at the airport. It also guarantees your baggage allowance at the best available rate. Always prepay to avoid last-minute fees.

### Does Delta Allow Free Baggage For Elite Members?

Elite members of Delta’s SkyMiles program may receive free checked bags. The number and weight limits depend on the elite status level. Benefits vary for Medallion members and Delta SkyMiles credit cardholders. Check your membership benefits before flying.
