## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Luggage Is Best For Europe Trips?



A carry-on sized suitcase (20-22 inches) is ideal for Europe. It fits overhead bins and avoids checked bag fees. Expandable options add flexibility for souvenirs and longer stays.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Suitcases Better For Europe?



Hardside luggage offers better protection and durability. Softside suitcases are lighter and expand more. Choose based on your packing style and travel needs in Europe.



### Which Luggage Brands Are Best For European Travel?



Samsonite, Amazon Basics, and American Tourister are reliable. They offer durable, lightweight, and TSA-approved luggage perfect for European trips. SwissGear and Coolife also provide excellent carry-on options.



### Do Expandable Suitcases Add Useful Packing Space?



Yes, expandable suitcases increase capacity by up to 15%. This is handy for longer trips or when bringing back extra items from Europe.



### Is Tsa-approved Luggage Necessary For Europe Travel?



TSA-approved locks help secure your bags during flights. While TSA agents are US-based, many European airports respect these locks for easier security checks.



### What Features Matter Most In European Rolling Luggage?



Look for lightweight, smooth-rolling spinner wheels, durable materials, and TSA locks. Extra pockets and USB ports add convenience for European travelers.
