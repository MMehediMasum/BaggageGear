## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Fees

Spirit Airlines is known for its ultra-low fares, but those cheap ticket prices often come with extra fees—especially for carry-on bags. Understanding how much Spirit charges for a carry-on can save you money and frustration at the airport. Knowing the details of their carry-on fees helps you plan better and avoid surprises during your trip.

### Standard Carry-on Costs

Spirit Airlines charges for carry-on bags depending on when and how you pay. Typically, the fee ranges from **$35 to $65**per carry-on bag if you pay during booking.

If you wait until check-in or the gate, the cost can jump to **$65 or more**. Paying early can save you a significant amount, so keep an eye on this when booking your ticket.

### Fare Types And Bag Allowances

Spirit offers different fare types that impact your carry-on allowance. The **“Bare Fare”**usually does not include any free carry-on bags.

The **“Bundle It”**option, which costs more upfront, lets you add a carry-on and sometimes a personal item without extra charges. If you travel light, the personal item allowance lets you bring a small bag onboard for free.

* Personal item (like a backpack or purse): free
* Standard carry-on (larger bag): fee applies unless bundled

### Booking Time Impact On Prices

When you add your carry-on during booking, fees are the lowest. Waiting until online check-in or at the airport means higher charges.

Imagine booking your flight and adding a carry-on for $35. If you forget and pay at the gate, you might pay nearly double. Booking early is an easy way to control costs and avoid last-minute stress.

Have you ever been surprised by unexpected baggage fees? Planning ahead with Spirit can make your travel smoother and cheaper.

Spirit has strict size and weight limits for carry-on bags. If your bag exceeds the allowed dimensions or weight, expect extra fees. For example, a bag that is too large may be checked at the gate and incur a $60 to $100 fee, depending on the route.

Weight limits are usually around 40 pounds for carry-ons. If your bag is overweight, Spirit charges an additional fee, which can be as high as $100. You can save money by weighing your bag at home and repacking if necessary.

### Bag Handling At The Airport

Spirit encourages passengers to pay for carry-ons online to avoid higher prices at the airport. If you wait until you arrive at the gate, the fee for carry-on bags increases significantly.

At the airport, you might pay up to double the online price for adding a carry-on. This policy pushes travelers to plan ahead. Have you ever been surprised by a last-minute baggage fee at the airport? It’s a common frustration.

### Fees For Changing Or Adding Bags

Changing your bag or adding an extra carry-on after booking can lead to more charges. Spirit charges fees for modifying your baggage options, which vary based on timing and route.

Adding a carry-on after booking but before the flight usually costs less than adding it at the gate. Waiting too long can turn a small fee into a hefty one. To avoid this, review and adjust your baggage needs as soon as you book your ticket.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does Spirit Charge For A Carry On Bag?

Spirit Airlines charges between $35 and $65 for a carry on bag. Prices vary by route and purchase time. Booking online early usually gets the lowest fee.

### Are There Size Limits For Spirit’s Carry On Bags?

Yes, carry on bags must not exceed 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Oversized bags incur extra fees or must be checked.

### Can I Bring A Personal Item For Free On Spirit?

Yes, Spirit allows one free personal item. It must fit under the seat in front. Typical sizes are 18 x 14 x 8 inches.

### Does Spirit Charge Extra For Carry On At The Airport?

Yes, carry on fees are higher if paid at the airport. Paying online in advance saves money. Airport fees can be double the online price.
