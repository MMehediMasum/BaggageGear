## Water Resistance Vs Waterproof

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-the-difference-between-water-resistance-and-waterproof-is-key-to-choosing-the-right-backpack.-these-terms-often-confuse-buyers-but-mean-very-different-things.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding the difference between water resistance and waterproof is key to choosing the right backpack. These terms often confuse buyers but mean very different things. Knowing what each term means helps set the right expectations about protection from water.

### Definitions And Differences

Water resistance means a bag can handle light rain or splashes. It slows water from entering but does not block heavy rain or submersion. Water-resistant fabrics often have a coating that repels water temporarily.

Waterproof means no water can get inside the bag under normal conditions. This includes heavy rain or brief underwater exposure. Waterproof backpacks use special materials and sealed seams to stop water completely.

### Borealis Backpack's Classification

The Borealis backpack is water resistant, not fully waterproof. It features durable fabric with a DWR (Durable Water Repellent) finish. This protects against light rain and snow.

For heavy rain, the Borealis backpack may let some water in. It is best to use an additional rain cover in very wet conditions. The [bag’s zippers](https://baggagegear.com/how-to-sew-a-makeup-bag-with-a-zipper/) and seams are designed for daily use but are not sealed like waterproof bags.

## Maintaining Water Resistance

Keeping your Borealis Backpack water-resistant is key to protecting your gear during unexpected downpours or wet conditions. Over time, water resistance can wear off, especially with frequent use and exposure to dirt. Staying on top of maintenance ensures your backpack continues to shield your belongings effectively.

### Cleaning Tips

Dirt and grime can clog the fabric's protective layers, reducing water resistance. Clean your backpack gently using a soft brush or cloth to remove surface dirt. Avoid harsh detergents; instead, use mild soap mixed with water to spot-clean stains.

Rinse with clean water and air dry your backpack away from direct sunlight or heat sources. This process helps maintain the fabric’s integrity without damaging its water-repellent finish. Have you noticed how a dirty backpack lets water soak in faster? That’s why regular cleaning matters.

### Reapplying Waterproof Coating

Over time, the DWR (Durable Water Repellent) coating on your Borealis Backpack may lose effectiveness. You can refresh this layer with a waterproof spray designed for technical fabrics. Make sure your backpack is clean and dry before applying the spray evenly across the surface.

Allow the backpack to dry completely, ideally in a warm, ventilated area. Reapplication frequency depends on use—heavy outdoor use might require it every few months, while lighter use may extend that period. How often do you check your gear’s water resistance? It’s worth paying attention to.
