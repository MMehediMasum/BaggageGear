## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=the-aren-flap-hobo-bag-in-embossed-monogram-leather-deserves-gentle-care-to-keep-its-charm.-proper-maintenance-helps-preserve-its-look-and-feel.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

The Aren Flap Hobo Bag in Embossed Monogram Leather deserves gentle care to keep its charm. Proper maintenance helps preserve its look and feel. A little attention extends the bag's life and keeps it looking fresh.

### Cleaning Tips

Use a soft, dry cloth to wipe dust and dirt. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. Lightly dampen a cloth with water for tougher spots. Test any cleaner on a small, hidden area first. Let the bag air dry naturally, away from heat or sunlight.

### Longevity And Durability

Keep the bag away from direct sunlight for long hours. Avoid contact with water, oils, and makeup to prevent stains. Regularly condition the leather with a suitable product to maintain softness. Handle the bag gently to avoid scratches and dents. Small care habits add years to your bag’s life.

### Storage Recommendations

Store the bag in a dust bag or cotton pillowcase. Keep it in a cool, dry place with good air circulation. Avoid plastic bags that trap moisture and cause damage. Stuff the bag with tissue paper to keep its shape. Store upright to prevent creases and deformities.
