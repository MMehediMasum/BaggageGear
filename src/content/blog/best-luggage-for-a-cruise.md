## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Luggage Size Is Best For A Cruise Trip?



A 28 to 29-inch checked luggage works best for cruises. It fits most cruise cabin spaces and holds enough clothes and essentials without being bulky.



### Are Hardshell Or Softside Suitcases Better For Cruises?



Hardshell suitcases, like Coolife and LONG VACATION sets, offer better protection and are water-resistant. Softside bags like SwissGear are lighter and expandable but less durable.



### Do Tsa Locks Matter For Cruise Luggage?



Yes, TSA locks are essential for cruise luggage security. They allow TSA agents to inspect your bags without damage, keeping belongings safe throughout your trip.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Cruise Luggage?



Spinner wheels provide 360-degree movement, making luggage easy to maneuver on ship decks and terminals. Most sets like Coolife and Travelers Club include spinner wheels for convenience.



### Should I Use Packing Cubes For Cruise Luggage Organization?



Yes, packing cubes help organize clothes and accessories efficiently. Sets like the 10/8 Piece Packing Cubes reduce clutter and maximize luggage space on cruises.
