## Alaska Airlines Carry-on Policy

Alaska Airlines allows passengers to bring carry-on bags and personal items onboard. Knowing the airline’s carry-on policy helps avoid surprises at the gate. The rules focus on size and weight limits to keep the cabin safe and comfortable. Passengers must follow these guidelines to ensure smooth boarding and travel.

### Size And Weight Limits

Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. Alaska Airlines allows a maximum size of 22 x 14 x 9 inches. This includes handles and wheels. The weight limit for carry-ons is 25 pounds. Bags heavier or larger than this may need to be checked. Measuring your bag before packing saves time and stress at the airport.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Each passenger can bring one personal item in addition to a carry-on bag. Personal items must fit under the seat in front of you. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. The typical size allowed is 17 x 13 x 8 inches. Personal items should hold essentials like documents, electronics, or medication. Keep these items easy to reach during the flight.

### Essential Electronics

Your electronic devices are important companions during travel. Alaska Airlines lets you bring laptops, tablets, smartphones, e-readers, and cameras in your carry-on. You can also pack chargers, headphones, and power banks, but make sure batteries meet airline safety rules.

Try to pack your electronics in an easily accessible part of your bag. This helps when security agents ask you to take them out for screening. Have you ever fumbled with your devices at the checkpoint? Organizing them beforehand saves time and stress.

### Liquids And Gels Rules

Liquids, gels, and aerosols have strict limits for carry-ons. You can carry containers of 3.4 ounces (100 milliliters) or less, all fitting comfortably inside a single quart-sized, clear plastic bag. This includes items like shampoo, lotion, toothpaste, and hand sanitizer.

Remember, the bag must be easy to remove during security checks. If you forget this rule, your favorite lotion might get confiscated. Would you rather pack travel-sized bottles or buy toiletries after you land?

### Medications And Special Items

Medications are allowed in carry-ons without size restrictions, but it’s best to keep them in original packaging. You can also bring medical devices, baby formula, breast milk, and special dietary items. Just declare these items at the security checkpoint to avoid delays.

Carrying your prescriptions with you is smart, especially for long flights or layovers. Have you ever faced a situation where your medication was delayed in checked baggage? Keeping these items close can make all the difference.

  


## Carry-on Restrictions

Understanding carry-on restrictions is key before you board an Alaska Airlines flight. These rules help ensure safety and smooth travel for everyone. Knowing what you can and cannot bring in your carry-on saves time and spares you from last-minute surprises at the gate.

### Prohibited Items

Alaska Airlines follows strict guidelines about what items can be carried on board. Sharp objects like knives and scissors with blades longer than 4 inches are not allowed. Liquids over 3.4 ounces must be checked or left behind.

Electronic cigarettes and vaping devices are banned from checked baggage but can be carried on, just not used during the flight. Flammable items such as lighters, fireworks, and aerosols are off-limits in both carry-on and checked bags.

Have you ever tried bringing a large bottle of shampoo or your favorite hot sauce, only to have it taken away at security? It’s frustrating but knowing these rules beforehand lets you pack smarter.

### Security Screening Tips

Preparing your carry-on for security screening can speed up the process. Place laptops and tablets in an easily accessible spot for separate screening. Keep liquids and gels in a clear, quart-sized bag to show TSA agents quickly.

Wearing slip-on shoes makes removing footwear easier during the scan. Avoid packing prohibited items to prevent delays or confiscation. Think about what you really need during your flight and pack accordingly.

Have you ever noticed how a well-organized bag can cut your wait time significantly? Small changes in packing can lead to big improvements in your travel experience.




## Additional Baggage Options

Traveling with Alaska Airlines means you might need more than just a carry-on bag. Understanding your additional baggage options can save you money and stress at the airport. Let’s look at what choices you have beyond your carry-on allowance.

### Checked Baggage Policies

Alaska Airlines allows checked bags, but fees and allowances depend on your ticket type and destination. Typically, the first checked bag costs $30, and the second is $40. Some fare types or loyalty statuses include free checked bags—so check your ticket details before packing.

Remember, checked baggage has size limits: the total of length, width, and height can’t exceed 62 inches. If your bag is larger, you’ll face extra fees. Planning ahead and weighing your luggage can help avoid surprises at the counter.

### Overweight And Oversized Fees

If your checked bag is over 50 pounds but under 100 pounds, Alaska Airlines charges an overweight fee of $75. Bags exceeding 100 pounds are not accepted. Oversized bags between 63 and 115 inches in total dimensions will cost $100 in extra fees.

Have you ever been caught off guard by a heavy bag fee? It happens to many travelers. To dodge these charges, consider distributing weight across multiple bags or using travel scales before leaving home.

* Strollers or car seats
* Child safety seats

Strollers can be gate-checked at no cost. This helps parents move easily through the airport. Children under two years old may be held on an adult’s lap. Carry-on limits remain the same, but extra baby items are permitted.

### Assistance And Service Animals

Passengers traveling with service or assistance animals do not have to count these animals as carry-on items. Alaska Airlines allows service animals to accompany their owners in the cabin. Proper documentation may be required before travel.

Emotional support animals follow specific rules and policies. Alaska Airlines recommends contacting customer service ahead of time to confirm requirements. This ensures a smooth boarding experience for all travelers.

### Does Alaska Airlines Allow Carry-on Bags?

Yes, Alaska Airlines allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. Carry-on bags must fit in the overhead bin.

Carry-on bags should not exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including handles and wheels. Personal items must fit under the seat.

### Are There Any Fees For Carry-on Bags On Alaska Airlines?

No, carry-on bags and personal items are free on Alaska Airlines. Checked bags may incur fees depending on your fare.
