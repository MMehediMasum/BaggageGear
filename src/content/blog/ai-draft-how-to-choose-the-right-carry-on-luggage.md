# How to Choose the Right Carry-On Luggage

Choosing the right carry-on luggage can make your air travel experience significantly smoother. Avoid gate-checking hassles and keep your essentials close. This guide will walk you through the key factors to consider when selecting the perfect carry-on for your needs.

## Size and Weight Restrictions

Airline carry-on size restrictions vary, so it's crucial to check with your specific airline before you buy. Most U.S. airlines adhere to a general guideline of 22 x 14 x 9 inches (including wheels and handles). However, budget airlines often have stricter rules.

Weight limits are also important. Some airlines have very specific weight restrictions, while others don't enforce them as strictly. Be mindful of how much your packed bag weighs to avoid surprises at the gate.

## Types of Carry-On Luggage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=there-are-several-types-of-carry-on-luggage-to-choose-from:&w=424&h=324&c=7)

There are several types of carry-on luggage to choose from:

* **Rolling Suitcases:** These are the most common type, offering ease of maneuverability. Look for spinner wheels for effortless gliding through airports.
* **Backpacks:** A comfortable option, especially if you anticipate doing a lot of walking. Choose one with padded straps and a supportive frame.
* **Duffel Bags:** Versatile and often lighter than rolling suitcases, but they can be less comfortable to carry for extended periods.
* **Underseat Bags:** Designed to fit under the seat in front of you, these are great for keeping essentials within easy reach.

## Material and Durability

The material of your carry-on luggage affects its weight, durability, and water resistance.

* **Hard-shell luggage:** Typically made from polycarbonate or ABS plastic, these offer excellent protection for your belongings.
* **Soft-sided luggage:** Usually made from nylon or polyester, these are more flexible and often lighter than hard-shell options. Look for reinforced stitching and durable zippers.

## Features to Consider

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=think-about-the-features-that-are-important-to-you:&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Think about the features that are important to you:

* **Pockets and Compartments:** Well-organized compartments can help you keep your belongings tidy and easily accessible.
* **TSA-Approved Locks:** These locks allow TSA agents to open your luggage for inspection without damaging the lock.
* **Expandable Design:** An expandable carry-on provides extra packing space when you need it.
* **USB Charging Port:** Some carry-ons feature a built-in USB charging port, allowing you to charge your devices on the go (requires a power bank).

## Price vs. Quality

While it's tempting to opt for the cheapest option, investing in a higher-quality carry-on can save you money in the long run. Durable luggage will withstand the rigors of travel and last for years to come. Read reviews and compare features to find the best value for your budget.
