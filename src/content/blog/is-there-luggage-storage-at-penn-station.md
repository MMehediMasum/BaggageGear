## Tips For Safe Luggage Storage

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=storing-your-luggage-safely-at-penn-station-can-make-your-travel-experience-much-smoother.-you-want-to-be-sure-your-belongings-are-secure-while-you-explore-or-wait-for-your-next-train.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Storing your luggage safely at Penn Station can make your travel experience much smoother. You want to be sure your belongings are secure while you explore or wait for your next train. Here are some practical tips to help you keep your luggage safe and avoid common pitfalls.

### Checking Operating Hours

Before you leave your bags, check the storage facility’s operating hours carefully. Penn Station’s luggage storage options may not be open 24/7, and missing the closing time could leave you locked out from your belongings. If you’re catching a late train or arriving early, verify the opening times in advance to plan accordingly.

Have you ever been stuck waiting around because the storage counter closed early? Avoid that hassle by confirming hours online or calling ahead.

### Packing And Labeling Advice

Pack your items neatly and avoid overstuffing your bags. Overpacked luggage is harder to close properly and draws unwanted attention. Use sturdy locks if allowed, but remember that these should be TSA-approved for inspections.

Label your bags clearly with your name and contact details. This simple step helps in case your luggage gets misplaced. You can even add a bright ribbon or tag to spot your bag quickly in crowded storage areas.

### Avoiding Common Storage Mistakes

Don’t leave valuables like electronics, jewelry, or important documents in your stored luggage. These items can be targets for theft or could get damaged. Instead, carry them with you or use a smaller bag that you keep close.

Be cautious about storing fragile items without proper padding. A soft jacket or bubble wrap inside the bag can protect delicate belongings from bumps and drops. Also, avoid storing perishable food or liquids that might leak and spoil.
