## American Airlines Carry-on Policy

American Airlines has clear rules for carry-on bags to make travel smooth. Knowing these rules helps avoid extra fees and delays at the airport. The airline allows passengers to bring a carry-on bag and a personal item on board. Each item must follow specific size and weight limits.

### Standard Allowance

Passengers can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item for free. The personal item includes items like a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. Both must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat in front.

* Carry-on bag
* Personal item

These items do not have a separate charge unless they exceed the allowed size or weight.

### Dimensions And Weight Limits

The carry-on bag must not be larger than 22 x 14 x 9 inches, including handles and wheels. The personal item should fit under the seat, usually smaller than the carry-on. There is no specific weight limit for carry-on bags, but passengers must be able to lift them into the overhead bin without help.

| Item | Max Dimensions | Weight Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carry-on Bag | 22 x 14 x 9 inches | No official weight limit |
| Personal Item | Smaller than carry-on, fits under seat | No official weight limit |

Extra or oversized bags may incur charges or need to be checked. Following these rules helps keep your travel hassle-free.

## Fees For Carry-on Bags

American Airlines usually allows one free carry-on bag per passenger. This bag must meet size and weight limits. Passengers can bring a personal item like a purse or laptop bag as well. The policy helps travelers avoid extra fees and pack smartly.

Still, some cases require passengers to pay for carry-on bags. These fees depend on the ticket type and route. Knowing the rules can save money and reduce surprises at the airport.

### When Fees Apply

Fees for carry-on bags apply mainly to Basic Economy tickets. Passengers with this ticket often pay a fee to bring a carry-on on board. The fee varies by route and flight length.

Other ticket types like Main Cabin, Premium Economy, Business, or First Class usually include free carry-on bags. Basic Economy is the main exception to this rule.

Some international flights may have different rules and fees. Checking the airline’s website before travel helps avoid unexpected charges.

### Exceptions And Waivers

Some passengers do not pay carry-on fees. These include:

* Active military members with valid ID
* Passengers with elite status in American Airlines’ loyalty program
* Travelers with certain credit cards linked to the airline
* Children under two years old traveling with an adult

Special items like medical equipment or assistive devices also get fee waivers. Always check the official policy to confirm eligibility and rules.

## Personal Item Vs. Carry-on

Understanding the difference between a personal item and a carry-on is important when flying with American Airlines. These two types of baggage have different size limits and rules. Knowing what each one means can help avoid extra fees and make traveling easier.

### What Counts As A Personal Item

A personal item is usually smaller than a carry-on bag. It must fit under the seat in front of you. Common personal items include:

* Backpacks
* Handbags
* Laptop bags
* Small duffel bags

This item should hold your essentials like a wallet, phone, or a book. It does not need to be checked or gate-checked.

### Allowance Differences

American Airlines allows each passenger one personal item and one carry-on bag for free. The size limits are different:

| Bag Type | Maximum Dimensions |
| --- | --- |
| Personal Item | 18 x 14 x 8 inches (45 x 35 x 20 cm) |
| Carry-On Bag | 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm) |

Carry-on bags are larger and go in the overhead bin. Personal items must fit under the seat. Exceeding these sizes may cause extra fees or require checking the bag.
