## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Suitcase Is Best For European Travel?



A 21 to 28-inch suitcase is ideal for European trips. Carry-ons like the Samsonite Freeform fit cabin requirements. Larger checked options like the SwissGear Sion offer more space for longer stays. Choose based on trip length and airline rules.



### Are Hardside Or Softside Suitcases Better For Europe?



Hardside suitcases like Samsonite Freeform offer better protection and durability. Softside options such as SwissGear Sion are lighter and expandable. Both types have spinner wheels for easy maneuvering on European streets. Select based on your packing needs and travel style.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels For European Trips?



Spinner wheels provide 360-degree movement, making navigation through airports and cobblestone streets easier. Models like Samsonite Omni 2 and Coolife sets have smooth, multi-directional wheels. They reduce strain and improve travel convenience, especially in busy European cities.



### Should I Choose Expandable Luggage For Europe?



Expandable luggage offers extra packing space for souvenirs and essentials. Samsonite Freeform and Amazon Basics carry-ons expand up to 25%. This flexibility is useful for unpredictable trip needs. However, check airline size limits before expanding.



### What Accessories Enhance Suitcase Organization For Europe Travel?



Packing cubes like Veken's 8 or 10 set help organize clothes and essentials efficiently. They maximize space and keep items separated. Also, European plug adapters with USB ports are essential for charging devices abroad.



### Are Tsa Locks Necessary For European Luggage?



TSA locks provide security and allow airport security to inspect bags without damage. Most suitcases listed, including Samsonite and WireHot sets, have built-in TSA locks. They ensure peace of mind and comply with security protocols.
