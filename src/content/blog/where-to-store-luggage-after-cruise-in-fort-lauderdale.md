## Safety And Security Tips

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=keeping-your-luggage-safe-after-a-cruise-in-fort-lauderdale-is-important.-choosing-the-right-storage-spot-protects-your-belongings.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Keeping your luggage safe after a cruise in Fort Lauderdale is important. Choosing the right storage spot protects your belongings. Knowing the rules about insurance helps you avoid surprises. Taking simple steps with your personal items adds extra security. Follow these tips to store luggage safely and enjoy peace of mind.

### Choosing Secure Facilities

Pick storage locations with strong security features. Look for places with:

* 24/7 surveillance cameras
* Secure locks and gates
* Staff presence or access control
* Good customer reviews on safety

Choose facilities near your cruise port for easy access. Confirm hours of operation to match your schedule.

### Insurance And Liability

Check if the storage provider offers insurance for your luggage. Understand what is covered and any limits. Keep receipts and item lists to support claims. Ask about liability policies before storing your bags. This helps avoid unexpected costs if damage or loss happens.

### Personal Item Precautions

Keep valuables like passports, money, and electronics with you. Avoid storing fragile or high-value items in luggage storage. Use TSA-approved locks to secure your bags. Label your luggage clearly with contact information. Take photos of your bags before leaving them to record condition.
