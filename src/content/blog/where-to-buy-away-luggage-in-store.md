## Tips For Shopping Away Luggage In-store

Shopping for Away luggage in-store offers a chance to see and feel the product before buying. It helps to make a better choice. Use these tips to make the shopping experience smooth and satisfying. Focus on key details that matter most for travel gear.

### Checking Warranty And Return Policies

Always ask about warranty coverage. Away luggage often comes with a warranty against defects. Know how long the warranty lasts and what it covers. Check the return policy too. Understand the time frame allowed for returns or exchanges. This helps avoid problems if the luggage has issues after purchase.

### Testing Luggage Features In Person

Try out the luggage wheels to see if they roll smoothly. Spin the handle to check its sturdiness and comfort. Open and close the zippers several times. Make sure the zippers work without sticking. Feel the material for durability and weight. Check the interior compartments for useful pockets and space. Testing these features helps ensure the luggage fits your travel needs.
