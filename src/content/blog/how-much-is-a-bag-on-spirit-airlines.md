## Spirit Airlines Baggage Fees

Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares. But baggage fees can add up quickly. Understanding these fees helps travelers plan better and avoid surprises at the airport.

Spirit charges separately for carry-on bags and checked luggage. The fees depend on when and how you pay. Booking early online usually costs less than paying at the airport.

### Carry-on Bag Costs

A standard carry-on bag on Spirit Airlines is not free. The price varies based on timing and route.

* Booking online in advance: $35 to $45
* Paying during online check-in: $45 to $55
* Paying at the airport counter: $65 to $75

Carry-on bags must fit under the seat or in the overhead bin. Size limits are strict. Overweight or oversized bags face extra charges.

### Checked Bag Prices

Checked bags cost more than carry-ons. Spirit Airlines charges per bag and per flight segment.

* First checked bag: $30 to $50
* Second checked bag: $40 to $60
* Third and additional bags: $100 or more each

Fees increase if you pay after booking or at the airport. Prepaying online saves money and time at check-in.

### Personal Item Policy

Spirit allows one free personal item per passenger. This item must fit under the seat.

* Examples: purse, small backpack, laptop bag
* Maximum size: 18 x 14 x 8 inches
* No extra charge for this item

Larger items require a carry-on bag fee. Check dimensions carefully to avoid fees.

## Bag Size And Weight Limits

Knowing the exact dimensions and weight restrictions helps you avoid surprises at the airport and keeps your travel experience smooth.

### Carry-on Dimensions

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item free of charge, such as a small backpack or purse. This item must fit under the seat in front of you with maximum dimensions of **18 x 14 x 8 inches**. If your carry-on bag is larger than these limits, you will need to pay for a carry-on or check it at the gate, which can be costly.

Think about how often you pack a bag that just barely fits these dimensions. Could you save money by downsizing your carry-on? Many travelers overlook this and end up paying extra fees unnecessarily.

### Checked Bag Weight Rules

Checked bags on Spirit Airlines must not exceed **40 pounds (18 kg)**. If your bag is heavier, you will face overweight charges. The maximum weight allowed for checked bags is **50 pounds (23 kg)**; anything beyond that is not accepted.

Weigh your luggage at home before leaving for the airport. It’s easy to add a few pounds with souvenirs or extra clothes. This simple step can prevent those frustrating overweight fees.

### Oversized And Overweight Charges

Spirit charges extra fees for bags that exceed size or weight limits. Oversized bags larger than **62 linear inches (length + width + height)**face additional fees, which can be quite steep. Similarly, bags weighing between 41-50 pounds incur overweight charges.

## How To Save On Baggage Fees

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=saving-on-baggage-fees-with-spirit-airlines-is-easier-than-you-might-think.-knowing-a-few-smart-strategies-can-keep-your-travel-budget-intact-and-reduce-stress-at-the-airport.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Saving on baggage fees with Spirit Airlines is easier than you might think. Knowing a few smart strategies can keep your travel budget intact and reduce stress at the airport. Let’s look at some practical ways you can cut down those extra charges before you even pack your bag.

### Prepaying Bags Online

Paying for your bags before you arrive at the airport can save you a significant amount of money. Spirit Airlines charges much higher fees for baggage checked at the counter or gate, so booking your bags online is a simple way to avoid surprise costs.

When you book your ticket, add your bags during the checkout process. You can also manage your booking later on Spirit’s website to prepay for bags up to a few hours before your flight. This approach often cuts the baggage fee by nearly half compared to paying at the airport.

### Using Spirit’s Bundles

Spirit offers bundles that combine bags, seat selection, and other perks for a single price. These bundles, like the “$9 Fare Club” or “Travel Bundle,” often offer better value if you plan to check bags or want more control over your seat choice.

Buying a bundle can be cheaper than paying for each service separately. If you travel frequently or with family, these bundles can add up to big savings. Have you checked if your trip fits into one of these bundles yet?

### Packing Tips To Avoid Fees

How you pack can directly affect the fees you pay. Carry-on baggage is often free if it meets Spirit’s size requirements, so packing smartly can help you skip checked bag fees altogether.

Use lightweight, flexible bags to maximize your space without exceeding weight limits. Rolling clothes instead of folding saves room and lets you fit more into your carry-on. Also, wear your heaviest shoes and jacket to lighten your luggage.

Try to stick to essentials only and avoid packing items you can buy at your destination. Are you confident your bag meets Spirit’s rules before you leave home? Checking this now can save you money and hassle later.
