**Choosing the best backpack for thru hikes is key to a successful adventure. It must be light, durable, and hold all your gear comfortably.**



Thru hiking means walking long trails over many days. You carry everything you need on your back. A good backpack helps reduce strain and keeps your items organized. You want a pack that fits well and matches your hiking style.



Some backpacks offer hydration systems, others fold small for easy storage. Waterproof materials protect your gear in bad weather. Capacity matters too—too small won’t fit all gear, too big adds weight. This guide covers popular options like the Mountainsmith Zerk, Osprey Sportlite, Maelstrom, and YETI Crossroads. Each has unique features suited for different hikers. Find the right backpack to make your thru hike safer and more comfortable.
