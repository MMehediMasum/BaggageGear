## Samsonite Brand History

Understanding the history of Samsonite gives you a clearer picture of why many travelers trust this brand. It's not just about making luggage; it’s about years of innovation and adapting to the needs of people on the move. Let’s take a closer look at how Samsonite grew into a global name.

### Origins And Evolution

Samsonite started back in 1910, founded by Jesse Shwayder in Denver, Colorado. His goal was simple: create durable luggage that could withstand rough travel. Over the decades, Samsonite introduced new materials like lightweight plastics and unique designs that improved ease of use.

Have you ever struggled with heavy suitcases? Samsonite was among the first to add wheels and telescoping handles, changing the way we travel. This focus on innovation helped the brand evolve with changing travel habits, always aiming to make your journey easier.

### Global Presence

Today, Samsonite is found in over 100 countries, proving its appeal worldwide. The brand’s ability to blend style, durability, and functionality resonates across cultures and travel preferences.

Whether you’re a business traveler in Tokyo or a backpacker in Europe, Samsonite offers products tailored to your needs. Their widespread stores and online availability mean you can rely on the brand no matter where you are.

## Material And Build Quality

Samsonite is known for its strong material and solid build. The brand uses high-quality materials that protect your belongings during travel. Each suitcase is crafted to last through rough handling and frequent trips.

Samsonite offers both hard-shell and soft-shell luggage, each with unique benefits. The build quality ensures good resistance to scratches and impact. This focus on durability makes Samsonite a trusted choice for many travelers.

### Hard-shell Vs Soft-shell

Hard-shell Samsonite luggage uses materials like polycarbonate or polypropylene. These shells are tough and resist cracks or dents. They offer excellent protection against drops or rough treatment.

Soft-shell luggage is made from durable fabrics like ballistic nylon. It provides more flexibility and extra pockets for storage. Soft-shell bags handle expansion better and can fit into tight spaces more easily.

Both types have strong zippers and reinforced corners. The choice depends on your travel style and what protection you prefer.
