## Allegiant Carry-on Size Limits

Allegiant Air has specific rules for carry-on luggage size. These rules help ensure smooth boarding and safety. Understanding these limits can save time at the airport and avoid extra fees.

Knowing Allegiant's carry-on size limits helps travelers pack smart. It also reduces stress during security checks. Below are the key details about allowed sizes, weight, and personal item rules.

### Allowed Dimensions

Allegiant allows one carry-on bag per passenger. The maximum size is 22 x 14 x 9 inches. This includes wheels and handles. Bags larger than this may not fit in overhead bins.

Small backpacks, duffel bags, and suitcases are usually accepted. Measuring your bag before travel avoids surprises. Oversized bags might need to be checked in and could incur fees.

### Weight Restrictions

Allegiant does not have a strict carry-on weight limit. Still, bags should be light enough to lift into overhead bins easily. Heavy carry-ons can slow boarding and cause safety issues.

Passengers should consider their strength and the airline staff's ease. Carry-ons that are too heavy may be asked to check in. This can lead to added charges and delays.

### Personal Item Guidelines

Each passenger may bring one personal item in addition to their carry-on. Examples include a purse, laptop bag, or small backpack. The personal item must fit under the seat in front.

The maximum size for a personal item is 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Personal items should be compact and easy to stow. Larger items might need to be counted as carry-ons and follow those rules.

### Gate Checks And Fees

If your carry-on bag exceeds Allegiant’s size limits, it will likely be gate-checked. This means you’ll have to hand over your bag at the gate, and pick it up at the baggage claim after your flight. While gate-checking isn’t always a big deal, it can be inconvenient if you packed essentials in your carry-on.

Be aware that Allegiant may charge fees for gate-checked bags. These fees vary, so it’s smart to check the latest policy before your trip. Would you rather pay in advance online or risk a last-minute fee at the gate?

### Measuring Your Bag

Before heading to the airport, measure your carry-on carefully. Use a tape measure to check length, width, and height, including wheels and handles. Don’t guess – even a small difference can cause a problem.

Remember to compare your measurements with Allegiant’s official carry-on size limits. If your bag is close to the limit, try repacking to reduce bulk or use a slightly smaller bag. Is your carry-on really the right size, or could it be the reason for unexpected fees?

### Common Mistakes To Avoid

## Tips To Maximize Carry-on Space

### Packing Efficiently

Start with a clear plan for your items. Roll clothes instead of folding to save space. Place heavier items at the bottom to keep your bag balanced. Use small pouches to organize accessories and toiletries. Avoid packing duplicates and limit shoes to one pair.

### Choosing The Right Bag

Select a carry-on that fits Allegiant’s size limits exactly. Soft-sided bags offer flexibility and can squeeze into tight spaces. Lightweight bags reduce overall weight, allowing more items inside. Check the bag’s external pockets for extra storage. Measure your bag before traveling to avoid surprises.

### Using Compression Bags

Compression bags reduce bulk by removing air from clothes. They protect fabrics from wrinkles and spills. Use these for bulky items like sweaters and jackets. Seal bags tightly to get maximum compression. This method creates extra room for other essentials in your carry-on.
