## Spirit Carry On Sizes

Knowing the exact size limits for Spirit carry-ons can save you from unexpected fees and hassle at the airport. Spirit Airlines has specific dimensions you need to follow to fit their carry-on and personal item policies. Understanding these sizes helps you pack smarter and travel lighter.

### Standard Dimensions

Spirit Airlines allows one standard carry-on bag with maximum dimensions of **22 x 18 x 10 inches**(including handles and wheels). This size fits in the overhead bin, so your bag must meet these exact limits.

Think about your last trip—did your suitcase fit easily in the overhead bin or did you have to gate-check it? Staying within these dimensions means you avoid extra fees and keep your bag close by during your flight.

### Personal Item Specifications

Besides the carry-on, you can bring one personal item like a backpack, purse, or laptop bag. Spirit’s limit for personal items is smaller: **18 x 14 x 8 inches**.

This personal item must fit under the seat in front of you. If it’s too large, it won’t qualify as a free personal item, and you might be charged for a carry-on instead.

Consider a slim backpack or a compact tote that meets these specs. Have you checked your bag’s size before packing? Measuring your bags now can prevent surprises at the airport gate.

Knowing exactly what you get included and what costs extra helps you plan better and avoid surprise fees.

### Included Carry On Options

Spirit Airlines offers a very basic carry on option included in the ticket price, but it’s limited. Typically, you are allowed to bring one personal item for free, such as a small backpack or purse that fits under the seat in front of you.

If you want to bring a larger carry on bag that goes in the overhead bin, this usually isn’t included in the base fare. This personal item limit means you need to pack smart or be prepared to pay extra.

### Additional Fees For Larger Bags

Spirit charges additional fees for larger carry on bags that exceed the personal item size. These fees can vary depending on when you pay—online during booking, before check-in, or at the airport.

Paying ahead online usually saves you money. For example, a carry on bag might cost around $35 if added during booking but can jump to $65 or more if you pay at the gate. This pricing strategy is a smart nudge to encourage early planning.

Ask yourself: Are you really saving money by flying ultra-low cost if you don’t factor in these carry on fees? Planning your bag size and timing your payments can make a big difference in your travel budget.

### Priority Boarding Costs

Spirit charges a fee if you want to board earlier and secure overhead bin space for your carry-on. This priority boarding fee usually ranges from $9 to $25, depending on the route and how early you pay. Paying for priority boarding means you won’t have to worry about gate checking your bag, which can be a real hassle if you’re carrying essentials.

Think about how much time you waste waiting in the general boarding line. Is it worth a small fee to breeze through and get your bag stored safely? Many travelers say yes, especially if they’ve experienced their bag being gate-checked unexpectedly before.

### Early Purchase Discounts

Spirit often offers discounts on carry-on upgrade fees if you buy them early during your booking process. These early purchase prices can be significantly lower, sometimes less than half of the fee charged at the gate. It pays to plan ahead and add your carry-on upgrade as soon as you book.

Waiting until check-in or at the airport usually means paying the highest fee. Have you ever booked last minute and ended up paying more just for convenience? Securing your carry-on upgrade early is a smart move that can save you money and avoid surprises at the airport.

### Weight Limits

Spirit limits carry-on bags to 40 pounds (18 kg). Bags over this weight may face extra fees or be denied boarding as carry-ons. Keeping your bag under this limit saves money and hassle. Weigh your bag before heading to the airport to avoid overweight charges.

### Bag Count Restrictions

Spirit allows only one carry-on bag per passenger. This includes personal items like backpacks or purses. Carrying more than one bag usually means paying extra fees. Planning your packing carefully can prevent extra costs and keep your trip smooth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Does A Spirit Carry-on Cost?

Spirit Airlines carry-on fees vary by fare type. Basic fares usually charge $35-$45 for carry-ons. Higher fares may include free carry-on bags. Prices depend on booking time and route.

### What Size Is Allowed For Spirit Carry-ons?

Spirit allows carry-ons up to 22 x 18 x 10 inches. This includes handles and wheels. Oversized bags incur extra fees or must be checked. Check Spirit's website for exact size rules.

### Are Personal Items Free On Spirit Airlines?

Yes, one personal item under 18 x 14 x 8 inches is free. It must fit under the seat in front. Larger bags or carry-ons require a fee. Personal items include backpacks, purses, and laptops.

### Can I Avoid Spirit Carry-on Fees?

Booking a higher fare or adding bags early can reduce fees. Paying for carry-ons during booking is cheaper than at the airport. Consider Spirit's "Carry-On Priority" option for faster boarding and included bags.
