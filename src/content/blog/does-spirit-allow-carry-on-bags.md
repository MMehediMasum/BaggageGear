## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Policy

Spirit Airlines has a specific policy for carry-on bags. Understanding this policy helps avoid extra fees and travel stress. Knowing the rules about size, weight, and allowance makes packing easier. Travelers should check these details before heading to the airport.

### Standard Allowance

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item for free. This item must fit under the seat in front. Examples include a small backpack or purse. A larger carry-on bag is not included in the free allowance. Passengers must pay extra for a standard carry-on bag.

### Bag Size Limits

The personal item must not exceed 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Carry-on bags have a limit of 22 x 18 x 10 inches. Bags larger than these sizes need to be checked or paid for separately. Measuring bags before travel saves time and money at the airport.

### Weight Restrictions

Spirit Airlines does not have a specific weight limit for carry-on bags. The main concern is that the passenger can lift and store the bag safely. Overly heavy bags may be asked to be checked at the gate. Light packing is recommended for easier travel.

### Maximizing Your Carry-on Space

Choose a soft-sided bag to fit tight spaces easily. Roll clothes instead of folding to save space. Use packing cubes to keep items organized and compact. Place heavier items at the bottom for balance. Fill shoes with small items like socks or chargers.

### Essential Items To Pack

* Travel documents and ID for quick access
* Medications and basic first aid supplies
* Electronics and chargers
* A light jacket or scarf for changing temperatures
* Snacks and a reusable water bottle (empty before security)
* Personal hygiene items in travel-size containers

### Avoiding Common Packing Mistakes

Don’t overpack and risk extra fees. Avoid packing prohibited items like liquids over 3.4 ounces. Double-check Spirit’s size limits for carry-on bags. Skip bulky items that can go in checked luggage or be worn. Label your bag with contact info to prevent loss.
