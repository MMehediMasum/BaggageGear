## Read Prevention Bags Explained

Read prevention bags are designed to protect your Ez Pass from unauthorized scans. These bags block radio signals that can read your toll tag without your knowledge. This helps keep your personal information and toll account safe. Many drivers use these bags to prevent accidental charges and maintain privacy.

### How They Work

Read prevention bags use special materials to block radio frequency signals. These materials create a shield around the Ez Pass tag. When inside the bag, the tag cannot send or receive signals. This stops toll sensors and scanners from reading the tag. The bag acts like a barrier that stops communication with the tag.

### Key Features

* Made with RFID-blocking materials to stop signal transmission
* Lightweight and easy to carry in a wallet or bag
* Durable and reusable for long-term protection
* Fits most standard Ez Pass tags and similar devices
* Helps prevent unauthorized toll charges and protects privacy
