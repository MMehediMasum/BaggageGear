## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Ideal For Medium Luggage?



Medium luggage typically ranges from 24 to 26 inches. It offers ample space for week-long trips. This size balances portability and packing capacity effectively.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better For Medium Luggage?



Yes, spinner wheels provide 360-degree rotation. They enhance maneuverability and reduce wrist strain. Most medium luggage models, like Samsonite and Travelpro, feature spinner wheels.



### Is Expandable Luggage Worth Buying?



Expandable luggage adds up to 15% more space. It’s useful for souvenirs or extra items. Many medium suitcases, such as Amazon Basics and Samsonite Omni, offer this feature.



### How Durable Are Hard Shell Suitcases?



Hard shell suitcases use materials like polypropylene or PC+ABS. They resist scratches and impacts well. Brands like Samsonite and COOLIFE provide durable medium hard shell luggage.



### Do Tsa Locks Enhance Luggage Security?



TSA locks allow airport security to inspect bags without damage. They provide added safety and peace of mind. Most quality medium luggage, including Samsonite and SHOWKOO, includes TSA-approved locks.



### Which Luggage Brands Are Best For Medium Suitcases?



Samsonite, Travelpro, Amazon Basics, and SwissGear are top brands. They offer reliability, durability, and smart features. These brands suit various travel needs and budgets.
