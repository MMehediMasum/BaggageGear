## Carry-on Allowance With Aeromexico

Aeromexico allows passengers to bring carry-on bags on board. Knowing the rules helps avoid extra fees and delays. This section covers size, weight, and item limits for carry-on baggage.

### Size And Weight Limits

Aeromexico sets clear size and weight limits for carry-on luggage. The maximum size is 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm). The bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat.

The weight limit for carry-on bags is 22 pounds (10 kg). Bags heavier than this may need to be checked in or pay extra fees. Passengers should weigh their bags before arriving at the airport.

### Number Of Carry-on Items

Each passenger can bring one carry-on bag on board. This bag should meet the size and weight limits. Bringing more than one carry-on bag can cause problems at the gate.

There is no allowance for extra carry-on bags unless you pay for them or have special status with Aeromexico.

### Personal Item Policies

Besides the carry-on bag, Aeromexico allows one personal item per passenger. Personal items include purses, laptop bags, or small backpacks.

This item must fit under the seat in front of you. It should be smaller than the carry-on bag but still hold essentials.

Personal items do not count toward the carry-on weight limit but must follow size rules.

## Tips For Efficient Carry-on Packing

Packing your carry-on efficiently can transform your travel experience with Aeromexico. It saves time, reduces stress at security, and ensures you have everything you need at hand. Let’s explore some practical tips that help you pack smart and travel light.

### Maximizing Space

Use packing cubes or compression bags to organize your items and squeeze more into your carry-on. Roll your clothes instead of folding them; this reduces wrinkles and saves space. Place heavier items at the bottom and fill gaps with smaller accessories like socks or chargers to avoid wasted space.

I once managed to fit a week's worth of clothes and essentials into a single carry-on by rolling and layering strategically. It made navigating airports so much easier, especially when dealing with tight overhead bin space.

### Packing Smart For Security Checks

Keep liquids, gels, and aerosols in a clear, resealable bag to speed up security screening. Pack electronics like laptops and tablets in an easily accessible spot to remove quickly when needed. Avoid overpacking pockets or compartments with items that might trigger extra scrutiny, such as large metal objects.

Have you noticed how quickly the line moves when you’re prepared? Being organized not only saves time but also reduces the anxiety of missed connections or delays.

### What To Leave Out

Resist the urge to pack “just in case” items that add unnecessary weight and bulk. Skip bulky books or heavy gadgets if you can use digital versions instead. Also, avoid carrying large toiletries; most hotels provide basic items, and you can buy essentials at your destination.

Ask yourself: Do you really need three pairs of shoes for a short trip? Prioritizing essentials over extras keeps your carry-on light and manageable.
