### Founding And Early Years

Rimowa was founded in 1898 in Cologne, Germany. The company started as a small workshop making wooden suitcases. The founder, Paul Morszeck, focused on strong, functional luggage. Early products combined wood with leather for durability and elegance. This approach set the foundation for Rimowa’s reputation.

### Evolution Of Craftsmanship

Rimowa’s craftsmanship evolved with new materials and technology. In the 1930s, the brand introduced aluminum suitcases. This innovation made luggage lighter and stronger. Today, Rimowa uses high-quality aluminum and polycarbonate. Skilled artisans carefully assemble each suitcase by hand. This blend of tradition and modern methods ensures lasting quality.
