## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Is Best For Checked Bag Luggage?



A 28 to 30-inch suitcase is ideal for checked luggage. It offers ample space while fitting most airline size requirements. This size balances capacity and ease of handling during travel.



### Are Hardshell Suitcases Better For Checked Bags?



Hardshell suitcases provide excellent protection from impacts and rough handling. They resist scratches and keep belongings safe, making them ideal for checked luggage. They are durable and often expandable for extra space.



### How Important Is Wheel Design For Checked Luggage?



Multi-directional spinner wheels improve maneuverability and ease of transport. Eight wheels offer smoother rolling and stability on various surfaces. Quality wheels reduce strain when navigating airports and crowded spaces.



### Is Tsa Lock Necessary For Checked Luggage?



Yes, TSA-approved locks allow security agents to inspect bags without damage. They enhance luggage security while complying with airport regulations. TSA locks are a practical feature for checked bags.



### Can Softside Luggage Withstand Rough Handling?



Softside luggage offers flexibility and lighter weight but may be less durable than hardshell. High-quality fabrics and reinforced corners increase durability. Softside options often include external pockets for easy access.



### What Features Add Value To Expandable Checked Luggage?



Expandable luggage provides extra space for souvenirs or bulky items. Features like scratch-resistant surfaces, sturdy handles, and smooth spinner wheels improve convenience and longevity. Expandability enhances packing flexibility during trips.
