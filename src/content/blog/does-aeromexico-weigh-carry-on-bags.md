## Aeromexico Carry-on Policies

Aeromexico has clear rules for carry-on bags. Knowing these rules helps avoid problems at the airport. Travelers should understand size, weight, and special exceptions.

### Size Limits For Carry-on Bags

Aeromexico allows one carry-on bag and one personal item per passenger. The carry-on bag must not exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm). Personal items include purses, laptop bags, or small backpacks.

### Weight Restrictions And Allowances

Carry-on bags must weigh no more than 22 pounds (10 kilograms). Aeromexico staff may weigh bags at the gate. Bags heavier than allowed may need to be checked in, possibly with extra fees.

### Exceptions And Special Cases

Some tickets, like business class, may have higher carry-on weight limits. Musical instruments and medical devices often have special rules. Always check specific policies before flying.
