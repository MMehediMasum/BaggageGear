## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Temperature Can A Fireproof Document Bag Withstand?



Most fireproof document bags withstand temperatures between 2000℉ and 6400℉. This protects important documents and valuables from intense fire damage effectively.



### Are Fireproof Document Bags Waterproof As Well?



Many fireproof bags, like Andyer and DocSafe models, offer waterproof protection. This ensures documents stay safe from water damage during floods or spills.



### Can Fireproof Bags Securely Store Valuables And Cash?



Yes, models with zipper locks and multiple compartments, such as ROLOWAY and Noiposi, provide secure storage for cash, passports, and jewelry.



### What Sizes Are Available For Fireproof Document Bags?



Sizes range from compact 10"x13" to larger 15"x11. 5" bags. This variety fits documents, tablets, files, and other important items conveniently.



### Are Fireproof Document Bags Portable For Travel?



Yes, many bags are lightweight and portable, designed for home, office, and travel use. They offer convenient protection wherever you go.
