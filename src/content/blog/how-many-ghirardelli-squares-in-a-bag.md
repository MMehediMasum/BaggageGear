## How To Estimate Squares When Bag Size Varies

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=estimating-the-number-of-ghirardelli-squares-in-a-bag-can-be-tricky.-bags-come-in-different-sizes.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Estimating the number of Ghirardelli squares in a bag can be tricky. Bags come in different sizes. The number of squares changes with bag size. You can find a good estimate using simple tips. These help you guess how many pieces to expect.

### Using Weight To Calculate

Look at the total weight of the bag. Each square weighs about the same. Divide the bag’s weight by the weight of one square. This gives a rough count of squares inside. For example, if the bag weighs 100 grams and each square weighs 10 grams, expect around 10 squares.

This method works well for most bags. Keep in mind small differences may exist. Weight can vary slightly due to packaging or air inside the bag.

### Checking Packaging Labels

Most bags list the number of squares on the label. Check the front or back of the package. Sometimes you find the exact count or a range. Some labels say “about 20 squares” or “net weight 200g.”

Labels also show serving size and weight per square. Use this info to confirm your estimate. Packaging can help you plan servings or recipes easily.
