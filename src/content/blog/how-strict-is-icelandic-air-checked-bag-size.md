## Icelandic Air Baggage Policy

Icelandic Air has clear rules about baggage size and weight. Passengers need to know these rules to avoid extra fees at the airport. The airline checks bags strictly to keep flights safe and on time.

Understanding the baggage policy helps you pack smarter. It saves money and prevents delays during boarding. Here is a detailed look at Icelandic Air’s baggage policy.

### Allowed Dimensions

Icelandic Air sets specific size limits for checked bags. Each bag must not exceed these measurements:

* Length: 62 inches (158 cm) total
* Width and height combined must fit within the total

Bags larger than these dimensions may incur extra charges or be refused.

### Weight Restrictions

The weight limit per checked bag is usually 50 pounds (23 kg). Some tickets allow up to 70 pounds (32 kg).

Bags over the allowed weight will have an overweight fee. It is best to weigh bags before arriving at the airport.

### Carry-on Vs Checked Bags

Carry-on bags must fit in overhead bins or under the seat. The size limit is smaller than checked bags.

* Carry-on maximum size: 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm)
* Weight limit for carry-ons is usually around 22 pounds (10 kg)

Checked bags go in the cargo hold. Carry-ons stay with you in the cabin.
