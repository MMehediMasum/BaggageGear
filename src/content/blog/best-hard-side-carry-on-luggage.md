## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Hardside Carry-on Luggage Size?



The ideal size for a carry-on is typically 20 to 22 inches. This size, like the Amazon Basics 21" or Wrangler Astral 20", ensures compliance with most airline restrictions. It offers ample space for a short trip while being compact enough for overhead bins.



### How Durable Are Hardside Carry-on Luggage Options?



Hardside carry-ons are made from robust materials like polycarbonate and ABS. These materials provide excellent durability and resistance to scratches. Models like Samsonite Omni 2 and American Tourister Stratum 2. 0 are designed to withstand rough handling during travel, ensuring longevity and protection for your belongings.



### Do Hardside Carry-ons Have Expandable Features?



Many hardside carry-ons offer expandable features for additional packing space. For instance, the Amazon Basics and Samsonite Freeform models allow for up to 25% more space. This feature is perfect for travelers who might return with more items than they initially packed.



### Are Hardside Carry-ons Easy To Maneuver?



Hardside carry-ons often come with multi-directional spinner wheels for easy maneuverability. Luggage like the Travelers Club Chicago and LEVEL8 Grace models feature 360-degree spinner wheels. This allows travelers to glide smoothly through airports and crowded spaces, reducing the effort needed to transport luggage.



### Do All Hardside Carry-ons Have Tsa Locks?



Not all hardside carry-ons come with built-in TSA locks. However, models like the KROSER Hardside and LEVEL8 Grace include this feature. TSA locks provide added security and peace of mind, as they allow TSA agents to inspect luggage without damaging the lock.
