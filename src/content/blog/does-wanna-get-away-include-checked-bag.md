### Fare Features

* Lowest price among Southwest fares
* Tickets are refundable as travel funds, not cash

* Boarding position assigned at check-in

### Who Should Choose It

### Carry-on Allowance

Wanna Get Away tickets include one free carry-on bag. This bag must fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. The size limits are usually 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Passengers can also bring one personal item, like a purse or laptop bag. These items should fit under the seat in front of you.

### Checked Bag Rules

Checked bags are not included for free with Wanna Get Away fares. You must pay extra to check a bag. The first checked bag fee is usually around $30 each way. Fees may increase for additional bags or overweight luggage. It is best to pay for checked bags online before the airport. This saves money and time during check-in.

## Packing Strategies

Packing smartly can make a big difference, especially when your ticket type affects your baggage options. Wanna Get Away fares often don’t include a checked bag, so knowing how to pack efficiently becomes key.

Think about your packing strategy as a puzzle. Each item either fits in your carry-on or it needs to be checked. Getting this balance right saves money and hassle at the airport.

### Maximizing Carry-on Space

Your carry-on is your best friend when avoiding checked bag fees. Use packing cubes or compression bags to squeeze in more without creating a mess.

Roll your clothes instead of folding to save room and reduce wrinkles. Tuck smaller items like socks and chargers into shoes or corners.

Consider versatile clothing that you can layer or mix and match. This cuts down on the number of items you need to bring.

### Choosing What To Check

Decide what really needs to go in a checked bag. Bulky items like jackets or shoes often take up too much carry-on space.

Think about what you can live without during the flight. If you don’t need something immediately, it might be better checked.

Remember, checking a bag adds both cost and time at baggage claim. Is the convenience worth the fee? Ask yourself what’s most important for your trip.
