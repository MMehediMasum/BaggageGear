## Care And Maintenance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=taking-good-care-of-your-bogg-bag-ensures-it-stays-looking-fresh-and-lasts-for-years.-different-sizes-might-need-slightly-different-cleaning-approaches,-so-knowing-the-right-method-for-your-bag’s-size-is-key.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Taking good care of your Bogg Bag ensures it stays looking fresh and lasts for years. Different sizes might need slightly different cleaning approaches, so knowing the right method for your bag’s size is key. Proper storage also plays a big role in maintaining its shape and appearance.

### Cleaning Tips By Size

Smaller Bogg Bags, like the Mini or Lunch sizes, are easier to clean with a quick wipe-down using a damp cloth. You can use mild soap to remove any stubborn dirt or stains, but avoid harsh chemicals that could damage the material.

For Medium and Large Bogg Bags, you might want to rinse them gently with water for a deeper clean. Pay attention to the handles and bottom, where dirt tends to build up the most. Using a soft brush can help remove grime without scratching the surface.

Extra large sizes may require extra care because of their bulk. Consider washing them in a bathtub or large basin with mild soap, then rinse thoroughly. Avoid wringing or twisting to prevent warping the bag’s shape.

### Storage Recommendations

Store your Bogg Bag in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent fading. If you keep it in a cramped space, it may lose its shape over time.

Using a bag organizer or stuffing your Bogg Bag with tissue paper helps maintain its form. This simple trick works especially well with medium and large sizes, which can get floppy when empty.

Have you ever noticed your bag smelling musty after being stored for a while? Leaving it slightly open to air out before putting it away can prevent this. Regularly check your bag for any signs of wear or mold, especially if you live in humid areas.
