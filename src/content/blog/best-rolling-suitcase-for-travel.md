## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Size Carry-on Luggage Is Best For Air Travel?



A 20 to 23-inch carry-on suitcase fits most airline cabins. Models like Samsonite Freeform 23" and American Tourister 20" are ideal. They comply with TSA rules and maximize packing space without extra fees.



### Are Expandable Rolling Suitcases Worth Buying?



Yes, expandable suitcases like Amazon Basics 21" offer up to 25% more space. They provide flexibility for souvenirs or extra items. Expandability enhances convenience without compromising durability or maneuverability.



### Which Suitcase Brands Offer Tsa-approved Locks?



Samsonite, Coolife, and Sweetcrispy include TSA-approved locks on their luggage. These locks allow TSA agents to inspect bags without damage. They add security and peace of mind during travel.



### How Important Are Spinner Wheels On Rolling Suitcases?



Spinner wheels improve suitcase maneuverability, allowing 360-degree movement. Brands like Samsonite Omni PC and Travelers Club Chicago use smooth, multi-directional wheels. They reduce effort and enhance travel comfort.



### Can I Find Lightweight Hardshell Luggage Sets?



Yes, Coolife offers lightweight 3-piece hardshell sets in various sizes. Their PC ABS material balances strength and lightness. This reduces travel strain while protecting belongings effectively.



### Do Rolling Suitcases With Usb Ports Really Help Travelers?



Yes, KROSER carry-on luggage includes built-in USB ports for charging devices. This feature adds convenience during long trips. It lets travelers stay connected without hunting for outlets.
