## Backpack Policy At Petco Park

Heading to Petco Park with a backpack? Knowing the backpack policy can save you time and hassle at the entrance. Petco Park has specific rules designed to keep fans safe while making entry smooth and fast. Let’s break down what you need to know before you pack your bag.

### Allowed Backpack Sizes

Petco Park allows backpacks, but they must fit within certain size limits. The maximum size permitted is 16" x 16" x 8".

If your backpack is larger, you’ll likely be asked to check it at the gate or leave it outside. Think about what you really need to carry to avoid any surprises during entry.

### Restricted Items Inside Backpacks

Even if your backpack meets size requirements, some items are not allowed inside.

* Outside food and beverages (except sealed water bottles)
* Weapons or sharp objects
* Alcohol or illegal substances
* Large umbrellas or selfie sticks

These rules are in place to keep everyone safe and comfortable. Have you ever been caught off guard by a restricted item at a venue? Double-check your bag before heading out.

### Clear Bag Policy Details

Petco Park enforces a clear bag policy to speed up security checks. You can bring one clear bag no larger than 12" x 6" x 12".

This bag can hold your essentials like wallet, phone, and sunscreen. If you bring a backpack, it does not have to be clear but must meet the size rules.

Consider using a clear bag for quicker entry, especially if you want to bring more than just a small purse or clear pouch.

* **Stadium lockers:**Located near main entrances, these provide secure, temporary storage for your backpack.
