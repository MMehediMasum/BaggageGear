## Spirit Airlines Carry-on Fee Basics

Spirit Airlines is known for its low base fares and extra charges for additional services. One key fee travelers must understand is the carry-on bag charge. This fee can vary based on the type of bag and when you pay for it. Knowing the basics helps avoid surprises at the airport.

### Types Of Carry-on Bags

Spirit Airlines classifies carry-on bags into two main types:

* **Personal Item:**Small bags like backpacks, purses, or briefcases that fit under the seat. These are free of charge.
* **Standard Carry-On Bag:**Larger bags that go in the overhead bin. These usually have a fee.

The personal item must fit under the seat with dimensions usually around 18 x 14 x 8 inches. Standard carry-ons are bigger and must meet size rules set by Spirit.

### When Fees Apply

Fees for carry-on bags depend on when you add them to your booking. Prices rise as you get closer to the flight date.

* **During Booking:**The cheapest option to pay for carry-ons.
* **After Booking but Before Check-in:**Higher fees than booking time.
* **At Airport Check-in or Gate:**The highest fees for carry-ons.

Paying early saves money. Carry-ons brought without payment may not be allowed on the plane or will cost more at the gate.

## Personal Item Vs Carry-on Allowance

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=understanding-the-difference-between-a-personal-item-and-a-carry-on-allowance-is-crucial-when-flying-with-spirit-airlines.-these-two-categories-determine-what-you-can-bring-onboard-without-extra-charges.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

Understanding the difference between a personal item and a carry-on allowance is crucial when flying with Spirit Airlines. These two categories determine what you can bring onboard without extra charges. Knowing their limits and fees can save you money and avoid last-minute hassles at the gate.

### Size And Weight Limits

Spirit Airlines allows one personal item per passenger, which must fit under the seat in front of you. The maximum dimensions are **18 x 14 x 8 inches**. This includes small backpacks, purses, or laptop bags.

Carry-on bags are larger and go in the overhead bin. They can measure up to **22 x 18 x 10 inches**. Unlike many airlines, Spirit does not list a specific weight limit for carry-ons, but they must be light enough for you to lift into the overhead compartment.

Have you ever tried squeezing a bag that’s just slightly too big under the seat? It’s frustrating and can cause delays. Stick strictly to these size rules to avoid such problems.

### Fee Differences

Spirit charges differently depending on whether you bring a personal item or a carry-on. The personal item is free, which is perfect if you travel light or want to avoid fees altogether.

Carry-ons, however, come with a fee that can range from around **$35 to $65**if purchased during booking. Prices rise significantly if you pay at the airport. This fee applies every time you bring a carry-on, so it adds up fast for frequent flyers.

Think about your travel style: can you fit everything into a personal item, or do you need more space and are willing to pay? Sometimes, choosing the personal item can be a smart way to save money and speed up boarding.
