## Frequently Asked Questions



### What Is The Best Airline Crew Luggage Size?



The ideal size for airline crew luggage is usually between 20 and 22 inches. This size is compact enough for easy storage in overhead bins. It also offers ample space for essentials. Popular options include the SwissGear Sion Softside 21-Inch and Travelpro Maxlite 5 22-Inch.



### Are Spinner Wheels Better Than 2-wheel Luggage?



Spinner wheels offer greater maneuverability and ease of movement. They can rotate 360 degrees, making navigation in crowded spaces easier. However, 2-wheel luggage often has better stability on uneven surfaces. Choose based on your travel needs and preferences.



### Why Choose Expandable Luggage For Travel?



Expandable luggage provides extra packing space when needed. This feature is beneficial for unexpected items or souvenirs. Many airline crew luggage options, like the Travelpro Crew Classic, offer expandable designs. This ensures flexibility and convenience for various travel situations.



### Is A Tsa Lock Necessary For Carry-on Luggage?



A TSA lock adds an extra layer of security to your luggage. It allows TSA agents to inspect bags without damaging locks. This feature is especially useful for international travel. Luggage like the KROSER Softside comes with a built-in TSA lock for added peace of mind.



### What Features Make Luggage Lightweight?



Lightweight luggage often uses materials like polyester or nylon. These materials offer durability without adding weight. Features like spinner wheels and telescopic handles also contribute to lighter designs. Options like the Travelpro Maxlite 5 series prioritize lightweight construction for ease of travel.
