## Privacy Policy

At **BaggageGear**, your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we **collect, use, and protect your personal information** when you visit our website, interact with our content, or use our services. By using BaggageGear, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

![](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?q=baggagegear-may-collect-**both-personal-and-non-personal-information**-to-improve-your-experience-and-provide-better-services.&w=424&h=324&c=7)

BaggageGear may collect **both personal and non-personal information** to improve your experience and provide better services.

### Personal Information

Personal information is any data that can identify you. Examples include:

* Name
* Email address
* Contact information (phone number, address)
* Billing and payment details (if you make purchases through affiliate links)

We collect this information **only when you voluntarily provide it**, such as when subscribing to newsletters, submitting forms, or contacting us.

### Non-Personal Information

Non-personal information does not identify you directly and may include:

* Browser type and version
* Operating system and device type
* IP address
* Pages visited and time spent on the site
* Referral website or search engine used

This information is collected automatically using cookies, analytics tools, and server logs to help us improve our website.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for purposes including:

1. **Providing Services:** Delivering content, product reviews, guides, and recommendations tailored to your interests.
2. **Improving the Website:** Analyzing usage patterns, optimizing content, and enhancing site functionality.
3. **Communication:** Sending newsletters, updates, promotional content, or responding to inquiries.
4. **Affiliate Transactions:** Processing purchases made through affiliate links and ensuring accurate commission tracking.
5. **Legal Compliance:** Ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

We **do not sell or rent your personal information** to third parties.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

BaggageGear uses **cookies and similar technologies** to improve your browsing experience and track website performance.

### Types of Cookies

* **Essential Cookies:** Required for website functionality, such as navigation and secure areas.
* **Performance Cookies:** Track user interactions to analyze website usage and improve content.
* **Functional Cookies:** Remember your preferences, such as language or location settings.
* **Advertising/Targeting Cookies:** Display relevant ads based on browsing behavior (used with third-party services).

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.
